finding is checked against the installed library source or the repo code, not

## Severity key

- **P1**: exploitable or data-loss risk in production.
- **P2**: real defect or hardening gap with a plausible failure path.
- **P3**: best-practice gap, low impact.

Appendix: FK-without-index query

```sql
select c.conrelid::regclass, a.attname, c.confrelid::regclass
from pg_constraint c
join pg_attribute a on a.attrelid = c.conrelid and a.attnum = c.conkey[1]
where c.contype = 'f'
  and not exists (select 1 from pg_index i where i.indrelid = c.conrelid and i.indkey[0] = c.conkey[1]);
```
