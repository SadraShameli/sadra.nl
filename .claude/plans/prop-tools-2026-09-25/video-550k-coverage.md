- V-81, V-82: the substantive error is the model. (1 - p)^n is P(no payout from n attempts); it ignores payout size and so ignores EV, and it ignores the video's own points that payouts per funded account are 0 or 2 (lines 177-179), arrive over the account's lifetime (961-962) and are reinvested. At p = 0.1 with $1,000 per paying $100 attempt (zero EV), 0.9^50 = 0.515% looks safe, yet P(net < 0 after 50 attempts) = P(K <= 4 given Bin(50, 0.1)) = 43.1%. The tools lead with P(batch net < 0) from the compound per-attempt net distribution and path ruin from the bankroll timeline, and show (1 - p)^n only as "P(no payout from N attempts)". Arithmetic footnote: 0.9^50 = 0.515%, so 51 attempts are needed for 0.5% on that measure; at $250 evals, $5,000 buys 20 attempts and 12.2%; "sub 0.005" at line 857 is a verbal slip (the same line says half a percent); with the unrounded 0.33 x 0.33 = 0.1089, 50 attempts give 0.31%.
