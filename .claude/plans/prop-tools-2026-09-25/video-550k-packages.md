## 1. Ground rules this plan adds

1. The video's numbers belong to its author (the author's 150K accounts, per-firm totals and 3.5x). They are never defaults, constants or seed data. They appear only as worked examples inside unit tests, named as such. The user's figures come from their own ledger (VD-1).
2. The tools compute; they never restate the video's claims. Its ruin model is wrong in substance, not only in arithmetic: (1 - p)^n is P(no payout from n attempts), which ignores payout size and so ignores EV; the tools call it that and put the loss risk (P(batch net < 0) and path ruin) in front (VD-26). Its tree totals are also wrong (V-69). The tests pin the computed values (VD-2).
3. The user's own rules outrank the video. The user's confirmed inputs are 40% at a fixed 1:2, a win rate that holds across risk sizes, and independent (not copy-traded) accounts; Hard Rules 1 to 8 stay the documented headline, and expected monthly net per slot stays the objective and the first KPI (Hard Rule 8, Q1, VD-28). Every video-driven alternative (flat-risk warning, take-profit choice, smaller payouts under a small bankroll, split vs concentrate, bankroll-driven objective, reduced risk while payout-eligible, retiring a funded account) is a labelled engine row or what-if, and each conflict is a user question (VD-7).
4. Realized (ledger) figures are display and cross-check only; none feeds the engine, the rulebook or advice unless QV-6 says so (the F-95 display-first rule, VD-5). Realized rates are computed on horizon-matched cohorts so open accounts do not bias them (VD-29).
5. Firm data only from the firm's own live pages. A firm or size the engine does not model is a research item (R-V*), never a fact from memory or a third-party site (CLAUDE.md, SKILL "HOW HE WANTS ME TO WORK").
6. Reuse before writing: Kelly, expectancy, histogram and percentile, the noise check, ended-status predicates and the fee retry primitives already exist; new code calls or lifts them into one shared place, never a second copy (CLAUDE.md DRY, VD-25).
