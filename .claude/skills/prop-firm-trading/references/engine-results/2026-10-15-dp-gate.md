# DP validation gate re-run (PT-30a2)

**Question:** after G2 (closed 2026-10-15), does the average-reward DP's own policy, replayed end to end by `simulate()`, earn at least the best flat funded policy on every DP-eligible plan, on both the credit-inclusive and the credit-free monthly net per account slot, with the same retained cushion, payout policy and request size? This is the gate `DpValidatedPlans` reads (`DP_GATE_RUNS`).

**Engine state:** commit `7037fcfa` for every row. The build PC ran its clean checkout of `7037fcfa`; the Mac ran its main checkout before any later commit, then a frozen worktree of `7037fcfa`.

**Run:** 2026-10-15, about 06:28 to 10:40 UTC, on the build PC (Windows, 32 threads) and the Mac (14 cores). From about 10:10 UTC every run had a hard 5-minute cap (the user's rule: no single run over 5 minutes); runs before that were not capped and several took 10 minutes to hours, some while the PC was paging.

## Inputs

For each plan (all $50K, default size), the DP: `bun run cli prop optimize dp --firm <f> --variant <v> --eval-days 15 --funded-days 252 --rebuy-lag-days 0 --trials 20000 --seed 42 --winrate 0.4 --rr 2 --retain-cushion 2000 --request-size 500 --payout-policy full-request-only --iterations 12 --workers <5 to 10>` (the DP raises the request to the plan minimum where it is above $500 and prints the request it used).

The flat baseline (Stage V): `bun run cli prop optimize funded --firm <f> --variant <v> --ladder <the plan's recorded Stage A ladder, LedgerRecordedLadders> --eval-days 15 --funded-days 252 --rebuy-lag-days 0 --trials 20000 --seed 42 --winrate 0.4 --rr 2 --retain-cushion 2000 --request-size <the DP's request> --payout-policy full-request-only --sort monthly --idle-day-probability 0 --flat 150,200,250,300,400,500,600,800,1000`. Percent candidates need `--stop-points` and were left out on both sides. "Best flat" is the highest monthly net of the nine flat rows and, separately, the highest ex-credit figure.

Every plan floor printed is $2,000 or less, so the retained cushion is $2,000 on both sides. Instrument and stop are unset on both sides. The eval objective is cash at pass (today's renewal objective).

None of these is validated. `dpValidationFor` returns NoGateRun for them.

## Findings

1. **12 of the 13 plans with a full result pass the gate**, on both metrics, by 1.09x (FTMO Growth) to 4.29x (E8 Zero Max 100) on the credit-inclusive figure. Every solve converged within 12 rate-search solves with no unconverged funded level and exit 0.
2. **FTMO Pro fails: 0.43x the best flat** ($372 against $861), and its prediction overstates its own replay by $351 a month (it predicts $723). This is the N-89 pattern on one plan at the default grid; the other 12 gaps run from -$131 to +$137.
3. **The DP is too slow for most of the remaining eligible plans.** Funded-consistency plans (a best-day grid) and the DLL variants of TopStep run past 5 minutes; the non-DLL TopStep Standard plans solve in about 4.5 minutes, their DLL variants do not finish in 5. A faster solver for these plans is open.
4. Wall time per solve above 5 minutes on Alpha Advanced, FTMO Pro and the Tradeify Select plans came from runs before the cap, with several solves sharing a machine; their results are complete and recorded.
