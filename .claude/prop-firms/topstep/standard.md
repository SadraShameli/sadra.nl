## Overview

Topstep's path to funding runs through two stages: the Trading Combine® (a monthly-subscription simulated evaluation) and, on passing, the Express Funded Account® (XFA, a simulated funded account that pays real-money Payouts). Both stages are sold at three account sizes, $50K, $100K, and $150K. At XFA activation a trader separately chooses a Payout path: Standard (this file: 5 winning days of $150+ Net P&L per Payout cycle, no consistency check) or Consistency (a different day-count requirement plus a percentage-based consistency check on Payout requests instead, documented separately in consistency.md). The Trading Combine's own rules, pricing, and Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) mechanic are identical no matter which Payout path is chosen later, so the Evaluation section below is the same shared content as consistency.md's own Evaluation section. The Scaling Plan is a separate, XFA-only mechanic ("The Scaling Plan is an Express Funded Account® (XFA) objective") that likewise does not vary by Payout path; see the Sim Funded section below, not the Evaluation section, for its figures.

Topstep names two different, unrelated things "Standard": a pricing path (chosen at Trading Combine purchase, Standard vs No Activation Fee, affecting only the monthly subscription and the one-time XFA Activation Fee) and the Payout path this file documents (chosen at XFA activation, Standard vs Consistency, affecting funded-stage payout eligibility). The two are independent: either pricing path can be paired with either Payout path. Because the pricing-path split affects fees only, not trading rules, the Evaluation table below gives both pricing paths' fee figures side by side in the same fee rows, while every Sim Funded and Payouts row reflects only this file's own Standard Payout path. This repository's own simulator (`src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/topstep/TopStep.ts`) currently models only the $50K tier end to end; the $100K and $150K figures throughout this file are confirmed firm data with no corresponding engine implementation yet (see the Engine cross-check notes below).

## How the Drawdown Works

The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is Topstep's single drawdown mechanic across both the Trading Combine and the Express Funded Account: "a trailing limit. It rises as your end-of-day balance grows, but never moves down. Once it reaches your starting balance, it locks permanently." The two stages apply this identical mechanic to two different starting points, and the resulting numbers must not be read interchangeably.

In the Trading Combine, the account starts at the full account size ("Your account starts at the full account size (e.g., $50,000 for a 50K Trading Combine)"), so the MLL is a nominal-balance-plus-drawdown figure: it starts at (account size minus MLL amount), $48,000 for $50K (stated literally), and $97,000 / $145,500 for $100K / $150K (this file's own arithmetic from each size's independently confirmed account size and MLL amount, not a literal source figure for those two sizes), and it locks once it climbs back to the account's own full starting size. Topstep's own worked example, for the $50K tier: "You start a 50K Trading Combine. Balance: $50,000. MLL: $48,000. You make $500 on day 1, balance rises to $50,500, MLL trails up to $48,500. You lose $500 on day 2, balance drops back to $50,000, but MLL stays at $48,500" (the floor holds on a losing day; it never trails down).

In the Express Funded Account, the account starts at a literal $0 ("Your Express Funded Account starts with a $0 balance… The 50K, 100K, or 150K label refers to your buying power, not your starting balance"), so the same mechanic runs on a $0-based, profit convention instead: the MLL starts at -(MLL amount) and locks at $0 once profit reaches the MLL amount, not at the account's nominal size. See the Sim Funded table's Drawdown Lock row above for the exact trigger and locked value, including the independent, payout-forced reset to $0 that applies on top of this natural trail. These are genuinely two different conventions for the same MLL mechanic, applied to two different stages of this same plan; a Combine-stage MLL dollar figure must not be read as if it described the XFA, or vice versa.

If an XFA is "closed due to a rule violation before taking a Payout" (an MLL breach being one such violation, not the only one), Back2Funded allows up to 2 Reactivations at the same size and Payout path, without a new Trading Combine: "Back2Funded gives you up to 2 Reactivations per account if you lose your Express Funded Account® (XFA) before your first Payout. Keep the same account size and Payout rules — no Trading Combine® required." Reactivation costs $599 / $699 / $829 at $50K / $100K / $150K (flat, both pricing paths), and everything resets to zero (balance, P&L, trade history, winning days) on the same Standard Payout path as the original account. Once a Payout has been taken from an XFA, it is no longer Back2Funded-eligible.

### Worked Example

Using the XFA's own literal $0-based balance convention throughout ("Your Express Funded Account starts with a $0 balance"), not a nominal-balance-plus-profit convention, for the $50K Standard Payout path tier. Every number below re-derives from the previous step by hand.

1. XFA activation. Balance = $0. The MLL starts at -$2,000 ("For a 50K XFA, your MLL starts at -$2,000 and trails upward.").
2. Day 1: +$500 Net P&L. Balance = $0 + $500 = $500. Profit ($500) is still below the $2,000 MLL amount, so the MLL trails up by the same $500: -$2,000 + $500 = -$1,500 (source's own figure: "Day 1: +$500, balance $500, MLL -$1,500").
3. Day 2: +$1,500 Net P&L. Balance = $500 + $1,500 = $2,000. Profit has now reached the $2,000 MLL amount, so the MLL locks permanently at $0 (source's own figure: "Day 2: +$1,500, balance $2,000, MLL locks at $0"). From here on the account must never close at or below $0.
4. Trading continues under the Standard Payout path's own eligibility bar: 5 winning days of $150+ Net P&L, non-consecutive, plus positive net profit since the last Payout (waived on the first Payout only). No dollar figure in these sources states how many further days or how much further profit this specific $50K account needs beyond the $2,000 already reached here; see the Payouts section below for the source's own separately worked Payout sequence, which is deliberately not spliced onto this $2,000 figure as if it were a continuation of the same account (see the caveat there about which tier it actually illustrates).

## Other Confirmed Rules

These are firm-stated rules that the template's fixed table rows have no slot for, all confirmed in articles this file already cites. They apply to the Trading Combine and Express Funded Account stages documented above.

**Trading-day boundary.** "The trading day runs from 5:00 PM CT through 3:10 PM CT the next calendar day. Trades placed after 5:00 PM CT (e.g., 6:30 PM CT Tuesday) count toward Wednesday's activity." A winning day for the Payout objective "locks in at 4:00 PM CT", and for the Consistency Target "At 3:10 PM CT, that day's value locks into your trading history." Passing on a Friday does not give immediate access: "You can pay the Activation Fee immediately, but your XFA won't be available to trade until markets reopen at 5 PM CT Sunday."

**What a Daily Loss Limit breach actually does.** When "Net P&L hits or exceeds the DLL during the trading day (5 PM CT - 3:10 PM CT)": "Open positions are flattened", "Pending orders are canceled", "No new trades until 5 PM CT next session", and the account "stays eligible for funding." A DLL breach is a named "Temporary Violation" that lifts at the start of the next trading session; it does not end the account.

**What a Maximum Loss Limit breach does, and that the MLL is immovable.** "⚠️ The MLL cannot be adjusted or changed. Topstep makes no exceptions." On a Combine breach: "Your account is liquidated for the rest of the trading day and becomes ineligible for funding until you Reset. You can still practice trade."

**Optional personal risk tools, not the same as the firm's limits.** Topstep offers a trader-set "Personal Daily Loss Limit" and "Personal Daily Profit Target" that auto-generate a liquidating order when hit. Note the firm's own caveat that a liquidation order is not a guaranteed fill price: slippage can carry realized or unrealized losses past the stated DLL or MLL figure before the liquidation completes.

**Position sizing beyond the flat contract cap.** Micros convert at 10:1 against minis by default, but three products do not: "Micro Silver (SIL) | 5:1 ratio vs. Silver (SI); counts as 2 of any other micro", and "Micro Bitcoin (MBT)" and "Micro Ether (MET)" are each "Capped at mini-equivalent lot sizes, not standard micro scaling." An accidental overage has a short grace window: "Errors corrected in under 10 seconds are ignored. Leave too many contracts on for 10+ seconds and your account may be reviewed."

**Trade copiers and account count.** "Yes, up to $750K buying power using a trade copier... Hedging across accounts and coordinated trading with others is prohibited." There is no cap on Combine purchases: "Yes. There's no limit to the number of Trading Combines you can have." Note this is a technical limit only: the Prohibited Conduct article lists "Excessive purchases of Trading Combines or Resets" as its own category of prohibited conduct, carrying that article’s stated consequences up to permanent account closure. The 5-account cap documented above applies to Express Funded Accounts, not Combines.

**Economic releases.** Beyond the CPI window documented in the News Trading row, the firm states: "Trades impacted by economic releases are not eligible for exceptions or Reset credits." The release-time-by-product table in the Economic Releases article is not reproduced here; check it before trading a scheduled release.

**Recurring costs the tables do not carry.** Level 1 data is included; Level 2 (Depth of Market) is a paid add-on at "$38/month", which "Rebills monthly on the 28th day of the month", is "Nonrefundable", "is billed on the 28th of each month and is not prorated", and applies "to all Trading Combines® under the same username". A Reset "adds 30 days to your rebill date". Passing one Combine cancels only that subscription: other active Combines keep rebilling until cancelled manually.

**Refund and cancellation limits.** An accidental rebill is refundable only with "no trading activity" and only if raised "within 28 days of the charge", and such a refund "will result in the immediate cancellation of your subscription, closure of the associated Trading Combine". There are "No refunds on Activation Fee purchases, including accidental activations" and "No refunds on Back2Funded purchases, including accidental reactivations", and "Topstep does not provide refunds or make exceptions for activating the wrong XFA". Passing shortly after a payment does not earn a refund: "If you pass shortly after a monthly payment, we cannot refund or prorate that payment." Cancelling is irreversible, with "No resubscribe option", and a failed renewal payment triggers a retry period during which "account Resets are temporarily disabled".

**Back2Funded, full eligibility.** All four of these must hold: "The XFA was earned through Topstep's Trading Combine®", "The XFA was closed due to a rule violation before taking a Payout", "The XFA is on TopstepX™ in the new Topstep Dashboard", and "Trader is in good standing with no compliance concerns". The decision window is "30 calendar days from account closure", pricing "excludes sales tax", each purchase is "separate, final, and non-refundable", and a Back2Funded-eligible XFA does not count toward the 5-account limit until its Reactivation fee is paid.

**Responsible Trading Discount.** Adding a Daily Loss Limit at checkout discounts the purchase: "$10 off → 50K $20 off → 100K $30 off → 150K" on No Activation Fee Trading Combines, and "$50 off Reactivation Fees" on Back2Funded Reactivations.
