## Overview

The Focused Trader Program (FTP) and Responsible Trading Program (RTP) are behavioral-restriction overlays applied by Topstep's Risk Team to an existing trader's account after a review; neither is a purchasable plan. FTP is the stricter program, triggered by conduct violations (hedging accounts, excessive recycling, losing multiple LFAs, behavior against the Terms of Use). RTP is a support-focused program for risk-management concerns: traders showing unsustainable trading patterns ("Multiple accounts hit the Maximum Loss Limit in one day", max-positioning excessively, disrespecting daily limits, trading on tilt or FOMO) are placed into RTP to rebuild discipline and consistency. A trader can be moved from RTP into FTP if violations continue. Both programs restrict an existing Trading Combine, XFA, or LFA rather than being their own funding path.

## Focused Trader Program

The Focused Trader Program resets a trader's approach after conduct issues or unsustainable trading patterns, and provides two distinct paths depending on the violation type: its Slowdown Path is explicitly for traders "who follow the Terms of Use but haven't found success in the LFA or show patterns of unhealthy trading", not a conduct breach.

### Corrective Path vs. Slowdown Path

| Parameter                   | Corrective Path                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Slowdown Path                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Entry trigger               | Violations of Terms of Use after multiple warnings: hedging accounts against each other to gain funding, excessive recycling of Trading Combines or XFAs, losing multiple LFAs, or behavior against the Trader Pledge or real-world market discipline | Following Terms of Use but unsuccessful in the LFA or showing patterns of unhealthy trading: excessive Resets and Trading Combine purchases, activating and losing many XFAs in a short period, overtrading, or other unsustainable habits |
| Account limit               | 1 active $50K account at a time (Trading Combine, XFA, or LFA)                                                                                                                                                                                        | 1 active $50K account at a time (Trading Combine, XFA, or LFA); current active accounts can remain open but limited to 1 active $50K when closed                                                                                           |
| Immediate consequences      | All current accounts closed; no refunds issued; any pending payouts denied; any pending XFAs or banked resets removed (if applicable); trader immediately placed into FTP structure                                                                   | May continue trading existing active accounts; cannot purchase new Trading Combines, Resets, or Back2Funded Reactivations                                                                                                                  |
| Time requirement            | 6 months of consistent trading                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | None                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Exit requirement            | 6 months of consistent trading AND $10,000 net LFA profits, OR demonstrated good trading behavior (Risk Team discretion)                                                                                                                              | $10,000 net LFA profits, OR demonstrated good trading behavior (Risk Team discretion)                                                                                                                                                      |
| Risk Team review outcome    | Does not guarantee approval to rejoin regular program or purchase multiple accounts                                                                                                                                                                   | Does not guarantee approval to rejoin regular program or purchase multiple accounts                                                                                                                                                        |
| Next step if accounts close | Not applicable: a Corrective Path trader is placed into the full FTP structure immediately on entry, with no prior "keep trading existing accounts" stage to close out of                                                                             | If all existing accounts close or expire, the trader moves into the full FTP structure (Corrective Path rules)                                                                                                                             |

## Responsible Trading Program

The Responsible Trading Program applies mandatory risk-management overlays to a trader placed for unsustainable patterns, without the account-closure consequences of FTP's Corrective Path. It is not a punishment but a structured path to rebuild discipline, consistency, and sound risk management.

### Entry and Placement Behavior

A trader may be placed into RTP based on patterns observed over time by Topstep's Risk Team if showing any of the following: multiple accounts hitting the Maximum Loss Limit in one day; max-positioning the majority of trades; keeping losers larger than winners; trading without stops; going full port or trading on tilt, FOMO, or revenge; or disrespecting daily limits. Warnings are often sent first, but Topstep reserves the right to place traders directly to RTP based on violation severity.

### Mandatory Daily Loss Limit

All new Trading Combines and XFAs created while in RTP automatically receive a Daily Loss Limit applied to the account. This Daily Loss Limit amount is mandatory and not optional, unlike the Daily Loss Limit feature documented in `standard.md` and `consistency.md` as an optional, elective add-on at Trading Combine checkout.

| Size  | Daily Loss Limit |
| ----- | ---------------- |
| $50K  | $1,000           |
| $100K | $2,000           |
| $150K | $3,000           |

These dollar amounts ($1,000, $2,000, $3,000) are identical to the optional Daily Loss Limit figures already documented in the Evaluation and Sim Funded tables of `standard.md` and `consistency.md`. RTP does not introduce new DLL figures; it makes the existing optional figures mandatory for every new account created during RTP placement.

### Consistency Path Forced for Passing Trading Combines

A trader in RTP who passes a Trading Combine must activate it as an Express Funded Account on the Consistency Payout path only (best day of profit must stay under 40% of total profits per Payout period). The Standard Payout path is unavailable until RTP is completed.

### Existing Accounts at RTP Placement

**If placed with an active Trading Combine subscription (not yet passed):** Any Standard Trading Combine subscription started before RTP placement remains Standard. If that Combine is passed, it may be activated as either a Standard or Consistency XFA.

If the Standard Trading Combine is Reset after RTP placement, the new Trading Combine follows RTP rules: it is a Consistency Trading Combine with a Daily Loss Limit (the appropriate amount for its size), and if passed, it must activate as a Consistency XFA.

**If placed with an active Standard XFA:** The existing Standard XFA account is unaffected. Any new XFAs created during RTP placement are Consistency only with a Daily Loss Limit. However, XFAs activated from Trading Combines purchased before RTP placement can be either Standard or Consistency, both with the mandatory Daily Loss Limit applied.

**If removed from RTP with an active Consistency XFA:** The Consistency XFA remains unchanged. Going forward, the trader can choose Standard or Consistency for new accounts.

### Account Limits and Payout Rules

| Parameter                | Value                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Max concurrent XFAs      | Up to 5 active Express Funded Accounts (compared to Focused Trader Program's limit of 1 active account total)                                                                                       |
| Payout request frequency | Up to $6,000 every 3 trading days, provided consistency requirements are met                                                                                                                        |
| Back2Funded availability | Yes. Reactivated accounts follow RTP rules and are created on the Consistency Path with a Daily Loss Limit, regardless of whether the original account was purchased before or after RTP placement. |

### Exit Requirements

There is no set timeline for exiting RTP; progression depends on the trader's discipline and consistency. To exit RTP:

1. Qualify for and reach the Live Funded Account. RTP's own source states only "you need to qualify and start trading in a real, Live Funded Account (LFA)"; the underlying pass-a-Combine-then-earn-a-call-up mechanism is not restated here, see `standard.md`'s and `consistency.md`'s own Live Transition sections for that mechanism.
2. Achieve $10,000 in net profit in the Live Funded Account itself. Per RTP's own source, verbatim: "This profit must come from trading in the Live Funded Account itself, not from a Trading Combine or an Express Funded Account." (RTP's source does not separately address Payouts; do not assume Payouts are excluded from this profit figure without a citation.)
3. Once $10,000 in LFA-only profit is reached, the trader is removed from RTP

## Prohibited Conduct and Its Consequences

The behaviours below sit alongside the two programs above: they are firm-stated grounds for review, restriction or removal, not FTP/RTP triggers in themselves, and they were previously undocumented anywhere in this tree.

**Behaviour that puts an account at risk** ("Professional Behavior at Topstep"): "Excessive use of Express Funded Accounts® (XFAs) and trading irresponsibly within them", and "Threatening to write a bad review to get free or discounted Resets or Trading Combines".

**Account stacking, distinct from RTP's own multi-account trigger.** "Account stacking — repeatedly hitting the Maximum Loss Limit in one account and switching to another to repeat high-risk attempts" is named as prohibited conduct in its own right. RTP's trigger ("Multiple accounts hit the Maximum Loss Limit in one day") is a same-day pattern; account stacking is the repeated-attempt pattern, and the two are stated separately by the firm.

**VPN and geographic circumvention.** "Do not use a VPN. VPNs, proxy services, TOR ... are not permitted at Topstep", enforced with an Error 403 Forbidden. "Circumventing geographic restrictions" is separately listed as prohibited conduct, tied to the country-based eligibility gating documented in [live.md](live.md) and [pro-account.md](pro-account.md).

**Possible responses to a violation** include "Delay or denial of a Payout request", a payout-level consequence for any conduct violation, not only for the programs above.

**Bans are final.** "New profiles created after a ban will be closed immediately and no refunds will be issued." This is the enforcement side of Topstep's Single Profile Policy.

**Chargebacks.** "Filing triggers an account review. Excessive or unjustified chargebacks may result in account suspension or permanent removal from the Topstep program."

**FTP blocks Back2Funded entirely.** "Traders on the Focused Trader Plan (FTP) cannot use Back2Funded." This is a program-wide ban, not a restriction scoped to one FTP path.

**RTP does not cap Combine purchases.** While in RTP a trader "can purchase any size and type of Trading Combine — there's no limit on how many you can hold. Each one will automatically have a Daily Loss Limit." That contrasts with FTP's own explicit single-account cap documented above.

- **Hedging enforcement escalates, and a hedging closure kills payouts permanently**: "After the first Hedging attempt, you’ll be required to sign an acknowledgement upon login on the next trading day... Any future hedging attempt will trigger immediate liquidation... A Temporary Hedging Violation will be issued and you will be prohibited from trading for the remainder of the trading day." And: "accounts closed for hedging violations are not eligible for payouts, and any associated profits cannot be withdrawn."
- **A prohibited-conduct violation has five possible responses, not one**: "Possible responses to a Prohibited Conduct violation: - Warning - Deletion of the impacted trading day - Account Reset - Permanent account closure - Delay or denial of a Payout request." A single violation can retroactively erase a trading day’s results or force an account reset.
- **Appeals are Topstep-initiated only**: "You don’t need to initiate an appeal. The Trust Team will contact you directly if one is available. Trader Support cannot assist with appeals... Appeals can be denied if the review finds ToU violations, fraudulent activity, or account manipulation. If accepted, you’ll receive an email with terms and conditions you must accept to return. Breaching those terms will result in immediate account termination."
- **The card on file must be the trader’s own**: "Use a card in your name — must match your Topstep profile exactly. Using someone else’s card violates the Terms of Use."

## Relationship Between Programs

RTP's own FAQ states, verbatim: "a Trader may be moved from RTP to FTP if they continue to violate our Trader Pledge or carry out the other prohibited trading strategies outlined here." linking to "Prohibited Trading Strategies at Topstep" (article 10305426, a different, already-cited article, not "Prohibited Conduct"; see the Sources footer). This file does not reproduce that article's own list here; see `consistency.md`'s own citation of it, or the article directly. This transition represents escalation from a support-focused risk-management overlay to a conduct-enforcement program.

## Other Confirmed Rules

- **Conduct that gets a trader flagged**: "Being rude or disrespectful to Topstep employees or Discord Ambassadors", "Constantly asking for Resets, freebies, or special exceptions".
- **Group-trading and account-sharing are out**: "Just keep it by the book — no coordinated group trading, no account-sharing, no single-account rule workarounds."

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Fixed numeric trigger for RTP placement** — sources state that placement occurs after "thorough reviews from our Risk Team" and "patterns" observed over time; no specific threshold is quantified anywhere in either source (for example, no stated count of Maximum Loss Limit hits or a time window). Do not assume any specific numeric trigger drives automatic RTP placement.
- **Discretionary approval in Risk Team review (FTP)** — the statement "Risk Team review does not guarantee approval" is qualitative, not a numeric confirmation. Do not assume any specific success rate or set of conditions that would guarantee approval.
- **LFA transition from RTP** — the requirement is $10,000 in LFA profit, not a specific day count or other metric. Do not assume any other measure (e.g., number of trading days in LFA) is required beyond the profit target.
- **Back2Funded Reactivation fee for RTP** — the standard Back2Funded article documents Reactivation fees ($599/$699/$829 for $50K/$100K/$150K); whether the same fee structure applies to a Reactivation requested while in RTP is not stated. Do not assume the standard fee applies without explicit confirmation.
- **Slowdown Path account resolution** — the article states that if Slowdown Path accounts "close or expire," the trader moves into "the full FTP structure," which is interpreted as Corrective Path rules. The exact transition mechanics (whether immediately or after all accounts close) are not detailed. Do not assume a specific timing.
- **Pro Account eligibility under RTP** — RTP and FTP are not stated in these sources to affect eligibility for the Pro Account (mentioned in the Live Funded Account documentation for certain countries). Do not assume RTP/FTP traders are or are not eligible for a Pro Account track without a separate citation.
