# `Topstep` Plans Reference

**Firm website:** `https://www.topstep.com`
**Last Verified:** `2026-10-02`
**Last Updated:** `2026-10-02`
**Source:** see each plan's own file for plan-specific claims; the Firm-Wide Rules below quote the help-center articles and the Terms of Use named in each bullet.

## Overview

Topstep runs a single evaluation, the Trading Combine®, sold at three account sizes ($50K/$100K/$150K), which funds into an Express Funded Account® (XFA). At XFA activation a trader picks one of two Payout paths, Standard or Consistency, documented as two separate plan files here because the funded-stage payout mechanic genuinely differs between them (day-count-only vs. a 40%-consistency-percentage check), even though the Trading Combine Parameters article states one rule set with no Payout-path variants (see `standard.md`'s Not Confirmed). A separate, structurally different product, the Pro Account, substitutes for the Live Funded Account (LFA) for traders in jurisdictions that don't support live market access; it is entered from existing XFAs via a Risk-team call-up and is not purchased; its article describes no Challenge phase. The LFA itself is documented once in a shared live file: the Program Overview shows one Live stage after the XFA ("3 stages, 1 destination") with Standard and Consistency as alternative XFA paths, and the LFA Parameters article states one parameter set with no Payout-path variants, but whether the LFA's own payout requirement depends on the XFA Payout path a trader used is Unconfirmed (see `live.md`'s Not Confirmed). Two more files round out the tree: `labs.md`, a rotating series of limited-availability experimental products sold alongside the standard lineup, and `trader-programs.md`, two behavioral-restriction overlays Topstep applies to an existing account rather than a purchasable product in its own right.

## Plans

- **[Standard](standard.md)**: Trading Combine + XFA, Standard Payout path: $50K/$100K/$150K, 5 winning days of $150+ Net P&L per Payout cycle, no funded-stage consistency check, Max Payout per Cycle capped at $2,000/$3,000/$5,000.
- **[Consistency](consistency.md)**: Trading Combine + XFA, Consistency Payout path: same Combine as Standard, but funded-stage eligibility requires 3 trading days plus a 40% Consistency Objective (Largest Single-Day Net Profit ÷ Total Net Profit) instead, with a higher Max Payout per Cycle ($3,000/$4,000/$6,000).
- **[Pro Account](pro-account.md)**: $50K/$100K/$150K, a simulated LFA substitute for traders whose jurisdiction doesn't support live market access. The article describes no Challenge phase; entered from existing XFAs via Risk-team call-up. Its article states the Maximum Loss Limit only as mirroring the XFA's (no Pro-specific amount, Unconfirmed); its own Starting Balance, Daily Loss Limit, and Payout Cap are separate fixed-by-size tables.

## Labs

- **[`labs.md`](labs.md)**: Topstep Labs, a rotating series of limited-availability experimental products, documented as 6 numbered Drops (#001–#006) as of 2026-10-02. Drops #001, #002 and #006 are 3-phase products like the standard lineup but at different sizes; Drops #001, #006 and the three Challenges use a static (non-trailing) Maximum Loss Limit, which appears nowhere outside `labs.md` in this tree; Drops #003–#005 are 2-round fixed-Payout Challenges with a one-time fixed Payout and no XFA or Live stage. Each Drop is a genuinely different product, not a size variant of one plan, so this file uses one subsection per Drop rather than the single size-tiered table the other plan files use.

## Live Accounts

- **[`live.md`](live.md)**: the Live Funded Account (LFA), shared by `standard.md` and `consistency.md` (the Program Overview shows one Live stage after the XFA, with the Standard and Consistency paths as alternatives within it; whether the LFA's payout requirement depends on the path used is Unconfirmed, see `live.md`'s Not Confirmed). Size and starting balance are derived from a trader's cumulative XFA balances (20% unlocked immediately, minimum $10,000; 80% held in Reserve, released in four 25% increments at profit thresholds). The LFA Parameters article states no Maximum Loss Limit; it states a Daily Loss Limit that expands with profit (Dynamic Live Risk Expansion) or contracts if tradable balance falls (Daily Loss Limit Safeguard), plus a flat $1,000 LFA-balance auto-liquidation floor (which balance it reads is not stated, and the Program Overview states a $0 Rule instead; an unresolved live conflict, see `live.md`).
- `pro-account.md`: the Pro Account article describes no onward transition to the LFA under performance-based terms. See that file's own Live Transition section for the one statement it makes, a policy-contingent notification.

## Behavioral Programs

- **[`trader-programs.md`](trader-programs.md)**: the Focused Trader Program (FTP) and Responsible Trading Program (RTP), both behavioral-restriction overlays Topstep's Risk Team applies to an existing Trading Combine/XFA/LFA after a review, not purchasable products. FTP (Terms of Use violations on its Corrective Path, unsustainable trading patterns on its Slowdown Path) caps a trader to 1 active $50K account; RTP (risk-management concerns) forces the existing optional Daily Loss Limit figures onto every new account and forces the Consistency Payout path, without FTP's account cap. A trader can be escalated from RTP into FTP for continued violations.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Two kinds of rule appear below, and they are not equally sourced. The first bullet (news trading) is stated by one firm-wide article and restated by `standard.md` and `consistency.md`; `pro-account.md` and `live.md` record it as Unconfirmed for their account types because the article does not name them. The bullets added by the 2026-09-19 re-audit (eligibility, Single Profile Policy, VPNs, identity verification, never owing losses back, Futures-only scope, and the trading-day boundary) are sourced from firm-wide help-center articles that state their own scope, and are documented here once rather than in each plan file; most are not restated in any plan file's own body text. Treat them as firm-level, source-confirmed rules, not as facts each plan file independently corroborates.

- **No forced flattening around economic releases**: the Economic Releases article states "Topstep doesn't require you to flatten positions during economic releases — in SIM or Funded Accounts" and does not name Pro Account or the LFA. `standard.md` and `consistency.md` restate it for the Trading Combine and XFA, together with a temporary CPI-window restriction and the prohibition on trading maximum position size into a scheduled major news event; `pro-account.md` and `live.md` list the news-trading rule for their account types as Unconfirmed.
- **Baseline eligibility**: "No US citizenship required", "Minimum age: 18", and "Citizens or residents of OFAC-sanctioned or partner-restricted countries are not eligible to trade, earn funding, or receive payouts" ("Am I Eligible to Trade with Topstep?"). Two distinct country lists exist and should not be conflated: a **fully ineligible** list whose citizens "can't trade or receive payouts" (Afghanistan, Algeria, Angola, Belarus, Burkina Faso, Burma/Myanmar, Burundi, Chinese Military Companies, Cote d'Ivoire, Crimea including Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia, Cuba, Dem. Rep. Congo, Haiti, Iran, Iraq, Kenya, Kosovo, Lebanon, Libya, Mali, Morocco, Nicaragua, Nigeria, North Korea, Pakistan, Russia, Somalia, South Sudan, Sudan & Darfur, Syria, Turkey, Ukraine, Venezuela, Yemen), and a narrower **XFA-but-not-LFA** list of 25 countries named in the same article (not reproduced in the plan files). Residing in an ineligible country disqualifies: "No. Residing in an ineligible country makes you ineligible — regardless of citizenship." A citizen of an ineligible country living in an eligible one is answered "Possibly", after a Trust team review. Travel counts too: "Trading from an ineligible country — even briefly — may trigger a review by the Trust team." The firm notes the list "can change at any time". **Three further disqualifying conditions, stated only in the Terms of Use, not in any help-center article**: a felony conviction, prior discipline by the National Futures Association or the CFTC, or "an outstanding balance with a trading firm" (any trading firm, not just Topstep) each independently make a person ineligible to use Topstep at all.
- **Single Profile Policy**: one profile per trader, enforced across the Dashboard, Combine-parameters and platform articles, with "Profiles or Trading Combines® closed without warning" and suspension as stated consequences. `trader-programs.md` also quotes the Trust FAQ: "New profiles created after a ban will be closed immediately and no refunds will be issued."
- **No VPNs**: the Prohibited Conduct article says "Do not use a VPN. VPNs, proxy services, TOR, geo-location obfuscation, and other identity-masking services are not permitted at Topstep. If you see an Error 403 Forbidden message, disable your VPN or proxy and try again." and separately lists "Circumventing geographical or technical restrictions" as prohibited conduct. The Terms of Use state a further consequence: "Using any VPN or VPS on Accounts is strictly prohibited and may result in account termination and forfeiture of profits."
- **Identity verification blocks purchases while pending**: during IDV "You can still log in and trade during this time, but no additional purchases — new accounts, Resets, etc. — will be processed until verification is complete." Only "a valid, unexpired original physical government-issued ID" is accepted; "photocopies, images displayed on another screen, or IDs with sticky notes or coverings" are not.
- **Traders never owe losses back**: "We don't require you to pay back losses — as long as you're willing to work and learn, we're willing to have you trade." (article: "New to Topstep? Start here")
- **Futures only**: "Topstep is a Futures-only program. We don't offer Forex trading." (article: "New to Topstep? Start here")
- **The trading day runs 5:00 PM CT to 3:10 PM CT the next calendar day**, per "Trading Combine® Parameters". The Payout Policy repeats the boundary when assigning the Payout request day and says "This applies to all account types"; that sentence is scoped to the Payout request-day assignment, not to the trading day as a whole. Trades placed after 5:00 PM CT count toward the next calendar day's activity.

Rules that looked firm-wide but are NOT included above, because at least one plan's own file does not independently confirm them: the Maximum Loss Limit's general trailing/locking mechanic (stated in the MLL article for the Combine and XFA and cross-referenced by `pro-account.md`, but the MLL article's LFA section says only "refer here" and `live.md` records no LFA MLL mechanic as stated); the more-than-30-days inactivity closure (confirmed for the XFA in `standard.md`/`consistency.md` and for the LFA in `live.md`, but explicitly Unconfirmed for Pro Account in `pro-account.md`); the discretionary, Risk-Team-driven call-up to the LFA (confirmed identically in `standard.md`, `consistency.md`, and `live.md`, but `pro-account.md`'s own transition mechanic back toward live trading is structurally different: policy-contingent, not performance-based).
- **Stop trading immediately if something goes wrong**: "If you have an issue while trading — stop trading immediately. If you keep trading after an issue occurs, we may not be able to approve exception requests." (article: "Contact Support")
- **Identity verification is required before a second purchase**: "It’s required before your second purchase and takes only minutes to complete." (article: "How Identity Verification Works")
- **Tax forms are part of the Payout request**: "W-9 for US persons, W-8BEN for non-US persons. Submitted as part of the Payout request process." On 1099s: "Topstep sent 2025 1099s on January 31, 2026, via email to US citizens who received over $600 in Payouts in 2025." (article: "Topstep Payout Policy; Funded Trader Tax Questions")
- **Connectivity problems are the trader’s risk**: "Topstep does not offer courtesy adjustments for connectivity issues. Internet instability is outside Topstep’s control and responsibility." (article: "General Platform Troubleshooting")
- **Day-trading only, with a hard daily flatten**: "Topstep is a day trading program. All positions must be closed by 3:10 PM CT every weekday. You can resume trading at 5:00 PM CT. No swing trading. No Forex." (article: "When and What Products Can I Trade?")
- **Trading must originate from your own device**: "All trading activity must originate from your personal device. The use of VPS, VPNs, and remote servers is prohibited by Topstep’s Terms of Use." (article: "TopstepX API Access")
- **Early-close days carry their own flatten deadline**: "Holidays bring shortened or closed markets. All account types must close positions 15 minutes before any early close. Positions still open at the cutoff are auto-liquidated." (article: "Topstep Holiday Trading Hours")
- **Two further prohibited practices**: "Using Unfair Technology" ("Using software, AI, ultra-high speed systems, or mass data entry that manipulates, abuses, or provides an unfair advantage on the platform or in the program."), and "Trading Outside the Best Bid or Offer" ("Placing orders at prices outside the current best bid or offer.") (article: "Prohibited Trading Strategies at Topstep")
- **A free Practice Account comes with an active subscription**: "The Practice Account is a free 150K simulated account available to any Trader with an active Trading Combine subscription." (article: "Practice Account")
- **API access is a paid add-on**: "API Access is $29/month. Topstep Traders get 50% off with code topstep — that’s $14.50/month, valid every month with no end date." (article: "TopstepX API Access")
- **Front-month contract requirement**: "The front month is the futures contract with the highest trading volume at any given time. At Topstep, this is the contract you're required to trade." (article: "How to identify the Futures Front Month Contract")
- **No holding a position within 2% of a product’s price lock limit**: the Prohibited Conduct article, which states "Prohibited Conduct applies at every level of the Topstep program", lists "Holding a position within 2% of a product’s price lock limit" as its own category of prohibited conduct, and its stated possible responses are "Warning", "Deletion of the impacted trading day", "Account Reset", "Permanent account closure" and "Delay or denial of a Payout request" (article: "Prohibited Conduct"). The how-to article gives the reason and the method: "When a product is within 2% of a price limit, market conditions are extreme and execution is unreliable." and "Stop trading when % Net Change approaches the limit minus 2%." (article: "Staying Outside the 2% Price Limit Zone"). That article states no consequence of its own and says price limits "update at 4:05 PM CT after each session and vary by product, contract month, and time of day"; it points to CME Group's price-limit page ("they change regularly") for current limits, so no per-product limit is documented here.

## Documentation Scope

- **Topstep Labs (`labs.md`) and the behavioral programs (`trader-programs.md`) are now documented**, closing a gap an earlier pass in this same session initially mischaracterized (see `SOURCES.md`'s Discovery completeness section for how that was caught and fixed). Both files deliberately deviate from `CONVENTIONS.md`'s single-file, size-tiered-columns plan layout, for reasons stated in each file's own Overview: Labs' 6 Drops are unrelated products, not size variants of one plan, and the two behavioral programs are account-restriction overlays, not purchasable plans at all.
- **The Reset Credit Bank mechanic** (Getting Started with the Topstep Dashboard: each Trading Combine rebill earns a Reset Credit, tied to a specific size/path, expiring after 1 year) is not reflected in `standard.md`/`consistency.md`'s own flat Reset Fee rows. Flagged, not fixed, in this pass.
- **The Topstep Octagon** (the monthly leaderboard competition that replaced the retired TopstepX™ Live Performance Bonus) is now documented in full (both per-rank payout tables, the net-positive gate, the tie-pooling rule and the opt-out cost) in `live.md`'s own "The Topstep Octagon" section, but is deliberately not modeled in the engine: it is a population-relative mechanic incompatible with this simulator's single-account design, the same reasoning already applied to Shoulder Tap review.
- **This repository's simulator (`src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/topstep/TopStep.ts` and `TopStepLive.ts`) still does not fully match this tree's scope, though several gaps closed since the last pass.** Both files hardcode a single $50K tier; the $100K and $150K Trading Combine/XFA/LFA figures throughout this tree are stated by Topstep's own sources (except the cells marked Unconfirmed) with no corresponding engine implementation. `TopStepLive.ts` now models the Reserve/Capital Expansion mechanic, the $1,000 auto-liquidation floor, and the Daily Loss Limit Safeguard (see `live.md`'s Engine Cross-Check for what changed and what remains unmodeled at that one tier). `TopStep.ts` now includes Pro Account (`buildProAccountPlan()`, `TopStepVariant.ProAccount`, wired into `TopStep.plans`), confirmed 2026-09-21 per `pro-account.md`'s own note; this bullet's prior claim that Pro Account had no engine representation was stale and is corrected here. Each remaining gap is traced and flagged, not fixed, in that file's own Engine cross-check notes; none were resolved silently in either direction.
- **Pro Account's account-type scope differs between Topstep's own help-center articles.** Two firm-wide articles enumerate their scope without naming Pro Account (Holiday Trading Hours: "Trading Combine®, Express Funded Account® (XFA), Live Funded Account® (LFA)"; Understanding Hedging: "the Trading Combine, Express, and Live Accounts"), while a third (Risk Adjustments: High Risk/High Volatility) explicitly groups Pro Account with the Trading Combine and XFA for its own, unrelated, temporary volatility position-limit table. This tree does not assume either inclusion or exclusion where a Pro-Account-specific citation is missing; see `pro-account.md`'s own Not Confirmed section for the full list.
