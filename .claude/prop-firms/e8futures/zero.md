## Overview

E8 Zero is E8 Markets' single-phase Futures product ("Built for Futures traders who want minimal payout restrictions"), sold in two tiers, E8 Zero Starter and E8 Zero Max, at three account sizes: $50,000 / $100,000 / $200,000. Each tier offers either an 80% or 100% payout share, for four total purchasable variants (Zero Starter 80%, Zero Starter 100%, Zero Max 80%, Zero Max 100%). Its one consistency rule (a 40% Best Day Rule) applies only in the SimFi Challenge stage; the SimFi Performance (funded) stage that follows carries no consistency rule, no daily loss limit, and no minimum-profitable-days requirement. Beyond a per-cycle payout cap, its funded stage carries profit-based contract scaling, the 7-day inactivity rule and a one-payout-request-per-day limit; whether the lifetime payout cap is 4 or 5 payouts is Unconfirmed (a live conflict on the e8-zero page, see Not Confirmed).

Per the plan's own article, "Both versions have identical rules; the only difference is that the starter version is cheaper with smaller payout caps, and Zero Max has a slightly higher price but offers bigger payout caps." Every table below therefore applies identically to both tiers except the One-Time Eval Fee (list price: see the sub-table under Evaluation; the 100%-payout-share price and every discount price are unresolved, see Not Confirmed) and Max Payout per Cycle (payout cap: see the Starter/Max sub-table under Payouts). Firm-wide trading policies that apply across every E8 Futures plan (bots/algorithmic trading, hedging, copy trading, the Front Month Contract requirement, trading platform, restricted countries) are documented once in this firm's README.md rather than repeated per-plan here.

## How the Drawdown Works

E8 Zero's drawdown is the EOD Dynamic Drawdown, using the same formula in both the Challenge and Performance stages: "Highest End-of-the-day balance - Drawdown amount (3%) = Loss level" The loss level rises only when you close a profit at end-of-day, never from intraday equity swings, and it does not fall on a losing day either: it tracks the _highest_ end-of-day balance reached to date, not the latest one, so a down day leaves the prior day's loss level unchanged. It recalculates once per day at market close; intraday moves between closes do not affect it. If account equity or balance ever reaches or falls below the current loss level, the account is permanently closed for breaching the rule.

In the SimFi Performance (funded) stage, once your account's closed profit reaches the EOD Dynamic Drawdown dollar amount (3% of starting balance), or your first payout happens (the plan article says "you request your first payout", the payout-request article says "processed" and the e8-zero page says "right after your first payout"; the moment is open, see Not Confirmed), whichever comes first, the loss level locks permanently at the initial balance level and stops trailing altogether, even past that point.

In the SimFi Challenge stage specifically, E8 Zero does **not** carry this lock at all. Per the source's own words: "In challange stage of E8 Zero, the Eod Drawdown scales with your profit, no matter what amount you make. Meaning that the loss level is not being locked at the initial balance and can go further." The Challenge-stage floor keeps trailing upward with profit indefinitely. This exception is stated only for E8 Zero's Challenge stage, not for the Performance stage, and not for any other E8 Futures product.

### Worked Example

This example tracks E8 Zero's SimFi Performance stage at the $50,000 size using the account's literal, nominal dollar balance, not a $0-based profit convention. This matches the EOD Dynamic Drawdown article's own dollar-denominated framing, which illustrates the identical mechanic with a table of Starting Balance $100,000 / EOD Dynamic Drawdown (3%) $3,000 / Loss level $97,000. The drawdown amount used at this size, $1,500 (3% of $50,000), is this file's own arithmetic and is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed); the example is illustrative.

1. You start the Performance stage with a $50,000 balance (Unconfirmed, see Not Confirmed). Highest end-of-day balance so far: $50,000. Loss level: $50,000 − $1,500 = $48,500 (the $1,500 drawdown is this file's own Unconfirmed figure). Cumulative closed profit: $0.
2. Day 1: you close $600 in profit. EOD balance = $50,600 (a new high). Tomorrow's loss level = $50,600 − $1,500 = $49,100. Cumulative closed profit = $600 (below the $1,500 lock threshold, no lock yet).
3. Day 2: you close $500 more profit. EOD balance = $51,100 (a new high). Loss level = $51,100 − $1,500 = $49,600. Cumulative closed profit = $600 + $500 = $1,100 (still below $1,500).
4. Day 3: you close a $300 loss. EOD balance = $50,800, below the existing $51,100 high, so the loss level does **not** fall; it stays at $49,600 (the loss level only rises on a profit day). Cumulative closed profit = $1,100 − $300 = $800.
5. Day 4: you close $1,000 in profit. EOD balance = $51,800 (a new high). Cumulative closed profit = $800 + $1,000 = $1,800: this passes the $1,500 EOD Dynamic Drawdown amount within this single day (it was $800 before Day 4, never landing exactly on $1,500). Per the source, the drawdown locks the moment cumulative closed profit reaches the drawdown amount: the loss level locks here, permanently, at your literal starting balance, **$50,000** (Unconfirmed, see Not Confirmed). Note this is *not* the same figure the ordinary trailing formula would separately produce at this new high ($51,800 − $1,500 = $50,300); the source is explicit that the lock target is always the account's initial balance level, not a continuation of the trailing computation, and the $300 gap between $50,300 (naive trailing math) and $50,000 (the actual locked floor) is exactly the distinction this file's Drawdown Lock row states as Trigger vs. Locked value.
6. From Day 4 onward, the loss level is fixed at $50,000 regardless of further balance moves. If the account balance ever reaches or falls below $50,000, it is permanently closed for breaching the EOD Dynamic Drawdown rule.

(A first payout before cumulative closed profit reaches $1,500 would trigger the lock earlier in time, whether at request or at processing is not stated, see Not Confirmed, but it does not change where the floor lands: the source states "The moment your first payout is processed, the EOD loss level moves up to your initial balance and stays there permanently." The locked value is $50,000 under either trigger. See Payouts.)

No. Each cycle’s payout comes only from profits generated in that cycle. Whatever stays in the account after a request is considered a buffer" and is not eligible for a later request.

## Other Confirmed Rules

**Three days is the practical floor, not a stated minimum-days rule.** "The earliest you can pass phase-1 on E8 Zero is 3 days from the start of your trading period. This is not a separate waiting rule - it is simply how the Best Day Rule works in practice, as 3 days is the fastest the math can work in your favour." The Minimum Trading Days row above therefore reflects the absence of a stated rule, not the absence of a practical constraint.

**Splitting a large win to dodge the Best Day Rule is itself prohibited.** The 40% Best Day Rule article states an anti-circumvention clause: breaking a single large win across hedged positions or partial closures to keep any one day's recorded profit under the threshold is treated as circumventing the rule. If a trader is judged to have done this, the source states "all profit from that position" is "consolidated into a single day" for Best-Day-Rule purposes, which can retroactively push that day over the 40% cap.

**A breach closes the position on the system's own schedule, not instantly.** "After the account breaches your loss level, the system will enforce closing the position automatically, which might take a while. This might result in closing your balance above the loss level; however, closing your balance above the loss level after you breach the drawdown level" does not reverse the breach.

**Minimum Payout Request: $100, with an open net-versus-gross conflict.** E8 Zero's own dedicated article states a flat "Minimum Payout: $100" with no net/gross qualification, and the e8-zero page says "Minimum profit and payout is just $100" and lists "$100" for "First payout and between cycles." The firm-wide "Everything about Payouts" article states elsewhere that a $100 minimum is net-of-share "with every product type", which read literally would make it $125 gross for an 80%-share account, while that same article's own sentence contrasting E8 Zero against E8 One/Signature calls Zero's minimum plain "$100". No source reconciles these, so this file records $100 as the figure Zero's own sources state and leaves the net/gross question open, see Not Confirmed.

**How the 40% Best Day Rule is stated.** The dedicated "40% best day rule (challenge)" article's worked example uses a $6,000 target: "40% of your $6,000 profit target = $2,400". A $2,500 best day then makes "The new effective target becomes $2,500 ÷ 0.40 = $6,250". "E8 Zero Starter and Max" ("no single trading day exceeds more than 40% of your total generated profits") and the e8-zero page ("No single day above 40% of total profit", fetched 2026-10-03) state the rule against total profit instead of against the Profit Target. No source says the two bases are equivalent and this file does not assume they are, see Not Confirmed. Any per-size dollar threshold is this file's arithmetic, see Not Confirmed.

**Live-site facts not in the help-center sources (e8futures.com, 2026-09-23).** The e8-zero page states "No activation fee" (its configurator panel: "No Activation Fee") and "Futures only". Its $100K configurator panel shows Profit Target $6,500, EOD Dynamic Drawdown $3,000 for both stages, Consistency rule 40% and "First payout in as little as" / "0 Days". The embedded config for both tiers (`products.e8zero_max_fu`, `products.e8zero_start_fu`) matches the tables above: `profitTarget` 6 / 6.5 / 6.75 (%) at $50,000 / $100,000 / $200,000 equals the tables' $3,000 / $6,500 / $13,500 when applied to the size labels (a check this file did); `evaluation.drawdown: 3`; `consistency: 40`; `payoutShares: [80, 100]`; `firstPayout: 0`; `platforms: ["TRADOVATE"]`. It also carries `funded.passDays: 1`, which no page text explains; it is not interpreted in this file.

## Live Transition

E8 Zero has no live or broker-funded account stage. The SimFi Performance account (reached after passing the SimFi Challenge) is the final, payout-eligible stage for both E8 Zero Starter and E8 Zero Max; no source read for this file describes any transition out of the simulated environment into a live/real-capital account. This matches E8 Markets' own description of its whole product line: "All accounts at E8 Markets are demo accounts within a simulated environment powered by real market data. This applies to all accounts, including SimFi™ Challenge accounts (Phase-1) and SimFi™ Performance accounts (After you reach the profit target in the Challenge stage)."
