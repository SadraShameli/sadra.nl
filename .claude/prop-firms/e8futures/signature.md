## Overview

E8 Signature Futures is the Futures-market variant of E8 Markets' "E8 Signature" product line (also offered for Forex and Crypto on the same help center; this file covers the Futures variant only). It is a single-phase ("1-Step") SimFi™ Challenge offered at four account sizes, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000 and $150,000, each with its own Profit Target and EOD Dynamic Drawdown amount. As a Futures product it trades on a netting account system (an opposing position in the same instrument automatically cancels out the existing one) rather than the hedging-account system used by E8's Forex/Crypto products, positions cannot be held overnight (the product article states "the permitted trading hours are between 17:00 to 15:10 CT" and "All open positions are being forced to close every day at 15:10 CT"), and the account is a simulated ("SimFi™") account throughout, never a live or broker-funded one (see Live Transition below).

Passing the Challenge moves a trader to the SimFi™ Performance stage, where the same EOD Dynamic Drawdown continues, a soft Daily Pause limit is added, and payout eligibility is gated by a 35% Best Day Rule rather than a fixed schedule. Firm-wide trading-conduct rules (the bot/algorithmic-trading and hedging-across-accounts prohibitions, the tick-scalping and front-month-contract requirements, copy-trading restricted to a trader's own accounts, restricted countries, and standing discount mechanics) also apply to this plan but are documented at the firm level rather than repeated in this plan-specific file.

## How the Drawdown Works

E8 Signature Futures uses an EOD Dynamic Drawdown in both the Challenge and Performance stages: a trailing loss floor recalculated once per trading day at market close (not tick by tick, so intraday equity swings do not move it), based on the account's highest-ever end-of-day balance. Each day, Loss Level equals the Highest End-of-Day Balance minus the size's own Drawdown Amount. The Loss Level rises only when a closed profit sets a new EOD-balance high; a losing day, or a day that does not set a new high, leaves it unchanged. If account balance or equity ever falls to or below the current Loss Level, the account is permanently closed for breaching the EOD Dynamic Drawdown.

The Loss Level stops trailing and locks permanently, becoming a fixed floor at the account's own Starting Balance, the moment either of two things happens, whichever comes first: cumulative closed profit since the stage began reaches the size's own Drawdown Amount (equivalently, the computed Loss Level reaches the Starting Balance), or the trader requests their first payout in the Performance stage. Once locked, the Loss Level never moves again even if EOD balance keeps climbing. The source describes this lock identically for the Challenge and Performance stages of E8 Signature; the "first payout requested" trigger can only occur in the Performance stage, since payouts are not available during the Challenge stage.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the account's full, nominal end-of-day (EOD) balance in dollar terms throughout, matching E8's own convention: the EOD Dynamic Drawdown mechanic is defined entirely in terms of "Highest End-of-day balance," not a $0-based profit figure. As with the Sim Funded Starting Balance row above, no cited source states a Performance-stage starting balance in so many words; this example infers the full nominal balance from the EOD Dynamic Drawdown article's own worked table (which illustrates the identical mechanic starting from a literal "$100,000" balance), corroborated but not independently confirmed by this repository's engine (`FundedStateValue.ts`, which sets the funded stage's starting balance to the plan's full `accountSize`). Using the $50,000 size (Drawdown Amount $2,000): every number below is a full account balance, never a profit-only figure.

1. Day 0 (Performance stage begins): balance is $50,000. This is also the highest EOD balance so far. Loss Level = $50,000 minus $2,000 = $48,000. The trader must stay above $48,000.
2. Day 1: closes $900 in profit. EOD balance becomes $50,900, a new high. Tomorrow's Loss Level rises to $50,900 minus $2,000 = $48,900.
3. Day 2: closes a $500 loss. EOD balance falls to $50,400, below the $50,900 high, so it is not a new high. Per the source ("The system always takes the highest End-of-the-day balance you have achieved"), the Loss Level does not fall back; it stays at $48,900.
4. Day 3: closes $700 in profit. EOD balance rises to $51,100, a new high. Loss Level rises to $51,100 minus $2,000 = $49,100.
5. Day 4: closes $1,500 in profit. EOD balance rises to $52,600, a new high. Cumulative closed profit since Day 0 is now $900 minus $500 plus $700 plus $1,500 = $2,600, which has crossed the $2,000 Drawdown Amount for this size. This is the trigger: per the source, the Loss Level now locks permanently at the account's Starting Balance, $50,000, rather than continuing to trail to $52,600 minus $2,000 = $50,600. $50,000 is also exactly the first number this example started with.
6. From Day 5 onward, the Loss Level is fixed at $50,000 no matter how much further the EOD balance climbs. The trader must keep balance and equity strictly above $50,000 at all times; reaching or falling below it permanently closes the account for breaching the EOD Dynamic Drawdown (the source: "if your account equity or balance reaches/falls below the loss level, your account will be permanently closed"). The same lock would also have triggered immediately, at this same $50,000 value, the moment the trader requested a first payout, if that had happened before the profit trigger did.

## Other Confirmed Rules

**Payout cycles do not accumulate.** "On E8 Signature Futures, for your current payout, only profit earned during the current cycle is eligible. Meaning Unused profits from previous cycles don't carry over." This applies only to Signature Futures accounts "purchased after 14.07.2026 20:00 UTC +2".

**Splitting a large win to dodge the Best Day Rule is prohibited.** Both the product article and the dedicated 35% Best Day Rule article state the anti-circumvention clause: spreading one large win across hedged positions or partial closures to keep a single day's recorded profit under the threshold is treated as circumventing the rule. The stated consequence is concrete: the source article says doing this "may result in all profit from that position being consolidated into a single day," which can retroactively push that day over the 35% cap.

**A breach closes positions on the system's own schedule.** "After the account breaches your loss level, the system will enforce closing the position automatically, which might take a while. This might result in closing your balance above the loss level"; closing above the level afterwards does not undo the breach.

**The Daily Pause 2% limit is not a guaranteed stop.** "It is not instant, so we advise using SL on each trade to avoid losing more than 2%. If you don't use a Stop-Loss at the 2% limit, or if you risk more and due to the delay your positions close at a loss greater than 2%, we bear no risk and you are not entitled to any rebalance."

**Five is the active-management cap, not a purchase cap.** "you can only actively manage 5 performance accounts for each market in the Performance stage at any given time; any additional accounts will be kept in reserve." Challenge accounts themselves are unlimited: "you can purchase as many Challenge accounts as you like, regardless of account size."

**E8 Signature Futures is confirmed to be a real, currently-sold product.** Two general platform articles ("Available Trading platforms for Futures," "How can I change my trading platform?") each state, in passing, that "E8 Zero Futures is currently the only available product for this market." Per this tree's source-authority rules, a dedicated, plan-specific article outranks a general article on that plan's own facts, and the dedicated E8 Signature Futures article plus the dedicated Signature payout-caps-and-buffers article, both of which document Signature Futures in full, specific detail, control here. The platform articles' claim is treated as stale or inaccurate boilerplate, not a live, unresolved conflict.

**The live site's own config corroborates the help-center rule figures (2026-09-23).** The product config embedded in `e8futures.com` (`products.e8signature_v_fu`) gives a per-size drawdown of 4% at $25,000 and $50,000 and 3% at $100,000 and $150,000, identical for `evaluation` and `funded`. The product-level default (`evaluation.drawdown: 3`, `settings.drawdowns: [3]`) is overridden by those per-size keys; the configurator's "3% Max" label is the one shown for its default-selected $100K tile. **Buffer Requirement at $100,000 is $3,000, not $4,000.** The dedicated E8 Signature Futures article and the dedicated payout-caps-and-buffers article both state the $100,000 tier's EOD Dynamic Drawdown, and therefore its Buffer Requirement, as $3,000 (3%). A separate, more general "What is Payout On Demand?" article illustrates the same buffer mechanic with a hypothetical "if your EOD is 4%" worked example, which is not itself a claim about Signature's real $100,000-tier rate.
