## Overview

FTMO Futures Growth is a three-stage programme: a recurring-subscription Evaluation, a Sim-Funded Account earning real payouts on simulated capital, and, for a small, FTMO-selected group, an eventual, fully discretionary Live Funded Account. Growth is the firm's "lower-priced option" relative to Pro at the same size: it "has no Daily Loss Limit during the Evaluation and a soft Daily Loss Limit on the Sim-Funded Account, so a daily breach does not automatically end the account," and "In each payout cycle, you can request a payout of up to 50% of the available profit in your Sim-Funded Account" (FAQ, "How do the Growth and Pro products differ?": a statement made specifically about Growth, not inferred from Pro). This file documents only the $50K Growth size, the one in scope for this task; Growth is also offered at $100K and $150K (see Not Confirmed), and those sizes are deliberately not documented here.

Two firm-wide rules frame every stage below. The trading day is defined by the rules page itself, identically for Evaluation and Sim-Funded: "a trading day is defined as the period beginning at 6:00 p.m. ET and ending at 4:10 p.m. ET on the following calendar day." And positions may not be carried across that boundary: "You must not carry any open position or active resting order past the end of the permitted trading day. Positions or orders remaining open at session close are automatically liquidated and/or cancelled by the Trading Platform" (Forbidden Trading Practices, item 7); T&C cl. 5.5 restates it as "All positions must be closed and all resting orders (including stop-loss orders, limit orders, and bracket orders) must be cancelled before 4:10 pm Eastern Time or the relevant market close, whichever is sooner ('session close') on each trading day."

An engine model for this plan exists at `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/ftmo-futures/FtmoFutures.ts` (`FtmoFuturesVariant.Growth`, $50K only) and was cross-checked against this file on 2026-09-21 per CONVENTIONS step 3; it corroborates every figure in the tables below. Engine simplifications that apply to any simulator output taken from it: hard and soft Daily Loss Limits are distinguished as of 2026-09-21, and Growth's Sim-Funded limit is modeled as the soft one it is (hitting it ends the day, never the account), matching "The account is not terminated"; the $109 Reset Fee is charged on every Evaluation bust except the last rather than modeling the free next-billing-date replacement; the 30-consecutive-calendar-day inactivity rule is counted in simulated trading days, and suppressing the unstated Evaluation inactivity rule also makes the modeled Evaluation trade every day, which puts a floor under its subscription cost; the 3-account cap is applied per plan and cannot express that it is shared with Pro; only the $50K size is modeled; "every 4 days" is not a separate timer, only the 4-Qualifying-Days gate (see Not Confirmed); the simulated payout cushion is $2,000, a payout-discipline default chosen for this repo rather than an FTMO figure, since FTMO states no buffer at all; it keeps a locked Pro account from withdrawing 100% of profit onto its own $50,000 limit and busting, at the cost of delaying the first modeled payout until profit clears $2,000; and the $52,000-EOD-close lock trigger is the engine's own derivation of "reaches the Initial Simulated Capital", not a figure FTMO states.

## How the Drawdown Works

FTMO Futures Growth uses the same Maximum Drawdown mechanic at both the Evaluation and Sim-Funded stages, at the $2,000 Maximum Drawdown Amount for the $50K size throughout. The rules page states the mechanic generally: "The Maximum Drawdown rule establishes an end-of-day trailing limit (the Maximum Drawdown Limit) which your account equity (i.e., Balance + Open Positions P&L – Commissions) cannot hit at any point." The Limit itself "is calculated at the start of each trading day as the difference between the highest account balance recorded at the close of any preceding trading day (or the Initial Simulated Capital if higher) and the applicable Maximum Drawdown Amount," and "can only increase, never decrease. Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Simulated Capital, it locks permanently at that figure": for the Evaluation, "for the remaining duration of the Evaluation, unless (i) you pay a Reset Fee, or (ii) you breach the Maximum Drawdown Limit and wait for the next monthly subscription"; for the Sim-Funded Account, "for the remaining life of the account," with no reset path at all ("You cannot reset a Sim-Funded Account. A hard violation there means you lose the account, and if you wish to continue, you have to purchase a new Evaluation," firm-wide FAQ). The only functional difference between the two stages is the breach consequence: Evaluation failure vs. outright Sim-Funded Account termination.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the Growth Sim-Funded Account's literal, real account balance, starting at $50,000 (matching the source's own "Initial Simulated Capital = $50,000" in this exact Growth $50K Sim-Funded Max Drawdown example). It never switches to a $0-based or profit-only convention.

1. Day 1: Initial Simulated Capital = $50,000. Maximum Drawdown Limit = $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000.
2. Day 2: Closing Balance of Day 1 = $51,500. Limit = $51,500 − $2,000 = $49,500.
3. Day 3: Closing Balance of Day 2 = $51,000. Because Day 1's closing balance ($51,500) was higher, the highest preceding balance ($51,500) is used again: Limit = $49,500.
4. Day 4: Closing Balance of Day 3 = $53,000. Calculation = $53,000 − $2,000 = $51,000, but this exceeds the $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital, so the Maximum Drawdown Limit locks at the Initial Simulated Capital instead. Limit for Day 4 = $50,000, "equal to the Initial Simulated Capital," permanently, for the remaining life of the account.

Re-derivation check, under the stated literal-balance convention: step 1 opens at $50,000 and yields a floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000; step 2, $51,500 − $2,000 = $49,500; step 3 reuses the higher $51,500, so $49,500 again; step 4 computes $53,000 − $2,000 = $51,000, which exceeds the $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital, so the floor is capped at $50,000. Every intermediate figure and the final $50,000 are the source's own example numbers; nothing here is this file's arithmetic beyond re-checking theirs. Internally consistent with the first sentence's stated convention throughout.

## Live Transition

The Growth Sim-Funded Account at $50K has no self-serve or performance-threshold path to a Live Funded Account; none of the cited sources describe one. Progression is entirely discretionary and by invitation: "Not automatic: the Live Funded Account is offered by invitation only, after FTMO validates your Sim-Funded trading" (How It Works). The FAQ "Who is eligible for a Live Funded Account?" is explicit: "The Live Funded Account is not something you apply for. Progression from a Sim-Funded Account to a Live Funded Account is at the discretion of our Trading Department, which will contact you directly with the next steps if the decision to provide you with a Live Funded Account is made." The FAQ "Who is an FTMO Trader, and how do I become one?" frames the same gate as a track-record requirement: "After building a track record of approved payouts, a small group of top-performing, consistent traders may be invited, at FTMO's discretion, to trade an FTMO Futures Live Funded Account with real capital as an FTMO Live Funded Trader," and separately confirms "A Live Funded Account is not granted based on any past trading track record" outside FTMO's own programme. This applies identically to Growth and Pro and to every account size; none of the cited sources describe a Growth-$50K-specific Live Funded pathway.

The Live Funded Account itself is shared across every plan and size and is documented once, in [live.md](./live.md), which this file does not duplicate. Per How It Works, the account: is entered from Sim-Funded by invitation only, at FTMO's sole discretion; requires clearing a one-time buffer before the first payout ("Before your first payout you clear a one-time buffer on your account. Then it's gone for good." No dollar amount is stated anywhere in the cited sources.); pays out daily once that buffer clears ("Once your buffer is cleared, you can request a payout daily"); has no payout cap ("Withdraw your profit above the buffer daily, with no payout cap"); keeps the same 90/10 split ("Your payout ratio stays 90/10"); and carries "a market data fee... for access to live market data - we cover one exchange for you" (no fee amount stated). A separate, Live-Funded-specific restricted-country list also exists (FAQ "Who is eligible for a Live Funded Account?"), on top of FTMO Futures' general restricted-country list; see live.md, not reproduced here.

If the Growth Sim-Funded Account is instead terminated by a Maximum Drawdown breach, the trader does not move toward a Live Funded Account at all: "You cannot reset a Sim-Funded Account. A hard violation there means you lose the account, and if you wish to continue, you have to purchase a new Evaluation" (firm-wide FAQ).

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Minimum Balance at Start (Evaluation)** — not named as a distinct concept anywhere in the cited sources; FTMO's Evaluation rules define only the Maximum Drawdown Limit (a trailing floor). Do not assume the Day 1 Maximum Drawdown Limit ($48,000, "$50,000 – $2,000 = $48,000") is a separate "minimum starting balance" requirement. It is the same drawdown floor already captured in Drawdown Type.
- **Inactivity Rule, Evaluation phase** — the firm-wide 30-consecutive-calendar-day closure rule (Forbidden Trading Practices page) names only "Sim-Funded Accounts" explicitly. Do not assume it also applies to the Evaluation phase; no Evaluation-phase inactivity rule is stated anywhere in the cited sources.
- **"Minimum Balance (ongoing)" row label (Sim Funded)** — FTMO does not separately name a concept called "Minimum Balance (ongoing)"; this file maps that template row onto the Maximum Drawdown Limit itself. Do not treat it as a second, independently-stated FTMO figure distinct from the Maximum Drawdown Limit already quoted in Drawdown Type/Drawdown Lock.
- **Buffer Requirement (Payouts)** — no cumulative dollar buffer is stated by name; the closest analog is the 4-Qualifying-Days-at-$150+ Payout Eligibility gate. Do not compute or assume an implied $600 (4 × $150) buffer; the source never frames the gate that way.
- **Which of the two Payout rules binds at $4,000 of profit** — the rules page states both "up to 50% of your Profit above the Initial Simulated Capital" and a "$2,500 per request" cap, then gives two worked examples for the same $4,000 of profit that disagree ($2,000 under Withdrawable Amount, $2,500 under Payout Cap). No source resolves the conflict explicitly; the page's own "the final amount of your payout is further influenced by the payout cap and payout ratio" is the only wording that addresses stacking, and this file (and the engine) read it as both caps applying with the lower binding. This is a confirmed internal inconsistency within the rules page itself, the plan's own single primary source, not a disagreement between two different sources of differing authority, so it is not resolvable by this tree's source-ranking methodology; no other cited source states the stacking order either. Do not treat $2,500 at $4,000 of profit as confirmed just because the page's Payout Cap example says so.
- **Maximum Total Payouts / Lifetime Cap** — not stated anywhere in any cited source for Growth or for the $50K size specifically. Do not assume there is no cap, and do not assume another firm's lifetime-payout structure carries over.
- **Live Funded Account one-time buffer, dollar amount** — How It Works states only that a trader must "clear a one-time buffer" before the first Live Funded payout; no dollar figure, formula, or relationship to the $50,000 Growth Sim-Funded size is stated anywhere in the cited sources. Do not assume any specific amount or that it scales with this file's $50,000 size.
- **Live Funded Account market data fee, amount** — How It Works states only that "A market data fee applies for access to live market data - we cover one exchange for you"; no fee amount, or the identity of the covered exchange, is stated. Do not assume a specific figure.
- **Live Funded Account restricted-country list** — the FAQ "Who is eligible for a Live Funded Account?" states a country list restricting Live Funded access specifically, separate from FTMO Futures' general restricted-country list (in "Who can join FTMO Futures?"). Neither list is reproduced in this file; see live.md. Do not assume the two lists are identical, and do not assume Growth $50K Sim-Funded eligibility is affected by the Live-Funded-only list.
- **100K and 150K account sizes** — the cited sources confirm Growth is also offered at $100K and $150K (e.g. Comparison Table's 100K/150K columns; How It Works: "Choose Pro or Growth and a size from $50K to $150K"). Per explicit user instruction, only the $50K size is documented in this file. Do not assume any $50K figure here (Profit Target, Drawdown Amount, fees, etc.) applies to the 100K or 150K sizes; those scale independently per the Comparison Table.
- **Engine model simplifications** — `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/ftmo-futures/FtmoFutures.ts` models this plan and corroborates every table figure above (see Overview), but it charges the $109 Reset Fee on every Evaluation bust except the last instead of modeling the free next-billing-date replacement, counts the 30-calendar-day inactivity rule in simulated days, applies the 3-account cap per plan rather than shared with Pro, models only the $50K size, treats "every 4 days" as nothing more than the 4-Qualifying-Days gate, and applies a $2,000 payout cushion that FTMO does not state, which delays the first modeled payout until profit clears $2,000 and so under-reports the small early payouts the 4-Qualifying-Days gate would allow. Do not read a simulator output as FTMO confirmation of any of those points, and do not treat the engine's $52,000-EOD-close lock trigger as a figure FTMO states; it is derived from "reaches the Initial Simulated Capital".
