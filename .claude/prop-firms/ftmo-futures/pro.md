## Overview

FTMO Futures Pro is one of two Futures products (the other is Growth); this file covers only the Pro Evaluation and its resulting Sim-Funded Account at the $50K size. Pro and Growth are also offered at 100K and 150K, each with their own distinct Profit Target, Drawdown, Daily Loss Limit and Payout Cap figures per the Comparison Table. Those sizes and the Growth product are deliberately out of scope here, and no figure below is carried over from them.

Two firm-wide rules frame every stage below. The trading day is defined by the rules page itself, identically for Evaluation and Sim-Funded: "a trading day is defined as the period beginning at 6:00 p.m. ET and ending at 4:10 p.m. ET on the following calendar day." And positions may not be carried across that boundary: "You must not carry any open position or active resting order past the end of the permitted trading day. Positions or orders remaining open at session close are automatically liquidated and/or cancelled by the Trading Platform" (Forbidden Trading Practices, item 7); T&C Cl. 5.5 restates it as "All positions must be closed and all resting orders (including stop-loss orders, limit orders, and bracket orders) must be cancelled before 4:10 pm Eastern Time or the relevant market close, whichever is sooner ('session close') on each trading day."

A Pro Evaluation is a single-phase assessment with no time limit, billed as a recurring monthly subscription rather than a one-time purchase: "Access to your FTMO Futures Pro Evaluation Account is billed on a monthly subscription basis. There is no activation fee." Passing it (reaching the Profit Target while respecting the Trading Objectives) leads toward a Sim-Funded Account (FTMO's own term for the stage the template below calls "Sim Funded"), though FTMO's own sources disagree on whether a pass assures one (the rules page is itself two-sided, "have the option to advance to a Sim-Funded Account" in its Evaluation section and "you will receive access to a Sim-Funded Account" in its Sim-Funded section, while the T&C says a pass "does not guarantee your acceptance"; live conflict 8 in `README.md`); that account "mirrors the size of your Evaluation account" and drops the Profit Target and Consistency Rule entirely. A small number of Sim-Funded traders may later be invited to a Live Funded Account, a real-money account offered "at the sole discretion of FTMO Trading" (Global T&C Cl. 6.3); because that stage is shared across both plans it is only summarised here, with full detail in `live.md` (see Live Transition). An engine model for this plan exists at `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/ftmo-futures/FtmoFutures.ts` (`FtmoFuturesVariant.Pro`, $50K only) and was cross-checked against this file on 2026-09-21 per CONVENTIONS step 3 and re-read on 2026-10-02; it corroborates every figure in the tables below except where Not Confirmed says otherwise. Engine simplifications the reader must know: it models Pro's hard Daily Loss Limit as the account-ending rule FTMO states (hitting the $1,000 limit in either phase terminates the account, per "the Evaluation is unsuccessful" and "immediately and permanently terminated"), added to the engine on 2026-09-21; two gaps remain around that rule, listed in Not Confirmed; it charges the $129 Reset Fee on every failed Evaluation attempt, a bust or a timeout at the modeled `--eval-days` horizon alike (as already noted, FTMO sets no Evaluation time limit), except the last, rather than modeling the free next-billing-date replacement; it counts the 30-calendar-day inactivity rule in simulated days; it applies the 3-account cap per plan and cannot express that it is shared with Growth; it models only the $50K size; "every 5 days" is modeled as nothing more than the 5-Qualifying-Days gate, not as a separate timer, an assumption no source states; its $53,000-EOD-close lock trigger is derived from "reaches the Initial Simulated Capital", not stated by FTMO; and, although FTMO states no Sim-Funded buffer, the engine applies a $2,000 payout cushion as a deliberate discipline default, which is what prevents the locked-account full-withdrawal bust the engine deduces (the FAQ says only that a full withdrawal can violate the Maximum Drawdown, with no mechanism; see Not Confirmed) at the cost of delaying the first modeled payout until profit clears $2,000. Every figure in this file is verified against the cited FTMO sources listed at the bottom, not against the simulator.

## How the Drawdown Works

The Maximum Drawdown rule is the same End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing mechanic at every stage of the Pro $50K plan, restated separately for the Evaluation and the Sim-Funded Account. Source, verbatim (Evaluation section): "The Maximum Drawdown rule establishes an end-of-day trailing limit (the Maximum Drawdown Limit) which your account equity (i.e., Balance + Open Positions P&L – Commissions) cannot hit at any point. If equity hits this limit, the rule is considered violated..." The limit is computed the same way at every stage: "The Maximum Drawdown Limit is calculated at the start of each trading day as the difference between the highest account balance recorded at the close of any preceding trading day (or the Initial Simulated Capital if higher) and the applicable Maximum Drawdown Amount [$3,000]. The limit can only increase, never decrease."

The source states that once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the account's own $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital, it locks there permanently (its own example caps a calculated $50,500 at $50,000): "Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Simulated Capital, it locks permanently at that figure." The two stages share the drawdown math and differ in what a breach does to the account and in how long the lock is stated to hold: on the Evaluation, a breach "is considered violated and the Evaluation is unsuccessful" (recoverable by paying the Reset Fee or waiting for the next monthly subscription cycle, see Evaluation table), and the Evaluation lock holds "for the remaining duration of the Evaluation, unless (i) you pay a Reset Fee, or (ii) you breach the Maximum Drawdown Limit or Daily Loss Limit and wait for the next monthly subscription"; on the Sim-Funded Account the lock holds "for the remaining life of the account" and a breach terminates the account outright with no reset path: "the rule is considered violated and the account is terminated," and the firm-wide FAQ confirms "You cannot reset a Sim-Funded Account. A hard violation there means you lose the account, and if you wish to continue, you have to purchase a new Evaluation."

Pro's Sim-Funded Account allows requesting up to 100% of profit in a single payout (see Payouts), and the source says a payout is deducted from the account: after the request, "the requested amount is deducted from their Sim-Funded Account balance" (FAQ "How do I withdraw my payout from a Sim-Funded Account?"). The same FAQ warns about the interaction with this rule, verbatim: "Please familiarise yourself with those payout conditions to understand them all and prevent violating the Maximum Drawdown on a Pro Sim-Funded Account by requesting a full withdrawal." No cited source states how a withdrawal triggers a breach or when the limit is tested against the reduced balance; this file does not supply a mechanism (see Not Confirmed).

### Worked Example

This example tracks the Pro $50K Sim-Funded Account's own literal, nominal balance, never a $0-based profit convention, starting at its own $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital. It reproduces the rules page's own Sim-Funded worked example verbatim for this exact plan and size; it is not this file's own derivation.

1. Day 1 opens at the account's own Initial Simulated Capital, $50,000. Limit for Day 1 = $50,000 − $3,000 = $47,000.
2. Day 1 closes at $51,500. Limit for Day 2 = $51,500 − $3,000 = $48,500 (the limit trails up with the higher EOD balance).
3. Day 2 closes lower, at $50,500. Because Day 1's closing balance ($51,500) is still the highest preceding balance, the Day 3 limit uses it again: $51,500 − $3,000 = $48,500, unchanged, per the source, "the limit can only increase, never decrease."
4. Day 3 closes at $53,500. The calculation, $53,500 − $3,000 = $50,500, exceeds the account's own $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital, so the Maximum Drawdown Limit locks at the Initial Simulated Capital instead: Limit for Day 4 = $50,000, and "from now on, the limit is equal to the Initial Simulated Capital."

Re-derivation check: the example opened at the account's own $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital, and the locked value it ends at is also exactly $50,000: the same figure, per the source's own rule that the limit locks "at the Initial Simulated Capital," not at some other derived number. Internally consistent.

## Live Transition

Pro's Sim-Funded Account can lead to FTMO's shared, invitation-only Live Funded Account, documented once for both plans in [live.md](./live.md) rather than duplicated here. What the cited sources say about it:

- **Not automatic, invitation-only, at FTMO's discretion.** Source (How It Works), verbatim: "Not automatic: the Live Funded Account is offered by invitation only, after FTMO validates your Sim-Funded trading." The Global Terms and Conditions frame it as a separate contract with a separate FTMO entity (the US Terms and Conditions name no such entity, and no source says which of the two applies to a given trader): "Select participants of the Sim-Funded Trader Programme and select holders of Evaluation Accounts may be offered, at the sole discretion of FTMO Trading, a contract whereby they gain access to an account for real futures trading ('Live Funded Account')" (T&C Cl. 6.3). The T&C is not consistent with itself on who may be offered it: Cl. 6.3 names "select participants of the Sim-Funded Trader Programme and select holders of Evaluation Accounts," while Cl. 20.1 defines a Live Funded Account as one "which may be offered by FTMO Trading to select participants of the Sim-Funded Trader Programme" only. How It Works and the FAQ "Who is an FTMO Trader..." only ever illustrate a Sim-Funded-track-record-first route. Both clauses sit in the same signed document, so no source ranks one above the other; this is recorded as a live conflict in `live.md` and in Not Confirmed below, not resolved here.
- **A track record of approved payouts is mentioned, with no stated threshold.** Source (FAQ "Who is an FTMO Trader..."): "After building a track record of approved payouts, a small group of top-performing, consistent traders may be invited, at FTMO's discretion..." No specific number of payouts, profit level, or time window is stated by any cited source.
- **An offer of a Live Funded Account lets FTMO terminate the Evaluation Agreement.** T&C Cl. 6.3: "Once you have an offer of such an account we may terminate the Agreement"; Cl. 1.2 separately makes a current Live Funded Account holder ineligible for Evaluation Services.
- **One-time buffer, then daily payouts, no payout cap, 90/10 stays the same.** Source (How It Works), verbatim: "Clear the One-Time Buffer... Before your first payout you clear a one-time buffer on your account. Then it's gone for good... Once your buffer is cleared, you can request a payout daily. Withdraw your profit above the buffer daily, with no payout cap. Your payout ratio stays 90 / 10." No dollar figure for the buffer is stated by any cited source.
- **A market data fee applies; one exchange is covered.** Same source, verbatim: "A market data fee applies for access to live market data - we cover one exchange for you." No fee amount is stated.
- **A separate restricted-country list applies to Live Funded eligibility specifically**, on top of the Futures-product-wide restrictions. Source (FAQ "Who is eligible for a Live Funded Account?"): "In addition to the countries restricted from using the FTMO Futures product... FTMO does not provide Live Funded Accounts to clients residing in the countries listed below... Clients in these countries can still enjoy the full FTMO Futures experience on a Sim-Funded Account, including payouts. Only the transition to a Live Funded Account is unavailable." The list itself is documented in full in `live.md`, not reproduced here.

This file does not model or assert any Live Funded parameter (buffer size, contract limits, drawdown amount) beyond what is quoted above; see `live.md` and Not Confirmed.

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Minimum Balance at Start (Evaluation)** — not stated in any cited source as a concept distinct from the account's own $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital. Do not assume any threshold below $50,000 is either required or permitted at the start of a Pro $50K Evaluation.
- **Inactivity Rule (Evaluation stage)** — the only inactivity rule stated anywhere in the cited sources names "Sim-Funded Accounts" specifically ("30 consecutive calendar days"). Do not assume the same 30-day closure rule applies to an Evaluation Account; it may be unrestricted, restricted differently, or simply undocumented.
- **Minimum Balance (ongoing), Sim Funded** — FTMO's own rule is framed as an equity floor (the Maximum Drawdown Limit, "your account equity... cannot hit"), not a separately named "Minimum Balance." This file's table row is therefore Unconfirmed; do not assume "Minimum Balance" is an FTMO-named concept distinct from the Maximum Drawdown Limit, and do not confuse the equity-based trigger with a pure balance-based one.
- **Buffer Requirement, Sim-Funded stage** — no cumulative dollar buffer is stated for the Sim-Funded Account. FTMO's only stated "buffer" is the Live Funded Account's one-time buffer (see Live Transition), a different, live-stage-only concept with no dollar figure of its own stated either. Do not assume the Live Funded buffer concept, or any inferred dollar figure, applies to the Sim-Funded stage.
- **Maximum Total Payouts / Lifetime Cap** — not stated by any cited source for the Sim-Funded Account. "No payout cap" is stated only for the post-buffer Live Funded stage (a different, live-only claim: see Live Transition), not for the Sim-Funded Account. Do not assume no lifetime cap exists on the Sim-Funded stage; absence of a stated figure is not confirmation of "unlimited."
- **How the modeled hard Daily Loss Limit differs from the real one** — the engine terminates a Pro account the moment the day's closed P&L reaches -$1,000, which is FTMO's stated trigger ("If the equity hits this limit, the rule is considered violated"). Two differences remain, neither stated by FTMO and neither to be read as confirmed: the engine marks to market only at trade close, so an open position excursing through the limit and recovering before the stop fills is invisible to it, even though FTMO's limit is explicitly on equity including open-position P&L; and because the engine sizes the last trade of a losing day down to the remaining headroom net of its round-trip commission, a Pro account busts only when the day's simulated losses and commissions reach the full $1,000, which makes the modeled bust rate a property of the simulated strategy as much as of FTMO's rule. Per-trade risk is capped net of the round-trip commission (audit fix N-56), so a full-loss day closes exactly on the $1,000 limit, not one commission past it. Do not assume the engine's modeled bust rate is a direct measurement of how often FTMO's own $1,000 Daily Loss Limit would trigger in live conditions; it is also a function of this repo's own execution-timing and position-sizing choices, not stated by FTMO.
- **Exact mechanics of a payout-triggered Maximum Drawdown breach** — the FAQ "How do I withdraw my payout from a Sim-Funded Account?" warns against "violating the Maximum Drawdown on a Pro Sim-Funded Account by requesting a full withdrawal," but no source states the exact arithmetic (e.g., whether the Drawdown Limit is recalculated immediately on withdrawal or only at the next trading day's open). This file's "How the Drawdown Works" section quotes the FAQ's warning and supplies no mechanism or triggering formula; do not assume one. The engine (`FtmoFutures.ts`) does deduce one specific outcome from the quoted rules: after the limit locks at $50,000, a 100% withdrawal leaves the balance exactly on the limit and the account is bust the next simulated day, while no pre-lock withdrawal can breach. That is why the engine models a $2,000 retained payout cushion: at a $0 cushion a default Pro run busts at its first post-lock payout in ~99.9% of trials, which is the modeled form of FTMO's warning rather than a rule FTMO states. Do not assume that deduction is an FTMO-stated mechanic.
