## Overview

The Live Funded Account is Phase 3 of the FTMO Futures journey, shared identically by Growth and Pro at every size, and documented once here rather than duplicated in [growth.md](./growth.md) or [pro.md](./pro.md) (both link to this file). Per the FAQ "Who is an FTMO Trader, and how do I become one?": "Phase 3: Live Funded Account. After building a track record of approved payouts, a small group of top-performing, consistent traders may be invited, at FTMO's discretion, to trade an FTMO Futures Live Funded Account with real capital as an FTMO Live Funded Trader." It is a real-money account, not a further simulated stage: "A small number of top-performing traders may later be moved to a Live Funded Account, which is a real-money account, where futures trading is done with actual capital" (FAQ, "How does a Sim-Funded Account work from the technical side?"). It is never granted on the strength of a trader's history outside FTMO's own programme: "it is not possible to obtain a Sim-Funded or Live Funded Account based on any external trading track record" (FAQ, "How to Start?"), and separately, "A Live Funded Account is not granted based on any past trading track record" (FAQ, "Who is an FTMO Trader..."). How It Works frames the stage's own remaining rule-set at its narrowest of the whole programme: "The only Trading Objective left is your Max Drawdown."

This file documents only what the two cited source bundles state about the Live Funded Account itself. Growth and Pro are also offered at $100K and $150K in addition to the $50K size this repo's tree otherwise documents (see growth.md's and pro.md's own Not Confirmed sections). The only cited statement linking the Live Funded Account to the trader's earlier choices is T&C Cl. 3.2, which ties it to the Order's Options (the Evaluation version and Initial Simulated Capital selected at purchase) without giving any figure or formula: "The Options you select also provide the basis for the subsequent Sim-Funded Account and Live Funded Account (see Clause 6 (FTMO Futures Trader Programme)). Once you finalise the Order, you may not change Options for that Order." No source states what "the basis" means in dollar terms, so this file carries no size-specific figure, confirmed or excluded. No dollar figure for a starting balance, drawdown amount, Daily Loss Limit, one-time buffer, or market data fee is stated anywhere in the cited sources — see "Live Funded Account: Trading Objectives and Risk Parameters" and "Not Confirmed By This Source" below. Because no such figure is confirmed, this file contains no Worked Example: fabricating one from an unconfirmed number would violate this file's own sourcing standard, so none is included.

## Eligibility and Invitation

The Live Funded Account cannot be applied for; it is offered, not requested. Per the FAQ "Who is eligible for a Live Funded Account?": "The Live Funded Account is not something you apply for. Progression from a Sim-Funded Account to a Live Funded Account is at the discretion of our Trading Department, which will contact you directly with the next steps if the decision to provide you with a Live Funded Account is made." The same discretion is restated across multiple firm-wide pages: "this progression is solely at FTMO's discretion" (FAQ, "Why should I join FTMO?"); "progression to a Live Funded Account is solely at FTMO's discretion" (FAQ, "How does a Sim-Funded Account work from the technical side?"); "this step is solely based on FTMO's individual assessment" (FAQ, "What is FTMO?"). How It Works names the one precondition the firm actually states, a validation step: "Not automatic: the Live Funded Account is offered by invitation only, after FTMO validates your Sim-Funded trading." No specific number of payouts, profit level, or time window is stated by any cited source as the trigger — only "a track record of approved payouts" and that validation.

An FTMO Live Funded Trader is defined narrowly: "An FTMO Live Funded Trader is an FTMO client who manages a Live Funded Account," and "A Sim-Funded Account and a Live Funded Account are accounts in which the FTMO client may be eligible for monetary reward, provided the rules are followed" (FAQ, "Who is an FTMO Trader..."). FTMO Futures overall frames the stage the same way in its own marketing copy: "Be the best. Only the top performers will be considered for a Live Account" (<https://ftmo.com/en/futures/>).

## Contract Structure: Two FTMO Entities

The Evaluation Terms and Conditions (Global), dated 31 August 2026, make the Live Funded Account a contract with a different FTMO legal entity than the one governing the Evaluation:

- The Evaluation itself is provided by **FTMO Evaluation Global s.r.o.** ("we," per the T&C's own introductory recital and Cl. 20.1's definition of "we").
- The Sim-Funded Account and the Live Funded Account are both provided by **FTMO Trading Global s.r.o.** ("FTMO Trading"), a separate company under the same jurisdiction: "'FTMO Trading' means FTMO Trading Global s.r.o., a company established and existing under the laws of the Czech Republic... Identification No.: 094 18 415" (Cl. 20.1).

Clause 6.3 states the operative offer mechanic verbatim: "Select participants of the Sim-Funded Trader Programme and select holders of Evaluation Accounts may be offered, at the sole discretion of FTMO Trading, a contract whereby they gain access to an account for real futures trading ('Live Funded Account'). Once you have an offer of such an account we may terminate the Agreement." Clause 12.2.2(e) restates the same termination trigger from the Evaluation Agreement's own side: "We may terminate the Agreement with immediate effect: ... (e) if you are offered a Live Funded Account as set out in Clause 6.3." Separately, Clause 1.2 closes the loop for a new Evaluation purchase: "In addition, you are not eligible to access the Services if you currently have a Live Funded Account (see Clause 6.3)." The contract for the Live Funded Account itself is between the trader and FTMO Trading directly, not FTMO Evaluation Global: "The terms and conditions and agreement between you and FTMO Trading are solely between you and FTMO Trading and you will be advised to read those documents carefully before agreeing to be bound by the terms and conditions" (Cl. 6.4).

**An internal inconsistency in the T&C's own text, not resolved by any cited source:** the Definitions section states a narrower eligible population than the operative clause it defines. Cl. 20.1 defines: "'Live Funded Account' means an account for real trading on financial markets which may be offered by FTMO Trading to select participants of the Sim-Funded Trader Programme" — Sim-Funded participants only. Cl. 6.3, quoted above, is broader: "select participants of the Sim-Funded Trader Programme **and** select holders of Evaluation Accounts." Neither clause explains the discrepancy. Every marketing-facing source describes the practical path as strictly Sim-Funded track record → invitation (see Eligibility and Invitation, above); this file does not treat Cl. 6.3's broader "Evaluation Account holders" language as confirmation that a trader can ordinarily be invited to a Live Funded Account straight from an Evaluation Account — see Not Confirmed.

## Country Eligibility for the Live Funded Account

A separate, Live-Funded-specific restricted-country list applies on top of FTMO Futures' general country restrictions. Per the FAQ "Who is eligible for a Live Funded Account?": "In addition to the countries restricted from using the FTMO Futures product, as described in the FAQ 'Who can use FTMO Futures?', FTMO does not provide Live Funded Accounts to clients residing in the countries listed below." A trader in one of these countries is not simply cut off, however: "Traders who are considered eligible for the Live Funded Account but reside in one of the listed countries may be contacted directly by our Trading Department, which will assess the available options and provide the FTMO Trader with the next steps. Clients in these countries can still enjoy the full FTMO Futures experience on a Sim-Funded Account, including payouts. Only the transition to a Live Funded Account is unavailable." The list itself: "This list is subject to internal business decisions and changes in compliance with evolving legal and regulatory standards."

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Starting Balance / Account Size derivation** — no cited source states a dollar figure for the Live Funded Account's size or a formula for it. T&C Cl. 3.2 says only that the Order's Options "provide the basis for the subsequent Sim-Funded Account and Live Funded Account," without saying how. Do not assume it equals $50,000, mirrors the Sim-Funded balance at the moment of the offer, or scales with the trader's prior Growth/Pro account size in any particular way.
- **Drawdown amount and mechanic** — How It Works confirms only that "The only Trading Objective left is your Max Drawdown," not a dollar amount, an End-of-Day Trailing calculation, or a lock-trigger/locked-value pair. Do not assume the $2,000 (Growth) or $3,000 (Pro) Sim-Funded Drawdown Amount, or the EOD-Trailing-with-lock mechanic documented in growth.md/pro.md, carries over to the Live Funded Account.
- **Daily Loss Limit** — not stated anywhere in the cited sources, including whether one exists at all. Do not assume the Growth ($1,000, Soft) or Pro ($1,000, Hard) Sim-Funded Daily Loss Limit carries over.
- **Max Contracts** — not stated anywhere in the cited sources. Do not assume the 5-contract Evaluation/Sim-Funded ceiling at the $50K size applies unchanged to the Live Funded Account.
- **One-Time Buffer, dollar amount** — How It Works states only that a trader must "clear a one-time buffer" before the first Live Funded payout; no dollar figure, formula, or relationship to the Growth/Pro Sim-Funded balance is stated. Do not assume any specific amount or that it scales with the $50,000 size this tree otherwise documents.
- **Market data fee, amount and covered exchange** — How It Works states only that "we cover one exchange for you"; no fee amount or the identity of the default covered exchange is stated. Do not assume a specific figure or exchange.
- **Minimum Payout Request (Live)** — the $20 minimum is stated only for the Sim-Funded Account (see growth.md, pro.md). No cited source restates, confirms, or varies this figure for the Live Funded Account. Do not assume it carries over unchanged.
- **Consistency on Payouts (Live)** — not stated anywhere in the cited sources. Do not assume none exists merely because none is mentioned, and do not assume the Evaluation-phase Consistency Rule percentages (40% Growth / 50% Pro) apply here; that rule is separately confirmed as Evaluation-only (see the table above).
- **"No payout cap" as covering both per-cycle and lifetime caps** — How It Works states "no payout cap" once, without separately addressing a per-request/per-cycle cap versus a cumulative lifetime cap the way growth.md/pro.md's Sim-Funded tables do (each stating a specific per-request Payout Cap). This file treats the single, unqualified statement as covering both; do not treat that as a second, independently confirmed lifetime-cap statement.
- **T&C Definitions (Cl. 20.1) vs. Cl. 6.3 scope mismatch** — the definition of "Live Funded Account" names only "select participants of the Sim-Funded Trader Programme" as eligible, while the operative Cl. 6.3 also includes "select holders of Evaluation Accounts." Neither the T&C nor any other cited source reconciles this. Do not assume an Evaluation-stage trader (one who has not yet reached a Sim-Funded Account) can ordinarily be invited directly to a Live Funded Account; every marketing description of the actual path (How It Works, the FAQ "Who is an FTMO Trader...") describes it strictly as Sim-Funded track record → invitation.
- **Scope of the Forbidden Trading Practices page for Live Funded trading** — that page states its own mandatory scope as "the Evaluation and the Sim-Funded Account" only, yet the same page separately states, as a direct statement about Live, "There is no inactivity rule on Live Funded Accounts." Do not assume the rest of that page's forbidden-practices list is confirmed to extend to Live Funded trading beyond this one explicit carve-out.
- **"Any phase" news-trading statement, Live-specific confirmation** — the FAQ "Can I trade news?" states news trading is allowed "during any phase" without naming the Live Funded Account specifically (unlike the explicit, Live-named inactivity-rule carve-out above). Treated here as covering Live given the unqualified wording, but no source names the Live Funded Account in this specific FAQ; do not treat this as being as firmly confirmed as the inactivity-rule statement.
- **100K and 150K Growth/Pro sizes** — both exist upstream of this shared Live Funded stage (see growth.md's and pro.md's own Not Confirmed sections); per explicit user instruction, this tree documents only the $50K Growth/Pro size. T&C Cl. 3.2 ties the Live Funded Account to the Order's Options in principle ("provide the basis for") but no cited source gives any size-specific Live Funded figure, so nothing here is confirmed or excluded per size. Do not assume any Live Funded figure (once confirmed elsewhere) is fixed at a single value across all upstream sizes, or that it scales with them, just because none is documented here.
- **Engine model** — `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/ftmo-futures/FtmoFutures.ts` models only the Evaluation and Sim-Funded stages and deliberately provides no Live Funded Account plan. This file cannot be cross-checked against a simulator implementation, and no live model should be assumed to exist.
