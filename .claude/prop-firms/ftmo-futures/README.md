## Overview

FTMO Futures is the futures product line of FTMO (FTMO Evaluation Global s.r.o., Prague; a separate product from FTMO's CFD "FTMO Challenge," which this tree does not cover). It launched publicly on 27 August 2026 per the firm's own Trading Updates page. There are two products, Growth and Pro, each sold as a single-phase Evaluation on a recurring monthly subscription (no activation fee, no minimum trading days, no time limit), each available at $50K, $100K, and $150K. Passing the Evaluation leads to a Sim-Funded Account of the same size (simulated capital, real payouts, no Profit Target, no Consistency Rule), and a small, FTMO-selected group of Sim-Funded traders may later be invited, at FTMO's sole discretion, to a real-money Live Funded Account. The Sim-Funded and Live Funded stages are contracted with a different FTMO entity (FTMO Trading Global s.r.o.) under a separate, non-public Sim-Funded Account Terms & Conditions.

By explicit instruction for this pass, **only the $50K size of each product is documented**. The $100K and $150K sizes exist and have their own distinct figures (Profit Target, Max Drawdown, Daily Loss Limit, Max Contracts, fees, Payout Cap, Payout Eligibility) on the firm's Comparison Table; none of those figures appear anywhere in this tree.

## Plans

- **[Growth](growth.md)**: $50K. $119/month, $109 Reset. Profit Target $3,000; EOD Trailing Max Drawdown $2,000 locking at the $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital; no Daily Loss Limit in the Evaluation, a soft $1,000 Daily Loss Limit on the Sim-Funded Account; 40% Consistency Rule (Evaluation only). Payouts every 4 days after 4 Qualifying Days of $150+, up to 50% of profit per cycle, capped at $2,500 per request, 90/10 split.
- **[Pro](pro.md)**: $50K. $139/month, $129 Reset. Profit Target $3,000; EOD Trailing Max Drawdown $3,000 locking at the $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital; hard $1,000 Daily Loss Limit in both the Evaluation and the Sim-Funded Account; 50% Consistency Rule (Evaluation only). Payouts every 5 days after 5 Qualifying Days of $200+, up to 100% of profit per cycle, capped at $5,000 per request, 90/10 split.

## Live Accounts

- **[Live Funded Account](live.md)**: shared by `growth.md` and `pro.md` (both transition into the same invitation-only stage). Documented from the firm's own statements only: offered at FTMO Trading's sole discretion after FTMO "validates your Sim-Funded trading"; a one-time buffer must be cleared before the first payout, then payouts are daily with no payout cap at the same 90/10 split; a market data fee applies with one exchange covered; no inactivity rule; a separate Live-Funded-only restricted-country list applies (reproduced in full in `live.md`). No dollar figure for its size, drawdown, Daily Loss Limit, buffer, or market data fee is stated by any source, so `live.md` carries no numbers and no worked example.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Two kinds of rule appear below. The first group is confirmed independently in both `growth.md` and `pro.md` from each plan's own cited sources (the rules page for that plan, the T&C, or a firm-wide page each file cites itself). The second group comes from firm-wide FAQ or policy pages that state their own scope and that no plan file restates in its own body; they are documented here once rather than in each plan file. Treat the second group as firm-level, source-confirmed rules, not as facts each plan file independently corroborates.

**Confirmed in both plan files:**

- **The trading day runs 6:00 p.m. ET to 4:10 p.m. ET the next calendar day**, stated identically on both plans' own rules pages for both the Evaluation and Sim-Funded stages, and every rule is assessed at that day's end ("Accounts are assessed for eligibility to pass after the end of the trading day"; "Qualifying Days are reviewed at the end of the trading day").
- **No positions or resting orders may be carried past session close**: "You must not carry any open position or active resting order past the end of the permitted trading day. Positions or orders remaining open at session close are automatically liquidated and/or cancelled by the Trading Platform" (Forbidden Trading Practices, item 7; T&C Cl. 5.5 restates the 4:10 p.m. ET deadline "or the relevant market close, whichever is sooner").
- **News trading is unrestricted** in every phase: "News trading is allowed with FTMO Futures during any phase, with no special news restrictions, provided you do not engage in any Forbidden Trading Practices" (cited by `growth.md`, `pro.md`, and `live.md`; `live.md` notes the sentence never names the Live Funded Account individually).
- **The Evaluation is a recurring monthly subscription with no activation fee**, billed "when you purchase the Evaluation and on the same date each month"; it "remains active even if the Evaluation Account is breached," and a breached account is replaced by "a new account on the Trading Platform on the next billing date." Passing cancels the subscription; "you won't be charged for the Sim-Funded Account or Live Funded Account."
- **Resets are unlimited and do not move the billing date**: "You may pay the Reset Fee any number of times" (T&C Cl. 5.13); "applying a Reset does not alter your monthly billing cycle" (both rules pages).
- **Fees are non-refundable once paid, and the 14-day consumer withdrawal right is lost on the first trade**: T&C Cl. 3.13 and Cl. 11.1, cited in both plan files' fee rows.
- **No minimum trading days** ("No Min Trading Days," Comparison Table, every account), but the Consistency Rule sets a mathematical floor: 3 trading days for Growth, 2 for Pro (FAQ "How long does it take to become an FTMO Trader?", cited by each plan file for its own figure).
- **The Consistency Rule exists only in the Evaluation** and can never fail an account: "Exceeding the Consistency Rule limit is not treated as a rule breach" (both rules pages); "The Consistency Rule cannot be violated; it can always be passed later by generating additional profit" (FAQ). There is "no Profit Target or Consistency Rule on the Sim-Funded Account."
- **Maximum Drawdown is End-of-Day Trailing at every stage, and locks at the Initial Simulated Capital**: "The limit can only increase, never decrease. Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Simulated Capital, it locks permanently at that figure" (both rules pages; the dollar amount differs per plan, see the table below).
- **A Sim-Funded Account cannot be reset**: "You cannot reset a Sim-Funded Account. A hard violation there means you lose the account, and if you wish to continue, you have to purchase a new Evaluation" (FAQ, cited by both plan files).
- **Sim-Funded Accounts close after 30 consecutive calendar days without trading**; "There is no inactivity rule on Live Funded Accounts" (Forbidden Trading Practices page, cited by all three files). No Evaluation-phase inactivity rule is stated anywhere, and both plan files flag that as Unconfirmed rather than assuming the 30-day figure carries over.
- **At most 3 Sim-Funded Accounts at a time, shared across Growth and Pro and all sizes**, with no limit on concurrent Evaluations (both rules pages; FAQ "How many accounts can I have?"). "There is no maximum total capital allocation across your accounts; the limit is simply the maximum number of Sim-Funded Accounts."
- **Payout ratio is 90/10** at the Sim-Funded stage ("FTMO Futures operates on a 90/10 ratio") and "stays 90 / 10" at the Live Funded stage.
- **Minimum payout is $20**, and **at least 50% of any payout request must be new profit from the current cycle** ("Min New Profit for Request"), both stated on each plan's rules page.
- **The Live Funded Account is invitation-only and discretionary**: "Not automatic: the Live Funded Account is offered by invitation only, after FTMO validates your Sim-Funded trading"; "The Live Funded Account is not something you apply for."

**Sourced from firm-wide pages only (not restated in a plan file's own body):**

- **Eligibility**: natural persons only, 18 or over; FTMO Futures "currently supports individual (B2C) clients only, so business verification (KYB) is not available." Excluded outright: "Individuals listed on international sanctions lists; Individuals with a criminal record related to financial crime or terrorism; Persons previously banned due to a breach of contract," and, outside the US, "Persons (both nationals and residents) from Iran, Syria, Myanmar, and North Korea" (with a conditional EEA-residency exception for Iranian, Syrian and Myanmar nationals). A residency-based restricted list of roughly 100 further countries and territories applies (FAQ "Who can join FTMO Futures?"); the Netherlands is not on it. US residents are accepted except in "Arkansas, Delaware, Louisiana, Montana, and South Carolina" and must hold a US Tax Identification Number and a bank account at "a fully licensed and regulated banking institution. Fintech and digital-only payment institutions (e.g., Wise, Chime) are not accepted." The T&C (Cl. 1.1) adds prior "disciplinary action from any financial regulatory authority including the National Futures Association and the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission" as disqualifying.
- **One User Area per person, one email, never changed**: "You can have only one User Area and all of the Services you order must be maintained in this User Area" (T&C Cl. 4.1); "The Profile Credentials are linked to a single email address which may not be changed after registration" (Cl. 4.2). "Holding more accounts through multiple separate registrations is not permitted" (FAQ).
- **Travel and VPN/VPS are allowed**: "Trading with FTMO while travelling to a different country is allowed. Using a VPN/VPS is also allowed, as long as you do not reside in a country we do not offer our services and you retain sole control over your registration."
- **Copy trading between your own accounts is permitted; copying anyone else is forbidden**: "Copy trading across your own accounts is permitted, provided each account independently and fully complies with all applicable rules." Replicating "the trading decisions of any third party... via signal services, master accounts, trade copiers, or any manual or automated arrangement" is a Forbidden Trading Practice, and a third-party EA risks denial of the Sim-Funded Account because "FTMO requires you to use a unique strategy."
- **Hedging or offsetting risk across accounts is forbidden**, "regardless of whether the instruments used are identical, related contract equivalents, or correlated products, and regardless of whether the accounts involved are held in your name or coordinated with others"; opposite positions are allowed only "on a single account."
- **No new positions within 2% of a CME daily price limit**: "You must not open new positions in any contract when price is within 2% of the CME-defined daily price limit for that contract and trading day." The FAQ's example: with a 7% first limit on equity index futures, new positions are blocked "once the price moves more than 5% from the previous day's settlement price." "If the rule is violated, FTMO has the right to terminate the trading account."
- **Automated trading is permitted**, but not "high-frequency strategies and latency arbitrage," and "You are fully responsible for all activity generated by your automated systems, including errors and malfunctions." Relying on the platform's drawdown protection "as the sole exit mechanism" is itself a Forbidden Trading Practice.
- **Risk-management standards are enforced as rules**: inconsistent position sizing, "News-Event Concentration," and an "Evaluation vs. Sim-Funded Mismatch" (trading a Sim-Funded Account "in a materially different manner" than the Evaluation) are each named as violations. Consequences range from trade removal and contract-limit reductions to account termination and "forfeiture of rewards" (Forbidden Trading Practices page; T&C Cl. 7.7).
- **Accounts are USD-denominated, no leverage, 30 instruments** (19 mini/E-mini and 11 micro contracts on CME Group exchanges, listed in the FAQ). FTMO's commission is "$0.50 per side for minis and $0.20 per side for micros," on top of exchange and NFA fees. Level 1 market data is included; Level 2 (Depth of Market) "cannot be purchased on trading platform, when logged with an FTMO credentials."
- **Platforms**: NinjaTrader, Tradovate, and TradingView (via Tradovate credentials), switchable at any time on one set of credentials; a CME Non-Professional Self-Attestation must be signed on first login before live market data is activated.
- **After passing**: the account closes at 4:10 p.m. ET, review "typically takes 1-3 business days," then FTMO Identity (KYC) and signing the FTMO Futures Sim-Funded Account Terms & Conditions are required before the Sim-Funded Account is issued. Evaluation accounts themselves are "created automatically, usually within an hour of your payment. It should never take more than 24 hours."
- **Payouts are paid in USD** via "Wise, Revolut bank wire, and instant withdrawal via Visa Direct and Mastercard Send (Mastercard Send is not available for US clients)." Confirming a withdrawal closes all open positions and pending orders. US clients must file a W-9 before the first payout.
- **A failed renewal pauses the account with a 48-hour grace period**, after which "the account is closed" (FAQ; `growth.md` cites this in its fee row, `pro.md` does not).
- **No free trial; FTMO Points cannot be used for FTMO Futures; accounts cannot be merged**; FTMO Futures can be held alongside other FTMO products on the same login.
- **Technical issues must be reported before continuing to trade**: "In case you proceed to perform new simulated trades after observing a technical issue without receiving a response from our team, you may be deemed fully responsible for the outcome of such simulated trades" (T&C Cl. 13.1). Chargebacks or disputed fees entitle FTMO "to stop providing any of the Services to you" (Cl. 13.3).
- **Terms can change on 7 days' notice, prices on 14 days' notice**, and FTMO "may terminate the Agreement without cause by providing a written notice to you at least five (5) calendar days in advance" (T&C Cl. 14.1, 3.6, 12.2.3).

Rules that looked firm-wide but are deliberately NOT listed as such: the Daily Loss Limit (absent in Growth's Evaluation, soft in Growth's Sim-Funded, hard in both Pro stages, and unstated for Live); the Payout Cap and Withdrawable Amount (differ per plan); the Max Drawdown Amount (differs per plan); any Live Funded Account parameter beyond "invitation-only, buffer, daily, no cap, 90/10" (all Unconfirmed in `live.md`).

## Documentation Scope

- **$100K and $150K sizes are not documented**, by explicit instruction for this pass. They exist for both products, and the firm's Comparison Table publishes distinct figures for each (Profit Target, Max Drawdown, Daily Loss Limit, Max Contracts, subscription, Reset Fee, Payout Cap, Payout Eligibility). The pasted rules-page sources used for `growth.md` and `pro.md` were captured with the 50K size selected; the live page carries all sizes in one document, and a future pass can extend the tree from it.
- **FTMO's CFD products** (the "FTMO Challenge," 1-Step/2-Step, Premium Programme, Scaling Plan) are a separate product line under separate terms and are not covered; the futures FAQ notes "you use a single login for FTMO Futures and other FTMO products."
- **The FTMO Futures Sim-Funded Account Terms & Conditions** governs the Sim-Funded stage (and, per the Evaluation T&C, the Live Funded offer) but is not publicly linked anywhere on the site; it is signed after passing, and FTMO says "If you are interested in a sample of the contract, please contact us." Every Sim-Funded and Live Funded rule in this tree therefore rests on the rules page, the FAQ, and the Evaluation T&C's references to that document, not on the document itself. Named here rather than left silent.
- **The US Terms and Conditions PDF** (the second document on the T&C page, for FTMO US / JV Prop Corporation) was not read; the Global Evaluation T&C governs a Netherlands-resident trader and was read in full.
- **The Affiliate Programme** (14 FAQ articles plus the launch competition) and the platform login how-tos were read and excluded by content: they contain no trading, drawdown, payout, or eligibility rule.
- **The Trading Updates page** (weekly market-break and maintenance notices) is not documented; it is operational, and the T&C obliges traders to follow it ("You are obliged to regularly follow our updates here and our official FTMO Discord profile regarding maintenance periods, holidays, Trading Platform outages").
- **Engine model**: `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/ftmo-futures/FtmoFutures.ts` models Growth and Pro at $50K (`FirmId.FtmoFutures`, `FtmoFuturesVariant.Growth`/`.Pro`), with no Live Funded Account model by design (`FirmId.FtmoFutures` is absent from `LIVE_PLAN_BUILDERS`). It was cross-checked against `growth.md` and `pro.md` on 2026-09-21 and corroborates every figure in the Key Cross-Plan Differences table above. Hard and soft Daily Loss Limits are distinguished as of 2026-09-21: Pro's limit terminates the account in both phases, Growth's Sim-Funded limit only ends the day, matching FTMO's own soft-vs-hard violation vocabulary. Four simplifications still apply to every simulator figure: the Reset Fee ($109/$129) is charged on every Evaluation bust except the last rather than modeling the free next-billing-date replacement; the 30-calendar-day Sim-Funded inactivity rule is counted in simulated trading days; the 3-account cap is applied per plan and cannot express that it is shared across Growth and Pro; and only the $50K size exists in the engine. See each plan file's Not Confirmed section for the plan-specific caveats (payout cadence modeled only as the Qualifying Days gate; the $2,000 payout cushion the engine applies, which FTMO does not state; Pro's full-withdrawal bust that the cushion prevents).
