## Overview

FTMO Futures is the futures product line of FTMO (the Global Evaluation T&C is issued by FTMO Evaluation Global s.r.o., Prague, and the US Evaluation T&C by JV Prop Corporation, Delaware; a separate product from FTMO's CFD "FTMO Challenge," which this tree does not cover). FTMO's own pages disagree on the launch date: the Trading Updates index shows the "FTMO Futures are launched" notice dated 27 Aug 2026, that notice's own page is dated 2 Sep 2026, and FTMO's press release and launch blog post, both dated 17 September 2026, say it "today launched" and "is live" (see Documentation Scope; this tree does not pick one). There are two products, Growth and Pro, each sold as a single-phase Evaluation on a recurring monthly subscription (no activation fee, no minimum trading days, no time limit), each available at $50K, $100K, and $150K. FTMO's own sources disagree on whether passing the Evaluation assures a Sim-Funded Account, and the rules page is itself two-sided on it (its Evaluation section says "To pass the Evaluation and have the option to advance to a Sim-Funded Account," its Sim-Funded section says "you will receive access to a Sim-Funded Account" of the same size), while the T&C says passing "does not guarantee your acceptance" (live conflict 8 in Documentation Scope). The Sim-Funded Account is described as simulated capital with real payouts, no Profit Target and no Consistency Rule, and a small, FTMO-selected group of Sim-Funded traders may later be invited, at FTMO's discretion, to a real-money Live Funded Account. Under the Global Evaluation T&C (Cl. 6.1, 6.3, 6.4) a passed Evaluation is reported to a different entity, FTMO Trading Global s.r.o., which may offer a contract under the non-public "FTMO Futures Sim-Funded Account Terms and Conditions"; the US Evaluation T&C (JV Prop Corporation) names no FTMO Trading entity and says "we may offer you" a contract under the "FTMO US Futures Sim-Funded Account Terms and Conditions". Which document governs the Live Funded Account is Unconfirmed (see `live.md`, Not Confirmed).

By explicit instruction for this pass, **only the $50K size of each product is documented**. The $100K and $150K sizes exist and have their own distinct figures (Profit Target, Max Drawdown, Daily Loss Limit, Max Contracts, fees, Payout Cap, Payout Eligibility) on the firm's Comparison Table; only the monthly price and Reset Fee of those sizes are recorded in this tree (see Documentation Scope); no other figure for them is recorded as a rule, in a table, or in a worked example.

## Plans

- **[Growth](growth.md)**: $50K. $119/month, $109 Reset. Profit Target $3,000; EOD Trailing Max Drawdown $2,000 locking at the $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital; no Daily Loss Limit in the Evaluation, a soft $1,000 Daily Loss Limit on the Sim-Funded Account; 40% Consistency Rule (Evaluation only). Payout Frequency "Every 4 days" and a Payout Eligibility gate of 4 Qualifying Days of $150+ (how the two relate is Unconfirmed, see `growth.md`); withdrawable up to 50% of profit above the Initial Simulated Capital, Payout Cap $2,500 per request (which of the two binds at $4,000 of profit is Unconfirmed), 90/10 split.
- **[Pro](pro.md)**: $50K. $139/month, $129 Reset. Profit Target $3,000; EOD Trailing Max Drawdown $3,000 locking at the $50,000 Initial Simulated Capital; hard $1,000 Daily Loss Limit in both the Evaluation and the Sim-Funded Account; 50% Consistency Rule (Evaluation only). Payout Frequency "Every 5 days" and a Payout Eligibility gate of 5 Qualifying Days of $200+ (how the two relate is Unconfirmed, see `pro.md`); withdrawable up to 100% of profit above the Initial Simulated Capital, Payout Cap $5,000 per request, 90/10 split.

## Live Accounts

- **[Live Funded Account](live.md)**: shared by `growth.md` and `pro.md` (both transition into the same invitation-only stage). Documented from the firm's own statements only: offered at the sole discretion of the offering FTMO entity ("FTMO Trading" in Global T&C Cl. 6.3, "us" in US T&C Cl. 6.3) after FTMO "validates your Sim-Funded trading"; a one-time buffer must be cleared before the first payout, then payouts are daily with no payout cap at the same 90/10 split; a market data fee applies with one exchange covered; no inactivity rule; a separate Live-Funded-only restricted-country list applies (reproduced in full in `live.md`). No dollar figure for its size, drawdown, Daily Loss Limit, buffer, or market data fee is stated by any source, so `live.md` carries no numbers and no worked example. ## Firm-Wide Rules

Two kinds of rule appear below. The first group is confirmed independently in both `growth.md` and `pro.md` from each plan's own cited sources (the rules page for that plan, the T&C, or a firm-wide page each file cites itself). The second group comes from firm-wide FAQ or policy pages that state their own scope and that no plan file restates in its own body; they are documented here once rather than in each plan file. Treat the second group as firm-level, source-confirmed rules, not as facts each plan file independently corroborates.

**Confirmed in both plan files:**

- **The trading day runs 6:00 p.m. ET to 4:10 p.m. ET the next calendar day**, stated identically on both plans' own rules pages for both the Evaluation and Sim-Funded stages, and several statements tie an assessment or update to that day's end (listed as read, not claimed exhaustive): eligibility to pass ("Accounts are assessed for eligibility to pass after the end of the trading day," rules page), Qualifying Days ("Qualifying Days are reviewed at the end of the trading day," rules page), the Consistency Rule's Best Day ("measured at the end of the trading day," rules page), eligibility to withdraw a payout ("assessed after the end of the trading day (4:10 p.m. ET)," FAQ "Who is an FTMO Trader, and how do I become one?"), and the Client Area progress update ("refreshes at the end of each trading day (after 4:10 p.m. ET)," technical-infrastructure FAQ). That is not stated for every rule: the rules pages say equity "cannot hit" the Maximum Drawdown Limit "at any point," and the technical-infrastructure FAQ says the platform "monitors your risk rules in real time" (see below).
- **No positions or resting orders may be carried past session close**: "You must not carry any open position or active resting order past the end of the permitted trading day. Positions or orders remaining open at session close are automatically liquidated and/or cancelled by the Trading Platform" (Forbidden Trading Practices, item 7; T&C Cl. 5.5 restates the 4:10 p.m. ET deadline "or the relevant market close, whichever is sooner").
- **News trading is stated as allowed in "any phase"**: "News trading is allowed with FTMO Futures during any phase, with no special news restrictions, provided you do not engage in any Forbidden Trading Practices" (cited by `growth.md`, `pro.md`, and `live.md`; `live.md` notes the sentence never names the Live Funded Account individually and keeps that Unconfirmed for Live).
- **The Evaluation is a recurring monthly subscription with no activation fee**, billed "when you purchase the Evaluation and on the same date each month"; it "remains active even if the Evaluation Account is breached," and a breached account is replaced by "a new account on the Trading Platform on the next billing date."
- **Resets are unlimited and do not move the billing date**: "You may pay the Reset Fee any number of times" (T&C Cl. 5.13); "applying a Reset does not alter your monthly billing cycle" (both rules pages).
- **Fees are non-refundable once paid, and the 14-day consumer withdrawal right is lost on the first trade**: T&C Cl. 3.13 and Cl. 11.1, cited in both plan files' fee rows.
- **No minimum trading days** ("No Min Trading Days," Comparison Table, every account), but the FAQ "How long does it take to become an FTMO Trader?" states "Considering the Consistency Rule, the minimum time to pass the Evaluation is" 3 trading days for Growth and 2 for Pro (cited by each plan file for its own figure; how the Consistency Rule produces the figure is not stated).
- **The Consistency Rule exists only in the Evaluation** and can never fail an account: "Exceeding the Consistency Rule limit is not treated as a rule breach" (both rules pages); "The Consistency Rule cannot be violated; it can always be passed later by generating additional profit" (FAQ). There is "no Profit Target or Consistency Rule on the Sim-Funded Account."
- **Maximum Drawdown is End-of-Day Trailing at both the Evaluation and the Sim-Funded stage, and locks at the Initial Simulated Capital** (the Live Funded stage's drawdown type is not stated, see `live.md`): "The limit can only increase, never decrease. Once the Maximum Drawdown Limit reaches the Initial Simulated Capital, it locks permanently at that figure" (both rules pages; the dollar amount differs per plan, see the table below).
- **A Sim-Funded Account cannot be reset**: "You cannot reset a Sim-Funded Account. A hard violation there means you lose the account, and if you wish to continue, you have to purchase a new Evaluation" (FAQ, cited by both plan files).
- **Sim-Funded Accounts close after 30 consecutive calendar days without trading**; "There is no inactivity rule on Live Funded Accounts" (Forbidden Trading Practices page, cited by all three files). No Evaluation-phase inactivity rule is stated on any page read for this tree, and both plan files flag that as Unconfirmed rather than assuming the 30-day figure carries over.
- **At most 3 Sim-Funded Accounts at a time, shared across Growth and Pro and all sizes**, with no limit on concurrent Evaluations (both rules pages; FAQ "How many accounts can I have?"). "There is no maximum total capital allocation across your accounts; the limit is simply the maximum number of Sim-Funded Accounts."
- **Payout ratio is 90/10** at the Sim-Funded stage ("FTMO Futures operates on a 90/10 ratio") and "stays 90 / 10" at the Live Funded stage.
- **Minimum payout is $20**, and **at least 50% of any payout request must be new profit from the current cycle** ("Min New Profit for Request"), both stated on each plan's rules page.
- **The Live Funded Account is invitation-only and discretionary**: "Not automatic: the Live Funded Account is offered by invitation only, after FTMO validates your Sim-Funded trading"; "The Live Funded Account is not something you apply for."

**Sourced from firm-wide pages only (not restated in a plan file's own body):**

- **Eligibility**: natural persons only, 18 or over; FTMO Futures "currently supports individual (B2C) clients only, so business verification (KYB) is not available." Excluded outright: "Individuals listed on international sanctions lists; Individuals with a criminal record related to financial crime or terrorism; Persons previously banned due to a breach of contract," and, outside the US, "Persons (both nationals and residents) from Iran, Syria, Myanmar, and North Korea" (with a conditional EEA-residency exception for Iranian, Syrian and Myanmar nationals). A residency-based restricted list of 101 further countries and territories applies (FAQ "Who can join FTMO Futures?"); the Netherlands is not on it. US residents are accepted except in "Arkansas, Delaware, Louisiana, Montana, and South Carolina" and must hold a US Tax Identification Number and a bank account at "a fully licensed and regulated banking institution. Fintech and digital-only payment institutions (e.g., Wise, Chime) are not accepted." The T&C (Cl. 1.1) adds prior "disciplinary action from any financial regulatory authority including the National Futures Association and the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission" as disqualifying.
- **One User Area per person, one email, never changed**: "You can have only one User Area and all of the Services you order must be maintained in this User Area" (T&C Cl. 4.1); "The Profile Credentials are linked to a single email address which may not be changed after registration" (Cl. 4.2). "Holding more accounts through multiple separate registrations is not permitted" (FAQ).
- **Travel and VPN/VPS are allowed**: "Trading with FTMO while travelling to a different country is allowed. Using a VPN/VPS is also allowed, as long as you do not reside in a country we do not offer our services and you retain sole control over your registration."
- **Copy trading between your own accounts is permitted; copying anyone else is forbidden**: "Copy trading across your own accounts is permitted, provided each account independently and fully complies with all applicable rules." Replicating "the trading decisions of any third party... via signal services, master accounts, trade copiers, or any manual or automated arrangement" is a Forbidden Trading Practice, and a third-party EA risks denial of the Sim-Funded Account because "FTMO requires you to use a unique strategy."
- **Hedging or offsetting risk across accounts is forbidden**, "regardless of whether the instruments used are identical, related contract equivalents, or correlated products, and regardless of whether the accounts involved are held in your name or coordinated with others"; opposite positions are allowed only "on a single account."
- **No new positions within 2% of a CME daily price limit**: "You must not open new positions in any contract when price is within 2% of the CME-defined daily price limit for that contract and trading day." The FAQ's example: with a 7% first limit on equity index futures, new positions are blocked "once the price moves more than 5% from the previous day's settlement price." "If the rule is violated, FTMO has the right to terminate the trading account." The FAQ also says "The trader is solely responsible for monitoring the current price limits, which CME publishes and updates after each trading day on its official Price Limits page."
- **Automated trading is permitted**, but not "high-frequency strategies and latency arbitrage," and "You are fully responsible for all activity generated by your automated systems, including errors and malfunctions." Relying on the platform's drawdown protection "as the sole exit mechanism" is itself a Forbidden Trading Practice.
- **Risk-management standards are enforced as rules**: inconsistent position sizing, "News-Event Concentration," and an "Evaluation vs. Sim-Funded Mismatch" (trading a Sim-Funded Account "in a materially different manner" than the Evaluation) are each named as violations. Consequences range from trade removal and contract-limit reductions to account termination and "forfeiture of rewards" (Forbidden Trading Practices page; T&C Cl. 7.7).
- **Accounts are USD-denominated, no leverage, 30 instruments** (19 mini/E-mini and 11 micro contracts on CME Group exchanges, listed in the FAQ). FTMO's commission is "$0.50 per side for minis and $0.20 per side for micros," on top of exchange and NFA fees. Level 1 market data is included; Level 2 (Depth of Market) "cannot be purchased on trading platform, when logged with an FTMO credentials." The 30 instruments, as listed in the FAQ: Standard/Mini (19) GC, SI, HG, PL, CL, NG, NQ, ES, YM, RTY, NKD, 6E, 6J, 6B, 6A, 6C, 6S, 6N, E7; Micro (11) MNQ, MES, MGC, MYM, M2K, M6E, MBT, MET, MCL, M6A, M6B. Trading hours "are set by the exchange and may vary due to scheduled exchange maintenance, daily settlement breaks, holiday sessions, and our own scheduled platform maintenance," and "any standard market closure countdown or session times displayed in the platform do not take into account amended market hours, platform maintenance times or other changes as shown on the Trading Updates page" (same FAQ).
- **Platforms**: NinjaTrader, Tradovate, and TradingView (via Tradovate credentials), switchable at any time on one set of credentials; a CME Non-Professional Self-Attestation must be signed on first login before live market data is activated.
- **After passing**: the account closes at 4:10 p.m. ET, review "typically takes 1-3 business days," then FTMO Identity (KYC) and signing the FTMO Futures Sim-Funded Account Terms & Conditions are required before the Sim-Funded Account is issued. Evaluation accounts themselves are "created automatically, usually within an hour of your payment. It should never take more than 24 hours."
- **Payouts are paid in USD** via "Wise, Revolut bank wire, and instant withdrawal via Visa Direct and Mastercard Send (Mastercard Send is not available for US clients)." Confirming a withdrawal closes all open positions and pending orders. The payout FAQ says "all US-based clients must submit the appropriate IRS Form (W-9) before the first Payout request", while two other FTMO FAQs say "all FTMO Traders" must submit it; whether a non-US trader must file it is a live conflict (see Documentation Scope, item 7). After the request "the requested amount is deducted from their Sim-Funded Account balance, and the payout is pending FTMO’s approval. At this point, the FTMO Trader can immediately resume trading on their Sim-Funded Account"; once approved, the trader "finalises the process in the Client Area by confirming the payout invoice." Taxes are the trader's: "you are solely responsible and liable for the payment of any and all taxes, levies, or fees that apply to you" in relation to the Sim-Funded Account Terms & Conditions, and FTMO "is neither able nor authorised to provide any tax advice or instructions." "Eligibility to withdraw a payout is assessed after the end of the trading day (4:10 p.m. ET)" (FAQ "Who is an FTMO Trader, and how do I become one?").
- **Passing ends the subscription**: "you won't be charged for the Sim-Funded Account or Live Funded Account" (FAQ "How does the payment for the Evaluation work?", cited only by this file and `live.md`; neither plan file states it).
- **A failed renewal pauses the account with a 48-hour grace period**, after which "the account is closed" (FAQ; `growth.md` cites this in its fee row, `pro.md` does not). Access "is paused immediately, but your account state (balance and parameters) is preserved," and a payment inside the window means "your account is restored exactly where you left off." T&C Cl. 3.8 lets FTMO terminate the Agreement if a payment is not credited, "subject to any applicable grace period specified on the Website."
- **No free trial; FTMO Points cannot be used for FTMO Futures; accounts cannot be merged**; FTMO Futures can be held alongside other FTMO products on the same login.
- **Technical issues must be reported before continuing to trade**: "In case you proceed to perform new simulated trades after observing a technical issue without receiving a response from our team, you may be deemed fully responsible for the outcome of such simulated trades" (T&C Cl. 13.1). Chargebacks or disputed fees entitle FTMO "to stop providing any of the Services to you" (Cl. 13.3).
