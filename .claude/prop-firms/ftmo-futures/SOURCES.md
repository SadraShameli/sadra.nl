# `FTMO Futures`: Source Ledger

## Discovery completeness

FTMO publishes a working sitemap (see the 2026-10-02 line-by-line audit section below for the correction to this paragraph: the other sitemaps in the index were not checked on 2026-09-21). `ftmo.com/robots.txt` names `Sitemap: https://ftmo.com/sitemap_index.xml` (a Yoast index); its children `page-sitemap.xml`, `faq-sitemap.xml`, and `faq-category-sitemap.xml` were fetched on 2026-09-21 (each timed out once at 100 s and succeeded on a longer-timeout retry) and diffed for every URL containing "futures". There is no separate help-center domain: the futures FAQ lives under `ftmo.com/en/futures/faq/`, and the page-level rules live under `ftmo.com/en/futures/`.

**73 of 73 English FTMO Futures URLs in the sitemap were fetched (all HTTP 200) and read in full**, plus the Global Evaluation Terms and Conditions PDF linked from the T&C page (18 pages, read in full). That is 9 section pages under `/en/futures/` (the landing page, How It Works, Comparison Table, Trading Objectives & Rules, Forbidden Trading Practices, Trading Platforms, Trading Updates, the T&C shell page, and the FAQ index) and 64 FAQ articles (60 under `/en/futures/faq/`, 4 under `/en/faq/`). Of those, 57 rows below are cited somewhere in `growth.md`, `pro.md`, `live.md`, or `README.md` (counted over the 73 English URLs, after the 2026-10-02 line-by-line audit); the other 16 were read end to end and found to state nothing applicable, with the reason stated per row. Groups:

- **Affiliate Programme (18 articles)**: 14 under `/en/futures/faq/` plus 4 under `/en/faq/` (3 of which duplicate the futures-FAQ versions word for word; the fourth is the affiliate launch competition, 21 Sep–18 Oct 2026). Commission rates, attribution, partner levels, promotional rules and partner eligibility; no rule a trader is bound by, so 13 of the 18 are "read, nothing applicable". Five carry one trader-relevant sentence each and have a cited row: "How do FTMO Futures commissions work?" (`live.md`, "not subscription products"), "In which currency are my commissions calculated and paid?" - **Marketing / contact (2 articles)**: "Is FTMO a trustworthy company?" and "Where is your office?". No rule content, so "read, nothing applicable". (The two neighbouring marketing FAQs, "What is FTMO?" and "Why should I join FTMO?", are cited by `live.md` because each carries a sentence about Live Funded discretion.)
- **FAQ index page**: "read, nothing applicable"; used as a second completeness check (its 60 listed titles all appear in the sitemap set). The cancel-subscription, payment-methods, technical-infrastructure, contract-expiry, balance-vs-equity and "trading according to a real market" FAQs were earlier "read, consistent, not separately quoted"; the 2026-10-02 audit found sentences in each that the tree lacked and now cites them in `README.md`.
