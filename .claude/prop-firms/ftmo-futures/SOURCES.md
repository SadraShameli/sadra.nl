# `FTMO Futures`: Source Ledger

## Discovery completeness

FTMO publishes a working sitemap. `ftmo.com/robots.txt` names `Sitemap: https://ftmo.com/sitemap_index.xml` (a Yoast index); its children `page-sitemap.xml`, `faq-sitemap.xml`, and `faq-category-sitemap.xml` were fetched on 2026-09-21 (each timed out once at 100 s and succeeded on a longer-timeout retry) and diffed for every URL containing "futures". There is no separate help-center domain: the futures FAQ lives under `ftmo.com/en/futures/faq/`, and the page-level rules live under `ftmo.com/en/futures/`.

**73 of 73 English FTMO Futures URLs in the sitemap were fetched (all HTTP 200) and read in full**, plus the Global Evaluation Terms and Conditions PDF linked from the T&C page (18 pages, read in full). That is 9 section pages under `/en/futures/` (the landing page, How It Works, Comparison Table, Trading Objectives & Rules, Forbidden Trading Practices, Trading Platforms, Trading Updates, the T&C shell page, and the FAQ index) and 64 FAQ articles (60 under `/en/futures/faq/`, 4 under `/en/faq/`). Of those, 45 rows below are cited somewhere in `growth.md`, `pro.md`, `live.md`, or `README.md`; the other 29 were read end to end and excluded on their content, with the reason stated per row. Exclusion groups:

- **Affiliate Programme (17 articles)**: 13 under `/en/futures/faq/` plus 4 under `/en/faq/` (3 of which duplicate the futures-FAQ versions word for word; the fourth is the affiliate launch competition, 21 Sep–18 Oct 2026). Commission rates, attribution, partner levels, promotional rules and partner eligibility; no rule a trader is bound by. One of them, "How do FTMO Futures commissions work?", is nevertheless cited by `live.md` for a single sentence ("the Sim-Funded Account and the Live Funded Account are not subscription products") and so has a cited row.
- **Platform login how-tos (3 articles)**: TradingView, NinjaTrader, Tradovate. Step-by-step login instructions only.
- **Marketing / contact (2 articles)**: "Is FTMO a trustworthy company?" and "Where is your office?". No rule content. (The two neighbouring marketing FAQs, "What is FTMO?" and "Why should I join FTMO?", are cited by `live.md` because each carries a sentence about Live Funded discretion.)
- **Read, consistent with the tree, not separately quoted (7 articles + the FAQ index)**: cancel subscription; payment methods; technical infrastructure; contract expiry; balance vs. equity; "trading according to a real market"; and the FAQ index page itself, which was used as a second completeness check (its 60 listed titles all appear in the sitemap set).

**Not fetched, by content type**: 18 non-English sitemap entries (`/cs/`, `/de/`, `/es/`, `/fr/`, `/it/`, `/pt/`), all translations of the three affiliate FAQs above, and 5 `/en/blog/faq-category/` entries, which are category index pages, not articles.

**Known gaps, named rather than left silent:**

- The **FTMO Futures Sim-Funded Account Terms and Conditions** governs the Sim-Funded stage and the Live Funded offer (Evaluation T&C Cl. 6.1, 6.4) but is not linked anywhere on the public site; FTMO says "If you are interested in a sample of the contract, please contact us." Every Sim-Funded and Live Funded rule in this tree rests on the rules page, the FAQ, and the Evaluation T&C's references to that document.
- The **US Terms and Conditions PDF** (`.../jvpc/terms-and-conditions-4/terms-and-conditions-4-v5.pdf`, FTMO US / JV Prop Corporation) was not read; the Global document governs a Netherlands-resident trader.
- The **Trading Objectives & Rules page carries all six plan/size combinations in one document**. Only the 50K Growth and 50K Pro views were used, by explicit user instruction; the 100K and 150K figures exist in the same source and are not documented.
- The rules page, the landing page, and every FAQ display **no on-page "updated" date**. The "Article's Own Updated Date" column below is the page's `article:modified_time` HTML meta tag as read on the 2026-09-21 fetch, which is the closest thing the site publishes; it is labelled as such in each file's own Sources block.
