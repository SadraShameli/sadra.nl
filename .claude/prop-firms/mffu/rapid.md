## Overview

Rapid is My Funded Futures' streamlined plan structure combining an evaluation stage and a simulated-funded stage with intraday trailing drawdown that locks at an absolute floor. It is offered at four account sizes ($25K/$50K/$100K/$150K), each with its own dedicated help-center article. The evaluation stage's drawdown is labeled "Maximum Loss Limit (EOD)" in every tier's own parameter table; upon passing evaluation, traders move to a sim-funded account where the drawdown mechanism becomes intraday-trailing instead. News trading during T1 (Tier 1) economic-data releases is confirmed, firm-wide, as permitted during Evaluation and prohibited on Rapid's own Sim Funded stage specifically ("News Trading Policy" article; see README.md's Firm-Wide Rules, which also records that "Fair Play and Prohibited Trading Practices" Section 2 lists Tier 1 Economic Data Trading as restricted with no qualification, a live conflict).

## How the Drawdown Works

The Rapid Sim Funded drawdown is an intraday trailing mechanism, confirmed identically across all four tiers. Your maximum loss distance stays fixed below your equity high-water mark (HWM) while the market is open, $1,000/$2,000/$3,000/$4,500 depending on tier, as you make profits and your HWM rises, the max loss floor rises with it. However, per each tier's own article, once the trailing Max Loss reaches $100 it locks there and does not trail anymore; you must always keep at least $100 in the account, and a balance below $100 breaches the account. `myfundedfutures.com/plans/rapid` words the trigger differently in its table, its STRAIGHT TALK section and its FAQ, the FAQ tying it to a first payout (live conflict; see Not Confirmed).

This trailing distance applies only during intraday trading. The mechanism is described as "intraday trailing" in every tier's own Sim Funded section, distinct from the evaluation stage's own "Maximum Loss Limit (End-of-Day)" labeling, the same source-stated labeling inconsistency already flagged before this pass (see Not Confirmed). The firm-wide "End of Day (EOD) Drawdown Explained" article (8348565), which names no plan, states "You must be aware that open equity losses are taken into consideration when calculating whether or not the account failed on this rule. This means that if an open position dips your account balance below the minimum account balance, your evaluation attempt will fail." No Rapid source restates this, and applying it to Rapid's Evaluation stage is not a source statement, the same reason this file declines to apply that article's lock examples (see Not Confirmed). Do not assume open equity losses are ignored in the Evaluation stage, and do not assume a source states that they count there.

### Worked Example

Starting Rapid Sim Funded at $0 (matching the source's own "Initial Balance: $0"), using the $50,000 tier. The high-water mark (HWM) only ever increases within this example. Source-stated figures: the $0 initial balance, the $2,000 distance from the HWM, the $100 lock and the 24-hour first-payout rule; every floor value below is this file's own arithmetic from them, marked derived, and the +$2,100 HWM at which the floor first reaches $100 is derived, not stated by any source (see Not Confirmed).

1. You begin with a $0 balance and your equity high-water mark (HWM) starts at $0. Derived: the max loss floor is $2,000 below that, $0 - $2,000 = -$2,000; you can lose up to $2,000 intraday.

2. During the trading day, you make $1,500 in profit and your HWM rises to $1,500. Derived: the floor trails up to $1,500 - $2,000 = -$500.

3. You continue trading and your HWM reaches $2,100. Derived: the floor would be $2,100 - $2,000 = $100, the level at which the source says the trailing Max Loss locks ("Once your trailing Max Loss reaches $100, it locks there"). The +$2,100 HWM is this file's own arithmetic, not a source statement.

4. From this point forward, per the stated lock, the max loss limit is fixed at $100 and does not trail anymore. You must always keep at least $100 in your account; if your balance goes below $100, the account is breached.

5. Payouts: the first payout becomes available exactly 24 hours after your first trade on the Sim Funded account, provided the $2,100 buffer in realized profits (stated for this tier) and the $500 minimum request are met.

## Live Transition

Per the "Understanding Rapid Live" article (<https://help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/13134718-understanding-rapid-live>, updated September 8, 2026), traders transition from Rapid Sim Funded to Live through two pathways: automatic transition upon achieving $10,000 in net profit within a single trading day, or via Risk Management Team approval following consistent payout performance. Neither the 25K, 100K, nor 150K tier articles restate live-transition parameters themselves, each instead links out to the same shared "Transition to Live Parameters" article, consistent with the $50K tier's own already-confirmed pattern.

For full live account parameters, the Reserve Program, and payout/breach mechanics, see [rapid-live.md](rapid-live.md) (shared by Rapid and Rapid EOD).
