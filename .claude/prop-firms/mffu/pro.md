# Pro ($50,000 / $100,000 / $150,000)

**Sources:** <https://help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/11802674-pro-plan-sim-funded-and-live-account-highlights> (updated June 30, 2026); the other sources are listed in the Sources footer.

**Last Verified:** 2026-10-02
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-03

## Overview

The Pro Plan is designed for experienced traders seeking frequent payouts and a clear path to live funding. It offers three account sizes ($50K, $100K, $150K) with tiered drawdown limits, no daily loss limit in the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages (the live stage carries a Daily Loss Limit Range, see Live Transition), and an 80/20 profit split favoring traders. The plan emphasizes high payout potential through a progressive structure that unlocks live funding once traders achieve consistent performance milestones or exceed the sim-funded payout cap.

After passing evaluation with a 50% consistency rule and no daily loss limit, traders enter the sim-funded phase, where payouts are gated by a 14-calendar-day window from the first trade and a payout buffer. The sources state a $100,000 maximum payout per user. Traders transitioning to live accounts can do so through three consecutive payouts (which the plan page words as a review gate, see Live Transition) or through excess-profit mechanisms, with the risk team also able to initiate transitions for high-performing traders at any time.

## How the Drawdown Works

The Pro plan uses an end-of-day (EOD) Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) for the sim-funded phase, confirmed both before and after the first payout. The source states the starting MLL distance ("$2,000," "$3,000," and "$4,500" for the three tiers respectively) and the post-first-payout locked value ("After first payout, MLL moves to $50,100 and remains static" at 50K, and likewise to $100,100 and $150,100 at the other two tiers). Stated directly: `myfundedfutures.com/plans/pro`'s own FAQ, fetched live 2026-09-23 and again 2026-10-02 (HTTP 200), states the pre-lock behavior: "Pro uses end-of-day trailing drawdown in both eval and sim funded. The max-loss line trails your equity high at the end of each trading day — not intraday. After your first approved payout, the MLL locks permanently at your starting balance plus $100 and stops trailing entirely. Until that first payout, the MLL continues to trail each time you set a new EOD equity high." The Worked Example's intermediate steps below now trace to this statement, not to an assumption borrowed from this firm's other EOD plans.

### Worked Example

Using the nominal-balance-plus-profit convention (balance = starting balance + profit). The $50,000 starting balance is derived, not stated: the sources state the post-first-payout locked value ("After first payout, MLL moves to $50,100 and remains static") and a lock at "your starting balance plus $100," but no Pro source states a starting balance (see Not Confirmed). Source-stated figures: the $2,000 distance, the $2,100 buffer, the 14-calendar-day gate, the $50,100 static locked value and the plan FAQ's end-of-day trailing formula; every other figure is this file's own arithmetic, marked derived.

1. Trader opens the $50K account at a derived $50,000 balance. Derived: the MLL begins at $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000 (maximum loss allowed is the stated $2,000).
2. Trader accumulates $1,000 in profit. Derived: the balance rises to $51,000 and the EOD MLL trails to $51,000 − $2,000 = $49,000, per the plan FAQ's end-of-day trailing statement.
3. Trader adds another $1,100 in profit (derived: cumulative $2,100 profit, $52,100 balance), clearing the stated $2,100 payout buffer requirement. Derived: the EOD MLL trails to $52,100 − $2,000 = $50,100, which equals the stated locked value in step 4.
4. Trader clears the buffer, fulfills other conditions (14 calendar days from first trade), and requests their first payout. The MLL locks at $50,100, stated: "After first payout, MLL moves to $50,100 and remains static."
5. Subsequently, if the account grows to $60,000 in profit (derived: $110,000 balance), the MLL remains locked at $50,100 and does not trail further upward; stated: "remains static."

Final verification (derived): the trader can now only lose down to $50,100; the gap at that point is $110,000 − $50,100 = $59,900, whereas an unlocked trailing floor would have reached $110,000 − $2,000 = $108,000. The static lock preserves a floor relative to the starting balance.

## Live Transition

Traders transition to live accounts through one of the following pathways:

**Transition Triggers:**

- Achieve 3 consecutive payouts on the sim-funded account, OR
- Exceed the $100,000 per-user payout cap (excess profits are "moved to their live account balance," up to tier-specific maximums: $5,000 for $50K, $7,500 for $100K, $10,000 for $150K), OR
- Be contacted by the risk team for a discretionary transition at any point during the sim-funded journey.

**Re-checked 2026-09-26, real conflict on whether the 3-consecutive-payouts trigger is automatic, flagged not blended (attribution corrected: a prior pass had the two sources reversed).** This section's own cited help-center source, article 11802674, states this trigger under an unqualified "Live Account Transition Triggers" heading: "Achieve 3 consecutive payouts, or [...]", worded as a definite trigger with no review language, matching the bullet list immediately above. But `myfundedfutures.com/plans/pro`'s own live plan page, re-fetched 2026-09-26, instead frames the same 3-payout threshold as a review gate, not an automatic promotion: its own table row reads only "Live Transition Trigger | 3 consecutive payouts" (no "achieve," no "or" list), and its own prose states "Three consecutive payouts → Risk Team review for live capital," "After three consecutive payouts on the same Pro account, the account is reviewed by our team for transition to a live brokerage account," and, most explicitly, "The trigger is documented; the review is human." Both are MFF's own current sources, re-fetched and confirmed by an independent verifier the same day; per this repo's standing rule to surface rather than blend conflicts, do not assume the 3-consecutive-payouts threshold guarantees an automatic transition the way the bullet list above (sourced from 11802674) might read on its own, since the firm's own live plan page describes a human review step the help-center article omits. This conflict is distinct from the $20,000 milestone note below, which every cited source already agrees is review-only.

Separately, the source states: "Reaching a $20,000 profit milestone will trigger an account for review but may not guarantee a transition to a Live account." This is a distinct, confirmed account-review trigger. It does not by itself guarantee a live transition, unlike the three triggers above (subject to the 3-consecutive-payouts caveat immediately above).

Traders may also voluntarily request to allocate a portion of a payout to their live account balance, up to the live account funding max, per the source: "Traders may also request to allocate a portion of their payout to live account balance up to the live account funding max."
