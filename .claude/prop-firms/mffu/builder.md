## Overview

The Builder Plan is offered at four account sizes, $25,000, $50,000, $100,000, and $150,000. The $50,000 size offers two checkout variants, differing in their Maximum Loss Limit (MLL), the Default variant with a $2,000 MLL or the Add-On variant with a $1,500 MLL, and, as the tables below record, in One-Time Eval Fee, Minimum Balance and Buffer Requirement. Neither the $25K, $100K, nor $150K size's own dedicated article mentions a second variant of any kind, and `myfundedfutures.com/plans/builder`'s rules table, re-fetched 2026-10-02, adds a "(with add-on)" figure only in its 50K column; the 25K, 100K and 150K Default/Add-On status stays Unconfirmed rather than assumed absent (see Not Confirmed). All four sizes share the same evaluation, simulated-funding, and live-transition structure, and all four now carry a confirmed soft-pause Daily Loss Limit that scales with tier: $600 (25K), $1,000 (50K), $1,750 (100K), $2,500 (150K), each directly stated by that tier's own current dedicated article and independently corroborated by `myfundedfutures.com/plans/builder`'s own rules table. The sizes allow a different number of concurrent Sim Funded accounts (see the tables below). The plan is designed for traders progressing from evaluation through simulated funding to a live funded account, with a structured payout progression capped at five payouts before live promotion at every size, each size's own dedicated article independently restating this same fifth-payout live-transition mechanic in its own words (see Live Transition). News trading is unrestricted during the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages at every size, independently confirmed firm-wide by "News Trading Policy": "Unrestricted Accounts: Trading on T1 News events is permitted for the following: ... Builder Plans" (live-stage news trading policy is not stated in any size's own source, and the firm-wide article does not name a Live stage either, see Not Confirmed; "Fair Play and Prohibited Trading Practices" Section 2 separately lists Tier 1 Economic Data Trading as restricted with no qualification, a live conflict recorded in README.md's Firm-Wide Rules). **Corrected 2026-09-26, replacing a 2026-09-22 finding that no longer holds.** The firm-wide "Payout Policy Overview" article claims "No daily loss limits on Builder, Rapid or Pro plans." A 2026-09-22 pass found this wrong for 50K (its own FAQ confirms a soft-pause DLL across all three stages) and "accidentally correct" for 25K, since 25K's then-current article stated no DLL at all. That 25K finding described the Legacy-titled article, not the current one: the current 25K article confirms a $600 soft-pause DLL, so every Builder tier article now contradicts the firm-wide "no daily loss limits" sentence, not just 50K's. No source reconciles them: the tables carry each tier article's DLL figure, which `myfundedfutures.com/plans/builder`'s rules table also states, and the Payout Policy Overview sentence is recorded as a live conflict (see Not Confirmed), not ranked below them.

## How the Drawdown Works

The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing drawdown that adjusts after each market close based on the account's closing equity, confirmed identically at all four sizes. The MLL trails upward as the account reaches new end-of-day highs, maintaining a constant distance between the account balance and the minimum allowed balance. The MLL never moves down during the trailing phase. Open equity losses are counted when determining whether the account has breached the MLL at end of day.

The MLL locks permanently once it reaches $100 above the starting balance, in every tier article's own wording. From that point, it does not trail further and remains static. The stated Buffer Requirement is "MLL + $100" ($1,100 at 25K, $2,100 (Default) / $1,600 (Add-On) at 50K, $3,100 at 100K, and $4,600 at 150K per that tier's own table; its own prose sentence instead says "$4,500," an internal inconsistency in the 150K article itself, see Not Confirmed). Applying "MLL reaches $100" to a $0 starting balance gives a derived trigger balance equal to those buffer figures, but no source states that the lock level and the buffer coincide, so the Drawdown Lock cells leave the balance level Unconfirmed. Separately, every size's own soft-pause Daily Loss Limit ($600/$1,000/$1,750/$2,500) is a distinct mechanic from the MLL: hitting it pauses trading for the rest of the session without breaching the account, per every tier's own article ("Soft DLL does not breach your account... Only breaching the Max Loss Limit (MLL) closes the account").

**Recorded 2026-10-02, a conflict inside the firm's own material that this file does not settle: what triggers the lock.** Every tier article states the lock as the MLL reaching $100 above the starting balance, with no payout condition. Two other current sources point to a first-payout trigger instead: the firm-wide Payout Policy Overview's "After your first payout, the Max Loss Limit resets to $100" (its Builder section), and the plan page's embedded payload field `moveMllToLockOnFirstPayout: true` (present on the payloads of every plan page, its meaning unexplained). The tables carry the tier articles' wording and attribute it; the other two are recorded in Not Confirmed, not blended in and not ranked.

### Worked Example

Starting Sim Funded at $0 (matching both sizes' own "begins at $0" / "account begins at $0" language), using the $50,000 tier, Default variant. Figures the 50K article states: the $0 start, the -$2,000 starting minimum balance, the $2,000 distance, "The MLL locks permanently once it reaches $100 above the starting balance," the $2,100 buffer, the $500 first-payout net profit, the $2,600 balance in its FAQ, the $500 minimum payout, the $2,000 per-cycle cap and the 80/20 split. Every other figure below is this file's own arithmetic, marked derived, and the +$2,100 balance at which the MLL first reaches $100 is derived (see Not Confirmed). The example follows the tier articles' lock trigger; the first-payout trigger recorded in Not Confirmed is not modeled.

1. Account starts with $0 balance and a -$2,000 MLL (Default variant), the article's stated "Starting Minimum Balance -$2,000". Minimum allowed balance is -$2,000.
2. After the first day, the account closes at +$500 profit. Derived: the new high is $500, so the MLL trails up to $500 - $2,000 = -$1,500; minimum allowed is now -$1,500.
3. Over subsequent sessions, the account reaches a new high of $2,100. Derived: the MLL trails to $2,100 - $2,000 = $100.
4. Per the tier articles, the MLL has now reached $100 above the starting balance ($0) and locks permanently; the minimum allowed balance stays at $100 and does not trail further, even if the account goes higher. The +$2,100 balance at which this happens is derived, and the sources that point to a first-payout trigger instead are recorded in Not Confirmed. The $2,100 buffer is now cleared, but net profit above the buffer is $0, so a payout cannot yet be requested (the 50K article requires at least $500 net profit above the buffer for the first payout).
5. The account continues trading and reaches a balance of $2,600, the figure the 50K article's FAQ gives ($2,100 buffer plus $500 net profit above it).
6. The trader requests a payout of $500, the stated minimum payout request and well within the stated $2,000 per-cycle cap. Derived: the trader receives 80% of $500, $400, after the firm's 20% cut, per the stated 80/20 split.

**Payout Requirements (step-by-step), all four sizes:**

- Buffer must be fully cleared before submitting any payout request.
- First payout: at least $250 (25K) / $500 (50K) / $1,000 (100K) / $1,500 (150K) net profit above the buffer.
- Subsequent payouts: the same amount per tier, since the last approved payout.
- Must have traded on at least 2 trading days in the current cycle.
- 50% consistency rule: single largest profit day cannot exceed 50% of total profits in the cycle; resets after each approved payout.
- Maximum payout per cycle is $1,000 (25K) / $2,000 (50K) / $3,000 (100K) / $4,500 or $3,500 (150K, see the unresolved conflict above). Trader receives 80% of the withdrawn amount.
