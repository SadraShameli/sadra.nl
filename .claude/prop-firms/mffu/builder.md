## How the Drawdown Works

The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing drawdown that adjusts after each market close based on the account's closing equity, confirmed identically at all four sizes. The MLL trails upward as the account reaches new end-of-day highs, maintaining a constant distance between the account balance and the minimum allowed balance. The MLL never moves down during the trailing phase. Open equity losses are counted when determining whether the account has breached the MLL at end of day.

The MLL locks permanently once it reaches $100 above the starting balance. From that point, it does not trail further and remains static. For Sim Funded accounts starting at $0, this lock corresponds to a buffer requirement of $1,100 at 25K, $2,100 (Default) / $1,600 (Add-On) at 50K, $3,100 at 100K, and $4,600 at 150K (per that tier's own table; its own prose sentence instead says "$4,500," an internal inconsistency in the 150K article itself, see Not Confirmed), each nominally equal to the tier's own MLL plus $100. Separately, every size's own soft-pause Daily Loss Limit ($600/$1,000/$1,750/$2,500) is a distinct mechanic from the MLL: hitting it pauses trading for the rest of the session without breaching the account, per every tier's own article ("Soft DLL does not breach your account... Only breaching the Max Loss Limit (MLL) closes the account").

**Resolved 2026-09-22, balance-based trigger vs. the site's own `moveMllToLockOnFirstPayout` field.** This balance-based trigger, stated directly and identically by both tiers' own dedicated help-center articles, is treated as controlling over two lower-ranked sources that point to a first-payout-tied trigger instead: the main pricing page's own embedded JSON field `moveMllToLockOnFirstPayout: true`, and the firm-wide Payout Policy Overview's "After your first payout, the Max Loss Limit resets to $100." Per this tree's sourcing hierarchy, a dedicated, plan-specific help-center article outranks both a firm-wide article (on that plan's own figures) and a marketing-page data field (help center over marketing/product pages), so the balance-based reading stands as the confirmed value; the first-payout-tied sources are flagged here, not blended into the table above.

### Worked Example

Starting Sim Funded at $0 (matching both sizes' own "begins at $0" / "account begins at $0" language), using the $50,000 tier, Default variant.

1. Account starts with $0 balance and a -$2,000 MLL (Default variant). Minimum allowed balance is -$2,000.
2. After the first day, the account closes at +$500 profit. The new high is $500, so the MLL trails up to $500 - $2,000 = -$1,500. Minimum allowed is now -$1,500.
3. Over subsequent sessions, the account reaches a new high of $2,100. The MLL trails to $2,100 - $2,000 = $100.
4. At this point, the MLL has reached $100 above the starting balance ($0) and locks there permanently, per the tier-specific help articles' own direct statement (see How the Drawdown Works for why this reading controls over the site's conflicting `moveMllToLockOnFirstPayout` field). From this moment, the minimum allowed balance remains at $100 and does not trail further, even if the account goes higher. The $2,100 buffer is now cleared, but net profit above the buffer is currently $0, so a payout cannot yet be requested (the FAQ requires at least $500 net profit above the buffer for the first payout at this tier).
5. The account continues trading and reaches a further new high of $2,600 ($2,100 buffer + $500 net profit above it).
6. The trader requests a payout: the buffer is cleared and $500 net profit above it meets the FAQ's stated minimum for a first payout. The trader submits a withdrawal request of $500 (well within the $2,000 per-cycle cap) and receives 80% of the withdrawn amount, $400, after the firm's 20% cut.

**Payout Requirements (step-by-step), all four sizes:**

- Buffer must be fully cleared before submitting any payout request.

- Must have traded on at least 2 trading days in the current cycle.
- 50% consistency rule: single largest profit day cannot exceed 50% of total profits in the cycle; resets after each approved payout.
