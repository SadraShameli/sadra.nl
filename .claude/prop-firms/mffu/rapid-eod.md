## Overview

Rapid EOD is, per its own 25K article, "built on the same foundation as the standard Rapid 25K plan"; that article names its key difference as the Sim Funded stage trailing end-of-day instead of intraday, with a lower evaluation consistency rule, and states it "keeps Rapid's 90/10 profit split." In both tiers' articles the Sim Funded Max Loss "does not move intraday – only at the close of each trading session." Confirmed at two account sizes, $25K and $50K. Both tiers' articles state that live transition follows the same rules as the standard Rapid plan at the matching size (see Live Transition).

## How the Drawdown Works

Your Max Loss Limit (MLL) trails upward each time your account closes at a new end-of-day high, stated in both tiers' own articles; the explicit "It never moves down" sentence appears only in the 50K article. The distance between your account balance and the Max Loss Limit stays fixed at the tier's own drawdown amount while it is trailing. It does not move intraday, only at the close of each trading session.

Once your trailing Max Loss Limit reaches $100, it locks at that value and stops trailing. From that point forward, it does not move again. You must always keep at least $100 in the account balance. If your balance drops below $100, the account is breached.

**Recorded 2026-10-02, a conflict inside the firm's own material that this file does not settle: when the lock happens.** `myfundedfutures.com/plans/rapid-eod`'s embedded pricing payload carries `moveMllToLockOnFirstPayout: true` on both the 25K and 50K records, the same field found on the payloads of the Builder, Pro and Rapid plan pages. No source explains what the field means, and both tiers' own help-center articles state the trigger as the trailing Max Loss reaching $100, with no payout condition. The table carries the articles' wording; the field is recorded here, not reconciled with it.

### Worked Example

Starting at a literal $0 balance (matching the source's own "Initial Balance | $0"), using the $50,000 tier. Every Max Loss figure below is this file's own arithmetic from the stated $2,000 distance and the stated $100 lock. The +$2,100 end-of-day high at which the trailing Max Loss would first reach $100 equals the $2,000 distance plus $100; neither tier article states it, and the firm's blog post of Aug 17, 2026 states it for the 50K plan in starting-balance terms ("Once your end-of-day balance is $2,100 above your starting balance, the max loss limit stops at $100 and never trails again"), with an example that starts at $50,000, so expressing it as +$2,100 profit under this example's $0 convention is this file's restatement (see Not Confirmed).

1. **Day 1:** Account closes at +$500 profit (new EOD high). Derived: the MLL trails to $500 − $2,000 = −$1,500, keeping the stated $2,000 distance; the account can decline down to −$1,500 without breaching.

2. **Day 2:** Account closes at +$1,800 profit (new EOD high). Derived: the MLL trails to $1,800 − $2,000 = −$200, distance maintained at $2,000.

3. **Day 3:** Account closes at +$2,300 profit (new EOD high). Derived: uncapped trailing arithmetic would put the MLL at $2,300 − $2,000 = $300, above the $100 at which the source says it locks ("Once your trailing Max Loss reaches $100, it locks there"). On the same arithmetic the trailing MLL first reaches $100 at a +$2,100 EOD high, which equals the blog post's "$2,100 above your starting balance" restated under this example's $0 convention (see Not Confirmed), so the MLL locks at $100 on Day 3, not $300, and does not trail further.

4. **Day 4 onward:** Account closes at +$1,800 profit (a decline from the +$2,300 peak, due to trading losses). The MLL remains locked at $100 per the stated lock, it does not move down with the balance decline, and it does not trail again on a future new high. As long as the account balance stays at or above $100, the account remains active; the source states a balance below $100 breaches the account.

## Live Transition

Both tiers' own articles state directly that live transition "follows the same rules as standard Rapid" at the matching tier size. Per the "Understanding Rapid Live" article (<https://help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/13134718-understanding-rapid-live>, updated September 8, 2026), those transition pathways are: automatic transition upon achieving $10,000 in net profit within a single trading day, or via Risk Management Team approval following consistent payout performance. The 25K article's own FAQ separately confirms: "Can I trade on live and sim at the same time? No, once you have a live account, you cannot trade on the sim and live environment simultaneously."

See [rapid-live.md](./rapid-live.md) (shared by Rapid and Rapid EOD) for full live account parameters, the Reserve Program, and payout/breach mechanics.

**No activation fee at either tier; "no monthly subscription" is 25K-specific wording.** The 25K article states: "There are no activation fees on this plan and no monthly subscription costs." The 50K article confirms only the activation-fee half: "There are no activation fees on this plan" / "Our Rapid EOD account does not have an activation fee," and never mentions a subscription at all. `myfundedfutures.com/plans/rapid-eod` states "No activation fee · One-time payment, no renewals" for the plan; the 50K article itself states nothing about a subscription, and this file does not carry the 25K article's wording over to it.
