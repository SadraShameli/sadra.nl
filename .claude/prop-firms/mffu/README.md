# `MyFundedFutures` (MFF / MFFU) Plans Reference

**Firm website:** `https://myfundedfutures.com`
**Last Verified:** `2026-09-18`
**Last Updated:** `2026-09-18`
**Source:** see each plan's own file for plan-specific claims; the Firm-Wide Rules below are drawn from two generic, non-plan-specific "Live Accounts" collection articles (both cited in full at the bottom of this file), used ONLY where those articles themselves state a rule applies across account types.

## Overview

MyFundedFutures documents four active plan families in this tree: **Rapid**, **Rapid EOD**, **Builder**, and **Pro**. All four follow the same broad progression — Evaluation → Sim Funded → Live — but differ materially in drawdown mechanics (intraday-trailing vs. EOD-trailing), consistency rules, contract limits, and payout structure. Rapid and Rapid EOD share one live-account stage (`rapid-live.md`); Builder and Pro each describe their own live transition inline in their own plan file, since their own source articles cover it directly.

MFF's help center also references a "Flex Plan" as a legacy/discontinued offering — seen only in the related-article titles of other sources provided in this batch (e.g. "Flex Plan - The Path Forward (Legacy)", "25K Flex Plan (Legacy)") — and "Core Plan" / "Scale Plan" articles referenced by URL in the generic Live Accounts FAQ but not provided as source material. None of these are documented here; see Documentation Scope below.

## Plans

- **[Rapid ($50,000)](rapid.md)** — intraday-trailing drawdown at the Sim Funded stage (locks at a $100 absolute floor), 90/10 sim payout split, $2,100 payout buffer.
- **[Rapid EOD ($50,000)](rapid-eod.md)** — same family as Rapid, but EOD-trailing (not intraday) drawdown at both Evaluation and Sim Funded stages; also locks at a $100 floor once the EOD high first reaches $2,100; 90/10 sim payout split.
- **[Builder ($50,000)](builder.md)** — two checkout variants (Default: $2,000 MLL / Add-On: $1,500 MLL), EOD-trailing drawdown locking at starting-balance+$100, 80/20 payout split, capped at 5 sim payouts before live promotion.
- **[Pro ($50K / $100K / $150K)](pro.md)** — three account sizes, EOD Maximum Loss Limit that locks permanently at starting-balance+$100 after the first payout, 80/20 split, $100,000 lifetime sim-funded payout cap per user, no daily loss limit. This plan's own source has no evaluation-rules table of its own — most Evaluation-stage facts are Unconfirmed here.

## Live Accounts

- **[MyFundedFutures Rapid Live](rapid-live.md)** — shared by Rapid and Rapid EOD. EOD-based Max Loss Limit that trails with account balance and locks at a $0 floor (not a +$100 buffer, unlike Builder/Pro's live stage); Reserve Program (up to $5,000 of Sim Funded profit held back, unlocked as a Performance Bonus after 20 profitable Live days + $10,000 cumulative gross payouts, or used to offset a breach); 21-day post-breach cooldown.

Builder and Pro's own live-account parameters are documented inline in `builder.md` and `pro.md` respectively (each plan's own source article covers its live transition directly, so no separate shared file was created for them).

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules stated by their own source as applying broadly across account types — not inferred by checking each plan's file individually — and only where no documented plan's own file contradicts them:

- **No activation fee on any documented plan** — confirmed independently in every plan's own file: Rapid ("there are absolutely no activation fees for any of our plans"), Rapid EOD ("There are no activation fees on this plan"), Builder ("$0" in its own pricing table; "There are absolutely no activation fees on any of our plans"), Pro ("Our new Pro account does not have an activation fee"). Independently corroborated a third time by "Traders Evaluation Simplified": "at MFFU, there are absolutely no activation fees for any of our plans." This is distinct from the *evaluation fee* itself, which is a real cost on the plans that state one (e.g. Builder's $153/$125).
- **Evaluation and Sim-Funded account-holding limits, by account size, not by plan name** — per "Traders Evaluation Simplified": traders may hold a maximum of ten (10) active Evaluation accounts at any time, firm-wide. For Sim-Funded accounts, the cap is by account size: up to five (5) active Sim-Funded accounts when holding only $25K and/or $50K sizes (combinable in any mix); if a trader holds any $100K or $150K Sim-Funded account, the cap drops to three (3) across all Sim-Funded sizes. The source does not state whether this bucket is shared across different plan families at the same size (e.g. a Rapid $50K and a Pro $50K counting against one combined cap) or applies per plan family — and it directly conflicts with two plan-specific figures already documented: Builder's own source states a flat cap of 1 Sim-Funded account, and Rapid EOD's own source states a flat cap of 3 regardless of size mix. Where a plan's own file states its own specific figure, that figure is kept as authoritative in that file; this generic bucket is applied only where no plan-specific figure exists (Rapid, Pro). See each plan's own Not Confirmed section for the specific conflict.
- **Profit-split direction** — per "Payout Policy Overview" (written throughout in second person to traders): "Builder and Pro Plans: Earn 80% of all profits" and "Rapid Plans: Earn 90% for Rapid sim funded plans as of January 12th, 2026" (the January date implies a prior, lower rate before then, not itself documented here). "Earn X%" directly identifies X as the trader's own share, resolving the direction question each plan's own file previously left open; the January-2026 rate-change date is new information not previously documented anywhere in this tree.
- **No inactivity timer/rule on any Live account, regardless of account type** — per the generic Live Accounts FAQ's own words: "All our live accounts, no matter what account type you were originally on, have no inactivity timer/rule." This is corroborated independently by Builder's own FAQ ("There is no inactivity rule on the live account") and by the Rapid Live article's own notes. Pro's own file does not separately confirm or contradict this for its live stage (marked Unconfirmed there) — included here on the strength of the generic FAQ's own explicit "no matter what account type" framing, not on a Pro-specific citation.
- **7-day inactivity rule, Evaluation and Sim-Funded stages, for the newer "one-time payment model" only** — per a dedicated firm-wide article, "Inactivity Rule - One-Time Payment Model" (`help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/16596524`, independently verified via raw HTML): "If a trader does not place a trade for 7 consecutive calendar days, the account could be closed. This includes simulated funded accounts." No specific plan is named, and the article never mentions Live accounts at all — distinct in scope from the Live-account rule above, not a contradiction of it. Its own text limits this to accounts bought under the newer "one-time payment model" (effective August 25, 2026); "legacy customer[s]... continue... under your existing price, plan, billing cycle, and terms." Nothing in this tree's sources states which pricing model any documented plan's own figures reflect, so this is not promoted to a confirmed per-plan fact anywhere — see each plan's own Not Confirmed section for where it's treated as a strong, unconfirmed candidate.
- **Strict no-hedging policy, sim and live alike** — per the generic Live Accounts FAQ: "MyFunded Futures maintains a strict no-hedging policy. We do not allow any kind of hedging, whether you are a sim trader or live."
- **Live payout cutoff and minimum** — per the generic Live Accounts FAQ: payout requests must be submitted before 11 AM EST to be processed the same day (otherwise the next business day); payouts are processed once per day by the firm's partner FCM (no multiple same-day payout requests); the minimum live withdrawal amount is $250 across account types.
- **Microscalping and Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) are both permitted on live accounts** — per the generic Live Accounts FAQ, framed as a general live-account policy (not scoped to one plan), though the FAQ itself cautions both carry real execution/liquidation risk.
- **Auto-close of open positions by 4:10 PM EST, no breach penalty** — per the generic Live Accounts FAQ: live trades are automatically closed by MFF's systems before 4:10 PM EST; a position still open past that time due to a system issue does not itself trigger a breach.
- **Live-trader-borne costs**: CME Professional Data Subscription, round-trip commissions/fees, and platform license/subscription — per the generic "Understanding Live Funded Account" article, deducted directly from the Live account balance. Framed generically, not scoped to one plan.
- **Sim Funded account(s) go dormant, and multiple Sim Funded accounts merge into one Live account, on Live transition** — per the generic "Understanding Live Funded Account" article. Independently, the Rapid Live article's own transition-process section states the same dormancy behavior for the Rapid family specifically.

## Documentation Scope

- **Flex Plan** — referenced only as related-article titles in other sources ("Flex Plan - The Path Forward (Legacy)", "25K Flex Plan (Legacy)"), both explicitly marked "(Legacy)" in their own article titles. No dedicated Flex Plan source was provided in this batch, so it is not documented here.
- **Core Plan and Scale Plan** — referenced by URL in the generic "Comprehensive FAQ - Live Accounts" article ("Please review our articles on this: * Core Plan... * Scale Plan...") but neither article was provided as source material in this batch. Not documented here; do not assume their rules resemble Rapid, Rapid EOD, Builder, or Pro.
- **Pro's standard Evaluation-stage rules** — Pro's own "Sim-Funded and Live Account Highlights" article explicitly links out to a separate, uncited "Traders Evaluation Simplified" article for evaluation rules rather than stating them itself. Most of Pro's Evaluation table is therefore genuinely Unconfirmed by the source actually provided, not an oversight.
- **Pro's 1-Day Addon** — a limited-time promotional evaluation variant for the Pro $50K plan specifically ($4,000 profit target, no consistency rule, no daily loss limit, pass in one day). Documented as a clearly-scoped aside in `pro.md`, not folded into Pro's baseline Evaluation table.
- **The generic Live Accounts FAQ's "$140 minimum balance" / "$5,000 account → $4,860 max drawdown" example** — this figure does not match any documented plan's own live-drawdown numbers (Rapid Live's floor is $0; Builder's live MLL locks at starting-balance+$100; Pro's own "$140" figure is a different mechanic — the point at which the *initial live allocation itself* becomes withdrawable, not a drawdown floor). Not assigned to any plan in this tree; flagged in the generic-source extract but deliberately not written into any plan file's numeric tables.
- **Builder's live-stage news trading policy** and **Pro's live-stage inactivity rule** — each plan's own source is silent on these specific points for the live stage; see each plan's own Not Confirmed section rather than assuming either the generic Live Accounts FAQ's broader claims or a sibling plan's rule applies.
- **"No daily loss limits on Builder, Rapid or Pro plans" (Payout Policy Overview) — researched and confirmed inaccurate, not a stage-scope mixup.** Live-fetched Builder's own article directly: its FAQ states "The Evaluation, Sim Funded and Live stage have a $1,000 soft pause DLL," naming all three stages explicitly, corroborated by three separate table rows within that same article. There is no stage of Builder's lifecycle where Builder's own source claims "no DLL" — the marketing bullet is simply wrong for Builder, a plan-scope error in that article, not an ambiguity in Builder's own data. It is also wrong for Pro's live stage specifically: Pro's own Live Account Parameters table (pro.md) has a confirmed Daily Loss Limit Range ($700–$1,800 on the $50K tier, scaling up by tier) — live-stage DLLs that this same marketing claim denies exist. The claim is accurate only for Rapid's and Pro's own Evaluation/Sim-Funded stages, where their own sources independently state "None." It is not treated as reliable anywhere in this tree, including for Rapid's still-unconfirmed Sim Funded DLL, where nothing in Rapid's own source directly contradicts it but the claim's proven unreliability elsewhere means it isn't trusted to resolve that gap either.
- **"The consistency rule is 50% for MyFunded Futures evaluations on the Rapid & Pro plans" (Consistency Rule at My FundedFutures) — researched and confirmed as a genuine same-stage contradiction for Rapid EOD, not a stage-scope mixup.** Both this claim and Rapid EOD's own confirmed 30% are explicitly scoped to the Evaluation stage by each source's own wording (live-fetched and confirmed against both articles directly) — there is no Evaluation-vs-Sim-Funded distinction that reconciles them. Standard/intraday Rapid and Rapid EOD are documented as separate sub-plans in MFF's own help center (distinct collections: "Intraday Drawdown Rapid Plans" vs. "End of Day Drawdown Rapid Plans"). Standard Rapid's own dedicated source confirms 50% is correct for that plan specifically; the generic article's "50% for Rapid" most plausibly describes standard Rapid without addressing Rapid EOD as a distinct sub-plan. Rapid EOD's own 30% remains controlling there — see rapid-eod.md's Not Confirmed section.
- **"Traders Evaluation Simplified"** — used across this tree as the primary source for Pro's Evaluation table (previously almost entirely unconfirmed) and as corroboration for Rapid, Rapid EOD, and Builder's own Evaluation tables. It is also the source of the account-size-based Sim-Funded holding-limit rule above, and of the firm-wide 10-account Evaluation-account cap.
