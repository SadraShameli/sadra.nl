## Overview

Select Flex is the 5-day payout track of Tradeify's Select product. Select Daily and Select Flex share one evaluation (a single 3-day-minimum, 40%-consistency evaluation per account size) and only branch into two different funded-stage payout policies after a trader passes and manually activates the funded account from the dashboard ("Select Evaluation Accounts"; "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies"); the choice is permanent once made per funded account: a trader with multiple funded accounts can pick different policies on each: "Yes, if you pass multiple evaluations, you can choose different payout policies for each funded account." (per account, not per user). There is no activation fee ("Are There Activation Fees?"). Once funded, Select Flex carries no consistency rule and no Daily Loss Limit ("Rules: Daily Loss Limit": "Select Flex accounts do not have a DLL"): only an EOD trailing max drawdown and a 5-winning-day payout cadence, with no minimum account balance required for payout eligibility.

Select Flex payouts are capped at 50% of the account's total profit position per cycle, up to a dollar cap that varies by account size and, as of September 1, 2026, by purchase date. Traders keep 90% of profit on funded payouts. Select Flex funded accounts can advance to Tradeify's Elite Live program at the firm's discretion after meeting a payout-count threshold, and up to 5 funded accounts (a pool shared across all Tradeify plan types) may be active at once.

**Inactivity rule.** "Guidelines for Traders" (August 12, 2026) states, under "Maximum Account Idle Time (Funded and Evaluation Accounts)": "To keep your funded or evaluation account active, you must place at least one trade per week (Monday through Friday). This is per account, not per user. Failure to do so may result in your account being marked as inactive." The article names no plan. This tree's simulator engine models a 7-day idle-closure rule for Select; whether "one trade per week" is the same rule as the engine's 7-day figure is not stated by the article. "Essential Trading Rules Overview" (lastUpdatedDate 2026-08-26T17:48:43Z, user browser dump 2026-09-18) words the same rule as "Minimum Activity: At least 1 trade per week (Monday-Friday) — applies to both evaluation and funded accounts" and adds "The trade can be as brief as 5 seconds as long as it satisfies the minimum activity requirement."

## How the Drawdown Works

Select Flex funded accounts use an End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing Max Drawdown. "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns" states this mechanic "Applies to: All Tradeify accounts (Growth, Select, and Lightning)." and separately reconfirms it by name for "both Select Flex and Select Daily funded accounts." The floor trails the account's highest-ever EOD balance (its high-water mark) and only ever moves up: a new EOD high raises the floor; a lower EOD balance than the prior day leaves the floor unchanged. Although the tracked floor value only updates once per day at EOD, the source stresses it is "enforced in real time against your net liquidation value": if net liq touches the floor at any moment intraday, the account fails immediately ("hard breach"), even if the account would otherwise have recovered by the close. Unlike Evaluation accounts, a Sim Funded breach cannot be reset: Tradeify's reset process is stated to apply only to Evaluation accounts, and "There is no reset option for Lightning Funded or Sim Funded accounts. If you fail these accounts, the failure is permanent and you will need to purchase a new account to continue trading." ("How do I Reset a Failed Evaluation?").

On Sim Funded accounts specifically (not during Evaluation), the floor eventually stops trailing and locks permanently: "On Sim Funded accounts (not Evaluations), your drawdown locks in place once you profit beyond the drawdown amount by $100, or immediately when you request a payout - whichever comes first. After locking, the drawdown limit becomes a fixed floor that never moves up again." For the Select Flex 50K account this produces two distinct numbers, which this file states separately rather than collapsing into a single "locks at $X" line: the **trigger**: an EOD balance of $52,100 (the $2,000 drawdown amount exceeded by $100, i.e. $2,100 profit); and the **locked value**: a floor fixed at $50,100 ($100 above starting balance). The source states both in one sentence: "a 50K Select account locks when EOD balance reaches $52,100, and the drawdown floor becomes $50,100 permanently."

This matches this repo's own engine exactly: `buildSelectFlexPlan` in `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/tradeify/Tradeify.ts` sets `fundedDrawdown`'s lock `atProfit: dollars(size.maxDrawdown + 100)` = $2,100 (the profit trigger) and `lockedThreshold: lockThresholdAt(100)` = starting balance + $100 = $50,100 (the locked floor), via the shared `lockThresholdAt` helper in `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/shared.ts` (`(start) => start + offset`). "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns" confirms the pair directly by name ("a 50K Select account locks when EOD balance reaches $52,100, and the drawdown floor becomes $50,100 permanently."). Growth's own Drawdown Lock row in `growth.md` rests on the same firm-wide article.

### Worked Example

This example uses the full nominal-balance convention: the account's real balance, starting at an assumed $50,000 (the "50K" tier-label reading in "Select Evaluation Accounts"; no table or parameter line states a Starting Balance, and the payout article's own examples use $50,000 only as an example figure, see Not Confirmed), and never switches to a $0-based or profit-only figure at any step. Every figure marked "derived" below is arithmetic on the assumed start.

1. The Select Flex 50K funded account opens at an assumed balance of $50,000. The $2,000 trailing max drawdown sets an initial floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000.
2. Profitable trading brings the EOD balance to $52,100 (a $2,100 profit, derived as $52,100 − the assumed $50,000, and $100 more than the $2,000 drawdown amount). This is the lock trigger: the floor stops trailing and locks permanently at $50,100 ($100 above the assumed $50,000 starting balance).
3. Trading continues, and the account records 5 winning days (each ≥$150 profit, the 50K tier's Winning Day Threshold), with the EOD balance now at $52,500 ("Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies," First Payout Example). Since the balance already exceeded $52,100 before this point, the drawdown floor is already locked at $50,100. Total profit is $52,500 − the assumed $50,000 = $2,500 (derived; the source's example states the same figure). Select Flex payouts are capped at 50% of total profit, so the trader is eligible to withdraw 50% of $2,500 = $1,250 (derived, matching the source's example). They request and receive $1,250; balance after payout is $52,500 − $1,250 = $51,250 (the source states $51,250).
4. A second payout cycle follows; a positive net profit for that cycle is required to request another payout (a rule the source states applies "This applies equally to both Flex and Daily"). The balance grows to $54,000. Total profit since the assumed $50,000 starting balance is $54,000 − $50,000 = $4,000 (derived; the source's example states the same figure), so the second payout's eligibility is 50% of $4,000 = $2,000 (derived, matching the source's example): recalculated from the total profit position each time, not just the second cycle's own gain, per the source's own emphasis: "The 50% is calculated from your TOTAL profit position (current balance minus starting balance), not just the profit made during the current cycle."

## Live Transition

Select Flex funded accounts can advance to Tradeify's Elite Live program at Tradeify's discretion. "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies" states the eligibility threshold as "3 payouts on a single account, or 10 total payouts since the last live transition across all plan types" explicitly describing this as "These thresholds represent the minimum requirements for consideration, not automatic qualification." and stating it "These eligibility criteria apply across all plan types — Select, Growth, and Lightning." The threshold is a live conflict between the firm's own sources, not resolved: "SELECT vs Growth: Choosing Your Evaluation Type" states Elite Live comes "after 5 successful payouts from your Sim Funded accounts," and the summary line of "Introducing the New Select Plan" reads "5-payout qualification for Growth/Lightning/Select" (its body says only "Meet the minimum payout thresholds"). The Funded Trader Agreement §5.2 lists 3 and 10 as internal guidelines but its §11 makes the Help Center rule prevail over the Agreement, so §5.2 does not decide it. This file reports the payout-policies article's figures as that article's own statement and picks none; see `elite-live.md`. Per "Introducing the New Select Plan...," the destination program (Tradeify Elite) has no cap on simulated profit built up before transitioning, no limit on how many times a trader may transition, and every funded account that receives a payout becomes its own Elite Live account (up to 5 simultaneously); Elite Live accounts carry no Daily Loss Limit. Traders using accounts purchased before December 3rd may instead opt into the previous live system (one shared live account, $100K simulated-profit cap) rather than the new per-account Elite Live structure. (This file's own cited source states only "December 3rd" with no year; the year 2025 is confirmed in the Tradeify Elite Program article, cited by [elite-live.md](elite-live.md).)

This file does not document Elite Live's own operating parameters (starting balance, profit split, scaling, payout mechanics): per this tree's own conventions those belong in a shared live-account file, now documented at [`elite-live.md`](elite-live.md), not duplicated here.
