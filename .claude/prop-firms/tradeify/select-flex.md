## Overview

Select Flex is the 5-day payout track of Tradeify's Select product. Select Daily and Select Flex share one evaluation — a single 3-day-minimum, 40%-consistency evaluation per account size — and only branch into two different funded-stage payout policies after a trader passes and manually activates the funded account from the dashboard ("Select Evaluation Accounts"; "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies"); the choice is permanent once made. There is no activation fee ("Are There Activation Fees?"). Once funded, Select Flex carries no consistency rule and no Daily Loss Limit ("Rules: Daily Loss Limit": "Select Flex accounts do not have a DLL") — only an EOD trailing max drawdown and a 5-winning-day payout cadence, with no minimum account balance required for payout eligibility.

Select Flex payouts are capped at 50% of the account's total profit position per cycle, up to a dollar cap that varies by account size and, as of September 1, 2026, by purchase date. Traders keep 90% of profit on funded payouts. Select Flex funded accounts can advance to Tradeify's Elite Live program at the firm's discretion after meeting a payout-count threshold, and up to 5 funded accounts (a pool shared across all Tradeify plan types) may be active at once.

**Inactivity rule, now directly confirmed, resolving a previously-flagged engine/doc disagreement.** This tree's own simulator engine had already modeled a 7-day idle-closure rule for Select, citing a "Guidelines for Traders" article that, at the time, hadn't been located or read as a source for this file. That article has now been read directly (found via a full sitemap sweep of `help.tradeify.co`): "To keep your funded or evaluation account active, you must place at least one trade per week (Monday through Friday). This is per account, not per user." Stated without a per-plan qualifier, so treated as firm-wide, independently corroborated for every plan in `README.md`'s Firm-Wide Rules. "Essential Trading Rules Overview" independently corroborates the same weekly requirement and adds a detail neither "Guidelines for Traders" nor the Funded Trader Agreement states: the qualifying trade "can be as brief as 5 seconds as long as it satisfies the minimum activity requirement," so any trade counts, not just a substantively held one.

## How the Drawdown Works

Select Flex funded accounts use an End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing Max Drawdown. "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns" states this mechanic "Applies to: All Tradeify accounts (Growth, Select, and Lightning)." and separately reconfirms it by name for "both Select Flex and Select Daily funded accounts." The floor trails the account's highest-ever EOD balance (its high-water mark) and only ever moves up: a new EOD high raises the floor; a lower EOD balance than the prior day leaves the floor unchanged. Although the tracked floor value only updates once per day at EOD, the source stresses it is "enforced in real time against net liquidation value" — if net liq touches the floor at any moment intraday, the account fails immediately ("hard breach"), even if the account would otherwise have recovered by the close. Unlike Evaluation accounts, a Sim Funded breach cannot be reset — Tradeify's reset process is stated to apply only to Evaluation accounts, and "there is no reset option for Lightning Funded or Sim Funded accounts — failure is permanent, must purchase a new account" ("How do I Reset a Failed Evaluation?").

On Sim Funded accounts specifically — not during Evaluation — the floor eventually stops trailing and locks permanently: "On Sim Funded accounts (NOT Evaluations), drawdown locks once profit exceeds drawdown amount by $100, OR immediately when payout requested — whichever first. After locking, drawdown limit becomes a FIXED floor that never moves up again." For the Select Flex 50K account this produces two distinct numbers, which this file states separately rather than collapsing into a single "locks at $X" line: the **trigger** — an EOD balance of $52,100 (the $2,000 drawdown amount exceeded by $100, i.e. $2,100 profit) — and the **locked value** — a floor fixed at $50,100 ($100 above the $50,000 starting balance). The source states both explicitly in one sentence: "50K Select locks at EOD balance $52,100, floor becomes $50,100 permanently."

This matches this repo's own engine exactly: `buildSelectFlexPlan` in `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/tradeify/Tradeify.ts` sets `fundedDrawdown`'s lock `atProfit: dollars(size.maxDrawdown + 100)` = $2,100 (the profit trigger) and `lockedThreshold: lockThresholdAt(100)` = starting balance + $100 = $50,100 (the locked floor), via the shared `lockThresholdAt` helper in `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/shared.ts` (`(start) => start + offset`). Prior to this pass, this figure was only an unconfirmed engine assumption for Select Flex specifically; the newly available drawdown-lock article now confirms it directly by name ("a 50K Select account locks when EOD balance reaches $52,100, and the drawdown floor becomes $50,100 permanently."), not merely assumed by analogy with Growth. (Growth's own equivalent row in `growth.md` is likewise confirmed by this same firm-wide article, not unconfirmed — do not treat that file's Drawdown Lock row as still-open.)

### Worked Example

This example uses the full nominal-balance convention — the account's real balance, starting at a literal $50,000 (matching the "Select Evaluation Accounts" 50K tier and the drawdown-lock arithmetic above, both of which imply a $50,000 starting balance for this tier) — and never switches to a $0-based or profit-only figure at any step.

1. The Select Flex 50K funded account opens at a balance of $50,000. The $2,000 trailing max drawdown sets an initial floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000.
2. Profitable trading brings the EOD balance to $52,100 (a $2,100 profit — $100 more than the $2,000 drawdown amount). This is the lock trigger: the floor stops trailing and locks permanently at $50,100 ($100 above the $50,000 starting balance).
3. Trading continues, and the account records 5 winning days (each ≥$150 profit, the 50K tier's Winning Day Threshold), with the EOD balance now at $52,500 ("Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies," First Payout Example). Since the balance already exceeded $52,100 before this point, the drawdown floor is already locked at $50,100. Total profit is $52,500 − $50,000 = $2,500. Select Flex payouts are capped at 50% of total profit, so the trader is eligible to withdraw 50% of $2,500 = $1,250. They request and receive $1,250; balance after payout is $52,500 − $1,250 = $51,250.
4. A second payout cycle follows; a positive net profit for that cycle is required to request another payout (a rule the source states applies "This applies equally to both Flex and Daily"). The balance grows to $54,000. Total profit since the account's $50,000 starting balance is $54,000 − $50,000 = $4,000, so the second payout's eligibility is 50% of $4,000 = $2,000 — recalculated from the total profit position each time, not just the second cycle's own gain, per the source's own emphasis: "The 50% is calculated from your TOTAL profit position (current balance minus starting balance), not just the profit made during the current cycle."

**The consistency threshold is inclusive, not strict.** "Any percentage at or below your limit meets the rule - 19.97% meets a 20% rule, and exactly 20.00% meets it too. Only a figure strictly above your limit fails."

## Live Transition

Select Flex funded accounts can advance to Tradeify's Elite Live program at Tradeify's discretion. "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies" states the eligibility threshold as "3 payouts on a single account, or 10 total payouts since the last live transition across all plan types" explicitly describing this as "These thresholds represent the minimum requirements for consideration, not automatic qualification." and stating it "These eligibility criteria apply across all plan types — Select, Growth, and Lightning." Per "Introducing the New Select Plan...," the destination program (Tradeify Elite) has no cap on simulated profit built up before transitioning, no limit on how many times a trader may transition, and every funded account that receives a payout becomes its own Elite Live account (up to 5 simultaneously); Elite Live accounts carry no Daily Loss Limit. Traders using accounts purchased before December 3rd may instead opt into the previous live system (one shared live account, $100K simulated-profit cap) rather than the new per-account Elite Live structure. (This file's own cited source states only "December 3rd" with no year; the year 2025 is confirmed in the Tradeify Elite Program article, cited by [elite-live.md](elite-live.md).)

This file does not document Elite Live's own operating parameters (starting balance, profit split, scaling, payout mechanics) — per this tree's own conventions those belong in a shared live-account file, now documented at [`elite-live.md`](elite-live.md), not duplicated here.

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Starting Balance (Evaluation and Sim Funded)** — no sentence in any cited source literally states "Starting Balance: $50,000." The $50,000 figure in both tables is derived from the "50K Account Evaluation" tier heading in "Select Evaluation Accounts" and from the $100-above-starting-balance arithmetic in "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns" (which states the 50K lock floor is "$50,100," i.e. $100 above a $50,000 starting balance) — not a directly quoted "Starting Balance" statement. Do not treat it as a literal quote.
- **Minimum Balance at Start (Evaluation)** — not stated in any cited source as a concept distinct from the account's own nominal starting balance. Do not assume it equals the drawdown amount or profit target.
- **Reset Fee — $99 vs. $109 conflict** — two of this file's own cited sources disagree. The live `SelectData` JS pricing object on `tradeify.co/select-plan` states "Reset Fee $99 (tooltip: 'Allowed up to 10 resets per month')" for Select 50K; "Tradeify Pricing Reference" states "$109" for the same tier. This file uses $99 in the Evaluation table because it also matches this repo's engine (`Tradeify.ts`'s `SELECT_RESET_FEE = 99`), whose own commit notes say $99 was independently corroborated on 2026-09-14 by reading `tradeify.co/select-plan`'s own live pricing data directly (the page's baked-in `SelectData` JS object) across all three broker tabs — not, as an earlier version of this note stated, a checkout screenshot; that screenshot method belongs to a different engine note (the 50% consistency add-on price, dated 2026-09-17), not this one. The $109 figure is not resolved by this pass, only outweighed by evidence outside this file's own source packet — do not treat $99 as certain, and do not assume "Tradeify Pricing Reference" is simply wrong or stale, since no source here states which article is more current.
- **One-Time Eval Fee — unreconciled $111 quick-preview conflict** — `tradeify.co/select-plan`'s own static "quick preview" radio-tag pricing widget displays $111 for Select 50K, conflicting with the $165 figure in the same page's structured `SelectData` JS object (used in the table above). $165 is now corroborated by a second, independent source — "Tradeify Pricing Reference" also states $165 for Select 50K — strengthening confidence in $165 over $111. The $111 figure still does not reconcile to $165 via any discount code documented on the page (35% off $165 = $107.25, not $111), and the new dump does not mention this widget at all, so this conflict is carried forward unresolved, not silently dropped. Do not treat $111 as a valid current or discounted price.
- **Max Contracts (Sim Funded) — engine vs. source scaling conflict** — this file's Sim Funded table states a start-at-2/scale-to-4-minis progression, sourced to "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies"' Contract Scaling Plan table. This repo's engine (`buildSelectFlexPlan` in `Tradeify.ts`) instead models `fundedMinis`/`fundedMicros` as `ContractLimitKind.Flat` at 4 minis / 40 micros with no scaling logic at all — i.e. the engine treats every Select Flex funded account as starting at the maximum contract size on day one. This is a genuine, unresolved conflict between this file's cited source and the engine's implementation. This file's figure has a direct, dated primary-source citation; the engine's flat model does not cite one for this specific scaling behavior — but that is not itself proof the engine is wrong (it may reflect a later product change not yet documented here). Flagged, not resolved; do not silently make one side match the other.
- **Minimum Balance (ongoing, Sim Funded)** — the cited source confirms no minimum balance is required specifically for _payout eligibility_, but does not separately address any minimum-balance requirement for the account merely to remain open/funded, distinct from breaching the drawdown floor. Do not assume one exists, or that "no minimum for payouts" also means "no minimum for anything," beyond what the drawdown floor itself already enforces.
- **Maximum Total Payouts / Lifetime Cap (Sim Funded) — reasoned inference, not a direct statement** — not stated as a cap on sim-funded payouts. The "3 payouts on a single account, OR 10 total payouts" figure found in the source is an Elite Live transition-_eligibility_ threshold, not a limit on how many payouts a sim-funded account may take — do not conflate the two. The payout mechanic itself (50% of total profit, recalculated fresh each cycle, no described termination) offers no structural evidence either way; treat the "no cap" reading in the table above as a weak inference from the absence of a stopping condition, not a confirmed absence. Do not read the Elite Live eligibility thresholds as a cap on how many sim-funded payouts a Select Flex account may take.
