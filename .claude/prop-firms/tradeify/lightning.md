## Overview

Lightning Funded is Tradeify's instant-funded product: traders purchase direct access to a Sim Funded account and skip the evaluation phase entirely: confirmed both by "Lightning Funded Accounts" ("A 'Lightning Funded' account allows traders to skip the typical evaluation phases and begin trading simulated funds immediately.") and by the homepage's structured pricing data (every tier's `evaluation` array is empty). Lightning is offered in four account sizes (25K/50K/100K/150K); this file documents the $50,000 tier only, matching the size this repo's simulator engine currently models.

**Inactivity rule.** "Guidelines for Traders" (August 12, 2026) states, under "Maximum Account Idle Time (Funded and Evaluation Accounts)": "To keep your funded or evaluation account active, you must place at least one trade per week (Monday through Friday). This is per account, not per user. Failure to do so may result in your account being marked as inactive." The article names no plan. This tree's simulator engine models a 7-day idle-closure rule for Lightning; whether "one trade per week" is the same rule as the engine's 7-day figure is not stated by the article. "Essential Trading Rules Overview" (lastUpdatedDate 2026-08-26T17:48:43Z, user browser dump 2026-09-18) words the same rule as "Minimum Activity: At least 1 trade per week (Monday-Friday) — applies to both evaluation and funded accounts" and adds "The trade can be as brief as 5 seconds as long as it satisfies the minimum activity requirement."

Once funded, Lightning Funded carries an End-of-Day trailing drawdown with the same lock mechanic used across Tradeify's other Sim Funded products, a Daily Loss Limit that escalates once the account crosses a 6%-profit threshold, and a progressive consistency requirement across payout requests (20% / 25% / 30%). Per Lightning's own dedicated payout-policy article, that consistency ladder applies only to accounts purchased after September 12, 2025 8:00 AM EST: accounts purchased before that cutoff remain on a flat 20% consistency rule for every payout. Lightning has no reset option at any stage: a failed account must be repurchased outright, not reset.

## How the Drawdown Works

Lightning Funded enforces an End-of-Day trailing max drawdown, applied identically to every Tradeify Sim Funded product per "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns": the drawdown limit trails the account's highest-ever EOD balance (its high-water mark) and only ever moves up, never down: a lower EOD balance than the prior day leaves the limit exactly where it is. Although the limit itself only _updates_ once per day at EOD, it is _enforced_ continuously: if net liquidation value touches or crosses the limit at any moment during the trading day, the account fails immediately and permanently, even if the balance recovers before that day's close ("a hard breach"... "different from the Daily Loss Limit (soft breach)").

On Sim Funded accounts specifically (not evaluations, irrelevant to Lightning, which has none), this trailing behavior eventually locks. Per "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns": "your drawdown locks in place once you profit beyond the drawdown amount by $100, or immediately when you request a payout - whichever comes first." For the $50,000 tier, that trigger point is an EOD balance of $52,100 (the article's own per-plan lock table lists $52,100 for Lightning at 50K). Once locked, "After locking, the drawdown limit becomes a fixed floor that never moves up again." For the $50,000 tier that fixed floor is $50,100 ("$100 above starting balance"). This is a separate mechanic from the Daily Loss Limit's own 6%-profit escalation (trigger: balance $53,000, described in the Sim Funded table above): the two thresholds are close in size ($52,100 vs. $53,000) but govern different things and must not be treated as the same event.

### Worked Example

This example uses the full nominal-balance convention: the $50,000 account size is used as an assumed starting balance of the Sim Funded account (Lightning has no separate evaluation stage to convert from; no accepted source states a Starting Balance, see Not Confirmed), and every step below tracks the account's actual EOD balance, not a separate $0-based profit counter. The floors below are arithmetic on the assumed start.

1. Account opens funded at an assumed $50,000. Initial drawdown floor: $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000. Initial Daily Loss Limit: $1,250.
2. Day 12 EOD: balance closes at $51,400, a new peak (profit $1,400, derived from the assumed $50,000 start). The floor trails up to $51,400 − $2,000 = $49,400 (derived). Profit has not yet crossed the $2,100 lock-trigger threshold, so the drawdown is still trailing normally, not locked.
3. Day 18 EOD: balance closes at $52,100, a new peak (profit exactly $2,100, derived from the assumed start; $52,100 is the lock-trigger balance stated in "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns"' own table). The drawdown now locks: its floor becomes permanently fixed at $50,100 (stated by the article as "$100 above starting balance," $50,100 for 50K) and will never move again, regardless of future EOD highs.
4. Day 25 EOD: balance closes at $54,500, a new peak (profit $4,500, derived from the assumed start, past the $53,000 / 6%-profit DLL-escalation point reached on an earlier day). Without the lock, ordinary trailing math would move the floor to $54,500 − $2,000 = $52,500 (derived), but because the account already locked on Day 18, the floor stays fixed at $50,100. Separately, because balance crossed $53,000 on an earlier day, the Daily Loss Limit is now $2,000 (up from $1,250) as of the next session after that crossing, a distinct, already-resolved event, not something Day 25 itself triggers.
5. Day 31: the balance falls to $49,900 (an assumed figure), below the locked $50,100 floor. Because the floor is enforced in real time against net liquidation value, the account failed at the moment net liquidation value touched $50,100 that day, not at the close, regardless of how far above the floor it had climbed on Day 25.

Re-deriving from the first sentence: the account never left nominal-dollar-balance terms in any step (no step switched to a $0-based profit figure), and the final breach (net liquidation value reaching the locked $50,100 floor, with the balance at $49,900) is consistent with a floor that stopped moving on Day 18 and never moved again, confirming the example is internally consistent under the stated convention.

## Live Transition

"Lightning Funded: Account Payout Policy" states Tradeify's discretionary right: "Tradeify reserves the right to move you to a Live Funded Account at any time after a successful payout. Tradeify will contact you via email regarding this decision." Separate homepage prose states a trader-facing bar: "Hit 3 approved payouts to qualify for Tradeify Elite." Other Help Center articles state further figures: the Elite Program article and the "Path to Live" subsection of "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies" state "3 payouts on a single account, or 10 total payouts since the last live transition across all plan types" (the latter says it applies across "Select, Growth, and Lightning"), while the summary line of "Introducing the New Select Plan & Changes to the Live Program" reads "5-payout qualification for Growth/Lightning/Select." These are recorded as stated and are a live conflict between the firm's own sources, not resolved: no source says which controls, or whether "any time after a successful payout" and a payout-count threshold describe the same rule, so the Live Transition trigger count is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed and `elite-live.md`). For context only, the Elite Program article (`elite-live.md`'s primary source) says its own eligibility thresholds are "minimum requirements for consideration, not automatic qualification" and that Tradeify "evaluates traders holistically... most traders will not be transitioned to live immediately upon hitting these thresholds"; that article does not mention Lightning's homepage "3 approved payouts" line, so it is not applied to it here.

Elite Live's own operating parameters (starting balance, drawdown, DLL, contract limits, payout split, eligibility mechanics) are documented in full in the shared [`elite-live.md`](elite-live.md) file, not duplicated here.
