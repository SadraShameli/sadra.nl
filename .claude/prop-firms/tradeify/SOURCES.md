## Known unresolved source conflicts (not fetch failures — do not re-attempt these URLs)

- **Select 50K eval fee: $165 vs. $111.** The live `SelectData` JS object states $165; a separate static "quick preview" widget on the same page shows $111 with no discoverable discount-code explanation. The later help-center dump does not mention the $111 widget at all. `select-daily.md`, `select-flex.md`.
- **Select 50K reset fee: $99 vs. $109.** The live `SelectData` JS object states $99 (matches this repo's engine); "Tradeify Pricing Reference" states $109. `select-daily.md`, `select-flex.md`.
- **Elite Live eligibility threshold: "5 successful payouts" vs. "3 on one account OR 10 total."** "SELECT vs Growth" states the former; the Elite Program article (this tree's primary Elite Live source) and "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies" both independently state the latter. `growth.md`, `elite-live.md` use the 3/10 figures as primary but flag the conflict, not silently discard it.
- **Lightning's own live-transition trigger.** Lightning's own payout-policy article says Tradeify "reserves the right to move traders to Live... any time after a successful payout"; separate homepage marketing copy says "Hit 3 approved payouts to qualify." `lightning.md` states both, not silently reconciled.
