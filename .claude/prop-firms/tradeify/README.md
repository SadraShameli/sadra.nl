## Overview

Tradeify documents four active evaluation/funded paths in this tree: **Growth**, **Select Daily**, **Select Flex**, and **Lightning Funded**. Growth and Select both run a traditional Evaluation → Sim Funded progression; Lightning skips evaluation entirely (instant funding). Select Daily and Select Flex are not two separate evaluations — Tradeify's own source confirms they share one evaluation product and only branch into two different funded-stage payout tracks after a trader passes and activates. All four converge on the same live-account stage, **Tradeify Elite Live**, at the firm's sole discretion.

Tradeify's own Funded Trader Agreement (§4.1) also names an **"Advanced Challenge"** account type, described elsewhere as a **legacy** product no longer actively sold ("Advanced (legacy): $125" activation fee, contrasted with "No activation fee" for the four current plans). It is not documented here — see Documentation Scope.

## Plans

- **[Growth ($50,000)](growth.md)** — fastest evaluation (pass in as little as 1 day, no eval consistency rule), EOD trailing drawdown, a $1,250 evaluation-stage Daily Loss Limit, 35% funded-stage consistency, 90/10 split.
- **[Select Daily ($50,000)](select-daily.md)** — 3-day-minimum evaluation with 40% consistency, no consistency once funded, daily payout requests up to a $1,000 cap, 90/10 split.
- **[Select Flex ($50,000)](select-flex.md)** — same evaluation as Select Daily; the funded stage pays out every 5 days instead, up to a $3,000 cap per request, 90/10 split.
- **[Lightning Funded ($50,000)](lightning.md)** — instant funding, no evaluation phase, a tiered consistency requirement across payouts (20% / 25% / 30%), 90/10 split.

## Live Accounts

**[Tradeify Elite Live](elite-live.md)** is the firm's single, shared live-account stage — Growth, Select Daily, Select Flex, and Lightning Funded all transition into it at Tradeify's sole discretion; it is not purchased directly. Tradeify's own Funded Trader Agreement (§5, "Move to Live (Tradeify Elite Live)") confirms it is issued through NinjaTrader and GFF Brokers (third-party regulated brokerages, since Tradeify itself does not hold a broker-dealer license). A trader moved to Elite Live cannot hold any other Tradeify account (Challenge, Funded, or Elite Live) while it remains active, and no other member of their household can hold a Tradeify account during that period either.

Elite Live's own operating parameters (starting balance, drawdown, DLL, contract limits, profit split, eligibility mechanics) are documented in full in `elite-live.md`, sourced from the "Tradeify Elite Program" help-center article, which was inaccessible to every fetch method tried in the earlier research pass but is now available as a user-provided paste. That file's own "Not Confirmed" section flags an unresolved eligibility-threshold conflict between two of Tradeify's own articles ("3 payouts on one account OR 10 total" vs. a separate FAQ's flat "5 successful payouts") — see `elite-live.md` rather than assuming either figure here.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules stated by their own source as applying broadly across account types — not inferred by checking each plan's file individually — and only where no documented plan's own file contradicts them. All of the below are sourced directly from the Funded Trader Agreement itself (a firm-wide legal document, not one plan's own page), per its own §4.1: "the rules published at the Help Center are incorporated into this Agreement by reference and are binding upon the Trader" — the Agreement states these rules are meant to govern every evaluation path and funded account type it names.

- **Maximum five (5) Simulated Funded Accounts per individual/household, across all funded account types combined** — per Agreement §4.2, which explicitly names Advanced Challenge, Growth Challenge, Lightning Funded, Select Flex, and Select Daily funded accounts as counting toward one shared pool of 5, not five each. Independently consistent with the "Max Active/Concurrent Accounts" figure already in each of this tree's four plan files (Growth: 5, Select Daily: 5, Select Flex: "Up to 5", Lightning: 5).
- **90% trader / 10% firm default profit split** — per Agreement §4.3: "Unless otherwise specified for a particular account type, profits withdrawn are split 90% to the Trader and 10% to the Company." Independently corroborated in each of the four plan files' own source (Growth: "Traders receive 90% of the payout amount requested"; Select: "You Keep 90% Profits"; Lightning: "Keep 90% of profit," though that particular phrase is page-wide marketing copy rather than Lightning's own structured pricing data — see lightning.md's Not Confirmed section).
- **Payout processing: 24–48 hours after approval, up to 72 hours if requested outside business hours** — per Agreement §4.4: "payment is typically issued within twenty-four to forty-eight (24-48) hours... Payout requests submitted outside of normal business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. EST/EDT...) may take up to seventy-two (72) hours to process." Growth's own payout-policy article independently states the same figures; Select's and Lightning's own sources did not separately restate this, so for those two plans this rule rests on the Agreement alone.
- **All fees are non-refundable, including after passing or failing an evaluation** — per Agreement §7.1–7.2: "All fees paid to the Company for evaluation programs, funded accounts, or any other services are strictly non-refundable," and "the Company considers the service rendered in full upon granting the Trader access... regardless of the Trader's progress or completion."
- **No recurring/subscription billing on any currently-offered account** — per Agreement §7.5: "All accounts and services currently offered by the Company are one-time purchases," explicitly distinguished from "legacy account types that were sold on a recurring basis" (i.e. the discontinued Advanced plan — see Documentation Scope).
- **No activation fees on any current account type** — per "Are There Activation Fees?", which names each plan individually: "Growth Evaluations: No activation fee. Select Evaluations: No activation fee. Lightning Funded: One-time purchase, no additional activation fee." "Tradeify Pricing Reference" independently corroborates: "There are no activation fees for any current Tradeify account type." This was previously excluded from Firm-Wide Rules because Lightning's own dedicated source didn't address it directly and only a generic article did — that generic article is now this section's own cited source, naming Lightning specifically, so the gap is resolved.
- **Microscalping restriction, funded accounts only: over 50% of trades AND over 50% of profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds** — per "Rules: News Trading" (dated August 13, 2026, live-browser paste, the most current and most specific of the three sources that mention this rule): "Keep in mind the Microscalping rule on funded accounts: over 50% of your trades must be longer than 10 seconds AND over 50% of your profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds. It does not apply during an evaluation. If you do not meet the criteria, you will not be able to request a payout." This explicitly two-part (trade-count AND profit) version, and the explicit **evaluation-stage exemption**, are both new — neither was stated this precisely by the other two sources that also mention microscalping: the Funded Trader Agreement §6.8 states only the weaker, one-part profit criterion with no stage qualifier, and a ~10-months-stale help-center article ("Essential Trading Rules Overview") independently corroborates the two-part criterion but doesn't state the evaluation exemption as explicitly. Given "Rules: News Trading" is a dedicated, current, specific article (not a general legal agreement or a stale summary), it is treated as controlling here; an earlier version of this note incorrectly treated the Agreement as controlling instead — corrected.
- **Drawdown lock: every Sim Funded account (Growth, Select Daily, Select Flex, Lightning) locks its trailing drawdown floor once EOD balance exceeds the drawdown amount by $100, or immediately on a payout request, whichever comes first — after which the floor is fixed at $100 above the account's starting balance and never moves up again** — per "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns," which gives a per-plan, per-tier lock-trigger table (e.g. at 50K, all four plans lock at an EOD balance of $52,100, floor fixes at $50,100; the 100K/150K trigger balances diverge between plans because their drawdown amounts differ at those tiers). Does not apply during Evaluation ("Drawdown does NOT lock on Evaluation accounts — only on Sim Funded"). Independently confirmed, plan-by-plan, in each of the four plan files' own Drawdown Lock table row.
- **All sales are final; no refunds on any purchase** — per "Tradeify Pricing Reference": "All sales are final. Tradeify does not offer refunds on any purchases. This includes account purchases, resets, and any other fees," with one narrow exception (a promo/discount code that failed to apply at checkout, handled as a billing correction, not a refund). Independently corroborated by the Funded Trader Agreement (§7.1–7.2, already cited above) and by "How do I Reset a Failed Evaluation?"'s own reset FAQ ("All sales are final per our Terms of Service").
- **Resets exist only for Growth and Select Evaluation accounts, never for Lightning or any Sim Funded account** — per "How do I Reset a Failed Evaluation?": "There is no reset option for Lightning Funded or Sim Funded accounts. If you fail these accounts, the failure is permanent and you will need to purchase a new account... Resets are only available for Evaluation accounts (Growth and Select Evaluations)." A Sim Funded breach on any of the four documented plans is a permanent, unrecoverable account failure — matches each plan file's own "How the Drawdown Works" section.
- **News trading is allowed with no restrictions, on every account type, trade at your own risk** — per "Rules: News Trading" (dated August 13, 2026, live-browser paste): "We do not have any rules against or guidelines around trading news events. Free reign, but beware of volatility... You trade news AT YOUR OWN RISK. Beware of slippage — Orders may execute far from expected prices. NEVER use the Daily Loss Limit as a stop loss." No account-type or stage qualifier is stated, so this is treated as firm-wide, not plan-specific. Independently consistent with Select's own eval-stage-specific confirmation ("Tier 1 News Trading allowed"), which this article's unscoped framing subsumes.
- **Hedging is prohibited in all forms, across every account type, including across a trader's own multiple accounts**, per "Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products" (dated August 19, 2026, found via the sitemap sweep, applies to "Evaluation, Sim Funded, and Elite Live"). Opposing positions (one long, one short) on the same instrument, or on two instruments from the same Product Group, are a violation, whether held in one account or spread across several. Product Groups: Equity Index (ES/MES/NQ/MNQ/YM/MYM/RTY/M2K/EMD/NKD plus the EUREX index futures), Energy (CL/QM/MCL/NG/QG), Metals (GC/QO/MGC/SI/HG/PL/PA), Currencies (6E/M6E/6B/6J/6A/M6A/6C/6S), Interest Rates (the four EUREX bond futures), Grains (ZS/ZL/ZM/ZC/ZW), Livestock (HE/LE/GF), and Volatility (FVS, treated as offsetting against any long Equity Index position since it moves inversely). Automated detection breaches an account only when three conditions are simultaneously met: opposing positions exist, the hedge duration exceeds 10 seconds, and profit from the hedge exceeds $150, a 10-second grace window for accidental opposing entries, not an allowance to hedge briefly on purpose. Cross-account and correlated-product hedging are reviewed separately by the risk team, not by the automated check. **Recent policy change, not previously documented anywhere in this tree**: minis and micros may now be held simultaneously in the same direction (e.g. 1 ES + 3 MES), reversing an older restriction; "broker-side contract fungibility" closed the gap that made the old restriction necessary. A mini and a micro in *opposing* directions on the same or a correlated product is still a hedging violation regardless of size.
- **Restricted countries**: residents of 58 listed countries are ineligible for Tradeify's services, per "Rules: Restricted Countries" (found via the sitemap sweep). Restriction is based on country of *residence*, not current physical location: a resident of an eligible country may trade while traveling in a restricted one, but a resident of a restricted country cannot trade from elsewhere. If a Live Account application is denied (including for country-eligibility reasons), the trader is not permitted to return to the Sim Funded stage. See `SOURCES.md` for the full country list rather than duplicating 58 names here.
- **Permitted trading hours: all positions must be closed by 4:45 PM ET daily (12:59 PM ET on holiday-shortened days); no overnight or swing trading, on every account type including Live**, per "Rules: Permitted Times to Trade" (found via the sitemap sweep). Market runs 6:00 PM ET (Sunday-Thursday) to 5:00 PM ET (Monday-Friday) with a 5:00-6:00 PM ET daily maintenance break; positions still open at close are auto-closed with no account failure, though slippage is possible. A single trading day runs from 6:00 PM ET to 4:45 PM ET the next calendar day, nearly 23 continuous hours, so a position opened Monday evening and closed Tuesday afternoon is one session, not an overnight hold. Tradeify's own definitional article, "What is a Trading Day?" (June 18, 2026), states the window as "6:00 P.M. EST/EDT to 5:00 P.M. EST/EDT the next day" (and suggests thinking of it as a "Market Session"); the 15-minute difference from the 4:45 PM figure is the position-close deadline versus the session boundary, and no trade can be placed in the 4:45 to 5:00 PM gap, so the two are operationally equivalent, but they are two different statements from two different articles, not one. The same article states the trading-day count is per session, not per calendar day: "if you placed a trade at 10:00 A.M EST and then another at 7:00 P.M EST on the same day... it's actually two, because the trades fall on two different market sessions." Holiday hours are announced in Discord, not the help center. **Rithmic-specific timing quirk**: on a holiday half-day, Rithmic records the shortened session's credit at the *next full* market close rather than at the half-day's own close, so a Friday holiday half-day's trading-day credit (and any payout depending on it) doesn't post until the following Monday's close.
- **Approved futures products and commissions**: Tradeify supports instruments from CME, COMEX, NYMEX, CBOT, and EUREX (EUREX requires additional exchange-data subscriptions), per "Rules: Supported Trading Products / Assets" and "Trading Commission Fees" (both found via the sitemap sweep). **EUREX and Level 2 data are Tradovate-broker-only, and the broker is locked at purchase**, per "Level 2 (L2) and EUREX Data: Availability by Broker" (August 5, 2026, located in a 2026-09-19 re-audit of every uncited sitemap article): "L2 and EUREX are available on Tradovate accounts only"; on Rithmic-broker accounts (Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, R|Trader) and WealthCharts accounts they "are not yet available and cannot be purchased... not from Tradeify, not from the broker, and not inside the platform." Per "Supported Platforms" (same date), the three brokers are priced identically at checkout, but a trader "cannot switch brokers without purchasing a new account." So a Rithmic- or WealthCharts-broker trader cannot trade the EUREX instruments listed below at all; the unqualified EUREX line in the products table is Tradovate-only in practice. No cryptocurrency derivatives of any kind are supported. The Tradovate API is available for Live funded accounts only, not Evaluation or Sim Funded. See the full instrument and commission table below; note the full-size 5,000oz Silver (SI) is supported but the CME Micro Silver (SIL) is explicitly *not*, the only supported micro metal is Micro Gold (MGC), do not assume a micro version exists for every listed product.
- **Two-step identity verification is mandatory before any funded account or payout**, per "KYC and AML Policy" (found via the sitemap sweep, "Updated this week" at fetch time, the most current article found in this pass). Step 1 (Tradeify's own KYC/AML, via Sumsub): required to activate a funded account, at purchase time for Instant Funding accounts, or after passing for evaluation accounts (2-Step, 1-Step, and a previously-undocumented "APE-X" evaluation type, see Documentation Scope); always required at purchase time for crypto purchases regardless of account type. Step 2 (payout-provider verification, via Rise): required before the first payout, separate from Step 1. AML screening checks sanctions lists, PEP databases, and adverse media, and re-runs on an ongoing basis for the funded relationship's duration, not just at signup.
- **Payouts are issued through Rise (default) or Plane (fallback only)**, per "Rise Payouts: Main Payout Method" and "Plane Payouts: An Alternative Payout Option" (both found via the sitemap sweep). Rise is used exclusively unless a trader's country isn't supported or their Rise application is denied, in which case Plane is offered instead (manual bank-to-bank transfer, up to 7 days to set up, vs. Rise's 1-3 business days for bank transfer or same-day-to-hours for crypto). Tradeify approves a payout, then funds appear in the trader's Rise wallet within 24-48 hours; the trader then withdraws from Rise separately.
- **Bots and algorithmic trading are allowed, with conditions; high-frequency trading bots are not**, per "Guidelines for Traders" (dated August 12, 2026, found by directly reading every remaining fetched article after the user pointed out this tree's earlier completeness claim for this firm undercounted unread articles by more than double). A trader must be able to prove sole ownership of the bot/strategy (no sharing with other traders, including a live video of enabling the code on the trader's own PC), may use it only within Tradeify (not across other firms), and it must not be an HFT bot; Tradeify reserves the right to request supporting documentation if risk monitoring flags an account. The same article restates a general "Good Faith Policy" (no exploiting platform errors, price-display discrepancies, or update delays) and explicitly confirms DCA/averaging-in is allowed as part of a structured strategy, though "averaging into oblivion" without one is discouraged.
- **Copy trading (mirroring trades via a platform's trade-copier feature) is allowed only between accounts a trader personally owns and manages**, per the order-entry/copy-trading sections of "Tradovate Order Entry & Copy Trading," "WealthCharts Order Entry & Trade Copier," and "Tradesea Setup Guide" (all three independently state the same rule; found by directly reading every remaining fetched article after the user's completeness pushback). Copying trades to or from an account owned by someone else is prohibited, echoing the sole-ownership condition on bots above. All copied accounts must trade in the same direction: an inverted copier, or a lead account flattened while its followers stay open, creates opposing positions across accounts, which is a hedging violation under "Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products" (see above). This specific mechanic, copy trading as a hedging-violation vector rather than just an ownership rule, had not been documented anywhere in this tree before this pass.
- **Payout-cycle boundary, post-request trading requirement, and related firm-wide rules from "Common FAQs" and "What is a Trading Day?"** (both located in a 2026-09-19 re-audit of every uncited sitemap article; "Common FAQs" showed "Updated today" relative to that capture, making it the most current article in this tree). Five points not stated anywhere else in this tree:
  1. **Payout cycles reset at the session boundary, not on approval.** "Before payout approval: All profits (before AND after your request) count toward the same payout cycle. If your payout is denied, everything stays in the current cycle. After payout approval, same session: Profits made after approval but in the same trading session count toward your next payout cycle." Tradeify's own stated best practice is to wait for a new session after approval before resuming trading. This governs every cycle-based Sim Funded payout policy in this tree.
  2. **A post-request trading requirement, stated once, in one article, and not restated anywhere else.** "What is a Trading Day?" states: "To qualify for a payout in Tradeify, users must fulfill trading activity requirements, including trading for at least one trading day after initiating a request and ensuring each trade remains open for at least 5 seconds." Read literally, the first clause gates every payout on trading at least one more session after the request is submitted; no plan file, the Funded Trader Agreement, or any plan-specific payout-policy article states this, and "Common FAQs" says only that one request may be open at a time. This tree records the sentence as quoted and does not resolve whether it is a live rule or stale wording; see Documentation Scope. The second clause (5-second minimum hold) is stated here as a payout-qualification floor, which is the opposite framing from the inactivity rule's own "a trade can be as brief as 5 seconds" reassurance; the two are consistent (5 seconds satisfies both) but are different rules.
  3. **"There is no time limit to complete a Growth or Select Evaluation."** Stated flatly in "Common FAQs." The same article separately says an account "remains active until you either pass, fail, or the account expires" and "Expired accounts cannot be reinstated," without defining what "expires" means for an evaluation that has no time limit. Not resolved by any source read; see Documentation Scope.
  4. **"Is there a minimum number of trading days required for payout? No. The previous 7-day requirement has been removed."** As a blanket statement this sits in tension with `growth.md`'s own confirmed 5+ profitable-days-per-cycle requirement (each showing more than $150 profit), sourced from Growth's own payout-policy article. The two are reconcilable if the FAQ answer refers only to a former firm-wide 7-day minimum and not to Growth's own per-plan day-count gate, but the FAQ does not say so. This tree keeps `growth.md`'s plan-specific figure and records the FAQ's blanket statement here as an unresolved tension.
  5. **VPN/VPS login is prohibited; martingale is explicitly allowed.** "You cannot log in with VPN/VPS. After logging in normally, you may use VPS for trading at your own risk." And: "Is martingale strategy allowed? Yes, as long as you're not hedging."
- **Chargebacks result in an immediate, firm-wide, permanent ban**, per "Chargeback Policy" (June 5, 2026, found via the same direct-read pass). Filing a chargeback is itself a Terms of Use violation: every account tied to the user is frozen immediately without notice, and the user is permanently banned from future purchases. Tradeify treats a chargeback on a delivered/accessed service as a fraudulent dispute and provides transaction records/platform logs to the payment processor to contest it.
- **Tradeify Crypto is a completely separate product, out of scope for this doc tree**, per "Common FAQs": different domain/platform (DXTrade, not Tradovate), its own dashboard, accounts, support team, and its own distinct rules (different drawdown percentages, profit targets, and payout policies) documented at a separate help center (`help.tradeifycrypto.co`). A Futures Prop account cannot trade crypto and vice versa. Not documented anywhere in this tree; do not assume any figure here applies to it.
- **Cryptocurrency is also a purchase method, separate from Rise/Plane's payout role**, per "How to Purchase a Tradeify Account Using Cryptocurrency (Confirmo)" (found via the same direct-read pass). Account purchases (not payouts) can be paid via Confirmo in BTC, USDT, USDC, ETH, LTC, TRX, or SOL; crypto purchases are non-refundable and non-cancellable for AML compliance, consistent with the KYC/AML policy above, and this is a distinct mechanism from Rise/Plane, which handle outgoing payouts, not incoming purchases.
- **One Tradeify account per user, personal or business**, per "Trade as a Company / LLC" (found via the sitemap sweep). A trader who wants payouts issued to a company/LLC does not need a separate account: they keep trading their existing personal account and complete a KYB (Know Your Business) verification with the payment processor once payout-eligible, which requires incorporation documents, proof-of-ownership documents, and photo ID for every individual owning 25% or more of the entity. The Tradeify account email and the payment-processor account email must match.

**Explicitly NOT included here, despite looking firm-wide at first glance:**

- **Inactivity rule (one trade per calendar week)** — Agreement §6.9 states this without an explicit account-type qualifier, under a general "Prohibited Trading Activities" heading. But the Agreement's own §11 states that "[i]n the event of any conflict or inconsistency between a provision of this Agreement and a rule published at the Help Center... the Help Center rule shall prevail... with respect to trading rules, account parameters." A separate, lower-confidence (Wayback-archived, ~10-months-stale) help-center article frames what reads like the same rule specifically under "Payout Eligibility Rules," which would narrow its scope to funded accounts requesting a payout rather than all account types including pure evaluations. Given the Agreement's own stated hierarchy and this unresolved scope question, this is not asserted as a clean firm-wide rule — see each plan's own Not Confirmed section. Note this is a **different** 30-day inactivity rule from Elite Live's own, which is confirmed and documented in `elite-live.md`. That same stale article adds an operational detail not otherwise documented anywhere in this tree: "Idle Time Warning: We'll contact you before taking action — if you anticipate an inactive period (e.g., vacations), you may notify Tradeify support in advance with specific dates [to be] temporarily placed on hold, exempting it from the weekly trading requirement," and an email warning is sent before any deactivation. Given the source's own staleness, this is noted as unconfirmed-but-plausible operational context, not a confirmed current policy.
- **5-account bundle discount (5%)** — confirmed by "Tradeify Pricing Reference," but not firm-wide: it applies only to Growth 25K, Growth 50K, Select 25K, and Select 50K Evaluations bought 5-at-once in a single checkout, explicitly excluding 100K/150K sizes and all Lightning purchases. Documented in `growth.md`; **not yet added to `select-daily.md` or `select-flex.md`** despite Select 50K being one of the four eligible sizes — a real documentation gap, not a firm-wide exclusion, flagged here so it isn't mistaken for "Growth-only" (see Documentation Scope).

Every EUREX round trip cost above is denominated in EUR, not USD; EUREX contracts trade in euros but settle in USD, so live P&L and account-balance figures can diverge by the EUR/USD conversion.

**Not supported, confirmed explicitly**: cryptocurrency derivatives of any kind (no Bitcoin futures); a micro version of Silver (the CME 1,000oz Micro Silver, SIL, is explicitly named as unsupported; only the full-size 5,000oz SI contract is offered, with no listed mini either). The Tradovate API is available for Live funded accounts only, never Evaluation or Sim Funded.

**A three-way discrepancy on E-Mini Silver (QI), not silently resolved.** "Rules: Supported Trading Products / Assets" never lists QI anywhere and explicitly frames Silver as SI-only with no mini/micro variant. But two other firm-wide sources disagree: "Trading Commission Fees" lists a priced entry ("E-Mini Silver, QI, COMEX, $5.00 round trip"), and "How to Select the Correct Trading Contract" (found by directly reading every remaining fetched article after the user pointed out this tree's earlier completeness claim for this firm had significantly undercounted unread articles) lists QI's own CME contract-calendar link directly alongside GC/SI/HG/PL/PA/QO under a header literally reading "CME Contract Calendars - Tradeify Supported Products." Two of three sources now indicate QI is genuinely supported; only the dedicated products article omits it, which may itself be an incomplete list rather than QI being unsupported. Given the split, do not treat this as resolved in either direction without a source that addresses the conflict directly.

**Micro-to-mini fee efficiency**: per "Trading Commission Fees," trading 10 micro contracts of a product costs more in round-trip fees than trading the equivalent 1 mini contract (e.g. 10 MES costs $18.20 vs. 1 ES at $5.76, a $12.44 difference); the article explicitly recommends switching to the mini once trading in 10-lot-equivalent micro sizes routinely.
