## Overview

Tradeify documents four active evaluation/funded paths in this tree: **Growth**, **Select Daily**, **Select Flex**, and **Lightning Funded**. Growth and Select both run a traditional Evaluation → Sim Funded progression; Lightning skips evaluation entirely (instant funding). Select Daily and Select Flex are not two separate evaluations: Tradeify's own source confirms they share one evaluation product and only branch into two different funded-stage payout tracks after a trader passes and activates. All four converge on the same live-account stage, **Tradeify Elite Live**, at the firm's sole discretion.

Tradeify's own Funded Trader Agreement (§4.1, §4.2) also names an **"Advanced Challenge"** account type, and its Trader Performance Statistics list "Growth, Advanced and Select evaluation products". The only Help Center statement of it read is "Advanced (legacy): $125" in the activation-fee list ("Activation fees vary by account type") of "How Do I Get Funded After Passing an Evaluation?" (June 5, 2026; live text in the user's browser dump of 2026-09-18); no source read states its rules or whether it is still sold. It is not documented here. See Documentation Scope.

## Plans

- **[Growth ($50,000)](growth.md)**: evaluation with a stated 1-day minimum, EOD trailing drawdown, a $1,250 Daily Loss Limit; the evaluation consistency rule is a live conflict between the firm's own sources (None on the live homepage and in "Growth Evaluation Accounts", "SELECT vs Growth" and "Select Evaluation Accounts", 35% in "Tradeify Pricing Reference"); the Sim Funded consistency rule is also a live conflict (35% on the live homepage and in "Growth Funded: Account Payout Policy", "Rules: Consistency Rule" and "Essential Trading Rules Overview", none per the homepage hero "No consistency once funded" and "Select Evaluation Accounts"); 90/10 split; payouts per "Growth Funded: Account Payout Policy": $53,000 balance to qualify at 50K, 5 trading days above $150, a $500 minimum and caps of $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000 by payout number (see `growth.md`); the live homepage also lists a 5 Days Payout Frequency without saying what it counts.
- **[Select Daily ($50,000)](select-daily.md)**: 3-day-minimum evaluation with 40% consistency, no consistency once funded, daily payout requests up to a $1,000 cap (accounts purchased before September 1, 2026; $1,250 after), 90/10 split.
- **[Select Flex ($50,000)](select-flex.md)**: same evaluation as Select Daily; the funded stage pays out every 5 days instead, up to a $3,000 cap per request (accounts purchased before September 1, 2026; $2,500 after), 90/10 split.
- **[Lightning Funded ($50,000)](lightning.md)**: instant funding, no evaluation phase, a tiered consistency requirement across payouts (20% / 25% / 30% for accounts purchased after September 12, 2025; "Essential Trading Rules Overview" lists only "Lightning Funded: 20% limit", recorded as a live difference in `lightning.md`), 90/10 split.

## Live Accounts

**[Tradeify Elite Live](elite-live.md)** is the firm's single, shared live-account stage: Growth, Select Daily, Select Flex, and Lightning Funded all transition into it at Tradeify's sole discretion; it is not purchased directly. Tradeify's own Funded Trader Agreement (§5, "Move to Live (Tradeify Elite Live)") confirms it is issued through NinjaTrader and GFF Brokers (third-party regulated brokerages, since Tradeify itself does not hold a broker-dealer license). Per Agreement §5.4, a trader moved to Elite Live is prohibited from opening or holding new Challenge, Straight-to-Sim, or any other Tradeify funded accounts while the Live account remains active, and no other member of their household may hold any Tradeify account during that period (`elite-live.md` documents up to five Elite Live accounts, one per transitioned funded account).

Elite Live's own operating parameters (starting balance, drawdown, DLL, contract limits, profit split, eligibility mechanics) are documented in full in `elite-live.md`, sourced from the "Tradeify Elite Program" help-center article, which was inaccessible to every fetch method tried in the earlier research pass but is now available as a user-provided paste. That file records the live-transition threshold as a live conflict between the firm's own sources and does not pick one: "3 payouts on a single account, or 10 total payouts" (the Elite Program article and the Select payout-policies article's "Path to Live"; Agreement §5.2 lists the same figures as internal guidelines, but §11 makes the Help Center rule prevail over the Agreement), against "5 successful payouts" ("SELECT vs Growth"), "5-payout qualification" ("Introducing the New Select Plan" summary line), "typically after 4-5 payouts" ("Common FAQs") and "4 payouts (20 days)*" (the comparison page). See `elite-live.md` rather than assuming any figure here.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Each rule below names its own source and is limited to rules that source states as applying broadly across account types (not inferred by checking each plan's file individually), and only where no documented plan's own file contradicts them. The Funded Trader Agreement states (§4.1) that "the rules published at the Help Center are incorporated into this Agreement by reference and are binding upon the Trader."

- **Maximum five (5) Simulated Funded Accounts per individual/household, across all funded account types combined**: per Agreement §4.2, which explicitly names Advanced Challenge, Growth Challenge, Lightning Funded, Select Flex, and Select Daily funded accounts as counting toward one shared pool of 5, not five each. Independently consistent with the "Max Active/Concurrent Accounts" figure already in each of this tree's four plan files (Growth: 5, Select Daily: 5, Select Flex: "Up to 5", Lightning: 5). "300K Select Account" states V2 300K funded accounts are "held on top of the standard 5 funded accounts" (see Documentation Scope), which differs from §4.2's flat five; the two are not reconciled by any source read.
- **90% trader / 10% firm default profit split**: per Agreement §4.3: "Unless otherwise specified for a particular account type, profits withdrawn are split 90% to the Trader and 10% to the Company." Independently corroborated by "Tradeify Pricing Reference"'s Profit Splits statement, "Sim Funded accounts (Growth, Select, Lightning): 90% to trader / 10% to Tradeify," and by the Select and Lightning plan files' own payout sources (Select: "Profit Split: 90 Trader / 10 Tradeify"; Lightning: "Traders receive 90% of the payout amount requested"). Growth's own payout-policy article states it too: "Traders receive 90% of the payout amount requested." ("Growth Funded: Account Payout Policy", lastUpdatedDate 2026-09-10T23:11:18Z, user browser dump 2026-09-18).
- **Payout processing: 24–48 hours after approval, up to 72 hours if requested outside business hours, and an approved-but-unpaid payout can still be suspended or withheld pending an investigation**, per Agreement §4.4: "payment is typically issued within twenty-four to forty-eight (24-48) hours... Payout requests submitted outside of normal business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. EST/EDT...) may take up to seventy-two (72) hours to process." Growth's own payout-policy article (lastUpdatedDate 2026-09-10T23:11:18Z, user browser dump 2026-09-18) states "Payments will be issued within 24-48 hours after the withdrawal has been processed" and that requests "outside of normal business hours (Mon-Fri, 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. EST and all Federal holidays) may take up to 72 hours to process," the same hour figures as the Agreement. Select's own payout article ("Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies") words timing differently for the two tracks: its Daily section says "Receive your payout within 24-48 hours" and its side-by-side table lists "Processing" as "Standard" for the 5-Day (Flex) policy against "24-hour guarantee" for Daily. Those wordings are not reconciled with the Agreement's 24-48 hours or with each other by any source read. Lightning's own payout-policy article states a different, tighter figure: "Payments will be issued within 24 hours after the withdrawal has been processed." That conflict with the Agreement's 24-48 hour range is not resolved by either source. The same Agreement section also states: "the Company may suspend the processing of any payout request, and may withhold payment of any payout that has been approved but not yet paid, pending completion of a review or investigation into suspected non-compliance with Section 6 (Prohibited Trading Activities) or with the rules published at the Help Center." An approved payout is therefore not guaranteed final until actually paid, and the stated time limits do not run during a suspension.
- **All fees are non-refundable, including after passing or failing an evaluation**: per Agreement §7.1–7.2: "All fees paid to the Company for evaluation programs, funded accounts, or any other services are strictly non-refundable," and "the Company considers the service rendered in full upon granting the Trader access to the evaluation or platform. Refunds will not be provided regardless of: the Trader’s progress or completion of the evaluation program."
- **No recurring/subscription billing on any currently-offered account**: per Agreement §7.5: "All accounts and services currently offered by the Company are one-time purchases," explicitly distinguished from "legacy account types that were sold on a recurring basis" (the Agreement does not name them).
- **No activation fees on any current account type**: per "Are There Activation Fees?", which names each plan individually: "Growth Evaluations: No activation fee. Select Evaluations: No activation fee. Lightning Funded: One-time purchase, no additional activation fee." "Tradeify Pricing Reference" independently corroborates: "There are no activation fees for any current Tradeify account type." This was previously excluded from Firm-Wide Rules because Lightning's own dedicated source didn't address it directly and only a generic article did. That generic article is now this section's own cited source, naming Lightning specifically, so the gap is resolved.
- **Microscalping restriction, funded accounts only: over 50% of trades AND over 50% of profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds**: stated identically by two Help Center articles, both dated August 12, 2026. "Rules: News Trading" (live-browser paste): "Keep in mind the Microscalping rule on funded accounts: over 50% of your trades must be longer than 10 seconds AND over 50% of your profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds. It does not apply during an evaluation. If you do not meet the criteria, you will not be able to request a payout." "Guidelines for Traders" (user paste): "This rule applies to funded accounts only. It does not apply during an evaluation... To request a payout on a funded account, you must meet BOTH of the below two criteria: Over 50% of your trades are longer than 10 seconds. Over 50% of your profit must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds. If you do not meet the criteria, you will not be able to request a payout. It does not fail your account." The Funded Trader Agreement §6.8 states a different, one-part version with no stage qualifier: "50% or more of the Trader's account profit must come from trades held longer than ten (10) seconds." The Agreement's §11 says that where a provision of the Agreement and a Help Center rule conflict, the Help Center rule prevails "with respect to trading rules, account parameters, product classifications and groupings, and the definition of prohibited trading conduct," so this tree states the two-part Help Center version. The §6.8 wording differs on the boundary too ("50% or more" against "over 50%"), and no source reconciles that. (A third Help Center article, "Essential Trading Rules Overview" (lastUpdatedDate 2026-08-26T17:48:43Z, user browser dump 2026-09-18), states the same two-part, funded-only rule: "Over 50% of your trades AND profits must come from trades held longer than 10 seconds", "Applies to funded accounts only, not evaluations." and "If you do not meet these two criteria you won't be able to request a payout.")
- **Drawdown lock: every Sim Funded account (Growth, Select Daily, Select Flex, Lightning) locks its trailing drawdown floor once EOD balance exceeds the drawdown amount by $100, or immediately on a payout request, whichever comes first, after which the floor is fixed at $100 above the account's starting balance and never moves up again**: per "Rules: Trailing Max Drawdowns," which gives a per-plan, per-tier lock-trigger table (e.g. at 50K, all four plans lock at an EOD balance of $52,100, floor fixes at $50,100; the 100K/150K trigger balances diverge between plans because their drawdown amounts differ at those tiers). Does not apply during Evaluation ("Drawdown only locks on Sim Funded accounts. Evaluation accounts do not have drawdown locking."). Independently confirmed, plan-by-plan, in each of the four plan files' own Drawdown Lock table row.
- **All sales are final; no refunds on any purchase**: per "Tradeify Pricing Reference": "All sales are final. Tradeify does not offer refunds on any purchases. This includes account purchases, resets, and any other fees," with one narrow exception (a promo/discount code that failed to apply at checkout, handled as a billing correction, not a refund). Independently corroborated by the Funded Trader Agreement (§7.1–7.2, already cited above) and by "How do I Reset a Failed Evaluation?"'s own reset FAQ ("All sales are final per our Terms of Service").
- **Resets exist only for Growth and Select Evaluation accounts, never for Lightning or any Sim Funded account**: per "How do I Reset a Failed Evaluation?": "There is no reset option for Lightning Funded or Sim Funded accounts. If you fail these accounts, the failure is permanent and you will need to purchase a new account... Resets are only available for Evaluation accounts (Growth and Select Evaluations)." A Sim Funded breach on any of the four documented plans is a permanent, unrecoverable account failure. This matches each plan file's own "How the Drawdown Works" section.
- **News trading is allowed with no restrictions, trade at your own risk**: per "Rules: News Trading" (dated August 12, 2026, live-browser paste): "We do not have any rules against or guidelines around trading news events. Free reign, but beware of volatility... You trade news AT YOUR OWN RISK. Beware of slippage - Orders may execute far from expected prices. NEVER use the Daily Loss Limit as a stop loss - System protection may not be effective during extreme volatility." "Guidelines for Traders" (dated August 12, 2026) states the same: "We do not have any rules against or guidelines around trading news events, Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA), flipping, or scaling." Neither article names an account type or a stage for this statement, and this bullet states no scope beyond that. Select's own sources separately state "Tier 1 News Trading allowed" for the Select evaluation stage.
- **Hedging is prohibited in all forms, across every account type, including across a trader's own multiple accounts**, per "Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products" (dated August 19, 2026, found via the sitemap sweep, applies to "Evaluation, Sim Funded, and Elite Live"). Opposing positions (one long, one short) on the same instrument, or on two instruments from the same Product Group, are a violation, whether held in one account or spread across several. Product Groups: Equity Index (ES/MES/NQ/MNQ/YM/MYM/RTY/M2K/EMD/NKD plus the EUREX index futures), Energy (CL/QM/MCL/NG/QG), Metals (GC/QO/MGC/SI/HG/PL/PA), Currencies (6E/M6E/6B/6J/6A/M6A/6C/6S), Interest Rates (the four EUREX bond futures), Grains (ZS/ZL/ZM/ZC/ZW), Livestock (HE/LE/GF), and Volatility (FVS, treated as offsetting against any long Equity Index position since it moves inversely). Automated detection breaches an account only when three conditions are simultaneously met: opposing positions exist, the hedge duration exceeds 10 seconds, and profit from the hedge exceeds $150, a 10-second grace window for accidental opposing entries, not an allowance to hedge briefly on purpose. Cross-account and correlated-product hedging are reviewed separately by the risk team, not by the automated check. **Recent policy change, not previously documented anywhere in this tree**: minis and micros may now be held simultaneously in the same direction (e.g. 1 ES + 3 MES), reversing an older restriction; "broker-side contract fungibility" closed the gap that made the old restriction necessary. A mini and a micro in _opposing_ directions on the same or a correlated product is still a hedging violation regardless of size. Its consequences are stated explicitly: "A hedging violation results in every account involved being set to violation status, forfeiture of any profits generated during the period of the violating activity, and possibly a permanent ban from" the firm. Two clarifications matter: "Your combined position must stay within your account's contract limit, counted at 10 micros = 1 mini", and a failed account is still live for this purpose, since "Violations can also occur when activity in a failed account overlaps with an active one." The category is open-ended: "This list is not exhaustive — Tradeify may treat any other position it determines to be offsetting as a hedge."
- **Restricted countries, and a separate, permanent eligibility ceiling on live transition specifically**: residents of 58 listed countries are ineligible for Tradeify's services at all, per "Rules: Restricted Countries" (found via the sitemap sweep). Restriction is based on country of _residence_, not current physical location: a resident of an eligible country may trade while traveling in a restricted one, but a resident of a restricted country cannot trade from elsewhere. If a Live Account application is denied (including for country-eligibility reasons), the trader is not permitted to return to the Sim Funded stage. See `SOURCES.md` for the full country list rather than duplicating 58 names here. Beyond the 58-country full-ineligibility list, the Funded Trader Agreement describes a second, narrower tier: "certain jurisdictions are eligible to purchase an evaluation account but are not eligible for a Tradeify Elite Live account; Traders residing in those jurisdictions will remain on a Simulated Funded Account for the duration of their contract with the Company." A trader from one of these jurisdictions can buy an evaluation and hold a Sim Funded account, but can never advance to Elite Live no matter how many payouts they earn. The specific jurisdictions in this second tier are not named in the Agreement itself and were not resolved by any Help Center source read for this tree; do not assume the same 58-country list applies to this narrower restriction. Per Agreement §5.6/§17.
- **Permitted trading hours: all positions must be closed by 4:45 PM ET daily (12:59 PM ET on holiday-shortened days); no overnight or swing trading, on every account type including Live**, per "Rules: Permitted Times to Trade" (found via the sitemap sweep). Market runs 6:00 PM ET (Sunday-Thursday) to 5:00 PM ET (Monday-Friday) with a 5:00-6:00 PM ET daily maintenance break; positions still open at close are auto-closed with no account failure, though slippage is possible. A single trading day runs from 6:00 PM ET to 4:45 PM ET the next calendar day, nearly 23 continuous hours, so a position opened Monday evening and closed Tuesday afternoon is one session, not an overnight hold. Tradeify's own definitional article, "What is a Trading Day?" (June 18, 2026), states the window as "6:00 P.M. EST/EDT to 5:00 P.M. EST/EDT the next day" (and suggests thinking of it as a "Market Session"); "Rules: Permitted Times to Trade" itself states both figures, a trading day that "spans from 6:00 PM ET to 5:00 PM ET the next calendar day" and a session that "runs from 6:00 PM ET to 4:45 PM ET the next calendar day." No source says whether trades may be placed between 4:45 PM and 5:00 PM ET, so the two figures are recorded as stated, not reconciled. The same article states the trading-day count is per session, not per calendar day: "if you placed a trade at 10:00 A.M EST and then another at 7:00 P.M EST on the same day... it's actually two, because the trades fall on two different market sessions." Holiday hours are announced in Tradeify's Discord server ("Holiday market hours are announced in our Discord server."). **Rithmic-specific timing quirk**: on a holiday half-day, Rithmic records the shortened session's credit at the _next full_ market close rather than at the half-day's own close, so a Friday holiday half-day's trading-day credit (and any payout depending on it) doesn't post until the following Monday's close.
- **Approved futures products and commissions**: Tradeify supports instruments from CME, COMEX, NYMEX, CBOT, and EUREX (EUREX requires additional exchange-data subscriptions), per "Rules: Supported Trading Products / Assets" and "Trading Commission Fees" (both found via the sitemap sweep). **EUREX and Level 2 data are Tradovate-broker-only, and the broker is locked at purchase**, per "Level 2 (L2) and EUREX Data: Availability by Broker" (August 5, 2026, located in a 2026-09-19 re-audit of every uncited sitemap article): "L2 and EUREX are available on Tradovate accounts only"; on Rithmic-broker accounts (Tradesea, Quantower, Sierra Chart, R|Trader) and WealthCharts accounts they "are not yet available and cannot be purchased... not from Tradeify, not from the broker, and not inside the platform." Per "Supported Platforms" (same date), the three brokers are priced identically at checkout, but a trader "cannot switch between brokers (e.g., from Tradovate to Rithmic) without purchasing a new account." So a Rithmic- or WealthCharts-broker trader cannot trade the EUREX instruments listed below at all; the unqualified EUREX line in the products table is Tradovate-only in practice. No cryptocurrency derivatives of any kind are supported. The Tradovate API is available for Live funded accounts only, not Evaluation or Sim Funded. See the full instrument and commission table below; note the full-size 5,000oz Silver (SI) is supported but the CME Micro Silver (SIL) is explicitly _not_, the only supported micro metal is Micro Gold (MGC), do not assume a micro version exists for every listed product.
- **Two-step identity verification is mandatory before any funded account or payout**, per "KYC and AML Policy" (found via the sitemap sweep, "Updated this week" at fetch time, the most current article found in this pass). Step 1 (Tradeify's own KYC/AML, via Sumsub): required to activate a funded account, at purchase time for Instant Funding accounts, or after passing for evaluation accounts (2-Step, 1-Step, and a previously-undocumented "APE-X" evaluation type, see Documentation Scope); always required at purchase time for crypto purchases regardless of account type. Step 2 (payout-provider verification, via Rise): required before the first payout, separate from Step 1. AML screening checks sanctions lists, PEP databases, and adverse media, and re-runs on an ongoing basis for the funded relationship's duration, not just at signup. A separate, generic "Common FAQs" answer to "When do I need to complete KYC verification?" states only "Only once you request a payout" with no mention of the earlier Step 1 requirement; the two answers are not reconciled by any source read, so this tree records the conflict and states the policy article's two-step description as that article's own statement, not as a resolution.
- **Payouts are issued through Rise (default) or Plane (fallback only)**, per "Rise Payouts: Main Payout Method" and "Plane Payouts: An Alternative Payout Option" (both found via the sitemap sweep). Rise is used exclusively unless a trader's country isn't supported or their Rise application is denied, in which case Plane is offered instead (manual bank-to-bank transfer, up to 7 days to set up, vs. Rise's 1-3 business days for bank transfer or same-day-to-hours for crypto). Tradeify approves a payout, then funds appear in the trader's Rise wallet within 24-48 hours; the trader then withdraws from Rise separately. A separate article describes Rise itself as "instant processing" once initiated; the two are reconcilable (24-48 hours may be Tradeify's own approval-to-transfer window, with Rise's own internal processing being instant once that transfer begins) but no source states this explicitly, so it is not silently resolved.
- **Bots and algorithmic trading are allowed, with conditions; high-frequency trading bots are not**, per "Guidelines for Traders" (dated August 12, 2026, found by directly reading every remaining fetched article after the user pointed out this tree's earlier completeness claim for this firm undercounted unread articles by more than double). A trader must be able to prove sole ownership of the bot/strategy (no sharing with other traders, including a live video of enabling the code on the trader's own PC), may use it only within Tradeify (not across other firms), and it must not be an HFT bot; Tradeify reserves the right to request supporting documentation if risk monitoring flags an account. The same article restates a general "Good Faith Policy" (no exploiting platform errors, price-display discrepancies, or update delays) and explicitly confirms DCA/averaging-in is allowed as part of a structured strategy, though "averaging into oblivion" without one is discouraged.
- **Copy trading (mirroring trades via a platform's trade-copier feature) is allowed only between accounts a trader personally owns and manages**, per the order-entry/copy-trading sections of "Tradovate Order Entry & Copy Trading," "WealthCharts Order Entry & Trade Copier," and "Tradesea Setup Guide" (all three independently state the same rule; found by directly reading every remaining fetched article after the user's completeness pushback). Copying trades to or from an account owned by someone else is prohibited, echoing the sole-ownership condition on bots above. All copied accounts must trade in the same direction: an inverted copier, or a lead account flattened while its followers stay open, creates opposing positions across accounts, which is a hedging violation under "Rules: Hedging & Correlated Products" (see above). This specific mechanic, copy trading as a hedging-violation vector rather than just an ownership rule, had not been documented anywhere in this tree before this pass. The consequence is stated: "Copying trades from or to accounts owned by other people is strictly prohibited and may result in account termination."
- **Payout-cycle boundary, post-request trading requirement, and related firm-wide rules from "Common FAQs" and "What is a Trading Day?"** (both located in a 2026-09-19 re-audit of every uncited sitemap article; "Common FAQs" showed "Updated today" relative to that capture, making it the most current article in this tree). Five points not stated anywhere else in this tree:
    1. **Payout cycles reset at the session boundary, not on approval.** "Before payout approval: All profits (before AND after your request) count toward the same payout cycle. If your payout is denied, everything stays in the current cycle. After payout approval, same session: Profits made after approval but in the same trading session count toward your next payout cycle." Tradeify's own stated best practice is to wait for a new session after approval before resuming trading. This governs every cycle-based Sim Funded payout policy in this tree.
    2. **A post-request trading requirement, stated once, in one article, and not restated anywhere else.** "What is a Trading Day?" states: "To qualify for a payout in Tradeify, users must fulfill trading activity requirements, including trading for at least one trading day after initiating a request and ensuring each trade remains open for at least 5 seconds." Read literally, the first clause gates every payout on trading at least one more session after the request is submitted; no plan file, the Funded Trader Agreement, or any plan-specific payout-policy article states this, and "Common FAQs" says only that one request may be open at a time. This tree records the sentence as quoted and does not resolve whether it is a live rule or stale wording; see Documentation Scope. The second clause (5-second minimum hold) is stated here as a payout-qualification floor.
    3. **"There is no time limit to complete a Growth or Select Evaluation."** Stated flatly in "Common FAQs." The same article separately says an account "remains active until you either pass, fail, or the account expires" and "Expired accounts cannot be reinstated," without defining what "expires" means for an evaluation that has no time limit. Not resolved by any source read; see Documentation Scope.
    4. **"Is there a minimum number of trading days required for payout? No. The previous 7-day requirement has been removed."** As a blanket statement this sits in tension with the Payout Frequency of "Every 5 winning days" that `select-flex.md` states for Select Flex and with the homepage's `Payout Frequency` `5 Days` field for Growth and Lightning (see `growth.md` and `lightning.md`), and "What is a Trading Day?" separately states a post-request trading-day requirement. This tree records the FAQ's blanket statement here as an unresolved tension and does not resolve it.
    5. **VPN/VPS login is prohibited; martingale is explicitly allowed.** "You cannot log in with VPN/VPS. After logging in normally, you may use VPS for trading at your own risk." And: "Is martingale strategy allowed? Yes, as long as you're not hedging."
- **Chargebacks result in an immediate, firm-wide, permanent ban**, per "Chargeback Policy" (June 5, 2026, found via the same direct-read pass). Filing a chargeback is itself a Terms of Use violation: every account tied to the user is frozen immediately without notice, and the user is permanently banned from future purchases. Tradeify treats a chargeback on a delivered/accessed service as a fraudulent dispute and provides transaction records/platform logs to the payment processor to contest it. **The trader also contractually waives the underlying right to dispute the charge, and must reimburse Tradeify's costs if one is filed anyway.** The Funded Trader Agreement states "the Trader expressly waives the right to initiate, pursue, or maintain any chargeback, payment dispute, reversal, or similar claim with any credit card issuer, bank, payment processor, or other financial institution in connection with fees paid to the Company," and additionally requires reimbursing Tradeify's "costs, fees, or losses associated with the chargeback (including reasonable attorneys' fees)." Per Agreement §7.6.
- **Tradeify Crypto is a completely separate product, out of scope for this doc tree**, per "Common FAQs": different domain/platform (DXTrade, not Tradovate), its own dashboard, accounts, support team, and its own distinct rules (different drawdown percentages, profit targets, and payout policies) documented at a separate help center (`help.tradeifycrypto.co`). A Futures Prop account cannot trade crypto and vice versa. Not documented anywhere in this tree; do not assume any figure here applies to it.
