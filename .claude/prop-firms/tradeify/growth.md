## Overview

Growth Evaluation has a stated 1-day minimum: "Growth can be passed in just 1 trading day if you hit the profit target. This is the fastest evaluation option." ("SELECT vs Growth: Choosing Your Evaluation Type"; the live homepage's Growth tile also reads "Pass in 1 day"). Whether the Growth Evaluation carries a consistency rule is a live conflict between the firm's own sources: the live homepage, "Growth Evaluation Accounts", "SELECT vs Growth", "Select Evaluation Accounts" and "Tradeify 3.0" say none, while "Tradeify Pricing Reference" lists "35% consistency rule" in its "Growth Evaluation" bullet (see Not Confirmed). Whether the Sim Funded stage carries a consistency rule is a second live conflict (see the Sim Funded table's Consistency Rule row and Not Confirmed). Where SELECT Evaluation has no Daily Loss Limit, Growth carries a Daily Loss Limit (soft breach) during the evaluation. There is no activation fee ("Are There Activation Fees?"), and that article states, without a per-plan qualifier, "Activation is not automatic — you must activate it yourself." Once activated, a failed Growth Sim Funded account cannot be reset (only Evaluation accounts can be reset). The Growth payout rules are stated by "Growth Funded: Account Payout Policy" (see Payouts): a 90% payout, a minimum balance to qualify for each request, 5 trading days that must each show a profit above a size-specific amount, a minimum payout and per-request caps by payout number.

**Inactivity rule.** "Guidelines for Traders" (August 12, 2026) states, under "Maximum Account Idle Time (Funded and Evaluation Accounts)": "To keep your funded or evaluation account active, you must place at least one trade per week (Monday through Friday). This is per account, not per user. Failure to do so may result in your account being marked as inactive." The article names no plan. This tree's simulator engine models a 7-day idle-closure rule for Growth; whether "one trade per week" is the same rule as the engine's 7-day figure is not stated by the article. "Essential Trading Rules Overview" (lastUpdatedDate 2026-08-26T17:48:43Z, user browser dump 2026-09-18) words the same rule as "Minimum Activity: At least 1 trade per week (Monday-Friday) — applies to both evaluation and funded accounts" and adds "The trade can be as brief as 5 seconds as long as it satisfies the minimum activity requirement."

## How the Drawdown Works

Growth uses an End of Day (EOD) Trailing Max Drawdown on both the Evaluation and Sim Funded stages: the floor trails the account's highest-ever EOD balance (the high-water mark), rising on a new EOD high and never moving down on a losing day. The limit only _recalculates_ at end-of-day, but it is enforced in real time against net liquidation value: if net liq touches the floor at any moment during the trading day, the account fails immediately (a "hard breach"), even if the balance recovers before the close. This is a permanent, unrecoverable failure, distinct from the Daily Loss Limit below.

The Daily Loss Limit ($1,250 at 50K, soft breach) pauses trading for the remainder of the day if breached, without failing the account; trading resumes the next session (after 6:00 PM ET). It resets every trading day. Because Max Trailing Drawdown and the Daily Loss Limit are independent mechanics, it is possible to hit the drawdown floor before the DLL triggers if the high-water mark is close enough. In that case the account fails on the drawdown breach regardless of the DLL.

On the Sim Funded stage only, the drawdown stops trailing and locks once EOD balance reaches $52,100 (profit exceeds the $2,000 drawdown amount by $100), or immediately upon a payout request, whichever happens first; the floor then fixes permanently at $50,100. This lock mechanic does not exist during the Evaluation stage. The Daily Loss Limit escalation to $2,000 (effective the next session after the balance reaches $53,000) is documented by "Rules: Daily Loss Limit" under its "Applies to: Growth, Lightning, and Select Daily Funded accounts" scope; whether it also applies during the Evaluation is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed).

### Worked Example

This example tracks the account's full nominal balance throughout (e.g., a $50,000 account showing $500 profit reads as a $50,500 balance), not a $0-based profit figure, and never switches to a $0-based convention. The $50,000 Evaluation opening balance is the size-label reading, and no source states it as an Evaluation Starting Balance (see Not Confirmed), so every Evaluation figure below that is derived from it is arithmetic on that assumption, not a quoted Tradeify figure. The Sim Funded opening balance is stated by the source (see the Sim Funded table).

**Evaluation:**

1. Trader purchases a $50K Growth Evaluation for $145.
2. The account opens at an assumed $50,000 balance (the size-label reading, Unconfirmed), with a $3,000 profit target, a $1,250 Daily Loss Limit (soft breach), and a $2,000 EOD Trailing Max Drawdown: the floor starts at an assumed $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000 (derived).
3. EOD Day 1: the balance falls to $49,500 (a $500 loss). This is not a new EOD high, so the floor does not move: a Trailing Max Drawdown floor only rises on a new peak and never trails down on a losing day. Floor stays at $48,000.
4. EOD Day 2: the balance rises to $52,000 (a new EOD high, $2,000 net profit). The floor trails up to $52,000 − $2,000 = $50,000.
5. EOD Day 3: the balance reaches $53,000 ($3,000 profit = the profit target). The profit target is reached and the 1-day minimum is satisfied. Whether an evaluation consistency rule applies is a live conflict (see Not Confirmed), so this step does not check one.

**Sim Funded:** "How Do I Get Funded After Passing an Evaluation?" states that the funded account starts at the base account size regardless of the evaluation ending balance and that evaluation profit does not carry over, so this example starts the Sim Funded account at $50,000, not at the Evaluation's $53,000 ending balance, which lets the drawdown-lock and DLL-escalation mechanics be shown from their own starting points.

6. Trader activates the Growth Sim Funded account at $50,000 (the stated base account size), with a $2,000 EOD Trailing Max Drawdown (floor again at $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000, derived) and a $1,250 Daily Loss Limit (soft breach).
7. EOD Day 1 of Sim Funded: the balance grows to $52,100 (a new EOD high, $2,100 profit, exactly $100 above the $2,000 drawdown amount). This is the exact drawdown-lock trigger: the floor stops trailing and locks permanently at $50,100.
8. EOD Day 2: the balance grows to $53,000 ($3,000 profit, 6% of the $50,000 account). This is the balance at which "Rules: Daily Loss Limit" lists the Growth 50K DLL becoming $2,000; the DLL increases from $1,250 to $2,000 effective the next trading session.
9. The example stops here: a payout request additionally needs 5 profitable trading days (each above $150 at 50K) and the Growth consistency requirement, which is a live conflict (see Not Confirmed), so no payout is walked through.

## Live Transition

Payouts are processed within 24-48 hours during business hours (Mon-Fri, 8 AM-5 PM EST), or up to 72 hours if submitted outside business hours, per the Funded Trader Agreement §4.4; "Growth Funded: Account Payout Policy" words it as "Payments will be issued within 24-48 hours after the withdrawal has been processed" and "Payout requests submitted outside of normal business hours (Mon-Fri, 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. EST and all Federal holidays) may take up to 72 hours to process." The same article states: "Tradeify reserves the right to move you to a Live Funded Account at any time after a successful payout. Tradeify will contact you via email regarding this decision." The live-transition threshold is a live conflict between the firm's own sources, not resolved here. "SELECT vs Growth" states both Select and Growth paths lead to Elite Live "after 5 successful payouts from your Sim Funded accounts," and the summary line of "Introducing the New Select Plan & Changes to the Live Program" reads "5-payout qualification for Growth/Lightning/Select" (its body says only "Meet the minimum payout thresholds"). "Tradeify Elite Program" states the threshold as "3 payouts on a single account, or 10 total payouts since the last live transition across all plan types," and the "Path to Live" subsection of "Select Flex and Select Daily Payout Policies" repeats it and says it applies across "Select, Growth, and Lightning." The Funded Trader Agreement §5.2 (fetched 2026-10-02) lists "three (3) approved payouts on a single Simulated Funded Account" and ten approved payouts since the last live account as internal guidelines that "do not create any right or obligation to be transitioned to Live," and its §11 makes the Help Center rule prevail over the Agreement on trading rules, so §5.2 does not decide between the Help Center articles. No source says which article controls, so this file states no single threshold (see `elite-live.md`). Elite Live's own operating parameters (starting balance, drawdown, DLL, contract limits, payout split, eligibility mechanics) are documented in the shared [`elite-live.md`](elite-live.md) file, not duplicated here.
