## Overview

Take Profit Trader (TPT) is structured as a single linear progression rather than sibling plans: a Test evaluation (billed as a recurring monthly subscription, not a one-time purchase) leads into a PRO account (sim-funded, day-one payouts), which can in turn be upgraded into PRO+ (TPT's live, real-capital stage). This file covers the Test and PRO stages only, at TPT's five Futures account sizes : $25,000, $50,000, $75,000, $100,000 and $150,000 : each with its own Profit Target and maximum trailing-drawdown amount; PRO+'s own rules are documented separately in `pro-plus.md` and not duplicated here.

The Test and PRO stages share the same maximum-drawdown dollar amount once a trader passes ("Maximum drawdown in PRO = maximum drawdown from your passed test"), but the drawdown **mechanic** itself changes at the Test-to-PRO transition: Test uses an End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing Drawdown, recalculated only once per day at the close; PRO uses an Intraday Trailing Drawdown, recalculated continuously off peak balance including open, unrealized profit. See "How the Drawdown Works," below, for the full mechanics of both. Firm-wide trading-conduct rules also apply to both stages : no bots/algorithmic trading, CME-compliance and independent-execution requirements, a no-counter-position rule (enforced across correlated contracts such as ES/MES, NQ/MNQ, YM/MYM), copy-trading restricted to a trader's own accounts, approved products/trading hours, commissions, and restricted countries : but are documented at the firm level in `README.md` rather than repeated in full here.

## How the Drawdown Works

Take Profit Trader uses two different trailing-drawdown mechanics depending on stage, not one mechanic that carries through unchanged:

- **Test (EOD Trailing Drawdown):** the Minimum Account Balance is recalculated once per day, at end-of-day, off the account's highest-ever EOD balance. Intraday price swings during an open trade do not move it; only a day's closing balance can set a new high (Rule 3: "the drawdown for test accounts is calculated only at the end of the trading day, not during open trades").
- **PRO (Intraday Trailing Drawdown):** the Minimum Account Balance is recalculated continuously, off the account's peak balance including both realized gains and open, unrealized gains (PRO Account Rules #5: "The Trailing Drawdown is calculated intraday using your peak balance, which includes realized gains and unrealized gains... trails intraday as your unrealized profits rise").

In both stages, the floor trails upward only (it never falls back on a losing day or a losing trade) and stops trailing once it reaches the account's own starting balance, becoming a fixed floor from then on. The Drawdown Amount is the same dollar figure at both stages for a given account size, and carries over unchanged from a passed Test into PRO ("Maximum drawdown in PRO = maximum drawdown from your passed test") : but the PRO account's own trailing calculation restarts fresh from its own starting balance rather than continuing from wherever the Test-stage floor had already locked (see the Worked Example below). If balance ever falls to the current Minimum Account Balance : through realized or unrealized losses, at either stage : the account is immediately liquidated.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the account's full, literal nominal balance in dollar terms throughout (matching the source's own convention in both worked examples quoted below, e.g. "The minimum account balance starts at 23,500 (25,000 − 1,500)."), never a $0-based profit figure. It follows a single $25,000-size account across both stages, since that is the only size TPT's own sources give a complete worked example for at each stage (Rule 3 for Test/EOD; PRO Account Rules for PRO/Intraday). At $25,000, Profit Target and Drawdown Amount happen to be the same figure, $1,500 (see Evaluation table above); this is specific to the $25,000 size : at $50,000, for example, Profit Target is $3,000 and Drawdown Amount is $2,000, two different figures.

**Test stage (EOD Trailing Drawdown):**

1. Start: balance $25,000. Drawdown = $1,500. Minimum Account Balance = $25,000 − $1,500 = $23,500 (source: "The trailing drawdown is 1,500." and "The minimum account balance starts at 23,500 (25,000 − 1,500)." (two separate bullets)).
2. Day 1: the trader earns $1,000 by end of day. EOD balance becomes $26,000, a new high. Minimum Account Balance moves up to $26,000 − $1,500 = $24,500, still $1,500 behind the highest EOD balance (source's own Day 1 figures).
3. Per the source, this trailing "continues until the minimum account balance reaches the original starting balance of 25,000" : i.e., once cumulative EOD profit reaches the $1,500 Drawdown Amount (EOD balance of $25,000 + $1,500 = $26,500), the Minimum Account Balance = $26,500 − $1,500 = $25,000, exactly matching the account's own starting balance. At that point it "stops trailing and remains fixed" at $25,000 for the rest of the Test stage, even if EOD balance keeps climbing.
4. The trader separately clears the $1,500 Profit Target, the trading-day minimum, and the consistency check, and passes the eval.

**Transition to PRO:** the $1,500 Drawdown Amount carries over unchanged ("Maximum drawdown in PRO = maximum drawdown from your passed test"), but the PRO account begins its own, fresh Intraday-drawdown calculation from its own $25,000 starting balance : the source's own PRO-stage example restarts from "For a $25,000 PRO Account: Drawdown = $1,500," not from wherever the Test-stage floor had already locked. Only the dollar _amount_ of the drawdown carries over; the already-locked floor _value_ does not.

**PRO stage (Intraday Trailing Drawdown), same $25,000 account, drawdown $1,500:**

5. Start: Minimum Account Balance = $25,000 − $1,500 = $23,500, by the same peak-minus-drawdown formula the source states generally ("calculated intraday using your peak balance, which includes realized gains and unrealized gains"); not separately spelled out as a dollar figure in the source's PRO example, which starts mid-scenario : computed here, not independently stated.
6. Intraday, unrealized profit reaches $1,000 on an open position. Because PRO trails on unrealized gains too, not just EOD closes, the Minimum Account Balance rises immediately, in real time, to $23,500 + $1,000 = $24,500 (source: "If unrealized profit reaches $1,000, your minimum account balance rises to $24,500 in real time").
7. The trader closes that trade, realizing only $500 of the $1,000 that had been unrealized. Balance becomes $25,000 + $500 = $25,500. The Minimum Account Balance does not fall back with it : it stays at $24,500, the peak it already reached intraday (source: "your minimum remains $24,500"). The gap to the drawdown is now $25,500 − $24,500 = $1,000 (source: "You are now $1,000 away from hitting the drawdown limit").
8. As in the Test stage, the floor keeps trailing intraday profits until it reaches the account's $25,000 starting balance : i.e., once peak balance (realized + unrealized) reaches $25,000 + $1,500 = $26,500 : at which point it locks permanently at $25,000 and "no longer moves," this time for the life of the PRO account.
9. If balance ever drops to the current Minimum Account Balance, through realized or unrealized losses, the PRO account is immediately liquidated (source, PRO Account Rules #5's own "Account Liquidation" clause) : this can happen either before or after the floor has locked at $25,000.

**Resetting after a loss.** A liquidated Test account can be reset immediately for a fee (see Reset Fee in the Evaluation table above); this does not change the subscription's original renewal date, and produces "a new trading test with the same parameters and rules as your original test." A disabled (failed) Test account is separately, automatically reset for free the next time its monthly subscription renews : "TakeProfitTrader resets trading accounts during monthly renewal only if the account is disabled. If your account is active, the monthly subscription renewal will not trigger a reset" ("How to Check Your Renewal Date"). A trader who fails early in the billing cycle therefore has a real choice between paying the Reset Fee for an immediate reset or waiting for the free reset at the next renewal date; no source states whether the paid and free reset paths produce identical "same parameters and rules" outcomes, so this file does not assume they do. A liquidated PRO account can separately be reset, up to three times per account, for a size-based fee: $449 ($25K) / $649 ($50K) / $799 ($75K) / $999 ($100K) / $1,499 ($150K) (source: "Resetting A PRO Account"). A PRO reset is "a fresh account, with all the same properties and rules as your original PRO account," including its day-one withdrawal ability : a same-stage recovery option, distinct from the Live Transition into PRO+, below. Per the source, "if the PRO subscription status is disabled, the PRO account is completely closed and no longer able to be reset" (quoted as stated; this file's own Test Subscriptions source separately confirms PRO accounts carry no recurring subscription fee, so "PRO subscription status" here appears to mean the account's own enabled/disabled status rather than a literal recurring subscription : not resolved further by any source read this pass).

## Live Transition

Before covering the upgrade path into PRO+, note a separate, termination-only way a PRO account's story can end. A trader may withdraw profit left sitting inside the buffer zone (see Buffer Requirement, above) that was never withdrawn under the ordinary day-one payout policy, but only once the PRO account is completely closed : and once closed this way, per the source, "the PRO account cannot be reactivated or reset." The split on that leftover buffer profit is time-based, keyed off trading days survived (not calendar days), counted to the account's last trading day: 50% if the account survived 60 or fewer trading days since opening, 80% if it survived more than 60 (source: "PRO Account Profit Split & Withdrawal Rules," "Withdrawing from the Buffer"). This mechanic is confirmed directly by this file's own cited source but is not currently modeled anywhere in this repository's engine, which has no bust/termination hook for a one-time leftover-buffer split; documented here as confirmed-but-unmodeled rather than treating the engine's silence on it as evidence it doesn't exist.

Separately, a PRO account can be upgraded into PRO+, Take Profit Trader's live, real-capital stage. This file's own cited sources describe the move only as a "potential upgrade" (Understanding the POA Form: "to ensure that any potential upgrade to a PRO+ account will proceed smoothly and efficiently"), consistent with it not being an automatic, guaranteed step for every PRO account, but do not spell out the upgrade's exact trigger, criteria, timing, or degree of discretion : see Not Confirmed. PRO+'s own full ruleset, including its own transition mechanics, is documented separately in `pro-plus.md` rather than duplicated here, per this tree's directory-layout convention for downstream-stage files. Per this documentation tree's overall scope, PRO+ is treated as Take Profit Trader's final, real-capital stage (see `pro-plus.md` for its own full ruleset, including a discretionary sub-variant documented there); no further live or broker-funded stage beyond it is documented in this tree.

## Other Confirmed Rules

**A rule break does not stop the billing.** "If you break a rule, your subscription will not cancel automatically. You must manually cancel it from your dashboard at takeprofittrader.com." A trader who breaches and walks away keeps paying.

**An overdue subscription blocks the PRO upgrade, and a final failed payment is terminal.** "Overdue subscriptions will be marked with a red card symbol in your user dashboard, with the note, “ Subscription balance is overdue. The account cannot move to PRO unless the payment issue is resolved.”" And: "if the final payment attempt fails, your account will expire. We are not able to reactivate expired accounts."

**Price limits liquidate a PRO account outright.** "If a price limit is hit, and you have not exited your position, you will lose your PRO Account."

**Withdrawals require a flat account, briefly.** "You cannot trade while the withdrawal is processing, but the wait is a matter of seconds. The only requirement is being flat with no open positions or working orders at the exact moment" of the request. Most withdrawals are automated, but "Rithmic PRO, PRO+ withdrawals, and a small number of archived Tradovate PRO accounts remain manual for now", where "Archived accounts are those that Tradovate flags after three or more days of liquidation as part of their routine system maintenance."
