## Overview

Take Profit Trader (TPT) is documented here as consecutive stages rather than sibling plans: a Test evaluation (billed as a recurring monthly subscription, not a one-time purchase) leads into a PRO account (sim-funded, day-one payouts), which TPT's upgrade article describes being upgraded into PRO+ (TPT's live, real-capital stage); the full set of routes into PRO+ is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed). This file covers the Test and PRO stages only, at TPT's five Futures account sizes: $25,000, $50,000, $75,000, $100,000 and $150,000: each with its own Profit Target and maximum trailing-drawdown amount; PRO+'s own rules are documented separately in `pro-plus-live.md` and not duplicated here.

The Test and PRO stages share the same maximum-drawdown dollar amount once a trader passes ("Maximum drawdown in PRO = maximum drawdown from your passed test"), but the drawdown **mechanic** itself changes at the Test-to-PRO transition: Test uses an End-of-Day (EOD) Trailing Drawdown, recalculated only once per day at the close; PRO uses an Intraday Trailing Drawdown, recalculated continuously off peak balance including open, unrealized profit. See "How the Drawdown Works," below, for the full mechanics of both. Firm-wide trading-conduct rules also apply to both stages: no bots/algorithmic trading, CME-compliance and independent-execution requirements, a no-counter-position rule (enforced across correlated contracts such as ES/MES, NQ/MNQ, YM/MYM), copy-trading restricted to a trader's own accounts (the Trade Copier Policy states no account-type scope of its own; the Independent Trade Execution Policy it supports reaches Test and PRO through UTP #4, whose text applies the Universal Trading Policies to "all TakeProfitTrader accounts, including Test, PRO, and PRO+"), approved products/trading hours, commissions, and restricted countries (the Restricted Countries article is worded platform-wide and names no stage, so its per-stage scope is Unconfirmed, see Not Confirmed): but are documented at the firm level in `README.md` rather than repeated in full here.

## How the Drawdown Works

Take Profit Trader uses two different trailing-drawdown mechanics depending on stage, not one mechanic that carries through unchanged:

- **Test (EOD Trailing Drawdown):** the Minimum Account Balance is recalculated once per day, at end-of-day, off the account's highest-ever EOD balance. Intraday price swings during an open trade do not move it; only a day's closing balance can set a new high (Rule 3: "the drawdown for test accounts is calculated only at the end of the trading day, not during open trades").
- **PRO (Intraday Trailing Drawdown):** the Minimum Account Balance is recalculated continuously, off the account's peak balance including both realized gains and open, unrealized gains (PRO Account Rules #5: "The Trailing Drawdown is calculated intraday using your peak balance, which includes realized gains and unrealized gains... trails intraday as your unrealized profits rise").

In both stages, the floor trails upward and stops trailing once it reaches the account's own starting balance, becoming a fixed floor from then on, in the sources' words: Test, the minimum account balance "follows your highest end-of-day balance upward" and, once it reaches the original starting balance of 25,000, "stops trailing and remains fixed"; PRO, the drawdown "continues to trail profits until it reaches your starting balance; once it reaches that level, it no longer moves". The only source statement of the floor holding while balance falls is the PRO example, where a trade closes at a $500 realized gain after a $1,000 unrealized peak and "your minimum remains $24,500"; neither article states in words what the floor does on an outright losing day (see Not Confirmed). The Drawdown Amount is the same dollar figure at both stages for a given account size, and carries over unchanged from a passed Test into PRO ("Maximum drawdown in PRO = maximum drawdown from your passed test"): but the source does not say whether the PRO floor restarts from the PRO account's own starting balance or continues from wherever the Test-stage floor had locked (the Worked Example below follows the PRO Account Rules example and flags this; see Not Confirmed). If balance ever falls to the current Minimum Account Balance: through realized or unrealized losses, at either stage: the account is immediately liquidated.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the account's full, literal nominal balance in dollar terms throughout (matching the source's own convention in both worked examples quoted below, e.g. "The minimum account balance starts at 23,500 (25,000 − 1,500)."), never a $0-based profit figure. Steps 3, 5 and 8 use figures derived from the source's wording, not stated by it, and each says so. It follows a single $25,000-size account across both stages, since that is the only size TPT's own sources give a complete worked example for at each stage (Rule 3 for Test/EOD; PRO Account Rules for PRO/Intraday). At $25,000, Profit Target and Drawdown Amount happen to be the same figure, $1,500 (see Evaluation table above); this is specific to the $25,000 size: at $50,000, for example, Profit Target is $3,000 and Drawdown Amount is $2,000, two different figures.

**Test stage (EOD Trailing Drawdown):**

1. Start: balance $25,000. Drawdown = $1,500. Minimum Account Balance = $25,000 − $1,500 = $23,500 (source: "The trailing drawdown is 1,500." and "The minimum account balance starts at 23,500 (25,000 − 1,500)." (two separate bullets)).
2. Day 1: the trader earns $1,000 by end of day. EOD balance becomes $26,000, a new high. Minimum Account Balance moves up to $26,000 − $1,500 = $24,500, still $1,500 behind the highest EOD balance (source's own Day 1 figures).
3. Per the source, this trailing "continues until the minimum account balance reaches the original starting balance of 25,000" (derived, not stated: the source gives no dollar profit level for this point; with the floor held $1,500 behind the highest EOD balance, it reaches $25,000 when the highest EOD balance is $25,000 + $1,500 = $26,500, since $26,500 − $1,500 = $25,000). At that point it "stops trailing and remains fixed" (source wording) at $25,000 for the rest of the Test stage.
4. The trader separately clears the $1,500 Profit Target, the trading-day minimum, and the consistency check, and passes the eval.

**Transition to PRO:** the $1,500 Drawdown Amount carries over unchanged ("Maximum drawdown in PRO = maximum drawdown from your passed test"), but the source does not say whether the PRO floor restarts from the PRO account's own starting balance or continues from wherever the Test-stage floor had locked (see Not Confirmed). This example follows the PRO Account Rules example, which opens "For a $25,000 PRO Account: Drawdown = $1,500" and whose figures imply a $25,000 starting point (step 5), not a floor already locked at the Test stage: a reading of that example, not a stated rule. Only the dollar _amount_ of the drawdown is stated to carry over.

**PRO stage (Intraday Trailing Drawdown), same $25,000 account, drawdown $1,500 (the $25,000 PRO starting balance is itself derived from the PRO example's figures, see Not Confirmed):**

5. Start: Minimum Account Balance = $25,000 − $1,500 = $23,500, by the same peak-minus-drawdown formula the source states generally ("calculated intraday using your peak balance, which includes realized gains and unrealized gains"); not separately spelled out as a dollar figure in the source's PRO example, which starts mid-scenario: computed here, not independently stated, and resting on the derived $25,000 start.
6. Intraday, unrealized profit reaches $1,000 on an open position. Because PRO trails on unrealized gains too, not just EOD closes, the Minimum Account Balance rises immediately, in real time, to $23,500 + $1,000 = $24,500 (source: "If unrealized profit reaches $1,000, your minimum account balance rises to $24,500 in real time").
7. The trader closes that trade, realizing only $500 of the $1,000 that had been unrealized. Balance becomes $25,000 + $500 = $25,500. The Minimum Account Balance does not fall back with it: it stays at $24,500, the peak it already reached intraday (source: "your minimum remains $24,500"). The gap to the drawdown is now $25,500 − $24,500 = $1,000 (source: "You are now $1,000 away from hitting the drawdown limit").
8. As in the Test stage, the floor keeps trailing intraday profits until it reaches the account's $25,000 starting balance (derived, not stated, from the floor being $1,500 behind peak balance as in step 5): the floor reaches $25,000 when peak balance (realized + unrealized) reaches $25,000 + $1,500 = $26,500, at which point it stays at $25,000 (derived, resting on the derived $25,000 PRO start) and "no longer moves" (source wording), for the life of the PRO account.
9. If balance ever drops to the current Minimum Account Balance, through realized or unrealized losses, the PRO account is immediately liquidated (source, PRO Account Rules #5's own "Account Liquidation" clause): this can happen either before or after the floor has locked (at $25,000 on the derived reading in step 8).

**Resetting after a loss.** A Test account can be reset "at any time for a fee" ("Test Subscriptions"; see Reset Fee in the Evaluation table above); this does not change the subscription's original renewal date, and produces "a new trading test with the same parameters and rules as your original test." A disabled Test account is separately reset automatically the next time its monthly subscription renews: "TakeProfitTrader resets trading accounts during monthly renewal only if the account is disabled. If your account is active, the monthly subscription renewal will not trigger a reset" ("How to Check Your Renewal Date", which states no reset fee for this path). TPT's homepage (`https://takeprofittrader.com/`, a marketing page, fetched 2026-10-03) embeds a dashboard string, key "cancelText", that states the cost: "Please note that if you keep your subscription active, your disabled account will be reset at no additional cost upon your next monthly subscription renewal payment, which will be on -" (the page does not say where that string is displayed; see Not Confirmed). A trader whose account is disabled therefore has two stated routes, a Reset Fee reset on demand or the automatic reset at the next renewal; no source states whether the two produce identical "same parameters and rules" outcomes, so this file does not assume that. A liquidated PRO account can separately be reset, up to three times per account, for a size-based fee: $449 ($25K) / $649 ($50K) / $799 ($75K) / $999 ($100K) / $1,499 ($150K) (source: "Resetting A PRO Account"). A PRO reset is "a fresh account, with all the same properties and rules as your original PRO account," including its day-one withdrawal ability: a same-stage recovery option, distinct from the Live Transition into PRO+, below. Per the source, "if the PRO subscription status is disabled, the PRO account is completely closed and no longer able to be reset" (quoted as stated; this file's own Test Subscriptions source separately confirms PRO accounts carry no recurring subscription fee, so "PRO subscription status" here appears to mean the account's own enabled/disabled status rather than a literal recurring subscription: not resolved further by any source read this pass).

## Live Transition

Before covering the upgrade path into PRO+, note a separate, termination-only way a PRO account's story can end. A trader may withdraw profit left sitting inside the buffer zone (see Buffer Requirement, above) that was never withdrawn under the ordinary day-one payout policy, but only once the PRO account is completely closed: and once closed this way, per the source, "the PRO account cannot be reactivated or reset." The split on that leftover buffer profit is time-based, keyed off trading days survived (not calendar days), counted to the account's last trading day: 50% if the account survived 60 or fewer trading days since opening, 80% if it survived more than 60 (source: "PRO Account Profit Split & Withdrawal Rules," "Withdrawing from the Buffer"). This mechanic is confirmed directly by this file's own cited source but is not currently modeled anywhere in this repository's engine, which has no bust/termination hook for a one-time leftover-buffer split; documented here as confirmed-but-unmodeled rather than treating the engine's silence on it as evidence it doesn't exist.

Separately, a PRO account can be upgraded into PRO+, Take Profit Trader's live, real-capital stage. "PRO+ Account Upgrade Process" states the move is not automatic: "Advancement into PRO+ is based on a comprehensive review of trading performance and behavior across multiple objective factors," with "consistency, risk management, execution patterns, and overall trading discipline over time" named, and "There isn't a single threshold that determines promotion." "Advantages of PRO+" adds that an official invitation is sent by email and that the decision is "made solely at the discretion of Take Profit Trader"; ("PRO+ Account Upgrade Process" separately states that once requirements are met, the PRO+ account setup "typically takes several days.") (Understanding the POA Form separately calls it a "potential upgrade.") The numeric criteria and invitation timing are not stated, and other routes into PRO+ that TPT's sources name (passing an evaluation, activating a passed test, a homepage "straight into PRO+" dashboard string) are not reconciled with this PRO-account upgrade: see Not Confirmed. PRO+'s own full ruleset, including its own transition mechanics, is documented separately in `pro-plus-live.md` rather than duplicated here, per this tree's directory-layout convention for downstream-stage files. Per this documentation tree's overall scope, PRO+ is treated as Take Profit Trader's final, real-capital stage (see `pro-plus-live.md` for its own full ruleset, including a discretionary sub-variant documented there); no further live or broker-funded stage beyond it is documented in this tree.

## Other Confirmed Rules

**A rule break does not stop the billing.** "If you break a rule, your subscription will not cancel automatically. You must manually cancel it from your dashboard at takeprofittrader.com." A trader who breaches and walks away keeps paying. The subscription renews at the same time of day each month, not just the same calendar date: "Your subscription renews at the same time of day each month."

**An overdue subscription blocks the PRO upgrade, and a final failed payment is terminal.** "Overdue subscriptions will be marked with a red card symbol in your user dashboard, with the note, “Subscription balance is overdue. The account cannot move to PRO unless the payment issue is resolved.”" And: "if the final payment attempt fails, your account will expire. We are not able to reactivate expired accounts."

**Price limits liquidate a PRO account outright.** "If a price limit is hit, and you have not exited your position, you will lose your PRO Account."

**A Friday pass delays the PRO Activation credit to Monday.** "If a test account is passed on a Friday trading day, the activation credit will be issued on the next business day (Monday) as part of our standard processing schedule."

**A credit-purchased Test account expires in 60 days.** "Test Accounts purchased using credits are only valid for 60 days", and promo codes cannot be applied to them.

**Withdrawals require a flat account, briefly.** "You cannot trade while the withdrawal is processing, but the wait is a matter of seconds. The only requirement is being flat with no open positions or working orders at the exact moment" of the request. A request can otherwise be denied for one of four stated reasons: "Insufficient funds in the account", "Open positions or working orders at the moment of the request", "Tradovate platform maintenance", or "Tradovate API not functioning properly." Most withdrawals are automated, but "Rithmic PRO, PRO+ withdrawals, and a small number of archived Tradovate PRO accounts remain manual for now", where "Archived accounts are those that Tradovate flags after three or more days of liquidation as part of their routine system maintenance." A cancelled or disabled PRO account is manual too, not just Rithmic or archived: "for cancelled/disabled PRO accounts and all Rithmic accounts once you click “Confirm & Withdraw” you will see a window indicating, “Your withdrawal process is in progress.”"

**Promotional benefits do not expire.** For the NOFEE40 promotion: "No, there is no time limit to pass the test or when these benefits expire. They are forever attached to your active account that's created with the NOFEE40 code."

**"Test Subscriptions" states no time limit to reach the profit target; "Cancelling My Subscription" states an end-of-billing-cycle consequence in the context of cancelling.** "Test Subscriptions": "This approach provides you with complete flexibility to trade at your own pace — there is no time limit to reach your profit target." "Cancelling My Subscription", after "Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period, after which it will automatically terminate.": "If you have not reached your profit target by the end of the billing cycle, your trading test will be considered unsuccessful." That sentence appears only in the cancellation article, and no source says whether it also applies to a subscription that is not cancelled (see Not Confirmed).
