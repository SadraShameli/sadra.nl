## Overview

FundedNext Futures currently sells five active, purchasable products, documented here as five plan files, plus two live-account stages. All five plans share the same overall progression: a Challenge (evaluation) phase, then a FundedNext Account (sim-funded) phase, then (for four of the five) a transition into one of two entirely different live-trading programs.

Two products offered by FundedNext Futures in the past, the plain **Rapid Challenge** and the **Bolt Challenge**, were both discontinued for new purchases and resets effective **10 July 2026**; existing pre-cutover accounts continue trading under their own terms, but per this repo's convention, discontinued plans are not documented with their own file (see Documentation Scope below).

## Plans

- **[Legacy](legacy.md)**: the original, most structured Challenge: $25K/$50K/$100K, 40% consistency rule during the Challenge only, no Daily Loss Limit at any stage, 80% funded reward share, payouts gated by a Benchmark Day system rather than a fixed schedule.
- **[Flex](flex.md)**: the lowest-profit-target Challenge: $50K/$100K/$150K, 40% consistency rule during the Challenge only, no Daily Loss Limit at any stage, 95% funded reward share, the highest reward share of the five plans.
- **[Rapid Pro](rapid-pro.md)**: one of two paths sold under a single "Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge" purchase: $25K/$50K/$100K, no consistency rule or Daily Loss Limit during the Challenge (an optional negative Daily Loss Limit Add-On is available), 40% consistency rule once funded, 90% reward share, payouts every 3 days.
- **[Rapid Daily](rapid-daily.md)**: the other Rapid Pro & Daily path: same sizes and profit targets as Rapid Pro, but a mandatory Daily Loss Limit at every stage, no consistency rule at any stage, a buffer-based (not consistency-based) payout eligibility rule, 90% reward share, daily payouts.
- **[FNL:003 50K Instant Account](fnl003-instant.md)**: a limited-release Labs product: a single $50K size with no Challenge phase at all (direct FundedNext Account access from purchase), a unique 20% Perpetual Consistency Rule, 90% reward share, no reset option, capped at 3 accounts per trader and not counted against the standard 5-account FundedNext Account allocation.

## Live Accounts

FundedNext Futures runs two entirely different live-trading programs, not one. Do not assume figures from one apply to the other.

- **[`live.md`](live.md)**: the newer program, shared by `flex.md`, `rapid-pro.md`, and `rapid-daily.md`. A small fixed deposit by account size ($1,500/$2,000/$3,000/$4,500 for 25K/50K/100K/150K), an EOD-trailing Max Loss Limit that starts from $0 and locks $1,000 below the starting balance, 100% trader share on the first $5,000 of cumulative lifetime withdrawals then 90/10 after, up to 5 live accounts.
- **[`legacy-live.md`](legacy-live.md)**: the older program, used by `legacy.md` only (and, per its own source, by any pre-10-July-2026 Rapid account still in flight, which is out of scope here as discontinued). Discretionary review after $100,000 in Total Active Profits, an Eligible Profit split into a Settlement Withdrawal / Live Deposit / Reserve, a percentage-of-deposit Auto Liquidation Threshold that locks permanently at the full deposit amount after the first withdrawal, 80/20 payout split.
- **`fnl003-instant.md`** has no confirmed live-transition path in either source used for this tree; see that file's own Live Transition section.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules independently confirmed, in each plan's own table, for every one of the five documented plans.

- **News trading is unrestricted**: confirmed in `legacy.md`, `flex.md`, `rapid-pro.md`, `rapid-daily.md`, and `fnl003-instant.md`. The underlying article states the policy for "the Challenge Account and the FundedNext Account" generically rather than naming every plan; `fnl003-instant.md`'s own Not Confirmed section flags that no FNL:003-specific restatement exists.
- **Inactivity: an account is breached after 30 consecutive calendar days without a trade** (calendar days, including weekends; the counter resets on any trade): confirmed in all five plan files' own tables, with the same FNL:003 category-level caveat as News Trading above.
- **The funded-stage drawdown mechanism is an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing Maximum Loss Limit** for all five plans: confirmed in each plan's own Sim Funded table. The specific lock offset is NOT uniform: Legacy locks at exactly the initial balance ($0 offset); Flex, Rapid Pro, Rapid Daily, and FNL:003 all lock at initial balance + $100. See each plan's own Drawdown Lock row and the Key Cross-Plan Differences table below.
- **Firm-wide conduct and risk rules, documented once in [`trading-rules.md`](trading-rules.md)**: the full prohibited-trading-strategies list (spoofing, layering, wash trading, latency arbitrage, reverse hedging including NQ against MNQ, correlated-instrument hedging such as ES against YM, simulated-execution exploitation, tick scalping, account rolling, and more), the 2% CME price-limit rule with its 7% equity-product figure and formula, the Micro-Scalping policy (10-second trades, 30% warning, 40% breach, with different Challenge-versus-FundedNext consequences) and the Clarity Cards that surface it, the HFT prohibition, the DCA/EA/copy-trading conditions, the no-margin statement, the 3:10 PM CT daily flatten and 5:00 PM CT reopen, the Disciplined Trader Program, the tradeable-instrument list, and the $250 withdrawal floor plus up-to-3.5% processing fee. Every one of these is stated by its own source for "both Challenge and FundedNext Accounts" or "regardless of the stage" rather than per plan, which is why they live in one shared file. These were entirely absent from this tree before a 2026-09-19 completeness re-audit found that the "Trading Rules and Guidelines FAQ" collection had never been catalogued; see `SOURCES.md`.

Rules that looked firm-wide but are NOT included above, because at least one plan's own file does not independently confirm them: the standard 5-account FundedNext Account cap (FNL:003 sits outside it, with its own separate 3-account cap); KYC-before-first-withdrawal (only directly cited in `rapid-pro.md` and `fnl003-instant.md`'s own files); "no monthly subscription or activation fee" (only directly cited in `rapid-daily.md`'s own file).

- **Challenge allocation and purchase limits**: "Challenge Accounts: Up to $750,000 total allocation", and "There is no limit on Challenges you can purchase per month." The 5-account cap applies to FundedNext (funded) accounts, not to Challenges.
- **Household definition**: "A household is defined as family members or multiple users sharing the same address or IP." Allocation limits are enforced at that level, not per login.
- **The Daily Loss Limit counts more than closed losses**: it is "the maximum you can lose in one day, including closed losses, floating losses, commissions, and fees." An open drawdown and the cost of trading both count toward it.
- **Mini/micro mixing ratio is per plan, not firm-wide**: the contract-limit policy article states a different "equivalence ratio" in each plan’s own section. Bolt Challenge and its FundedNext Account are 1:3 ("on the $50,000 Bolt Challenge or FundedNext Account, a trader could have 1 e-mini and 3 micro e-mini contracts open at the same time, which equals the allowed limit of 2 e-mini contracts"); Rapid Challenge is 1:5; Legacy Challenge is 1:10; Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge are 1:10 ("on a $25,000 account, a trader could have 1 e-mini and 10 micro e-mini contracts open at the same time, which equals the allowed limit of 2 e-mini contracts"). The article’s Flex section states no ratio at all, so Flex is Unconfirmed on this point (see `flex.md`). Per-size contract ceilings are in each plan file.
- **Accounts cannot be paused**: "You cannot pause your Challenge or FundedNext Accounts. Once a Challenge is purchased, FundedNext Futures immediately incurs ongoing platform fees to Tradovate or NinjaTrader, regardless of whether you trade."
- **Minimum age 18**: "Traders must be at least 18 years old to purchase a FundedNext Futures Challenge."
- **Refunds: a real, unresolved contradiction across three of FundedNext's own sources, not silently reconciled.** The help-center-sourced reset-fees article states flatly: "All fees paid for purchases or resets in FundedNext Futures are strictly non-refundable. Once processed, payments cannot be reversed." The main Terms of Service instead grant a narrow refund right in Section 7.7: "The User may request a refund within seven (7) days from the date of purchase, provided that no Trading Activity has occurred on the relevant Account," waived the moment any trade is opened or executed (Section 7.7.1). The separate Futures Challenge Terms document states a third position: "All transactions are final and non-refundable... under any circumstances." Section 1.6 of the main Terms of Service states that the main Terms text controls over conflicting Policy/FAQ content, which would make the 7-day, pre-first-trade window the operative right, but this is not independently confirmed against the Futures Challenge Terms document's own conflicting wording. Do not assume fees are unconditionally non-refundable, and do not assume a 7-day refund window is definitely available, on the strength of any single one of these three sources.
- **Accounts cannot be merged**: "FundedNext Futures does not allow account merging. Due to the nature of Futures trading, account merging is not supported in FundedNext Futures. Each account operates independently and must be managed separately by the trader."
- **Restricted countries, and a separate live-eligibility restriction**: "FundedNext Futures restricts access for residents of certain countries due to regulatory, compliance, and operational requirements. These restrictions apply to purchasing accounts and are subject to change." The country list itself is not reproduced here; see the source article for the current list, which the firm states is subject to change.
- **Public criticism of FundedNext is a contractual violation, enforceable by account termination and financial penalty.** The Terms of Service's "Non-Disparagement and Reputational Protection" section states: "The User undertakes not to engage, directly or indirectly, in any acts of defamation, discrediting, disparagement, reputational attack or negative propaganda against the Provider," explicitly covering social media, review sites, and support tickets, and explicitly naming unverifiable accusations like "does not pay" or "scam." A breach is a material breach entitling account suspension/termination, withholding or reversal of Performance Rewards, a contractual penalty, and injunctive relief, and the obligation survives account termination indefinitely. Per Terms of Service, Section 24.
- **A trader must keep the firm's own enforcement communications confidential, not just refrain from public criticism.** Separately from the non-disparagement clause above, the Terms of Service define "Confidential Information" broadly to include the Trading Rules, risk controls, account reviews, audit findings, and enforcement actions (warnings, breach reports, eligibility/suspension/termination decisions), and forbid a trader from sharing, screenshotting, or even paraphrasing any of it publicly, explicitly naming X, Facebook, TikTok, Instagram, Discord/Telegram, forums, and review sites: "The User shall keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not, directly or indirectly, publish, disclose, distribute, reproduce, paraphrase, summarise, screenshot, record, stream, or otherwise make available any Confidential Information to any third party or on any public channel." Breach can trigger immediate suspension/termination and Performance Reward ineligibility, and the obligation survives termination indefinitely. Per Terms of Service, Section 16.5.
- **A paid Performance Reward can be clawed back after the fact.** "If any Performance Reward has been paid and is subsequently identified as improper due to events covered by this Section 19 (including fraud, chargeback, unauthorised use or material breach), the User shall fully reimburse such amounts upon request," without prejudice to additional collection costs and legal measures. FundedNext may separately set off a Performance Reward against any amount the trader owes (fees, chargebacks, penalties, damages). Per Terms of Service, Sections 19.4 and 12.7.
- **Disputes go to mandatory DIAC arbitration in Dubai, with a class-action waiver and a strict 6-month time bar.** No file in this tree previously stated FundedNext's governing law or dispute-resolution mechanism. The Terms of Service set governing law as UAE federal law (Emirate of Ajman), require a written Dispute Notice and a 30-day negotiation window before any claim, then mandatory arbitration: "it shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration Rules of the Dubai International Arbitration Centre (DIAC) in force at the time of submission," seated in Dubai, English language, single arbitrator, with courts reserved mainly for the Provider's own urgent injunctive claims. Any claim is time-barred 6 months after the triggering event, and collective/class-action proceedings are waived entirely. Per Terms of Service, Section 27 and Sections 18.8-18.9.

- **The engine (`src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/fundednext/`) does not fully match this tree's scope.** `FundedNext.ts` models Legacy, Flex, Rapid Pro, Rapid Daily and FNL:003 (a 2026-09-19 re-audit corrected an earlier claim here that FNL:003 was not modeled; see `fnl003-instant.md`'s own engine cross-check, which found no doc/engine disagreement). `FundedNextLive.ts` models only the newer live program's 50K/$2,000-deposit tier (no 25K/100K/150K variants, and no model at all for the older Legacy live program in `legacy-live.md`).
