## Overview

FundedNext Futures currently sells four active, purchasable products (Legacy, Flex, Rapid Pro, and Rapid Daily), documented here as five plan files, plus two live-account stages. The fifth documented plan, FNL:003, is a Labs product that FundedNext no longer sells: the Labs page (`fundednext.com/labs`, re-fetched 2026-10-02) marks its card Expired (`"status":"expired"`, `"badgeLabel":"Expired"`, `"hideCta":true`, `ctaLabel` "Feedback"), and the help center now files its four articles under "FNL:003 (Not Eligible for purchase)" (the label read "FNL:003 (Eligible for purchase)" until FundedNext re-created its help articles on 2026-10-01). Only a backend price remains: the checkout API still prices `plan_id 99` at $149.99, and it still prices the retired Rapid and Bolt plans too, so that is not evidence of sale; the earlier help-center signal that conflicted with the Expired badge is gone. It remains documented and modeled here for reference, not as an active offer; see fnl003-instant.md and its own Sources for the full citation. Four of the five plans share the progression of a Challenge (evaluation) phase, then a FundedNext Account (sim-funded) phase; FNL:003 skips the Challenge phase and starts as a FundedNext Account. Two entirely different live-trading programs are documented (`live.md` and `legacy-live.md`), but no source names Rapid Pro or Rapid Daily for either, and Legacy's link to `legacy-live.md` rests on an article title alone; see Live Accounts below.

**Products sold today (2026-10-02).** The help center's own list of Challenge types, article 17225718 ("What types and sizes of Challenges are available at FundedNext Futures?", updated 2026-09-30), names three: "1. Futures Legacy Challenge", "2. Futures Flex Challenge" and "3. Futures Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge", in four sizes overall ($25K, $50K, $100K, $150K). The `/futures` page's "Choose your challenge" shows the same three cards (Flex, Legacy, Rapid, whose two journeys are Rapid Pro and Rapid Daily) plus "Explore Labs", and each card's sizes agree with this tree (Legacy $25K/$50K/$100K, Flex $50K/$100K/$150K, Rapid Pro and Rapid Daily $25K/$50K/$100K). The products on sale are therefore Legacy, Flex, Rapid Pro (with its optional Daily Loss Limit Add-On) and Rapid Daily. Not on sale: the plain Rapid Challenge and the Bolt Challenge (the previous version of that list, article 14255818, marked both "Not available for Purchase/Reset" and stated "Effective 10 July 2026, Rapid and Bolt accounts will no longer be available for new purchases or account resets."; the current list omits them, and article 17229667 still says "existing Rapid and Bolt Accounts purchased before discontinuation can continue trading but cannot be reset"), and FNL:003 (Labs, Expired). Two leftover signals remain and are not evidence of sale: `fundednext.com/futures/bolt` still loads with "Start Bolt from $99.99" and a Bolt $50K card, and the checkout API still returns prices for `plan_id` 58, 59, 60 ("Futures Rapid") and 81 ("Futures Bolt 50000 USD"). The Labs experiments FNL:001 and FNL:002 are CFD products, not futures plans, and are out of scope.

Two products offered by FundedNext Futures in the past, the plain **Rapid Challenge** and the **Bolt Challenge**, were both discontinued for new purchases and resets effective **10 July 2026**; existing pre-cutover accounts continue trading under their own terms, but per this repo's convention, discontinued plans are not documented with their own file (see Documentation Scope below).

## Plans

- **[Legacy](legacy.md)**: the original, most structured Challenge: $25K/$50K/$100K, 40% consistency rule during the Challenge only, no Daily Loss Limit at any stage, 80% funded reward share, payouts gated by a Benchmark Day system rather than a fixed schedule.
- **[Flex](flex.md)**: the Challenge the firm says "carries the lowest profit targets among all models" (article 17229829; at 100K its $5,000 target equals Rapid Pro's and Rapid Daily's): $50K/$100K/$150K, 40% consistency rule during the Challenge only, no Daily Loss Limit at any stage, 95% funded reward share per Flex's own articles (the firm-wide Reward Share article 17242096 also words it as "the option to upgrade to a 95% Reward Share"; see `flex.md` Not Confirmed), the highest stated reward share of the five plans.
- **[Rapid Pro](rapid-pro.md)**: one of two paths sold under a single "Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge" purchase: $25K/$50K/$100K, no consistency rule or Daily Loss Limit during the Challenge (an optional negative Daily Loss Limit Add-On is available), 40% consistency rule once funded, 90% reward share, payouts every 3 days.
- **[Rapid Daily](rapid-daily.md)**: the other Rapid Pro & Daily path: same sizes and profit targets as Rapid Pro, but a mandatory Daily Loss Limit at every stage, no consistency rule at any stage, a buffer-based (not consistency-based) payout eligibility rule, 90% reward share, daily payouts.
- **[FNL:003 50K Instant Account](fnl003-instant.md)**: a limited-release Labs product marked Expired on FundedNext's own Labs page and labelled "FNL:003 (Not Eligible for purchase)" in the help center (both re-checked 2026-10-02), a status this file follows (see Overview above): a single $50K size with no Challenge phase at all (direct FundedNext Account access from purchase), a unique 20% Perpetual Consistency Rule, 90% reward share, no reset option, capped at 3 accounts per trader and not counted against the standard 5-account FundedNext Account allocation.

## Live Accounts

FundedNext Futures runs two entirely different live-trading programs, not one. Do not assume figures from one apply to the other.

- **[`live.md`](live.md)**: the program described by article 16522296 (title "Road to Live Trading - Rapid Challenge and Flex Challenge"), linked from `flex.md`, `rapid-pro.md`, and `rapid-daily.md`; the article body names none of the three plans, Flex is named in its title and in Flex's own overview article, and no Rapid Pro or Rapid Daily article mentions a live stage (see `rapid-pro.md` and `rapid-daily.md` Not Confirmed); the article's Related Articles list links the Rapid Pro and Flex rules articles and no Rapid Daily or Legacy article, a link list that states no plan applicability (see `live.md` Not Confirmed). A small fixed deposit by account size ($1,500/$2,000/$3,000/$4,500 for 25K/50K/100K/150K), a trailing Max Loss Limit that starts from $0 (the articles do not say whether it is checked end-of-day or intraday) and locks $1,000 below the starting balance (the article's own Section 5 example row "MLL trails up with balance and locks" at a $3,000 balance conflicts with its $4,000 lock trigger, a Not Confirmed conflict in `live.md`), 100% trader share on the first $5,000 of cumulative withdrawals then 90/10 after, up to 5 live accounts. **Flex's own entry trigger is a confirmed, unresolved five-way conflict across FundedNext's own pages** (15 Performance Rewards journey-wide; 5 Performance Reward Cycles from a single account; five reward journeys from one account; $100,000 Total Active Profits or 5 withdrawals; and, found 2026-10-02 in the withdrawal article 17242064, 15 withdrawals from the Flex account), the first four re-verified live 2026-10-02; see `live.md`'s own callout for all five citations.
- **[`legacy-live.md`](legacy-live.md)**: the program described by article 14283903, titled "Road To Live Trading - Legacy Challenge & Rapid Challenge (Former)"; the article body never names Legacy, so linking it to `legacy.md` rests on the title (its callout says "New Rapid purchases after July 10th will follow the new live structure. Existing accounts will follow the current structure stated below.", without defining "Rapid"; its Related Articles list links three Legacy articles and 16522296, and the 16522296 list links it back, a link list that states no plan applicability, so Legacy's link stays title-only). Discretionary review after $100,000 in Total Active Profits, an Eligible Profit split into a Settlement Withdrawal / Live Deposit / Reserve, a percentage-of-deposit Auto Liquidation Threshold that locks permanently at the full deposit amount after the first withdrawal, 80/20 payout split.
- **`fnl003-instant.md`** has no confirmed live-transition path in either source used for this tree; see that file's own Live Transition section.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules stated firm-wide by a source and checked against each plan's own table. Where a plan file only inherits a rule by category (FNL:003, for News Trading and Inactivity), the bullet says so and the rule is not claimed as confirmed for that plan.

- **News trading is unrestricted**: stated in `legacy.md`, `flex.md`, `rapid-pro.md` and `rapid-daily.md` from the firm-wide statement ("FundedNext Futures does not restrict news trading.", FAQ 17225788; the older 14298245 said "during both the Challenge Account and the FundedNext Account"). That statement does not name any plan; for FNL:003 it applies only by category and no FNL:003-specific restatement exists (`fnl003-instant.md` Not Confirmed), so FNL:003 is not confirmed here.
- **Inactivity: an account is breached after 30 consecutive calendar days without a trade** (calendar days, including weekends; the counter resets on any trade): stated in the four plan files whose tables cite the firm-wide article, and for FNL:003 only by the same category-level basis as News Trading above, so FNL:003 is not confirmed here.
- **The funded-stage drawdown mechanism is an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing Maximum Loss Limit** for all five plans: stated in each plan's own Sim Funded table. The specific lock offset is NOT uniform: Legacy locks at exactly the initial balance ($0 offset); Flex, Rapid Pro, Rapid Daily, and FNL:003 all lock at initial balance + $100. See each plan's own Drawdown Lock row and the Key Cross-Plan Differences table below.
- **Firm-wide conduct and risk rules, documented once in [`trading-rules.md`](trading-rules.md)**: the full prohibited-trading-strategies list (spoofing, layering, wash trading, latency arbitrage, reverse hedging including NQ against MNQ, coordinated hedging between separate account holders (added to the source article by 2026-09-30), correlated-instrument hedging such as ES against YM, simulated-execution exploitation, tick scalping, account rolling, and more), the 2% CME price-limit rule with its 7% equity-product figure and formula, the Micro-Scalping policy (10-second trades, 30% warning, 40% breach, with different Challenge-versus-FundedNext consequences) and the Clarity Cards that surface it, the HFT prohibition, the DCA/EA/copy-trading conditions, the no-margin statement, the 3:10 PM CT daily flatten and 5:00 PM CT reopen, the Disciplined Trader Program, the tradeable-instrument list, and the $250 withdrawal floor plus up-to-3.5% processing fee. Each is stated by its own source without a plan qualifier (the prohibited-strategies article says "both Challenge and FundedNext Accounts" and "regardless of the stage"; the Micro-Scalping article says "across all account types"), which is why they live in one shared file; none names FNL:003, and `trading-rules.md` says so where it matters. The Disciplined Trader Program applies only to enrolled traders. These were entirely absent from this tree before a 2026-09-19 completeness re-audit found that the "Trading Rules and Guidelines FAQ" collection had never been catalogued; see `SOURCES.md`.

Rules that looked firm-wide but are NOT included above, because at least one plan's own file does not independently confirm them: the standard 5-account FundedNext Account cap (FNL:003 sits outside it, with its own separate 3-account cap); KYC-before-first-withdrawal (only directly cited in `rapid-pro.md` and `fnl003-instant.md`'s own files); "no monthly subscription or activation fee" (only directly cited in `rapid-daily.md`'s own file).

- **Challenge allocation and purchase limits**: "Challenge Accounts: Up to $750,000 in total allocation at any one time.", "You can purchase as many Challenges as you want in a month." and "Resets are unlimited, up to the maximum allocation." (article 17225812; the older 14261075 read "Challenge Accounts: Up to $750,000 total allocation" and "There is no limit on Challenges you can purchase per month."). The 5-account cap applies to FundedNext (funded) accounts, not to Challenges; a FundedNext Account earned at the cap "is placed in a pending state" and activates "when a new calendar month begins" or when an active account is breached (articles 17225812 and 17225772). **FNL:003 is outside the 5-account cap:** "FNL:003 50K Instant Accounts are not counted against your standard 5 FundedNext Account Futures maximum allocation limit." (article 16847874); the cap and the pending-activation rule above are stated for the standard FundedNext Account, not for FNL:003 (see `fnl003-instant.md`, which records its own 3-account cap).

(article 17225812; the older 14261075 read "...sharing the same address or IP."). Allocation limits are enforced at that level, not per login: "everyone trading from the same address or IP is counted together toward that limit".
- **The Daily Loss Limit counts more than closed losses**: it is "the maximum you can lose in a single trading day. It includes both closed and floating (unrealized) losses, plus broker commissions and fees." (article 17230292; the older 14298201 read "the maximum you can lose in one day, including closed losses, floating losses, commissions, and fees"). An open drawdown and the cost of trading both count toward it.

(article 17230194). Until the 2026-10-01 re-creation the article gave a different "equivalence ratio" in each plan's own section (Bolt Challenge 1:3, "on the $50,000 Bolt Challenge or FundedNext Account, a trader could have 1 e-mini and 3 micro e-mini contracts open at the same time, which equals the allowed limit of 2 e-mini contracts"; Rapid Challenge 1:5; Legacy Challenge 1:10; Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge 1:10) and its Flex section stated no ratio at all; the Bolt and Rapid sections are gone from the current article, and Flex now reads "Mixing ratio: 1:10." Breach can trigger immediate suspension/termination and Performance Reward ineligibility, and the obligation survives termination indefinitely. Per Terms of Service, Section 16.5.

FundedNext may separately set off a Performance Reward against any amount the trader owes (fees, chargebacks, penalties, damages). Per Terms of Service, Sections 19.4 and 12.7.
- **Disputes go to mandatory DIAC arbitration in Dubai, with a class-action waiver and a strict 6-month time bar.** No file in this tree previously stated FundedNext's governing law or dispute-resolution mechanism. The Terms of Service set governing law as UAE federal law (Emirate of Ajman), require a written Dispute Notice and a 30-day negotiation window before any claim, then mandatory arbitration: "it shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration Rules of the Dubai International Arbitration Centre (DIAC) in force at the time of submission," seated in Dubai, English language, single arbitrator, with courts reserved mainly for the Provider's own urgent injunctive claims. Any claim is time-barred 6 months after the triggering event, and collective/class-action proceedings are waived entirely. Per Terms of Service, Section 27 and Sections 18.8-18.9.
