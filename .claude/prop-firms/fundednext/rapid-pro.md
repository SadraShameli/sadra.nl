## Overview

The FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro Challenge is one of two trader-selected paths sold under a single "Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge" purchase (the other is Rapid Daily); the path is chosen at purchase and cannot be changed afterward. Rapid Pro is a 1-Step Challenge, passable in a single day or spread across multiple days, with no consistency rule and no Daily Loss Limit during the Challenge Account by default. After passing, traders move to a FundedNext Account, where a 40% Consistency Rule applies and Performance Rewards (FundedNext's term for payouts) are released every 3 days.

Rapid Pro's defining features relative to Rapid Daily are flexibility: no default Daily Loss Limit (Rapid Daily has one at every stage), a Consistency Rule that only applies once funded (Rapid Daily has none at any stage), and no buffer-based payout eligibility rule (Rapid Daily has one). An optional Daily Loss Limit Add-On is available exclusively on the Rapid Pro path: selecting it at purchase applies a daily loss limit from the Challenge Account through the FundedNext Account in exchange for a reduced Challenge price; it cannot be added or removed after purchase.

## How the Drawdown Works

Rapid Pro uses a single, continuous End-of-Day (EOD) trailing Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) that runs across both the Challenge Account and the FundedNext Account: "The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is the total amount you can lose before your account is permanently breached." (article 17229677; the older "The maximum loss limit is the total amount a trader can lose in their Futures Challenge or FundedNext Account" survives only at 14298225) The MLL is recalculated once per day, at end-of-day, based on the highest EOD balance the account has reached: it moves up as the account's EOD balance sets new highs, but never moves back down when the account loses money intraday or overnight. If equity falls to or below the current MLL level at any time, the account is hard-breached.

For Rapid Pro (as for Bolt, Flex, and Rapid Daily: but not Legacy, which locks at a $0 offset), the MLL does not trail forever: once it reaches Initial Balance + $100, it stops trailing and locks there permanently, even if the account's balance later continues to grow. This lock level is stated directly, per size, in the "MLL Locks At" column ($25,100 / $50,100 / $100,100, articles 17229677 and 17229713; the older articles worded it as the level the MLL "will stop trailing" at); see Not Confirmed for how the current articles word the trigger.

### Worked Example

Starting the Rapid Pro $50,000 FundedNext Account at the nominal starting balance of $50,000 (the account-size label used as this plan's own Starting Balance convention, per Not Confirmed above), tracking nominal balance (starting balance plus cumulative profit) throughout, never switching to a literal-$0-profit framing partway through.

1. Day 0: Balance = $50,000 (a new high). MLL = Highest EOD Balance − Drawdown Amount = $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000. Equity must stay above $48,000 or the account is hard-breached.

2. Day 1: The trader closes the day with $1,000 in profit. Balance = $51,000, a new high. MLL recalculates: $51,000 − $2,000 = $49,000.

3. Day 2: The trader closes with an additional $1,100 in profit. Balance = $52,100, a new high, equal to Initial Balance + Drawdown Amount + $100 ($50,000 + $2,000 + $100). MLL recalculates to $52,100 − $2,000 = $50,100: exactly Initial Balance + $100, the source's own stated stop-trailing level for the $50K tier. From this instant, the MLL locks permanently at $50,100.

4. Day 3 onward: even if the trader's balance keeps growing, or later gives back some profit (without falling to $50,100 or below), the MLL stays fixed at $50,100; it does not move up further and does not move down. If equity ever falls to $50,100 or below, the account is hard-breached.

Re-derivation check: starting figure $50,000, Drawdown Amount $2,000, +$100 lock offset → locked MLL $50,100, matching the source's own directly stated "MLL Locks At $50,100" figure exactly.

## Live Transition

A live stage is confirmed for the Rapid Challenge. On transition "All active Challenge Accounts are permanently closed", "FundedNext Accounts with at least 1 successful withdrawal will be transitioned to a live account", and accounts with 0 payouts are closed and refunded; transitions "only happen at the end of an active payout cycle — never mid-cycle." That article addresses the Rapid Challenge without separating Rapid Pro from Rapid Daily; see Not Confirmed. Full live-program parameters are in the shared [live.md](live.md).

Neither `bundle-rapid-pro.txt` nor `bundle-firmwide.txt` states the specific numeric criteria (profit thresholds, day counts, or approval mechanics) that gate a Rapid Pro trader's transition from the FundedNext Account into a live-funded account. The Payouts section above notes that the source states the FundedNext Account "is concluded after the 5th Performance Reward"; neither source explicitly states whether this conclusion is the live-transition trigger itself: this is not assumed here, see Not Confirmed.

Full live-account parameters, drawdown mechanics, and payout structure belong in [`live.md`](live.md). Neither bundle used for this file states which live-account program Rapid Pro transitions into; do not assume it matches this file's own numbers.

**What counts toward the Daily Loss Limit.** The firm-wide article 17230292 defines it as "the maximum you can lose in a single trading day. It includes both closed and floating (unrealized) losses, plus broker commissions and fees.": an open, unrealised drawdown counts, and so do trading costs. The floor also moves with the account: its comparison table says "Trails from highest EOD balance" (the older 14298201 wording "the maximum you can lose in one day, including closed losses, floating losses, commissions, and fees" and "The limit trails from the highest end-of-day (EOD) balance, so if your balance grows, the daily loss threshold adjusts upward accordingly" survives only at that ID).

**Contract limits mix minis and micros at 1:10.** The firm’s own text: "You can mix e-mini and micro e-mini contracts using the 1:10 ratio (1 e-mini = 10 micro e-mini)." (article 17229677) and "Mixing ratio: 1:10." under "Rapid Pro & Daily contract limits" (article 17230194); the older worked example "on a $25,000 account, a trader could have 1 e-mini and 10 micro e-mini contracts open at the same time, which equals the allowed limit of 2 e-mini contracts" survives only at 14262297.

- **Satisfying the consistency rule takes at least 3 days (calendar or trading days not stated)**: "There's no fixed number of days" and "it takes at least 3 days to satisfy the rule" (article 17229713; the older wording "There’s no fixed number of days.
