## Overview

The FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro Challenge is one of two trader-selected paths sold under a single "Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge" purchase (the other is Rapid Daily); the path is chosen at purchase and cannot be changed afterward. Rapid Pro is a 1-Step Challenge, passable in a single day or spread across multiple days, with no consistency rule and no Daily Loss Limit during the Challenge Account by default. After passing, traders move to a FundedNext Account, where a 40% Consistency Rule applies and Performance Rewards (FundedNext's term for payouts) are released every 3 days.

Rapid Pro's defining features relative to Rapid Daily are flexibility: no default Daily Loss Limit (Rapid Daily has one at every stage), a Consistency Rule that only applies once funded (Rapid Daily has none at any stage), and no buffer-based payout eligibility rule (Rapid Daily has one). An optional Daily Loss Limit Add-On is available exclusively on the Rapid Pro path: selecting it at purchase applies a daily loss limit from the Challenge Account through the FundedNext Account in exchange for a reduced Challenge price; it cannot be added or removed after purchase.

## How the Drawdown Works

Rapid Pro uses a single, continuous End-of-Day (EOD) trailing Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) that runs across both the Challenge Account and the FundedNext Account: "The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is the total amount you can lose before your account is permanently breached." (article 17229677; the older "The maximum loss limit is the total amount a trader can lose in their Futures Challenge or FundedNext Account" survives only at 14298225) The MLL is recalculated once per day, at end-of-day, based on the highest EOD balance the account has reached: it moves up as the account's EOD balance sets new highs, but never moves back down when the account loses money intraday or overnight. The firm-wide articles word the breach as equity falling below the MLL ("If equity falls below the MLL at any time, the account is breached.", article 14298225) or a floating loss reaching it ("your account is breached instantly", article 17230292); whether a balance exactly at the MLL breaches is not worded the same way in both.

For Rapid Pro (as for Flex and Rapid Daily, but not Legacy, which locks at a $0 offset), the MLL does not trail forever: once it reaches Initial Balance + $100, it stops trailing and locks there permanently, even if the account's balance later continues to grow. This lock level is stated directly, per size, in the "MLL Locks At" column ($25,100 / $50,100 / $100,100, articles 17229677 and 17229713; the older articles worded it as the level the MLL "will stop trailing" at); see Not Confirmed for how the current articles word the trigger.

### Worked Example

Starting the Rapid Pro $50,000 FundedNext Account at the nominal starting balance of $50,000 (the account-size label used as this plan's own Starting Balance convention, per Not Confirmed above), tracking nominal balance (starting balance plus cumulative profit) throughout, never switching to a literal-$0-profit framing partway through.

1. Day 0: Balance = $50,000 (a new high). MLL = Highest EOD Balance − Drawdown Amount = $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000. Equity must stay above $48,000 or the account is hard-breached.

2. Day 1: The trader closes the day with $1,000 in profit. Balance = $51,000, a new high. MLL recalculates: $51,000 − $2,000 = $49,000.

3. Day 2: The trader closes with an additional $1,100 in profit. Balance = $52,100, a new high, equal to Initial Balance + Drawdown Amount + $100 ($50,000 + $2,000 + $100); this $52,100 trigger is this file's own derivation and Unconfirmed in dollars (see Not Confirmed), not a source-stated figure. MLL recalculates to $52,100 − $2,000 = $50,100: exactly Initial Balance + $100, the source's own stated lock level for the $50K tier. From this instant, the MLL locks permanently at $50,100.

4. Day 3 onward: even if the trader's balance keeps growing, or later gives back some profit (without falling below $50,100), the MLL stays fixed at $50,100; it does not move up further and does not move down. If equity falls below $50,100, or a floating loss reaches it during a live trade, the account is hard-breached.

Re-derivation check: starting figure $50,000, Drawdown Amount $2,000, +$100 lock offset → locked MLL $50,100, matching the source's own directly stated "MLL Locks At $50,100" figure exactly; the $52,100 trigger in step 3 is derived, not stated.
