## Overview

The FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro Challenge is one of two trader-selected paths sold under a single "Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge" purchase (the other is Rapid Daily); the path is chosen at purchase and cannot be changed afterward. Rapid Pro is a 1-Step Challenge, passable in a single day or spread across multiple days, with no consistency rule and no Daily Loss Limit during the Challenge Account by default. After passing, traders move to a FundedNext Account, where a 40% Consistency Rule applies and Performance Rewards (FundedNext's term for payouts) are released every 3 days.

Rapid Pro's defining features relative to Rapid Daily are flexibility: no default Daily Loss Limit (Rapid Daily has one at every stage), a Consistency Rule that only applies once funded (Rapid Daily has none at any stage), and no buffer-based payout eligibility rule (Rapid Daily has one). An optional Daily Loss Limit Add-On is available exclusively on the Rapid Pro path: selecting it at purchase applies a daily loss limit from the Challenge Account through the FundedNext Account in exchange for a reduced Challenge price; it cannot be added or removed after purchase.

## How the Drawdown Works

Rapid Pro uses a single, continuous End-of-Day (EOD) trailing Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) that runs across both the Challenge Account and the FundedNext Account: "The maximum loss limit is the total amount a trader can lose in their Futures Challenge or FundedNext Account." The MLL is recalculated once per day, at end-of-day, based on the highest EOD balance the account has reached: it moves up as the account's EOD balance sets new highs, but never moves back down when the account loses money intraday or overnight. If equity falls to or below the current MLL level at any time, the account is hard-breached.

For Rapid Pro (as for Bolt, Flex, and Rapid Daily: but not Legacy, which locks at a $0 offset), the MLL does not trail forever: once it reaches Initial Balance + $100, it stops trailing and locks there permanently, even if the account's balance later continues to grow. This lock level is stated directly, per size, as the level the MLL "will stop trailing" at.

### Worked Example

Starting the Rapid Pro $50,000 FundedNext Account at the nominal starting balance of $50,000 (the account-size label used as this plan's own Starting Balance convention, per Not Confirmed above), tracking nominal balance (starting balance plus cumulative profit) throughout, never switching to a literal-$0-profit framing partway through.

1. Day 0: Balance = $50,000 (a new high). MLL = Highest EOD Balance − Drawdown Amount = $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000. Equity must stay above $48,000 or the account is hard-breached.

2. Day 1: The trader closes the day with $1,000 in profit. Balance = $51,000, a new high. MLL recalculates: $51,000 − $2,000 = $49,000.

3. Day 2: The trader closes with an additional $1,100 in profit. Balance = $52,100, a new high, equal to Initial Balance + Drawdown Amount + $100 ($50,000 + $2,000 + $100). MLL recalculates to $52,100 − $2,000 = $50,100: exactly Initial Balance + $100, the source's own stated stop-trailing level for the $50K tier. From this instant, the MLL locks permanently at $50,100.

4. Day 3 onward: even if the trader's balance keeps growing, or later gives back some profit (without falling to $50,100 or below), the MLL stays fixed at $50,100; it does not move up further and does not move down. If equity ever falls to $50,100 or below, the account is hard-breached.

Re-derivation check: starting figure $50,000, Drawdown Amount $2,000, +$100 lock offset → locked MLL $50,100, matching the source's own directly stated "$50,100 ($50K)" figure exactly.

## Live Transition

A live stage is confirmed for the Rapid Challenge. "Road To Live Trading - Rapid Challenge" (article 15900277, updated August 15, 2026), in the same "FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge FAQ" collection this file already cites, states the eligibility mechanic: "Earn 15 Performance Rewards during your FundedNext journey to enter the live review pool", with returning traders re-entering "after every 5 Performance Rewards", and warns "Meeting the threshold does not guarantee a transition." On transition "All active Challenge Accounts are permanently closed", "FundedNext Accounts with at least 1 successful withdrawal will be transitioned to a live account", and accounts with 0 payouts are closed and refunded; transitions "only happen at the end of an active payout cycle — never mid-cycle." That article addresses the Rapid Challenge without separating Rapid Pro from Rapid Daily; see Not Confirmed. Full live-program parameters are in the shared [live.md](live.md).

Neither `bundle-rapid-pro.txt` nor `bundle-firmwide.txt` states the specific numeric criteria (profit thresholds, day counts, or approval mechanics) that gate a Rapid Pro trader's transition from the FundedNext Account into a live-funded account. The Payouts section above notes that the source states the FundedNext Account "is concluded after the 5th Performance Reward"; neither source explicitly states whether this conclusion is the live-transition trigger itself: this is not assumed here, see Not Confirmed.

Full live-account parameters, drawdown mechanics, and payout structure belong in [`live.md`](live.md). Neither bundle used for this file states which live-account program Rapid Pro transitions into; do not assume it matches this file's own numbers.

**What counts toward the Daily Loss Limit.** The firm-wide DLL article defines it as "the maximum you can lose in one day, including closed losses, floating losses, commissions, and fees": an open, unrealised drawdown counts, and so do trading costs. The floor also moves with the account: "The limit trails from the highest end-of-day (EOD) balance, so if your balance grows, the daily loss threshold adjusts upward accordingly."

**Contract limits mix minis and micros at 1:10.** The firm’s own worked example, from the "What are the contract limits of Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge?" section of the contract-limit policy article: "Traders may also mix e-mini and micro e-mini contracts, maintaining the 1:10 equivalence ratio. For example, on a $25,000 account, a trader could have 1 e-mini and 10 micro e-mini contracts open at the same time, which equals the allowed limit of 2 e-mini contracts."

- **Satisfying the consistency rule takes a minimum of 3 trading days**: "There’s no fixed number of days. It depends on your performance. However, it takes at least 3 days to meet this rule."
