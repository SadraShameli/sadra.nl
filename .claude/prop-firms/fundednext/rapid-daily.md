# Rapid Daily ($25,000 / $50,000 / $100,000)

## Overview

Rapid Daily is one of the two trader-selected paths sold under FundedNext Futures' single "Rapid Pro & Daily Challenge" purchase (the other is Rapid Pro, documented separately in `rapid-pro.md`); the path is chosen at purchase and cannot be changed afterward. Rapid Daily is a single 1-Day-Pass, 1-Step Challenge available at three account sizes, built around speed and simplicity: a mandatory daily loss limit applies from the Challenge Account through the FundedNext (funded) Account, no consistency rule applies at any stage, and the profit target can be reached in a single day or spread across multiple days. There is no monthly subscription or activation fee, traders pay one One-Time Eval Fee at purchase, and moving from the Challenge to a FundedNext Account is free.

Both the Challenge and the FundedNext Account use an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) that locks once it reaches a fixed floor above the initial balance. In the FundedNext Account, Rapid Daily replaces the consistency rule used by Rapid Pro with a buffer rule: once the account's EOD balance reaches a stated buffer level, profit above that buffer becomes withdrawable as a daily Performance Reward, up to a maximum of five lifetime Performance Rewards, after which the account is concluded.

## How the Drawdown Works

Rapid Daily uses two separate risk mechanisms, distinct and not to be conflated. The Daily Loss Limit is a per-day, soft-breach guardrail: hitting it pauses trading for the rest of that trading day and resets automatically the next trading day, and it applies identically through the Challenge and the FundedNext Account. The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is the account-ending, hard-breach mechanism described below.

The MLL follows an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing system: it is recalculated once per day, based on the highest EOD balance reached, as (highest EOD balance) − (Maximum Loss Limit amount). It moves up as the account's EOD balance grows, but it never moves down when losses occur, losses reduce the account's balance toward the floor, not the floor itself. If equity ever falls to or below the current MLL floor, the account is permanently hard breached; a hard breach requires a paid account reset to resume trading (Challenge stage) or ends the account (FundedNext Account, no reset available).

For Rapid Daily (and Rapid Pro, and Flex), the trailing MLL does not keep rising indefinitely: once the floor itself reaches "Initial balance + $100," it stops trailing and locks there permanently, remaining fixed at that level regardless of further account growth. This is a different, higher lock point than Legacy's own zero-offset lock at the plain initial balance.

### Worked Example

This worked example uses the full nominal account balance throughout (matching the source's own Maximum Loss Limit worked example, which tracks a literal dollar balance such as "$100,000 balance... $101,000," not a $0-based profit figure), for the $50,000 Rapid Daily Challenge (Maximum Loss Limit $2,000, lock point "Initial balance + $100" = $50,100).

1. Day 0 (Challenge start): Balance = $50,000. The MLL trails at $2,000 below the highest EOD balance reached so far: $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000. If equity ever falls to $48,000 or below, the account is hard breached.

2. Day 1: The trader closes the day with $1,600 in profit, bringing EOD balance to $51,600. The MLL recalculates: $51,600 − $2,000 = $49,600. This is still below the stated lock point of $50,100, so the MLL keeps trailing.

3. Day 2: A further $500 in closed profit brings EOD balance to $52,100. The MLL recalculates: $52,100 − $2,000 = $50,100. This equals "Initial balance + $100" ($50,000 + $100 = $50,100), the plan's stated stop point, so per the source ("the maximum loss limit will stop trailing once it reaches ... $50,100 ($50K)") the MLL locks here and stops trailing for the remainder of the account's life.

4. Day 3: The trader gives back $1,000 in closed losses; EOD balance falls to $51,100. The MLL stays locked at $50,100, it does not decrease on a loss. Equity ($51,100) is still above the $50,100 floor, so no breach occurs.

5. From this point forward, the account's floor is permanently fixed at $50,100. Further profit no longer raises it; if EOD (or, per the general MLL mechanism, intraday floating-loss) equity ever falls to $50,100 or below, the account is permanently hard breached.

Re-deriving the final number from the first: Day 0's floor of $48,000 is $50,000 − $2,000. The lock point is reached the moment EOD balance hits $50,100 + $2,000 = $52,100, which is exactly the balance reached by Day 2 ($50,000 + $1,600 + $500 = $52,100), consistent throughout, and this $52,100 figure independently matches the Rapid Daily $50K Buffer Level stated in the Payouts section below, confirming the same EOD balance level governs both the MLL lock and payout-buffer eligibility.

## Live Transition

A live stage is confirmed for the Rapid Challenge. "Road To Live Trading - Rapid Challenge" (article 15900277, updated August 15, 2026), which sits in the same "FundedNext Futures Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge FAQ" collection this file already cites, states the eligibility mechanic: "Earn 15 Performance Rewards during your FundedNext journey to enter the live review pool", with returning traders re-entering "after every 5 Performance Rewards", and warns that "Meeting the threshold does not guarantee a transition." On transition, "All active Challenge Accounts are permanently closed", "FundedNext Accounts with at least 1 successful withdrawal will be transitioned to a live account", and accounts with 0 payouts are closed and refunded. Transitions "only happen at the end of an active payout cycle — never mid-cycle." That article addresses the Rapid Challenge without separating Rapid Pro from Rapid Daily; see Not Confirmed. Full live-program parameters and worked mechanics belong in the shared [live.md](live.md).

**What counts toward the Daily Loss Limit.** The firm-wide DLL article defines it as "the maximum you can lose in one day, including closed losses, floating losses, commissions, and fees": an open, unrealised drawdown counts, and so do trading costs. The floor also moves with the account: "The limit trails from the highest end-of-day (EOD) balance, so if your balance grows, the daily loss threshold adjusts upward accordingly."

**Contract limits mix minis and micros at 1:10.** The firm’s own worked example, from the "What are the contract limits of Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge?" section of the contract-limit policy article: "Traders may also mix e-mini and micro e-mini contracts, maintaining the 1:10 equivalence ratio. For example, on a $25,000 account, a trader could have 1 e-mini and 10 micro e-mini contracts open at the same time, which equals the allowed limit of 2 e-mini contracts."

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Minimum Balance at Start (Evaluation)** — not stated in either cited bundle. Do not assume it is $0 or otherwise different from the account's starting balance.

- **Minimum Trading Days (Evaluation)** — no article in either bundle states a numeric minimum-trading-days figure for the Rapid Daily Challenge phase itself. The plan's own "1-Day Pass" / "pass in a single day" framing (the Rapid Pro & Daily overview article) strongly implies 0, and this repository's `FundedNext.ts` currently models `minTradingDays: 0` for Rapid Daily. The code's own inline notes describe the reverse check: Legacy's `minTradingDays` was corrected to 0 by live-verification, with the note observing that this correction matches Rapid Pro/Daily's own already-coded 0, not that Rapid Daily's 0 was itself independently confirmed against a primary source. Do not assume 0 is confirmed by this file's own two sources, and do not conflate this row with the separately-stated "Minimum Trading Days: Not required" line under Rapid Daily's FundedNext Account withdrawal-eligibility criteria, that line is about payout timing after funding, not about passing the Challenge.

- **Max Contracts (Sim Funded / FundedNext Account)** — the firm-wide contract-limit-policy article gives dedicated Challenge-phase and separate FundedNext-Account tables for Bolt, Rapid (discontinued), and Legacy, but its "Rapid Pro and Daily Challenge" section has no corresponding FundedNext Account table at all. `FundedNext.ts`'s own notes independently reach the same conclusion and leave this field null in the engine. Do not assume the funded-phase limit equals the Challenge-phase figure (2/20, 4/40, 6/60) or scales up the way Legacy's and (discontinued) Rapid's funded-phase limits do over their own Challenge-phase figures.

- **Starting Balance (both tables)** — no article states "Starting Balance: $X" in those literal words for either stage. The figure is backed by arithmetic from the buffer-level formula rather than a direct label (see the Starting Balance note under the Evaluation table). Do not assume the account's real starting balance differs from its account-size label at any point.

- **Minimum Balance (ongoing) once the MLL locks** — the $25,100/$50,100/$100,100 figures are the source's own stated lock points for the trailing MLL floor, but "the account must maintain at least this much equity or be breached" is derived from the general Maximum Loss Limit mechanism article's separate statement that equity falling below the MLL causes a hard breach, not from a sentence specific to Rapid Daily using the words "minimum balance." Do not assume a different, undisclosed ongoing-minimum figure exists.

- **Live Transition, whether Rapid Daily is covered identically to Rapid Pro** — "Road To Live Trading - Rapid Challenge" states the 15-Performance-Reward eligibility mechanic for the Rapid Challenge as a whole and never distinguishes Rapid Daily from Rapid Pro anywhere in its text. Whether the two plans really share identical live-transition terms, or the article simply does not spell out a difference, is not stated. Do not assume any Rapid Pro-specific live figure applies unchanged to Rapid Daily.
