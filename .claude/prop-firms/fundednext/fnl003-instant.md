## Overview

The FNL:003 50K Instant Account is a limited-release FundedNext Futures Labs product that skips the Challenge Phase entirely: a trader purchases direct access to a FundedNext Account for a flat one-time fee and begins trading immediately under FundedNext Account rules, so the EOD Trailing Drawdown, the 20% Perpetual Consistency Rule, and the Performance Reward conditions all apply from the first trading day rather than after passing an evaluation. **As of 2026-10-02, FundedNext's own pages agree that this product is not for sale; only a backend price still exists.** The Labs page (`fundednext.com/labs`) marks its card `"status":"expired"`, `"badgeLabel":"Expired"`, `"hideCta":true`, hiding the buy button, and the card's `ctaLabel` is "Feedback," not a purchase label. The help center, which until the 2026-10-01 re-creation filed this product's articles under a collection labelled "FNL:003 (Eligible for purchase)," now files all four under "FNL:003 (Not Eligible for purchase)" (collection 19742648, read 2026-10-02), so the earlier help-center signal that conflicted with the Expired badge is gone. What remains: the checkout API still prices `plan_id 99` at $149.99 (it also still prices the retired Rapid and Bolt plans, so a priced `plan_id` is not evidence of sale), and the Labs card's `ctaHref` still holds a purchase URL that the hidden, "Feedback"-labelled button no longer exposes. This file therefore follows the Expired status and the "Not Eligible for purchase" label, and documents and models the plan for reference, not as an active offer. FundedNext has not announced FNL:003 as formally discontinued the way it did Bolt and the plain Rapid Challenge (see README.md's Documentation Scope). It is available in a single account size, has no reset option at any stage, and each trader may purchase up to three FNL:003 accounts, which do not count against the firm's standard 5-account FundedNext Account allocation limit.

FNL:003 is modeled in this repository's prop-calculator engine as `FundedNextVariant.Fnl003`, built by `buildFnl003Plan` in `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/fundednext/FundedNext.ts`, alongside Flex, Legacy, Rapid Pro, and Rapid Daily.

## How the Drawdown Works

FNL:003's EOD Trailing Drawdown recalculates once per trading day, at the close, off the highest end-of-day balance the account has reached so far. The floor sits $2,000 (the Drawdown Amount) below that highest EOD balance, and it moves up as the balance grows but never moves back down on a losing day. This continues "only until it locks at Starting Balance + $100" which, for this $50,000 account, the same sentence resolves: "for the FNL:003 50K Instant Account, this means the threshold locks at $50,100."; once the floor reaches that level it stops trailing entirely, even if the balance keeps rising afterward. The floor's trailing formula (highest EOD balance minus the $2,000 Drawdown Amount) and the breach condition are not stated in any of the four FNL:003-specific articles; both come from the firm-wide MLL article 14298225, whose tables include an FNL:003 column and which says "If equity falls below the MLL at any time, the account is breached." (see Not Confirmed).

### Worked Example

This example uses the account's real, literal nominal balance throughout, starting at the source's own stated $50,000 starting balance: FNL:003 has no separate $0-based Challenge stage to distinguish from, since it grants direct access to the FundedNext Account. Every balance figure below is that same literal account balance; the convention never switches.

1. The trader purchases the FNL:003 50K Instant Account. Balance: $50,000. The EOD trailing floor is Balance − $2,000 (Drawdown Amount) = $48,000 (a derived figure: the trailing formula comes from the firm-wide MLL article, see Not Confirmed).
2. Over subsequent trading days, EOD balance rises with profit. At each EOD close the floor recalculates as (highest EOD balance so far − $2,000), moving up but never down.
3. EOD balance reaches $52,100 (cumulative profit of $2,100; this $2,100 is this file's own derivation, $2,000 Drawdown Amount + $100, not a source-stated trigger, see Not Confirmed). The floor is then $52,100 − $2,000 = $50,100, which equals Starting Balance + $100, the source's own stated lock value. From this point the floor stops trailing and stays fixed at $50,100, even if the balance keeps climbing.
4. This file reads the source's "$2,100 buffer" (Condition 1 in the source) as $2,100 of cumulative profit from the $50,000 start, so the same $52,100 balance would clear it; that reading is derived, because the source never says whether the buffer is measured as profit or as a balance, or how it applies after a first withdrawal (see Not Confirmed). The drawdown-lock trigger and the buffer requirement are two separately-defined rules, and the source never states they are the same mechanism, only that the lock is at $50,100 and the buffer is $2,100.
5. To actually request a withdrawal, the trader must also satisfy the 20% Perpetual Consistency Rule and hold at least $800 above the buffer, per the source: "You must have at least $800 above the buffer to make a withdrawal." Under the derived reading in step 4, that means cumulative cycle profit of at least $2,100 + $800 = $2,900, i.e., a balance of at least $52,900 (derived, not source-stated).
6. Once eligible, the trader can request a withdrawal of between $800 (minimum) and $1,200 (maximum), paid at a 90% Reward Share.

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Drawdown Lock trigger, dollar profit** — the plan's own article states the lock rule ("trails upward only until it locks at Starting Balance + $100") and the locked value ("this means the threshold locks at $50,100") but no dollar profit or balance at which the lock happens. A $2,100 cumulative profit (the $2,000 Drawdown Amount plus $100) is this file's own arithmetic, used in the Worked Example only, so the Drawdown Lock row says Unconfirmed for the dollar trigger. Do not assume a source states $2,100 or $52,100 as the trigger.
- **Trailing formula and breach condition** — none of the four FNL:003-specific articles states the floor's trailing formula (highest EOD balance minus the $2,000 Drawdown Amount) or the breach condition. Both are read from the firm-wide MLL article 14298225's general description ("If equity falls below the MLL at any time, the account is breached."), whose tables had an FNL:003 column for the $2,000 figure and the $50,100 lock, and whose worked example is for a Legacy account, a different plan; the current merged article 17230292 has no FNL:003 column. The Minimum Balance (ongoing) cell's pre-lock floor and the Worked Example's $48,000 in step 1 therefore rest on that firm-wide wording plus the old-ID FNL:003 column, not on an FNL:003-specific statement. Do not assume the firm-wide trailing and breach wording is restated for FNL:003 by name.
- **Buffer measurement basis** — the FNL:003 articles state "Clear the $2,100 buffer. The buffer stays in the account, and profit above it is withdrawable.", "You must have at least a $2,100 buffer during the current trading cycle. Only profits above the $2,100 buffer limit are withdrawable." and "You must have at least $800 above the buffer to make a withdrawal.", but never say whether the buffer is $2,100 of profit or a $52,100 balance, whether it is measured from the account start or from the start of each cycle, or what the buffer is after a first withdrawal. The Worked Example's $52,100 and $52,900 balances are this file's own reading. Do not assume the buffer is a $52,100 balance, and do not assume it resets or stays at $2,100 in later cycles.
- **Daily Loss Limit** — FundedNext's own sources disagree. The Labs page (`fundednext.com/labs`, re-fetched live 2026-10-02) states "Daily Loss Limit $1,000" on this product's own card. The FNL:003-specific help-center articles (16847874, 16847913, re-read 2026-10-02) and the firm-wide Daily Loss Limit article (14298201, and its merged successor 17230292, whose daily loss limit table lists only Rapid Daily, Rapid Pro with the Add-On, Legacy and Flex) are all silent on it. The Labs card also gives no breach type for this $1,000 figure: it has no "soft breach" or "hard breach" wording, and no statement of whether the limit is flat or trails with the balance. The firm-wide articles' own soft-breach, EOD-trailing mechanics are stated only for the models in that table. Do not treat $1,000 as a settled figure, do not assume FNL:003 has no daily loss limit either, and do not assume a soft-breach or EOD-trailing mechanic carries over to FNL:003 from the firm-wide article's description; no source resolves any of this.
- **Payout Frequency / withdrawal cadence** — FundedNext's own sources disagree. The Labs page (`fundednext.com/labs`, re-fetched live 2026-10-03) labels a field "Get Rewards In" with the value "5 Days" on this product's own card. Neither FNL:003-specific help-center article (16847874, 16847913) states a fixed cadence; only the per-cycle buffer and consistency conditions are confirmed there. Do not treat "5 Days" as a settled figure, and do not assume a cadence carries over from another FundedNext plan; no source resolves the disagreement, and the engine's 5-day wait before the first reward follows the card as the tool's reading, not a confirmed rule (question U29).
- **Purchasability status, narrowed 2026-10-02** — the Labs page marks this product's card Expired with `hideCta:true` and a "Feedback"-labelled button, and the help center now files its articles under "FNL:003 (Not Eligible for purchase)" (the label was "FNL:003 (Eligible for purchase)" until the 2026-10-01 re-creation), so the two public signals agree. The only remaining contrary signals are backend: the checkout API still prices `plan_id 99` at $149.99 and the Labs card's `ctaHref` still holds a purchase URL; the same API still prices the retired Rapid and Bolt plans, so neither is evidence of sale. This file follows the Expired status and the "Not Eligible for purchase" label. - **News Trading** — the firm-wide news-trading statement (FAQ 17225788: "FundedNext Futures does not restrict news trading."; the older 14298245 worded it for "the Challenge Account and the FundedNext Account") does not name FNL:003 specifically. Applying it here relies on FNL:003's own article stating it operates "under the FundedNext Account rules" from purchase, not on an FNL:003-named restatement of the news-trading policy itself. Do not assume this is independently re-confirmed by an FNL:003-specific article.
- **Inactivity Rule** — the same category-level caveat as News Trading applies: the 30-day inactivity rule is stated for "FundedNext Accounts" generically, not for FNL:003 by name. Do not assume this is independently re-confirmed by an FNL:003-specific article.
- **Live Transition** — not addressed in any source cited by this file (see Live Transition section above). Do not assume any FundedNext live-account program applies to FNL:003 by analogy with another plan.
- **Engine cross-check, FNL:003 daily loss limit and 5-day payout wait, fixed 2026-10-02 by audit package WP61 (the 5-day wait is the tool's reading, open question U29)** — before WP61 the engine modeled no daily loss limit and no 5-day payout wait, while the Labs card states a $1,000 daily loss limit and "Get Rewards In 5 Days" (both detailed above). `buildFnl003Plan` in `src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/fundednext/FundedNext.ts` models FNL:003 and agrees with every figure in this file that traces to the FNL:003-specific and firm-wide help-center articles: `maxDrawdown` $2,000, lock `atProfit` $2,000 + $100 with `lockedThreshold: lockThresholdAt(100)` (the Starting Balance + $100 floor documented above), `contractLimitsOf(3, 3)` (3 Mini / 30 Micro), a 20% `ConsistencyScope.Funded` `ConsistencyBasis.Perpetual` rule, `evalDailyLossLimit` `DailyLossLimitKind.None` (there is no Challenge phase), `oneTimeEval` $149.99, `payoutRequestCap` $1,200, `maxLifetimePayouts` 5, and `maxFundedAccounts` 3. Reading "Get Rewards In 5 Days" as a wait before the first reward is the tool's assumption, not a stated rule: the card's tooltip reads "The timeframe within which you can expect your rewards," which can mean the delivery time of a reward rather than a waiting period before it can be requested, and articles 16847874 and 16847913 list only the buffer and the consistency rule as conditions, with no day wait. The sources disagree, so the 5-day gate is kept as the more conservative reading (open question U29: keep the 5-day gate as the tool's reading, or model no wait and record the Labs card as a conflict), and the card does not say whether the wait repeats each cycle or whether the days are calendar or traded days. A 2026-09-19 re-audit corrected this file's earlier claim that the plan was not modeled at all. Re-checked 2026-10-02: `buildFnl003Plan` sets `availability: PlanAvailability.Discontinued`, which matches the Expired status and the "Not Eligible for purchase" label, and every FundedNext plan the engine offers for purchase (Flex, Legacy, Rapid Pro, Rapid Pro with the DLL Add-On, Rapid Daily) is on the firm's current list of sold products (article 17225718). Do not assume the 5-day wait or the flat $1,000 lockout is a rule FundedNext states for FNL:003, and do not assume this agreement extends to figures the engine does not represent, such as the live stage.
