## Overview

This file documents FundedNext Futures' newer live-trading program, the one a Flex, Rapid Pro, or Rapid Daily FundedNext Account transitions into after a trader is selected for live trading. It is shared by `flex.md`, `rapid-pro.md`, and `rapid-daily.md` and is not duplicated in any of those files. It is a different mechanic from the older/legacy live program used by the Legacy Challenge, documented separately in [`legacy-live.md`](legacy-live.md); none of the three source articles cited here describe that program, so no legacy-live figures are stated in this file.

All three cited articles cover the same eligibility, transition-process, and breach/cooldown mechanics in substantially similar wording (since 2026-10-02 the two plan-specific articles are the older revision of the combined one: 16522296 adds the 25K and 150K rows to its Section 3 and 4 tables and its Locked MLL floors table, and replaces its Section 5 example), with minor phrasing differences between them, and they differ in which account sizes each one's Section 3/4 tables list. The two plan-specific articles (Rapid: 25K/50K/100K; Flex: 50K/100K/150K) are each scoped to the funded-account sizes that plan itself sells. This file does not independently confirm that Rapid Pro/Daily ever offers a 150K funded tier, or that Flex ever offers a 25K funded tier; each plan's own file is the source of truth for which sizes that plan sells. The combined, most-recently-updated article lists the union of all four sizes and is used below as the canonical per-size table.

A live account's starting balance is a small fixed deposit by size, not $0. The Max Loss Limit (MLL) itself starts from $0 at account open and trails up with the account's balance. Once transitioned, a trader holds up to 5 live accounts (one per eligible funded account transitioning).

## How the Drawdown Works

The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) on the live account is a trailing floor: "It moves up as your account grows, but never moves down." It starts from $0 at account open (when Balance = Starting Balance) and, while trailing, sits at Balance − Starting Balance.

The MLL locks (stops trailing permanently): "in any of two situations: When you request a payout; [or] When your profit equals your initial MLL amount." The source's own "MLL Lock Benefit" section clarifies that "your initial MLL amount" in that sentence means the starting balance: "The MLL locks when your profit equals your starting balance... or when you request a payout, whichever comes first."

Once locked, the floor is fixed: "If your balance touches or drops below this floor, the account is auto-liquidated." The source states this size-invariant rule for the locked value itself: "Your MLL locks $1,000 below your starting balance... This is the MLL Lock Benefit, and it applies automatically to every live account," and separately, "Every size follows the same rule: the lock is $1,000 below your starting balance."

**Section 5 and Section 6 now agree on the $1,000 floor but not on where the lock triggers, and two older copies still carry the earlier $2,000 rows (bug class 1):** until its 2026-09-27 revision, the "Trailing MLL Explained" section of 16522296 walked a 50K example where the MLL, at the moment of lock (balance reaches $4,000), locked at "$2,000," i.e. at the starting balance itself, not $1,000 below it, and a final row breached the account at a $2,000 balance. The "MLL Lock Benefit" section directly addressed that figure and overrode it: "On a 50K live account, that means your MLL locks at $1,000, not at the $2,000 starting balance." The revised article (re-fetched 2026-10-02, `lastUpdatedDate` 2026-09-27T10:33:53Z) gives these Section 5 example rows, quoted verbatim (columns Event | Balance | MLL Floor | What Happens):

- `Account opens | $2,000 | $0 | —`
- `Balance grows to $3,000 | $3,000 | $1,000 | MLL trails up with balance and locks`
- `Balance reaches $4,000 | $4,000 | $1,000 | Profit has equalled the initial balance amount`
- `Payout requested at any point | — | $1,000 | MLL locks at the starting balance—$1,000 if not already locked`
- `Balance drops to $1,000 | $1,000 | $1,000 | The account has reached the MLL Level, account breached`

The dollar floor is now $1,000 in every row, so the $2,000 conflict with Section 6 is gone from 16522296. A new conflict replaces it, and it is not resolved here: the second row says the MLL "locks" at a $3,000 balance (profit $1,000), while the same article's lock rule ("When your profit equals your initial MLL amount"), the "MLL Lock Benefit" section ("The MLL locks when your profit equals your starting balance (balance reaches $4,000)"), the third row ("Profit has equalled the initial balance amount" at $4,000) and the Section 6 table ("Balance reaches $4,000 (or payout requested) | $4,000 | Locks at $1,000") all place the trigger at a $4,000 balance (profit $2,000). The conflict matters beyond the floor, because Section 4 doubles the contract limits "once the MLL locks": a lock at $3,000 would double them $1,000 of profit earlier. The two plan-specific copies, 15430139 and 15900277 (still loading, no longer in any collection), keep the old rows, re-read 2026-10-02 ("Balance grows to $3,000 | $3,000 | $1,000 | MLL trails up with balance" without "and locks", "Balance reaches $4,000 | $4,000 | $2,000 | MLL locks — profit has equalled the MLL amount" and "Balance drops to $2,000 | $2,000 | $2,000 | The account has reached the MLL Level, account breached"). This file treats $1,000-below-starting-balance (the "MLL Lock Benefit" figure) as the operative locked value for every size, per the "Locked MLL floors per account size" table (which states $500/$1,000/$2,000/$3,500 for 25K/50K/100K/150K), and the $4,000 balance (profit equal to the starting balance) as the stated trigger, but the "and locks" row is a conflicting statement of the trigger inside the same article, so the trigger itself is Not Confirmed: see the Not Confirmed bullet on the Section 5 row. The older copies' $2,000 rows are superseded, not deleted, since a reader of those pages will still encounter them.

**A second ambiguity the source does not resolve, and this file does not invent an answer to:** a separate note in the "Trailing MLL Explained" section states, "If the requested withdrawal reduces the current account balance to the initial deposit amount, the account will be automatically liquidated." This is a distinct trigger from the locked-floor breach described above: it fires off the starting-balance level, not the (lower, $1,000-below-start) locked MLL floor. Neither cited article states how this interacts with an account whose MLL has already locked at the lower floor (e.g., whether a withdrawal that drops a locked 50K account's balance to exactly $2,000, above its $1,000 locked floor, triggers auto-liquidation anyway, or whether this note only applies pre-lock). Do not assume either resolution. Re-checked 2026-10-02: the withdrawal note is unchanged in the 2026-09-27 revision, so this ambiguity stands.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the FundedNext Live account's literal, nominal balance from its actual starting deposit (not a $0-based profit convention), matching the source's own balance-denominated language throughout its own worked tables ("Balance reaches $4,000," "Balance drops to $1,800"). It uses the 50K tier ($2,000 starting balance), the only size the source works through explicitly.

1. Account opens. Balance = $2,000 (the Starting Balance). MLL Floor = $0, matching the source's own "Account opens | $2,000 | Trailing from $0 | Active."

2. Balance grows to $3,000 (a new high). MLL Floor trails up: Floor = Balance − Starting Balance = $3,000 − $2,000 = $1,000, matching the figures of the source's own Section 5 row "Balance grows to $3,000 | $3,000 | $1,000 | MLL trails up with balance and locks" (this example follows the trailing figures of that row only; its "and locks" wording would put the lock at this $3,000 balance and conflicts with step 3's trigger, see Not Confirmed)

3. Balance reaches $4,000 (a new high). Profit at this point = Balance − Starting Balance = $4,000 − $2,000 = $2,000, exactly equal to the $2,000 starting balance: the source's stated lock trigger ("profit equals your starting balance"). The MLL now locks permanently. Per the "MLL Lock Benefit," the locked floor is set $1,000 below the $2,000 starting balance: $2,000 − $1,000 = $1,000 (the 2026-09-27 revision of the source's example table now shows $1,000 at this same balance level too; the older 15430139 and 15900277 still show $2,000, see the note above). This matches the source's own Section 6 row "Balance reaches $4,000 (or payout requested) | $4,000 | Locks at $1,000 | Active", and the Section 5 row "Balance reaches $4,000 | $4,000 | $1,000 | Profit has equalled the initial balance amount"

4. Balance drops to $1,800 (a loss from the $4,000 peak). $1,800 is still above the locked $1,000 floor, so the account remains active; the floor does not move, matching "Balance drops to $1,800 | $1,800 | $1,000 | Active."

5. Balance touches $1,000 (a further loss, reaching the locked floor exactly). Per the source, "If your balance touches or drops below this floor, the account is auto-liquidated." The account breaches, matching "Balance touches $1,000 | $1,000 | $1,000 | Breached."

Re-derivation check: Starting Balance $2,000 → lock trigger at profit = $2,000 (balance $4,000, stated explicitly in the source) → locked floor = $2,000 − $1,000 = $1,000, matching the $1,000 value on the 50K row of the "MLL Locks at" column.

## Live Account Eligibility & Transition

**Eligibility criteria.** "Earn 15 Performance Rewards during your FundedNext journey to enter the live review pool." Traders returning from a previous live journey "re-enter the review pool after every 5 Performance Rewards — since going live once already proves the trader's strategy." Meeting the threshold does not guarantee a transition: "The FundedNext risk team evaluates each trader's full performance history, and only traders who demonstrate consistent, responsible trading behaviour will be moved to live. If you're not selected, your accounts continue as normal." Transitions "only happen at the end of an active payout cycle — never mid-cycle": "If a trader is selected during the 3rd cycle, the transition will occur after the trader requests their 3rd payout from that cycle."

**A genuine, five-way conflict on Flex's own eligibility trigger (four statements confirmed live 2026-09-26 and re-fetched 2026-10-02, a fifth found 2026-10-02), not silently resolved.** This file's own canonical article (16522296, updated 2026-09-27) states the 15-Performance-Reward, journey-wide criterion quoted above and applies it to Flex as well as Rapid. The Flex-specific "Road To Live Trading - Flex Challenge" article (15430139, now an older revision of 16522296 outside any collection) states the same criterion verbatim: "Earn 15 Performance Rewards during your FundedNext journey to enter the live review pool," so it corroborates 16522296 rather than adding a distinct position. Four of FundedNext's other own pages state genuinely different criteria for Flex specifically: Flex's own overview article (14878751, re-fetched live 2026-09-26, and its re-created copy 17229807, identical text, checked 2026-10-02) states "Completion of 5 Performance Reward Cycles from a single account"; the public Flex marketing page (`fundednext.com/futures/flex`, re-fetched 2026-10-02) states "Five reward journeys from one FundedNext account. Complete all five to enter the live funding review pool"; the Legacy/former-Rapid live-transition article (14283903, re-fetched live 2026-10-02, not otherwise cited by this file) states in its own TL;DR that eligibility is reached "after reaching $100,000 in Total Active Profits or 5 withdrawals from a single Flex Account (or earlier by discretionary review)"; and the withdrawal article 17242064 (the current ID of 14269280) says in its Flex section "You can move toward live trading as early as 15 withdrawals from Flex Account.", in the same section that says "After 5 withdrawal the account gets concluded." (its older twin 14269280 says "as early as 5 withdrawals of your Performance Reward from a single Flex Account"). A second reviewer independently re-verified the first four quotes as genuine, verbatim text on FundedNext's own pages on 2026-09-26, but an earlier draft of this callout attributed each quote to the wrong source article (each shifted one article along); this text now states the corrected mapping. This is a real, unresolved conflict among FundedNext's own pages, not a stale-vs-current pair: do not pick one of the five as authoritative, and do not assume `flex.md`'s own Live Transition section (which cites the 5-Performance-Reward-Cycles criterion, sourced to 14878751 and now 17229807) is the settled rule for Flex; see that file's own Not Confirmed section for the same flag.

**Transition process, Challenge Accounts.** "All active Challenge Accounts are permanently closed at the time of transition" and "are not eligible for any refunds." The source notes: "Traders who prefer to preserve their Challenge Accounts should keep this in mind before stacking multiple Challenge Accounts."

**Transition process, FundedNext Accounts.** "FundedNext Accounts with at least 1 successful withdrawal will be transitioned to a live account." "FundedNext Accounts with 0 payouts will be closed, and the funded account's cost will be refunded within 24 hours once the live transition is completed."

**Accounts received.** "Traders receive one live account per eligible funded account transitioning, up to a maximum of 5 live accounts."

## Breach and Cooldown

Traders who breach a live account enter a mandatory cooldown before any trading is permitted:

- Standard cooldown: "2 weeks mandatory for all traders."
- Extended cooldown: "4 weeks or an extended period, at FundedNext's discretion, for traders who breach through reckless behaviour such as aggressive positions or breaching within a single trading session."

After completing the cooldown, "traders return to normal simulated trading, purchasing Challenge Accounts and following the standard path back to live."
