## Overview

FundedNext Futures enforces a set of trading-conduct and risk rules stated for "both Challenge and FundedNext Accounts" and "regardless of the stage" (Prohibited Trading Strategies article), or "across all FundedNext Futures account types" (Micro-Scalping article). This file collects the ones that are firm-wide by the source's own wording and are not already documented in a plan file. Two things in this file are narrower than firm-wide and are labeled as such where they appear: the Disciplined Trader Program (applies only to enrolled traders) and the $500 withdrawal-button lock (Legacy and Flex only, cross-referenced, not restated). Violations may result in compliance warnings, deductions from Performance Rewards, advancement holds, or account termination, depending on the rule and the account stage; each section below states the consequence its own source states, no more.

## 2% CME Price Limit Rule

Per the Price Limit article (updated 2026-03-30): "At FundedNext Futures, traders must not trade within 2% of the CME Price Limit. For equity futures like NQ, ES, YM, MNQ, MES, the CME price limit is 7%, meaning the restricted zone starts 2% before that limit."

**How the limit is set and updated, verbatim:** "Price Limits are calculated based on the end-of-day settlement price and vary by product, contract month, and time of day (i.e., Overnight price Limits differ from the price Limits used during business hours)" and "The price Limits are updated at 4:05 PM CT after each trading session and can be found on the CME Price Limits page." So the restricted zone must be recomputed after every session and differs between the overnight and regular-hours sessions.

**Formula, verbatim:**

- Upper Bound: Reference Price × (1 + 0.07 − 0.02)
- Lower Bound: Reference Price × (1 − 0.07 + 0.02)

**Worked example, verbatim (NQ):** "If the CME Price limit for NQ is 18,556 and the overnight limit is 7%: Upper Trading Limit: 18,556 × (1 + 0.07 − 0.02) = 19,483; Lower Trading Limit: 18,556 × (1 − 0.07 + 0.02) = 17,628." And: "If the market price reaches above 19,483 or drops below 17,628, traders must stop trading immediately."

**Monitoring, verbatim:** "One of the most effective ways to comply with the 2% Price Limit Rule is to monitor the % Net Change for the futures contract on your trading platform's quote board... If this data is not currently displayed, add the '% Net Change' column to your platform to track real-time movements."

**Consequence, verbatim:** "violations may result in a deducted profit amount, account suspension, or restrictions."

## Micro-Scalping Policy

**Definition and Evaluation:**

Micro-scalping is defined as opening and closing trades within 10 seconds to capture rapid, small price movements. The micro-scalping percentage represents the proportion of total recorded profit derived from trades closed within 10 seconds and is evaluated on a per-cycle basis. Losing trades are excluded from this count; only profitable trades contribute toward the micro-scalping threshold.

**Thresholds and Warnings:**

- **30% Threshold (Warning Stage):** When micro-scalping profit reaches 30% of total recorded profit, a compliance warning is issued. No deduction or suspension is applied at this stage. The warning helps traders identify when profit distribution is approaching the permitted limit and when trading activity needs rebalancing.
- **40% Threshold (Breach):** Once micro-scalping profit reaches or exceeds 40%, a violation occurs, with different consequences by account stage (see below).

**Challenge Account Consequence (40% Breach):**

Once micro-scalping profit reaches or exceeds 40%, the ability to reach the profit target and advance to the next stage is placed on hold. The account remains active and trading can continue, but advancement will not be processed until the micro-scalping percentage falls back below 40%. Eligibility is restored automatically once the threshold is cleared; no manual action is required.

**FundedNext Futures Account Consequence (40% Breach):**

Once micro-scalping profit reaches or exceeds 40%, the micro-scalping portion of profit for that cycle is deducted in full. The account remains active; micro-scalping does not result in account termination. Any future profit attributed to micro-scalping activity will be subject to the same deduction on an ongoing basis.

**When Deduction Exceeds Total Profit:**

If the total micro-scalping profit deduction equals or exceeds the account's net profit for the cycle, no Performance Reward is issued for that cycle and the withdrawal option is disabled. The account remains active. "A full breakdown of applied deductions is available in the account dashboard."

**Per-account independence, verbatim:** "Violations are tracked per account and evaluated per cycle. A warning or deduction on one account does not carry over to any other existing or future account. Each account is evaluated independently."

**Clarity Cards Dashboard:**

Clarity Cards are live tracking tools in the account dashboard that monitor micro-scalping in real time, updating after every trade closes. "The cards visible to you will depend on whether you hold a Challenge Account or a FundedNext Account." The Micro-Scalping Clarity Card displays four items:

- Micro-Scalped Trades: the total count of profitable positions opened and closed within 10 seconds
- Micro-Scalped Profit (%): the percentage of total recorded profit generated from these trades ("Keep this below 40%")
- Profit from Micro-Scalping ($): the dollar amount generated from these trades
- See Trade Details: "A full list of every trade that was categorized as 'micro scalping.'"

The Clarity Cards article restates the same 30% warning and 40% breach thresholds and the same Challenge-versus-FundedNext consequence split as the Micro-Scalping article; the two sources agree on every figure.

## High-Frequency Trading (HFT) Prohibition

FundedNext Futures strictly prohibits High-Frequency Trading, defined as an advanced trading strategy using powerful algorithms and ultra-fast network connections to execute a large number of trades within a few seconds, attempting to profit from tiny market inefficiencies by flooding the market with trades.

**Reasons HFT is Prohibited:**

- Market Manipulation Risk: HFT generates artificial trading activity, creating false impressions of demand or supply, misleading other market participants and disrupting genuine market flow.
- System Overload: HFT typically involves hundreds or thousands of orders in seconds, placing significant pressure on platform infrastructure and causing potential technical disruptions.
- Market Instability: Excessive volume and speed of HFT orders causes erratic price movements, creating uncertainty affecting the broader trading environment.
- Unfair Advantage: HFT relies on technological superiority and execution speed rather than market analysis or trading skill, inconsistent with FundedNext Futures' commitment to fair and equal trading environment.

**Consequence, verbatim:**

"Traders identified as engaging in HFT on a FundedNext Futures Account will be subject to corrective action, which may include an account termination." The TL;DR states it the same way: "Engaging in HFT on a Futures Account may result in account termination." Termination is the stated possible outcome, not the stated certain one.

**Note on Automated Systems:**

Automated trading systems and algorithms are monitored on FundedNext Futures Accounts. Where such systems are identified as executing trades at frequency and speed consistent with High-Frequency Trading, the account will be subject to review and enforcement action. The use of automated tools does not exempt a trader from compliance with prohibited trading parameters.

## Permitted Practices with Conditions

### Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)

FundedNext Futures allows Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) when used as a structured and disciplined strategy with clear risk management and a predefined plan. Traders may scale into positions at planned price levels or zones with no restrictions on timing, size, or placement, provided the approach reflects disciplined risk control.

**Permitted:** Structured DCA with a well-planned rationale for each entry, integrating risk management and discipline.

**Discouraged, not prohibited.** The DCA article never uses the word "prohibited" for any DCA behavior. Its exact wording: "continuously adding to losing positions without a predefined plan or exit strategy is not encouraged, as it may increase margin use and liquidation risk. Similarly, averaging down blindly in hopes of a reversal is considered poor risk management and should be avoided." The TL;DR says it "is discouraged." No consequence is stated for doing it; contrast the Prohibited Trading Strategies table above, where consequences are stated.

### Automated Trading Systems (EAs and Bots)

FundedNext Futures allows the use of automated trading systems, including Expert Advisors (EAs) and trading bots, during both the Challenge Phase and on FundedNext Accounts. However, FundedNext does not offer any support for setting up, configuring, or troubleshooting these systems. Any issues such as technical errors, execution delays, or system malfunctions must be managed by the trader; FundedNext is not responsible for any impact these may have on a trading account.

**Important Restriction:** While EAs and bots are permitted, any strategies that aim to exploit system vulnerabilities, such as latency abuse or order flooding, are strictly prohibited.

### Copy Trading

Copy trading is generally prohibited to ensure traders are evaluated based on individual skill, independent decision-making, and consistent risk management.

**Permitted Forms of Copy Trading:**

- Copy trading within FundedNext is allowed: traders can freely copy trades between their own FundedNext Accounts.
- Copy trading between FundedNext and other prop firms is allowed if the trader owns all accounts involved. FundedNext may request verification that all accounts involved are registered under the exact same name as per FundedNext records; any variation in spelling or identity will not be accepted.
- Use of any copy trading tools is allowed, including Tradovate's built-in copy trading feature, NinjaTrader modules like Replikanto, and any third-party platforms, provided they are used for copying one's own trades only.

**Prohibited Forms of Copy Trading:**

- Traders cannot group trade or coordinate trades with other traders, whether inside or outside FundedNext.
- Traders cannot subscribe to any trading signal services or use external signals to guide trading decisions.
- Traders are not allowed to copy trades from another person's account within FundedNext, even through automated tools or trade copiers.

## Trading Hours and Position Flattening

FundedNext Futures does not permit overnight or weekend trade holding.

**Daily Position Flattening:**

- **Flatten By:** 3:10 PM Central Time (CT) each trading day, Monday through Friday
- **System Auto-Close:** If traders fail to close trades on their own, the system will automatically close all open positions at the end of the trading day.
- **Resume Trading:** Traders can resume trading at 5:00 PM Central Time (CT).

**Weekend Trading Resumption:**

- **Weekend Reopen:** Trading resumes on Sunday at 5:00 PM Central Time (CT).

**Rationale:** Overnight and weekend holding exposes traders to potential gap risk when markets reopen. Automatic liquidation prevents traders from incurring unintended losses due to price fluctuations outside trading hours and ensures all traders operate under the same risk-controlled conditions.

## Disciplined Trader Program

The Disciplined Trader Program is a structured educational and corrective initiative "designed to support traders who display repeated high-risk behavior, inconsistent discipline, or trading patterns that may violate long-term risk principles." This program applies only to traders enrolled in it, not firm-wide; it is included here because enrollment can happen to a trader on any plan and its parameters override the plan's own. The source frames it as "not a penalty" but "a structured opportunity for traders to regain consistency."

**Entry Triggers (Official Warning First):**

"A trader will receive an official warning before being enrolled in the Disciplined Trader Program of FundedNext Futures." Enrollment applies "to traders whose activity is identified as high-risk or inconsistent by FundedNext's risk management systems." The source's own list is explicitly open-ended: "Such trading behavior may include, but is not limited to:"

- Losing trades that are significantly larger than average winning trades
- Breaching multiple accounts within a short period
- Gambling-style trading without defined risk parameters
- Risking substantial capital repeatedly over short durations
- Breaching multiple Live Accounts without proper risk management and awareness

Following the warning, the trader's trading activity is closely monitored. If the trader does not make necessary adjustments to high-risk or inconsistent trading behavior after the warning, they are enrolled in the Disciplined Trader Program.

**Enrolled-State Parameters:**

While enrolled in the Disciplined Trader Program, traders operate under strict and controlled parameters designed to build disciplined execution and long-term consistency:

- **Maximum Risk at a Time:** Must not exceed 1% of the account's initial balance.
- **Mandatory Stop Loss:** Every trade must include a predefined stop loss.
- **Maximum Loss Limit (MLL):** The MLL will be determined based on the account type and size purchased. Exceeding the MLL constitutes a hard breach.
- **Consistency and Minimum Trading Days:** Trader must adhere to the consistency and minimum trading day requirements specified by the selected plan.
- **News Trading:** Strictly prohibited during high-impact economic events.
- **Account Allocation:** Traders enrolled in the Disciplined Trader Program may continue using their existing account allocations (with no restrictions on accounts purchased before enrollment). Any new accounts purchased after enrollment are subject to a cumulative maximum allocation limit of $50,000. The maximum allocation applies to the total size of all newly purchased accounts after program enrollment. Breaching one of those accounts does not free up its allocation, verbatim: "If the trader breaches any of these accounts, they may purchase additional account(s) provided the total allocation remains within the $50,000 cumulative limit."

Upon successful graduation from the program, all account purchasing limitations are lifted, allowing the trader to purchase accounts without restriction.

**Graduation Milestones:**

A trader successfully graduates from the Disciplined Trader Program after completing all of the following:

1. Successfully completing five (5) trading cycles
2. Achieving a cumulative profit milestone of $30,000 in accordance with the requirements of their trading model
3. Achieving a minimum of 15 winning days (a day counts as a winning day when the trader makes at least $200 in profit)

Throughout this period, traders must remain fully compliant with all program rules, including strict risk management, consistency requirements, and trading discipline. Upon graduation, the trader may contact the FundedNext Trading Ethics Team to request restoration of standard allocation limits in accordance with FundedNext's Policy.

**Re-Enrollment:**

Traders who complete the program but later exhibit high-risk or erratic trading behavior may be re-enrolled to rebuild consistency and risk control. Traders who breach the Maximum Loss Limit during enrollment will remain in the program until consistent discipline is demonstrated. Traders who revert to excessive-risk behavior after graduation will be re-enrolled for further evaluation. There is no fixed timeline for graduation; a trader remains in the program until demonstrating consistent discipline and responsible risk management and meeting all program requirements.

**Non-Compliance Consequences (Three-Tier Ladder):**

| Violation Count | Consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Account Status                                    |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1st Violation   | Formal Warning: the violation is documented, and trading activity is closely monitored.                                                                                                                                                                               | Account remains active                            |
| 2nd Violation   | Performance Reward Adjustment: the trader's Performance Reward ratio is adjusted to 50/50 for all upcoming payout cycles. Enhanced supervision applies. Any further violations result in account termination.                                                         | Account remains active under enhanced supervision |
| 3rd Violation   | Account Termination: persistent disregard for program parameters or continued high-risk behavior results in loss of eligibility for future allocations. May only reapply after a defined cooling-off period, subject to approval from FundedNext Trading Ethics Team. | Account terminated                                |

**Non-Participation Consequences:**

If a trader is identified for enrollment but chooses not to participate or fails to acknowledge program terms:

- All active Challenge or FundedNext Accounts will be temporarily paused until the trader formally agrees to participate.
- If the trader decides not to participate, they can choose to exit the platform by requesting a refund or Performance Reward, whichever is applicable.
- Upon exiting, they are no longer eligible for the program and lose access to the FundedNext platform.
- If they wish to rejoin FundedNext in the future, they must enroll in the Disciplined Trader Program to continue participation.

## Performance Reward Withdrawal Mechanics

**A 24-hour processing guarantee, with a $1,000 penalty clause.** "FundedNext Futures processes Performance Reward within 24 hours from the moment a trader submits a withdrawal request through the FundedNext Dashboard." Those two carve-outs are what void the guarantee.

**FNmarkets transfers have their own, much lower floor.** Transferring a Performance Reward to an FNmarkets trading account, rather than withdrawing it, is subject to a different minimum: "The minimum transfer amount is $50." That is well below the $250 withdrawal floor documented below, and it is availability-gated by country (FNmarkets maintains its own restricted-country list).

- **U.S. traders must file a W-9 before any withdrawal**: "Only U.S. citizens and U.S. residents requesting a withdrawal must submit a W-9 form." If one is not on file: "You will not be able to request withdrawals. - Your withdrawal request will remain blocked until the W-9 is" submitted.
- **Crypto payouts must go to a personal wallet**: "To receive your Performance Rewards safely, please provide your own personal crypto wallet address — not one associated with a business or organization platform."
- **Bank transfer is unavailable as a payout method in certain countries**: "Due to regulatory requirements, bank transfer is not available as a Performance Reward method for the following countries." The country list is in the source article ("What methods are available for Performance Reward withdrawal in FundedNext Futures") and is not reproduced here.

## Trading Costs (All-In Rate)

Commissions are only one component of what a trade costs. Per "What are the commission rates in FundedNext Futures?": "Futures trading commissions are part of the total trading cost (all-in rate), which includes broker commissions, exchange & NFA fees, and clearing fees. The full cost per trade = Number of Contracts × All-in Rate × 2 (covering both entry and exit)." The per-product all-in rate table in that article is not reproduced here; read it before sizing a high-frequency strategy, since the doubling for entry and exit is what makes scalping expensive.

## Contract Limits Are Fixed, and Exceeding Them Is Asymmetric

"Each account has a fixed contract limit based on its model, size, and stage, and this limit does not increase as profits grow." The limit does not scale with account growth, unlike some firms' scaling plans.

Exceeding it does not automatically breach the account, but the correction is deliberately one-sided: "If a trader exceeds the allowed number of contracts, only the excess contracts are treated as violations. Any profit earned from those violated contracts will be deducted, while losses from them will still count and are not adjusted." A trader keeps the downside of an oversized position and loses the upside. The platform also blocks the order outright in some cases, showing "Your maximum order quantity has been reached."

## VPN and VPS Use

Permitted on both stages: "A trader may use a VPS (Virtual Private Server) or VPN (Virtual Private Network) while trading on both the Challenge and the FundedNext Account." Note this differs from several other firms in this tree, which ban VPNs outright. Where an IP pattern looks inconsistent, FundedNext may request documentation before continuing.

## KYC, Verification, and Compliance Sessions

- **Documents must be the account holder's own and current**: "All KYC information and documents must be accurate, current, and belong to the account holder."
- **Compliance sessions can be demanded at any point**: "FundedNext may request a Verification or Compliance Session to confirm your identity, and misconduct during this process, or any sign that an account involves more than one person's information, is treated as a serious violation."
- **The enforcement ladder is severe and explicit**: "Enforcement may include Account Termination, Permanent Platform Termination, Performance Reward Ineligibility or Adjustment, and Refund Ineligibility." Permanent platform termination and refund ineligibility are stated as distinct outcomes, not just account closure.
- **KYC expires every 90 days**: "FundedNext requires a mandatory 90-day KYC renewal for all verified traders. Every trader must re-submit their KYC documents after 90 days of their last approval date when they become eligible for KYC." The stated purpose is to "catch issues such as expired documents, name mismatches, or changes in country of residence" before they delay a Performance Reward.

- **KYC submission attempts are capped, and the cap varies by country**: "The number of allowed KYC submission attempts can vary based on the local regulations and provider requirements: - Nigeria: 5 attempts - All Other Countries: 3 attempts."

## Reset Rules

- **Trading resumes immediately after a paid reset**: "Maximum Loss Limit Breach: If you reset after a maximum loss breach, you can resume trading immediately once the reset is confirmed." The same article states the equivalent for a Daily Loss Limit breach.
- **Resets are unlimited, bounded only by allocation**: "Note: Resets will remain unlimited up to the maximum allocation."
- **A closed Challenge cannot be reset, and a closed FundedNext Account forfeits unclaimed rewards**: "Closed Challenges cannot be reset. - If a FundedNext Account with profits is closed, you cannot request or claim Performance Rewards."
- **Resets apply to the Challenge stage only**: "Yes, you can reset your account as long as it is still in the Challenge Phase, giving you the flexibility to start over if needed. However, once your account progresses to a FundedNext Account, the reset option is no longer available." A separate article states it as "FundedNext Accounts can not be reset; a new purchase is required to continue trading."
- **An account bought with an Add-On must be reset with that Add-On, and the Add-On is re-charged**: "Reset Criteria: If a trader opts for an Add-On, that account needs to be reset along with the Add-On. Resetting the account without the Add-On is not permitted." and "Note: If any add-on is purchased with the account, then the price will be added to the reset fee."

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Enforcement mechanisms for specific prohibited strategies**: the Prohibited Trading Strategies article states the prohibited practices and one blanket consequence ("account suspension and disqualification from further participation," restated as "may result in account suspension, disqualification, or restrictions") but does not quantify a review threshold, monitoring method, or violation count for any of them. Do not assume a specific number of violations or an automated trigger is required before enforcement.
- **Margin requirement interaction with contract limits**: the source states "trading is managed through contract limits" instead of margin but does not address whether negative equity is possible within the contract-limit framework. Do not assume either scenario.
- **Disciplined Trader Program enrollment threshold**: the source says enrollment follows identification "by FundedNext's risk management systems," an official warning, and a period where "trading activity will be closely monitored by our team," and gives an explicitly open-ended ("may include, but is not limited to") list of qualifying behaviors. No numeric threshold (breach count, time window, dollar amount) is stated for what triggers the warning or the enrollment. Do not assume any specific numeric trigger drives enrollment.
- **Disciplined Trader Program cooling-off period and "trading cycles"**: the 3rd-violation consequence says a terminated trader "may only reapply after a defined cooling-off period," but the source does not state its length. The graduation milestone of "five (5) trading cycles" does not define a cycle; the plan files use "cycle" to mean a payout cycle, but the source does not say so here. Do not assume either figure.
- **Copy trading verification process specifics**: the source states FundedNext "may request verification that all accounts involved are registered under the exact same name" but does not define the method, timeline, or consequence of failing it. Do not assume a specific process.
- **"No withdrawals before the EOD" rule, FNL:003**: stated per plan for Legacy, Rapid, Bolt, Flex, and Rapid Pro and Daily in the withdraw-your-Performance-Reward article, which does not list FNL:003 at all. Do not assume it applies to FNL:003 without that plan's own citation.
- **Processing fee, exact rate**: the source says "up to 3.5%," not a fixed 3.5%. Do not assume every withdrawal incurs the full 3.5%.
- **Clarity Cards historical data retention**: the source describes a real-time display that "update[s] after every trade closes" but does not state how long past-cycle data is retained or whether prior cycles are viewable. Do not assume either.
- **Consequence for trading within 2% of the CME price limit, profit deduction or not** — the two articles that state this rule’s consequence do not agree. The dedicated article, "What is the Price Limit & how to avoid trading within the 2% price limit" (`articles/14298626`), reads: "To ensure responsible risk management and market stability, trading in this zone is strictly prohibited, and violations may result in a deducted profit amount, account suspension, or restrictions." The Prohibited Trading Strategies article (`articles/14298337`), which is the article this table is introduced as being "per", reads the same sentence without the profit-deduction clause: "...violations may result in account suspension or restrictions." Nothing read this pass says which governs, and the difference is whether a trader loses banked profit on top of losing access. Do not treat profit deduction as settled either way for this rule.
