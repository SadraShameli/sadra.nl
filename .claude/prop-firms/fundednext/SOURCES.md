# `FundedNext Futures`: Source Ledger

## Discovery completeness

FundedNext Futures has no discoverable help-center sitemap: `helpfutures.fundednext.com/sitemap.xml` returns HTTP 200 but resolves to the firm's main _marketing_ site's sitemap-index (`fundednext.com/sitemap_pages.xml`, `sitemap_posts.xml`, etc.), not a help-center article index. Instead, the full article catalogue was enumerated directly from the help center's own Next.js `__NEXT_DATA__` payloads: the home page (`helpfutures.fundednext.com/en`) lists all 14 top-level collections, each collection page's own payload lists its `articleSummaries` and any nested `subcollections` (3 of the 14 collections hold their real content entirely inside `subcollections`, not the top-level list), and this was walked recursively to a verified, reconciled total.

**164 of 164 discovered articles were read in full**, across all 14 collections, confirmed by summing each collection's own stated `articleCount` against the recursively-walked total (164 = 164, exact match, no double-counting). Of those 164, the ones actually cited in this tree's 8 files are listed below (57 unique URLs: 42 from the original pass plus 15 added on 2026-09-19, see the re-audit note). The remainder were read but are legitimately out of scope for this tree, not skipped for convenience:

- **Trading Platform FAQ (21 articles)**: Tradovate/NinjaTrader/TradingView login, order-entry, and charting how-tos. No drawdown/eval/payout-rule content.
- **Refer & Earn (27 articles)**, **Ongoing Offers and Updates (3 articles)**, **Affiliate & Partner FAQ (7 articles)**: referral-commission and promotional-discount programs, not trading rules, with one exception found by a 2026-09-20 re-audit: article 16295692 ("Rapid Daily & Rapid Pro Up-to 50% Discount Offer") states a third, distinct pricing table for those two plans, now cited in both plan files' Not Confirmed sections and ledgered below.
- **About FundedNext Futures FAQ (9 articles)**: company background, support hours, Brand Promise, certificates, restricted-country lists, age restriction. General context, with one exception found by a 2026-09-20 re-audit: article 14273525 states the 24-hour Performance Reward processing guarantee and its $1,000 penalty clause, now cited in `trading-rules.md` and ledgered below.
- **General FAQ (38 articles)**: mostly KYC, payment methods, withdrawal methods, and account-mechanics articles that are either not plan-specific or already covered by a cited firm-wide article below. Three of the 38 turned out on a 2026-09-19 re-read to carry firm-wide rule content that was not covered: the withdrawal-amounts article ($250 floor, up-to-3.5% fee, per-method caps), the withdraw-your-Performance-Reward article (its per-plan "no withdrawals before the EOD" rule), and the account-sizes-and-prices article (a further data point on the Rapid Pro/Daily pricing conflict). All three are now cited; see the rows below.
- **Trading Rules and Guidelines FAQ (22 articles)**: this collection was never named in the original version of this list, which is exactly the gap a 2026-09-19 re-audit caught. Nine of its 22 (DLL, MLL, news trading, Challenge and FundedNext consistency, benchmark days, inactivity, soft/hard breach, order types for stops) were already either cited in the plan files under their public URLs or redundant with plan-specific sister articles that are. The other 13 had zero coverage anywhere in the tree despite carrying breach-triggering, firm-wide conduct and risk rules: prohibited trading strategies, the 2% CME price limit, micro-scalping, HFT, DCA, automated trading, copy trading, margin, the Disciplined Trader Program, and the tradeable-instrument list, plus two that are genuinely out of scope (a micro-versus-e-mini contract explainer and a no-daily-profit-cap FAQ with no figures). The 11 with rule content are now documented in `trading-rules.md` and cited below.
- **Bolt Challenge FAQ (4)**, **Rapid Challenge FAQ (4)**: the old Bolt and plain-Rapid Challenges, both discontinued for new purchases and resets effective 10 July 2026. Per this repo's own convention, discontinued plans are not documented with their own file; see `README.md`'s Documentation Scope.

**Re-audit note (2026-09-19).** Prompted by the user asking, firm by firm, whether every discovered article had genuinely been read against its content rather than categorized by title, all 122 articles not cited in any plan file were re-read from the 2026-09-18 cache. The first pass at this audit had a methodology error worth recording: the cached files are named by Intercom's internal article ID, not the public URL ID that citations use, so a naive diff flagged 164 "uncited" articles when the true figure was 122. Once the internal-to-public mapping was built from each file's own URL header, the audit found that the category reasoning above held for every article except the ones now noted in the General FAQ and Trading Rules bullets. The uncatalogued collection was the substantive miss; it is now fixed in both this ledger and the tree.
