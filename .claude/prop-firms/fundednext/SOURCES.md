# `FundedNext Futures`: Source Ledger

## Discovery completeness

FundedNext Futures has no discoverable help-center sitemap: `helpfutures.fundednext.com/sitemap.xml` returns HTTP 200 but resolves to the firm's main *marketing* site's sitemap-index (`fundednext.com/sitemap_pages.xml`, `sitemap_posts.xml`, etc.), not a help-center article index. Instead, the full article catalogue was enumerated directly from the help center's own Next.js `__NEXT_DATA__` payloads: the home page (`helpfutures.fundednext.com/en`) lists all 14 top-level collections, each collection page's own payload lists its `articleSummaries` and any nested `subcollections` (3 of the 14 collections hold their real content entirely inside `subcollections`, not the top-level list), and this was walked recursively to a verified, reconciled total.

**164 of 164 discovered articles were read in full**, across all 14 collections, confirmed by summing each collection's own stated `articleCount` against the recursively-walked total (164 = 164, exact match, no double-counting). Of those 164, the ones actually cited in this tree's 7 files are listed below (42 unique URLs). The remainder were read but are legitimately out of scope for this tree, not skipped for convenience:

- **Trading Platform FAQ (21 articles)**: Tradovate/NinjaTrader/TradingView login, order-entry, and charting how-tos. No drawdown/eval/payout-rule content.
- **Refer & Earn (27 articles)**, **Ongoing Offers and Updates (3 articles)**, **Affiliate & Partner FAQ (7 articles)**: referral-commission and promotional-discount programs, not trading rules.
- **About FundedNext Futures FAQ (9 articles)**: company background, support hours, Brand Promise, certificates, restricted-country lists, age restriction. General context, no plan-specific rule content beyond what is already cited below.
- **General FAQ (38 articles)**: mostly KYC, payment methods, withdrawal methods, and account-mechanics articles that are either not plan-specific or already covered by a cited firm-wide article below.
- **Bolt Challenge FAQ (4)**, **Rapid Challenge FAQ (4)**: the old Bolt and plain-Rapid Challenges, both discontinued for new purchases and resets effective 10 July 2026. Per this repo's own convention, discontinued plans are not documented with their own file; see `README.md`'s Documentation Scope.
