## Completeness verification (sitemap-based, not just search-driven)

`https://support.lucidtrading.com/sitemap.xml` was fetched directly on 2026-09-18 after the user directly questioned whether this pass's help-center research had been exhaustive. It lists 69 `<url>` entries: 10 collection pages and 59 articles. Every one of the 59 has now been read except "Our Mission" (fetched and read anyway; confirmed pure marketing copy with no rule or figure) and the 11 explicitly out-of-scope articles under LucidBlack (7, "(Legacy)" collection) and LucidMaxx (4, invite-only). This is a materially stronger completeness claim than "we searched for it and didn't find it": every gap listed below is now a confirmed absence from the full article index, not a possible search-methodology miss. Any future re-verification pass for this firm should re-fetch this sitemap and diff it against this table rather than relying on search alone.

## Known gaps needing a specific further source (not fetch failures — the articles below were never located, not blocked)

Two items originally listed here — dedicated LucidPro/LucidDirect Drawdown articles, and a LucidDaily DLL dollar-amount article — were subsequently found via further search and fetched directly; see the source table above (LucidPro Drawdown, LucidDirect Drawdown, LucidDaily Daily Loss Limit). What remains open, now cross-checked against the full sitemap above rather than only against search results:

- **LucidFlex's own DLL dollar-amount article genuinely does not exist in the help center.** The sitemap lists exactly 8 LucidFlex articles (Evaluation, Funded, Payouts, Consistency Percentage, Scaling Plan, Drawdown, Customization, Live Legacy); none is a DLL article, unlike LucidPro, LucidDirect, and LucidDaily, which each have one. This is now a confirmed absence, not a search miss: "LucidFlex Customization" confirms the DLL toggle exists and affects pricing, but no public help-center article anywhere states the dollar figure for any tier. The figure may exist only in-app at checkout, or may genuinely be undocumented; either way, it is not recoverable from this help center as it currently stands.
- **A dedicated LucidMaxx article set**: the sitemap confirms exactly 4 LucidMaxx articles (Overview, Eval Rules, Eval Pricing, Cooldown), matching what search had already found; not fetched or read in this pass, since LucidMaxx was scoped out as a separate, invite-only product (see `README.md`'s Documentation Scope). If LucidMaxx is ever brought into scope, these four are the complete, sitemap-confirmed starting point, not merely a partial list.
- **A dollar-pricing source for any tier other than 50K, across all four plans** — every non-50K price in this tree is Unconfirmed; the engine itself only models 50K for every plan, so there is no engine lead to follow for the other three tiers either. `lucidtrading.com`'s own pricing pages remained Cloudflare-blocked throughout this pass with no usable Wayback fallback (only the homepage itself was recoverable that way). The sitemap covers `support.lucidtrading.com` only, not the separate `lucidtrading.com` marketing/pricing domain, so it cannot close this particular gap.
