# LucidLive

**Sources:** `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/13425130-new-live-structure` ("New Live Structure"), `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/11404729-allowed-trading-times` ("Allowed Trading Times"), `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/15245873-new-live-scaling-plan` ("New Live Scaling Plan"), `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/16010520-luciddaily-live` ("LucidDaily Live"), `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/13432107-lucidpro-live-legacy`, `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/13424914-lucidflex-live-legacy`, `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/13425070-luciddirect-live-legacy` ("Live (Legacy)" articles)

## Overview

LucidLive is Lucid's shared live-account program: LucidPro, LucidFlex, LucidDirect, and LucidDaily funded accounts all transition into it at the discretion of the Lucid risk team, **except that LucidDaily's own Maximum Daily Profit trigger specifically is a real, unresolved conflict between two of Lucid's own dedicated LucidDaily articles over whether that one trigger is automatic or discretionary; see `daily.md`'s own Live Transition section.** It is not purchased directly: there is no evaluation stage or Sim Funded stage of its own; a trader arrives here from one of the four funded-account files in this tree, or directly from a LucidMaxx evaluation (see [lucidmaxx.md](lucidmaxx.md)). This file documents the base structure shared by LucidPro/LucidFlex/LucidDirect. **LucidDaily's transition into this same program differs in several confirmed, material ways** (a different entry trigger, a capped sim-profit payout on transition, different timing, and no Live Bonus eligibility). See `daily.md`'s own Live Transition section for those deviations rather than assuming this file's figures apply unmodified to LucidDaily-sourced live accounts.

## Live Contract Scaling Plan

**Source: "New Live Scaling Plan" (May 26, 2026).** The article states that the scaling plan "controls how much of your max size is available to trade based on the profits in your account," that max tradable contract size "moves up and down in direct correlation at the end of each trading day" with profits, and that limits depend on account size and product ("Certain extremely volatile contracts have adjusted position sizing to ensure traders do not exceed the live drawdown"). It does not define "your max size" and does not mention "New Live Structure"'s flat "Max Live Contract Limit" table, and no other article states how the two relate (see Not Confirmed). Observed against the tables, not stated by any article: the flat figures equal this table's CME top tier at every account size, and the CBOT and NYMEX top tiers at $25K/$50K/$100K, but not the CBOT (8 minis/80 micros) or NYMEX (6 minis/60 micros) top tiers at $150K, and not COMEX at any size (e.g. 5 minis/50 micros at $150,000 vs. the flat 10 minis/100 micros). ## Eligibility and Transition

A trader enters Lucid's "live review pool" when at least one of the following is met, for accounts other than LucidDaily (see `daily.md` for LucidDaily's own different trigger):

- After receiving the final payout (referred to as "Payout 5") on a funded plan,
- After being paid a significant amount of capital over their lifetime with Lucid,
- For exceptional sim-funded performance, or
- If the trader has previously been moved live before.

**Hitting a threshold is not a guarantee.** "Payout 5 represents the maximum payout level, not a guaranteed minimum for live eligibility. All live transitions occur at the discretion of the Lucid risk team" ("New Live Structure"). Neither LucidPro's, LucidDirect's, nor LucidDaily's own dedicated help-center payout articles independently confirm a "Payout 5" ceiling the way LucidFlex's own article does explicitly (see `flex.md`). The only other place the figure appears is the user-pasted homepage `LucidPricingConfig`, whose LucidPro and LucidFlex funded faces carry a meta entry "Payouts to Live: 5" (its LucidDirect and LucidDaily faces carry none); treat the figure as this article's own statement plus that config entry, not independently corroborated by a help-center article for LucidPro, LucidDirect or LucidDaily.

**On transition:** every eligible funded account (at least one payout received) is moved to its own separate live account, not pooled into one shared account. All eligible accounts move together, at once: "Do all funded prop accounts move live at once? Yes. All eligible funded prop accounts (with at least one payout) are moved when a trader transitions to live." Any funded account with zero payouts is closed instead, with its evaluation cost refunded, but only conditionally: "If a funded account is not moved live because it has 0 payouts, the evaluation cost of that account will be refunded, as long as the account has equal to or greater than 50% of the total drawdown remaining." "New Live Structure" carries `dateModified` 2026-09-23T17:00:43Z. **Unresolved conflict, not silently reconciled:** LucidDaily's own dedicated live-transition article ("LucidDaily Live," `dateModified` 2026-07-27T02:38:51Z) states the identical 0-payout refund with no drawdown-remaining condition at all: "If a funded account is not moved live because it has 0 payouts, the evaluation cost of that account will be refunded." Do not assume either the unconditional or the 50%-conditional wording governs a LucidDaily-sourced transition; see `daily.md`'s own Live Transition section, which now carries this specific conflict too, for the LucidDaily-specific caveat.

No firm page publishes a dollar or cumulative-profit figure for the "paid out a significant amount of capital lifetime" trigger; see Not Confirmed below.

## Live Bonus

The first time a trader is moved live under this program (and only if they have never held a "Legacy Live" account), they may earn a one-time bonus, itself "subject to a 90/10 profit split."

| Funded Account Size | Live Target (profit needed) | Live Bonus (gross, before split) |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| $25,000             | $1,100                      | $1,000                           |
| $50,000             | $2,100                      | $2,000                           |
| $100,000            | $3,100                      | $3,000                           |
| $150,000            | $4,600                      | $4,500                           |

To earn it: the source's own instructional line states finishing a trading session "with live profits equal to or above your starting live drawdown," i.e. the Drawdown Amount row above ($1,000/$2,000/$3,000/$4,500 by tier). **The same article's own Live Target column and its own worked example both use a different, higher figure per tier instead** (e.g. $2,100, not $2,000, at the 50K tier), a real inconsistency inside this one article, not two different articles disagreeing, and not reconciled here; see Not Confirmed below. The bonus is paid per account, so a trader moved live with multiple accounts earns it on each one that hits its own target. Worked example from the source itself: five $50,000 accounts moved live together, each finishing a session at $2,100 in live profit, earns a $2,000 gross bonus per account: $10,000 total gross, or $9,000 to the trader after the 90/10 split.

**The bonus applies only to the first live transition.** Traders moved live a second or later time are not eligible for the bonus on that subsequent set of accounts. "The bonus is not eligible for traders with LucidMaxx status."

## Cooldown After a Live Breach

Standard cooldown after blowing a live account: 2 weeks ("Standard cooldown period: 2 weeks"). The same "New Live Structure" section also says "Traders who have been moved live may return to purchase a new evaluation after blowing a live account"; it does not tie the 2 weeks to that purchase, so whether the evaluation can be bought only after the cooldown ends is not stated. Traders who repeatedly blow live accounts through reckless behavior may face a longer cooldown at the risk team's discretion. No specific extended duration is stated.

## Other Confirmed Rules

- **Household exclusivity:** if one household member is trading live, no other household member may trade a simulated (evaluation or Sim Funded) account: "If I am live, can someone in my household still trade sim? No."
- **Hedging live accounts is absolutely prohibited**, by both Lucid and CME rules, with violations resulting in a permanent ban.
- **LucidMaxx status** is a separate tier ("Select traders may eventually earn LucidMaxx status"); such traders "May purchase the LucidMaxx evaluation" and "Receive access to daily, uncapped payouts and instant live capital." This file's own source defers full detail to "the LucidMaxx documentation"; see [lucidmaxx.md](lucidmaxx.md). The Live Bonus described above is not available to traders with LucidMaxx status.
- **A "Legacy" live-transition structure exists for LucidPro/LucidFlex/LucidDirect**, per each plan's own "Live (Legacy)" article, each opening with "This is for accounts purchased/reset on 2/27/26 and prior." The legacy structure differs materially: transition after the sixth and final payout of that plan (or earlier at the risk team's discretion), not the fifth; a per-tier "Max Moved Live" cap on how much simulated profit converts to live capital; and a Day-1 deposit with the rest held in Escrow, released only after 10 profitable live days and $10,000 of live profit, then reviewed weekly with $5,000 released per additional $10,000 earned, and withdrawable only 60 days after account opening. Each article's own "Legacy ... Transition to Live" subsection states a narrower cutoff and says those traders "may choose between the below legacy structure or the and above new structure": accounts purchased or reset on 1/19/26 or earlier (LucidPro), and traders mid onboarding or sent a move-to-live email on 1/31 or earlier (LucidFlex, LucidDirect). The articles do not say how an account purchased or reset between those dates and 2/27/26 is treated. Not built out as its own section; see each article.

**Every simulated account closes on transition ("New Live Structure" FAQ).** "All simulated prop accounts are closed when a trader is moved live. If a funded account is not moved live because it has 0 payouts, the evaluation cost of that account will be refunded, as long as the account has equal to or greater than 50% of the total drawdown remaining." The same FAQ answer ends: "If you are not comfortable with evaluations being closed, do not keep evaluations in reserve." See Eligibility and Transition above for the refund sentence's unresolved conflict with LucidDaily's own unconditional refund wording.

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Live Bonus trigger figure** — the source's own instructional sentence ("with live profits equal to or above your starting live drawdown") names the Starting Live Drawdown figure (e.g. $2,000 at the 50K tier), but the same article's own Live Target column and worked example both use a higher figure instead (e.g. $2,100 at 50K). This is a genuine inconsistency inside a single article, not a conflict between two sources. Do not assume either figure alone is the controlling trigger; this file's table above reports the Live Target/worked-example figure since it is the more specific, directly-tabulated value, but the instructional sentence's own different figure is not thereby resolved.
- **Profit split on ordinary live trading profit** — "New Live Structure" states 90/10 only in the Live Bonus context ("The Live Bonus is subject to a 90/10 profit split", "after the 90/10 profit split"). No cited source states the split rate that applies to ordinary, non-bonus profit earned on a live account. Do not assume the Live Bonus's 90/10 rate is the general live split without a source that says so.
- **Exact extended-cooldown duration for repeat/reckless live breaches** — the source states this is discretionary and can exceed the standard 2 weeks, but gives no specific figure or formula. Do not assume any particular extended-cooldown length.
- **Significant lifetime payout amount (the "After being paid out a significant amount of capital lifetime" live-transition trigger, verbatim from both "New Live Structure" and "LucidDaily Live")** — no firm page publishes a dollar or cumulative-profit figure for it, across the entire help center. All 59 articles listed in `support.lucidtrading.com/sitemap.xml` (69 URLs) were fetched on 2026-10-02 and their text searched; none states a specific dollar threshold for this condition; "New Live Structure" and "LucidDaily Live" are the only two articles that mention it at all, and both use the same unquantified "significant amount" language. The main site's Terms of Use returned HTTP 403 on 2026-10-02 and was not checked. Do not assume any specific dollar figure (or that one is documented anywhere) governs this trigger, for any plan.
- **Live Bonus eligibility by source plan** — "New Live Structure" states the Live Bonus for a trader moved live "under the new program" and names no plan as eligible. Exclusions are stated for LucidMaxx status ("New Live Structure"), for LucidDaily ("LucidDaily Live": "There is no live bonus in for traders that are moved live from a LucidDaily account.") and, for LucidDirect accounts "purchased as of 1/18 at 3 PM EST," in "LucidDirect Live (Legacy)" (see `direct.md`). No article names LucidPro or LucidFlex as eligible. Do not assume a LucidPro or LucidFlex account is explicitly confirmed eligible, or that the LucidDirect exclusion is limited to pre-2/27/26 accounts.
- **Whether "Payout 5" is a literal, confirmed cap for LucidPro/LucidDirect specifically** — this file's own source states the "Payout 5" trigger firm-wide, but only LucidFlex's own dedicated payout article independently confirms a 5-payout structure for its own plan. LucidPro's and LucidDirect's own help-center payout articles do not state this cap themselves, see each plan's own Not Confirmed section. The pasted homepage `LucidPricingConfig` lists "Payouts to Live: 5" on LucidPro's funded face (and LucidFlex's) and nothing of the kind for LucidDirect; it does not say whether 5 is a cap on total payouts. Do not assume the "Payout 5" trigger is confirmed for LucidPro or LucidDirect specifically, and do not assume the config entry is absent, wrong, or a lifetime cap.
- **Initial live Max Loss Limit level and live breach rule** — "New Live Structure" states an EOD drawdown that trails with the account's highest closing balance "up to a defined point," the "Starting Live Drawdown" per size, a $0 starting balance and a $100 lock, but never states the level the live MLL starts at (for example whether it starts at $0 or at $0 minus the Starting Live Drawdown) and never states that a live balance reaching the MLL breaches the account; the sim drawdown articles state that breach rule for their own stages ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached"). The Cooldown section only presupposes that a live account can be "blown." Do not assume a starting MLL figure, and do not assume the sim breach rule's wording or consequences carry over to live accounts.
- **Live Max Contracts: how the flat table and the scaling ramp relate** — "New Live Structure" states one flat figure per account size ("Max Live Contract Limit", 2 minis/20 micros at $25,000 up to 10 minis/100 micros at $150,000) and "New Live Scaling Plan" (which says the plan "controls how much of your max size is available to trade") states a ramp by live profit and exchange group. No article defines "your max size", or says whether the flat figure is the ceiling the ramp reaches, a competing rule, or a rule that has since been replaced. The flat figure matches the ramp's top tier for CME at every size and for CBOT/NYMEX below $150K, and not for COMEX at any size; that match is an observation about the numbers, not a statement by either article. Do not assume the flat table is the limit a live account starts with, that it supersedes the ramp, or that the ramp supersedes it.
- **LucidMaxx's own full rule set** — out of scope for this file (pricing, eligibility criteria beyond "select traders," and its "daily uncapped payouts" mechanics); now documented in [lucidmaxx.md](lucidmaxx.md) from four dedicated LucidMaxx articles. Do not assume this file is a source for any LucidMaxx figure.
- **Whether the auto-liquidate risk-control option is on by default or must be actively enabled** — "Allowed Trading Times" describes it as available and configured during onboarding, but doesn't state a default state. Do not assume the auto-liquidate option is enabled by default.
