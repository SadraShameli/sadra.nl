## Overview

LucidDaily is offered in four sizes ($25K/$50K/$100K/$150K) with **two independent purchasable toggles at checkout**, confirmed explicitly by "LucidDaily Customization": Daily Loss Limit (ON/OFF) and Evaluation Drawdown Type (End-of-Day/Intraday): "These settings create four unique LucidDaily account configurations." **Critically, the Evaluation Drawdown toggle affects the evaluation stage only**: "Regardless of your selection, all LucidDaily Funded accounts use an Intraday Drawdown" ("LucidDaily Customization"), restated in different words by "LucidDaily Drawdown": "The LucidDaily funded accounts always use an Intraday drawdown.". This was previously a genuine, confirmed disagreement with this repo's own engine: `buildDailyPlan` (`LucidTrading.ts`) left `fundedDrawdown` unset for the EOD-selected variants (`LucidVariant.DailyEod`/`DailyEodDll`), silently falling back to the eval-stage `EodTrailingDrawdown` for the funded stage too. Corrected 2026-09-20: `fundedDrawdown` is now set unconditionally to `IntradayTrailingDrawdown` for every LucidDaily variant, matching this always-Intraday-funded rule.

Objectives update in real time: "Objectives on our dashboard update in real-time within 5-30 minutes of your last closed trade" ("LucidDaily Funded Account Benefits").

This file therefore documents LucidDaily as one plan family with a 2×2 configuration matrix, rather than splitting into separate EOD/Intraday files: since the funded stage, payouts, and live-transition mechanics are all identical regardless of which evaluation-drawdown option was purchased, a file split would duplicate nearly everything and differ only in the Evaluation-stage Drawdown Type row and the evaluation and reset price rows.

LucidDaily's other defining features: no consistency rule on the funded stage ("There is no Consistency Percentage on LucidDaily funded accounts"), a hard-breach restriction on trading high-impact USD news, and a "Maximum Daily Profit" mechanic that "LucidDaily Payouts" contrasts with the per-request payout cap structure of "our other plans": instead of a cap per request, "there is a maximum amount of sim profit that can be earned in a day." **Whether hitting that amount moves the trader live automatically or at discretion is itself a real, unresolved conflict between two of LucidDaily's own dedicated articles**, not settled fact; see Live Transition below rather than reading "automatically triggers" as confirmed.

## How the Drawdown Works

The evaluation stage uses whichever drawdown type was purchased (End-of-Day or Intraday); the funded stage always uses Intraday regardless. "LucidDaily Drawdown" states: "Intraday drawdown brings your MLL up with your real-time P&L," "EOD drawdown brings your MLL up based on your P&L at the end of a session," "The MLL increases up to a defined point," and "Once your account exceeds the Initial Trail Balance, the MLL locks and no longer moves," at the initial balance plus $100.

**LucidDaily's DLL, where enabled, is stated to stay fixed on the funded stage**: "LucidDaily Funded accounts use the same Fixed DLL values used in the evaluation" ("LucidDaily Daily Loss Limit"), and the Evaluation DLL "does not change over time." By contrast, "LucidPro Daily Loss Limit" and "LucidDirect Daily Loss Limit" state their Fixed DLL is replaced by a LucidScale DLL once the account closes above the Initial Trail Balance. The LucidDaily article describes no scaling mechanism.

The same source also clarifies the DLL-vs-MLL interaction precisely: "The DLL remains fixed throughout the trading session and does not trail upward with the Max Loss Limit (MLL). If the MLL trails above the DLL during the session, the MLL becomes the more restrictive threshold. Reaching the MLL at that point will result in a hard breach"; the article calls reaching the MLL in that case a hard breach.

Reaching the (trailing or locked) MLL is a breach ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached"); whether a breached LucidDaily Sim Funded account can be reset is Unconfirmed: the cited "Inactivity Policy" states, with no evaluation qualifier and naming no plan or stage, "All breached accounts will be automatically deleted after 30 days if not reset. Traders can manually purchase a reset at any time before that 30-day period expires." (see Not Confirmed). A DLL hit (where enabled) is a soft breach, pausing trading only until the next session, unless superseded by the MLL first, per the paragraph above.

### Worked Example

This example uses the nominal tier-size convention of "LucidDaily Drawdown"'s own table for the $50,000 tier, funded stage (always Intraday regardless of which evaluation configuration was purchased), tracked throughout. The funded Starting Balance is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed), so the $50,000 opening is that convention, not a stated Starting Balance.

1. The funded account opens at $50,000. The $2,000 Max Loss Limit is taken here to set an initial floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000 (derived: no article states the starting MLL level).
2. Because the funded stage is Intraday, the MLL is measured continuously, not just at session close. Mid-session, the account's real-time P&L reaches a new peak of $51,600 (still below the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance). The MLL is taken to trail up, in real time, to $51,600 − $2,000 = $49,600 (derived: no article states a peak-minus-MLL-amount trailing formula, only that "The MLL increases up to a defined point").
3. The account reaches a new peak of $52,200, above the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance. The MLL locks at $50,100 ("Locked MLL Balance" in "LucidDaily Drawdown"'s own table) and stops trailing. The article does not state whether the lock is checked intraday or only at a session close (see Not Confirmed); this step assumes the account is above the Initial Trail Balance at the check.
4. The account later reaches $55,000 (a new peak). The MLL does not move again: it stays fixed at $50,100.
5. If the account's balance ever fell to $50,100 or below, it would reach the locked MLL, which the article states breaches the account ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached"); what happens to a breached Sim Funded account afterward is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed). Because the funded stage is Intraday, the MLL tracks real-time P&L.

## Payouts

Instead, there is a maximum amount of sim profit that can be earned in a day." **Maximum Daily Profit** (a distinctive LucidDaily-only mechanic): $6,000 (25K) / $8,000 (50K) / $10,000 (100K) / $12,000 (150K). "If you meet or exceed the daily amount you are automatically moved live" ("LucidDaily Payouts"). See Live Transition below for how this differs from the payout-count-based trigger of LucidPro, LucidFlex and LucidDirect, and for a real, unresolved conflict between this article's "automatically" and "LucidDaily Live"'s own "at the discretion of the Lucid risk team" for the identical trigger.

Payouts are deducted within a few minutes of approval; funds are disbursed within 2 business days.

**Payout requests cannot be undone, and can be self-sabotaged.** "Payout requests are final once submitted, they cannot be edited or canceled." Trading while one is pending is allowed but risky: "Important: If you take a trade before your payout is processed that drops your balance into the buffer, your request may be denied." The qualifying balance has to survive until the request is processed, not just until it is submitted. Payout eligibility itself also requires rule compliance: "You must not violate any Trading Rules, Terms & Conditions, or the LucidDaily Account Agreement."

**Configuration is locked at purchase.** "Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidDaily account with your preferred options." The DLL and drawdown toggles documented above are therefore a one-time choice, not a setting.

## Live Transition

LucidDaily's live-transition mechanics differ materially from LucidPro/LucidFlex/LucidDirect's shared structure, documented in `live.md`: this section states LucidDaily's own confirmed deviations rather than deferring entirely to that shared file.

**Trigger:** a trader enters the live review pool upon hitting the tier's Maximum Daily Profit in a single day (in place of the "final payout" trigger used by the other three plans), after being paid a significant lifetime amount, for exceptional sim-funded performance, or if previously moved live before. "LucidDaily Live" states "All live transitions occur at the discretion of the Lucid risk team." For the Maximum Daily Profit trigger specifically, that discretionary framing is now one half of an unresolved conflict rather than settled fact, since "LucidDaily Payouts" describes the identical trigger as automatic (see the Conflict paragraph directly below); do not read this paragraph as having already picked "discretionary" for that trigger. For the other three conditions (significant lifetime payout amount, exceptional sim-funded performance, and prior live history), no other source contradicts the discretionary framing, including for a trader who has previously been moved live, who is not guaranteed automatic re-entry by that history alone.

**Conflict, not silently reconciled: "automatically moved live" vs. "at the discretion of the Lucid risk team."** "LucidDaily Payouts"' own Maximum Daily Profit rule (see Payouts above) states "If you meet or exceed the daily amount you are automatically moved live," while this file's own "LucidDaily Live" source states, of the identical trigger, that "All live transitions occur at the discretion of the Lucid risk team." Both quotes come from Lucid's own dedicated LucidDaily articles; this file does not pick one over the other. Re-checked against fresh fetches of both articles on 2026-10-02: both quotes read exactly as above. Do not assume hitting the Maximum Daily Profit figure guarantees an automatic transition, and do not assume it is purely discretionary either.

**Sim profit handling on transition (LucidDaily-specific, differs from the other plans per "LucidDaily Live"):** the funded account's buffer balance is used to fund the starting live drawdown and may not be withdrawn. Sim profit _above_ the buffer may be paid out once KYC with the broker partner is complete, but is capped at a flat **$15,000 total**: "Whether you have one 25k account or five 150k accounts, the maximum sim profit paid out on the move to live is $15,000" ("LucidDaily Live"). This flat, non-scaling cap has no equivalent in the base `live.md` article for LucidPro/LucidFlex/LucidDirect, and "Any profits in excess of the capped amount may not be withdrawn."

**Sim account closure and the 0-payout refund (conflicts with `live.md`'s own source on the refund's condition):** "All simulated prop accounts are closed when a trader is moved live. If a funded account is not moved live because it has 0 payouts, the evaluation cost of that account will be refunded. If you are not comfortable with evaluations being closed, do not keep evaluations in reserve." ("LucidDaily Live"). **Conflict, not silently reconciled:** `live.md`'s own source ("New Live Structure," re-fetched 2026-10-02) states the identical refund with a trailing condition this article does not carry: "...the evaluation cost of that account will be refunded, as long as the account has equal to or greater than 50% of the total drawdown remaining." Both are dedicated primary-source articles, each Lucid's own; this file does not pick one over the other. Re-fetched both articles 2026-10-02: "LucidDaily Live" `dateModified` 2026-07-27T02:38:51Z, "New Live Structure" `dateModified` 2026-09-23T17:00:43Z. Do not assume either the unconditional or the 50%-conditional wording governs a LucidDaily-sourced transition.

**Timing:** traders are moved live every evening after the 6:00 PM EST session report, a process that "can take several hours." Positions are flattened and accounts paused at that point; if moved live mid-position, the prior day's closing balance (not the current session's P&L) is used as the amount transitioned, "This ensures every trader is treated equally." ("LucidDaily Live").

**No Live Bonus:** "There is no live bonus in for traders that are moved live from a LucidDaily account." (the "in" is the source’s own typo) ("LucidDaily Live"), unlike the base structure in `live.md`, whose Live Bonus is stated without naming a plan (see `live.md`'s Not Confirmed).

Every other live-transition mechanic (one live account per eligible funded account, $0 starting live balance, EOD live drawdown, no live DLL, no live consistency rule, 2-week standard cooldown after a live breach, LucidMaxx status) is identical to the base structure in `live.md`, except for the 0-payout refund condition addressed just above, where the two articles conflict on wording.
