## Overview

Lucid Trading documents four active plan families in this tree: **LucidPro**, **LucidFlex**, **LucidDirect**, and **LucidDaily**. LucidPro and LucidFlex both run a traditional Evaluation → Sim Funded progression with a purchasable Daily Loss Limit (DLL) toggle at checkout. LucidDirect skips evaluation entirely (instant funding), with a fixed DLL at $50K+ and no toggle. LucidDaily has two independent toggles (DLL on/off, and an evaluation-stage-only drawdown-type choice between End-of-Day and Intraday) producing four purchasable configurations, documented together in one file rather than split, since the funded stage is identical — always Intraday — regardless of which evaluation drawdown type was chosen. All four converge on the same live-account stage, **LucidLive**, at the firm's sole discretion.

A fifth product, **LucidBlack**, is explicitly labeled "(Legacy)" in Lucid's own help-center collection structure and is not documented here — its own status was independently re-confirmed via page metadata during a 2026-09-19 re-audit, not just the collection label. A sixth product, **LucidMaxx**, is a repeatable eval-to-live cycle reserved for traders who have already earned "LucidMaxx status" through their track record elsewhere at Lucid; once earned, it functions as an ordinary self-serve, dynamically-priced evaluation program with daily uncapped live payouts. Documented in its own file, **[LucidMaxx](lucidmaxx.md)**. See Documentation Scope.

## Plans

- **[LucidPro](pro.md)** — $25K/$50K/$100K/$150K, optional DLL toggle, funded-stage DLL scales to 60% of peak profit once locked, 40% payout-cycle consistency, no funded contract scaling, 90/10 split.
- **[LucidFlex](flex.md)** — same four sizes, optional DLL toggle (dollar amount unconfirmed), no consistency once funded, no payout buffer, funded-stage contract scaling tied to simulated profit, capped at 5 lifetime payouts before live transition, 90/10 split.
- **[LucidDirect](direct.md)** — instant funding, no evaluation, fixed DLL at $50K+ only (none at $25K), 100K/150K drawdown amounts genuinely differ from LucidPro/LucidFlex's own 100K/150K figures, 20% payout-cycle consistency, 90/10 split.
- **[LucidDaily](daily.md)** — four purchasable configurations (DLL × evaluation drawdown type), funded stage always Intraday regardless of configuration, no consistency once funded, hard-breach high-impact-USD-news restriction (unique to this plan), no per-request payout cap — instead a same-day "Maximum Daily Profit" ceiling that auto-triggers live review, 90/10 split.
- **[LucidMaxx](lucidmaxx.md)** — access gated on already-earned "LucidMaxx status," not purchasable directly. $25K/$50K/$100K/$150K, 40% eval consistency, 5-day minimum, dynamic 4-tier pricing keyed to a trader's own prior blown-live-account count, direct-to-live on passing (no Sim Funded stage), daily uncapped payouts from the first payout, 90/10 split, 2-week cooldown after a blown live account.

## Live Accounts

**[LucidLive](live.md)** is the firm's shared live-account stage — every funded account across all four plans transitions into it at Lucid's sole discretion; it is not purchased directly. All live accounts start at $0, use an End-of-Day drawdown that locks at a flat $100 once the account's live profit reaches its own Starting Live Drawdown, carry no Daily Loss Limit and no consistency rule, and are eligible (except when sourced from LucidDaily, or when the trader holds LucidMaxx status) for a one-time Live Bonus on their first transition. Unlike every Sim Funded stage in this tree, live-account contract limits are not flat per tier: they scale with live profit and differ by exchange group (CME/CBOT/NYMEX vs. the more conservative COMEX), per `live.md`'s own Live Contract Scaling Plan section, a genuine correction found only via the sitemap sweep noted above. LucidDaily's own transition into this same program differs materially — a different entry trigger, a $15,000 flat cap on transferable sim profit, different transition timing, and no Live Bonus — documented in `daily.md`'s own Live Transition section rather than in `live.md`.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules stated by their own source as applying broadly across account types — not inferred by checking each plan's file individually.

- **Maximum 5 active funded accounts per household, shared across every plan type combined** — per "Maximum Number of Accounts," which gives its own worked example naming two different plans explicitly: "If you have 3 LucidDirect accounts, you may only maintain 2 LucidPro funded accounts." The clearest, least ambiguous shared-account-pool statement found anywhere in this doc tree across any firm researched this session — no plan-specific figure in this tree contradicts it. The same article also confirms up to 10 active evaluation accounts per household (shared with the funded-account count: "If you have 5 evaluation accounts, you may only hold 5 funded accounts"), and up to 5 active LucidLive accounts.
- **90% trader / 10% Lucid Trading default profit split, on every funded and live account** — independently stated in identical wording across every plan's own payout article (LucidPro, LucidFlex, LucidDirect, LucidDaily, and `live.md`'s own Live Bonus section) — the most consistently and repeatedly corroborated figure in this tree.
- **30-calendar-day inactivity closure, evaluation and funded stages** — per "Inactivity Policy": "Accounts that have not been traded resulting in at least a net profit or loss of $1 within 30 calendar days will be deemed abandoned and will be permanently deleted." Stated under a general "Active Status Policy" heading with no plan-type qualifier, and independently consistent with the confirmed 30-day figure in pro.md, flex.md, direct.md and daily.md; lucidmaxx.md marks its own Inactivity Rule Unconfirmed, so this is not corroborated there. Note this is separate from a breached (not merely inactive) evaluation account's own 30-day auto-deletion-if-not-reset window, described in the same article's "Breached Status Policy" section.
- **A breached account auto-deletes after 30 days unless reset**: "All breached accounts will be automatically deleted after 30 days if not reset. Traders can manually purchase a reset at any time before that 30-day period expires." Distinct from the 30-day inactivity closure above: this applies to an account that has already been breached, not merely idle.

- **All accounts are one-time purchases; no monthly subscription billing** — per "Simulated Account Fees": "There is no subscription and no automatic rebilling," independently corroborated by the homepage's own FAQ: "All of our accounts are a one-time fee, we do not have monthly subscriptions for trading accounts."
- **No activation fee to upgrade a passed evaluation to a funded account** — independently stated in LucidPro's and LucidFlex's own evaluation articles, and in "Simulated Account Fees" generically.
- **Real-time account-objective updates, and 5–30 minute funded-account activation after passing** — stated near-identically across LucidPro's, LucidFlex's, LucidDirect's, and LucidDaily's own articles ("Objectives on our dashboard update in real-time within 5-30 minutes of your last closed trade"); the homepage independently confirms the same 5–30 minute window for evaluation-to-funded activation, and a separate 5–15 minute window for LucidDirect's own purchase-to-trading activation.
- **Drawdown-lock mechanism: every Sim Funded account locks its Max Loss Limit once the balance exceeds starting balance + drawdown amount + $100, measured at session close on the EOD-drawdown plans (LucidPro, LucidFlex, LucidDirect) and continuously on LucidDaily, whose funded stage is always Intraday, after which the floor is fixed at starting balance + $100 and never moves again** — directly confirmed, with matching dollar tables including the locked value, in all four plans' own dedicated drawdown articles (LucidPro Drawdown, LucidFlex Drawdown, LucidDirect Drawdown, LucidDaily Drawdown — the first and third of these were located after an earlier pass had already drafted this tree using an inference-by-analogy for LucidPro/LucidDirect specifically; both are now directly confirmed, not inferred). The same $100-offset formula is independently confirmed for LucidLive itself (locking at a flat $100 against a $0 starting balance).
- **Trading hours: all non-LucidLive accounts must be flat by 4:45 PM EST Monday–Friday; market reopens 6:00 PM EST Sunday–Thursday** — per "Allowed Trading Times." Holding past 4:45 PM does not itself fail an account (positions are auto-closed), but market-holiday closes override the usual cutoff. LucidLive accounts have their own, different close times (4:45 PM EST Tradovate / 4:15 PM EST Rithmic) and a no-swing-trading rule not stated for any Sim Funded stage.
- **Hedging live accounts is prohibited firm-wide and by CME rule**, resulting in a permanent ban — confirmed in `live.md`'s own FAQ section.
- **News trading is allowed on LucidPro, LucidFlex, and LucidDirect; not allowed on LucidDaily (hard breach)**, per "Other Trading Activities": "Allowed on Flex, Pro and Direct" and, as its own sub-bullet, "Traders may enter or exit positions around scheduled or unscheduled news events on these plans without it being a breach" vs. "Not allowed on Daily / Trading red folder news on the LucidDaily is a hard breach." The article states this at the plan level, not per-stage; see each plan's own file (Evaluation and Sim Funded tables) for how this resolves stage by stage.
- **Hedging across simulated accounts is strictly prohibited in all forms**, per "Prohibited: Hedging": no same-user cross-account hedging, no cross-user hedging, no cross-firm hedging, and no other risk-bypass hedging. Concretely: going long and short the identical contract at the same size (e.g. two ES minis) is "strictly prohibited" both across accounts and within a single account. The mini-versus-micro variant of the same contract is the one case permitted within a single account ("You can go long / short the same contract but one is micros and one is minis in the same account"), and still prohibited across separate accounts. Going long one instrument while shorting a correlated one (e.g. long ES, short NQ) is prohibited across separate accounts but permitted within a single account. First flag: email notice plus a reset of the involved accounts to the prior day's balance. Repeated offenses: all involved accounts breached and the trader permanently restricted. (Distinct from the live-account hedging ban above, which is CME-rule-based and results in an immediate permanent ban with no warning step.)
- **Microscalping is prohibited**, defined as capturing small price movements with outsized size in extremely short holding windows (a few seconds), aimed at exploiting simulated fill behavior rather than genuine market activity; distinct from genuine scalping, which is explicitly permitted. Flagged automatically if more than 50% of an account's profit comes from trades held 5 seconds or less ("Prohibited: Microscalping"). Enforcement: manual review, then a written warning if bad faith is confirmed, then forfeiture of the flagged profits and a permanent restriction if the behavior continues after the warning. Appeal available.
- **High-frequency trading is prohibited**, defined as submitting a high volume of trades within seconds or milliseconds via algorithmic strategies exploiting price inefficiencies at speed ("Prohibited: High Frequency Trading"). First offense: written warning. Repeated offenses: HFT profits removed, accounts closed, trader permanently restricted. Stated rationale is platform-infrastructure load, not a trading-style objection. Appeal available.
- **Scaling into trades (DCA), genuine scalping, automated strategies (bots/copiers), and "flipping" (fast in-and-out trades to satisfy minimum-day requirements) are all explicitly permitted**, per "Other Trading Activities." Martingaling is discouraged, not banned. Traders remain responsible for losses caused by their own automated-strategy errors.
- **Restricted countries**: traders who are citizens or residents of roughly 80 listed countries are ineligible for any Lucid Trading service ("Restricted Countries"). The list separately names Crimea, Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk, and Sevastopol alongside Ukraine itself, a genuine redundancy present in Lucid's own source, not a transcription error carried over from a third-party document. See `SOURCES.md` for the fetch record rather than duplicating roughly 80 country names here.
- **General fair-use / anti-exploitation standard**, per "Trade with Integrity": Lucid prohibits "taking advantage of system errors or update delays," "exploiting discrepancies in simulated prices," and "tactics that do not reflect real-world trading conditions." Stated as a broad philosophy rather than a specific enforcement ladder with numeric thresholds, unlike the Hedging/Microscalping/HFT rules above, which are this same general standard's concretely-defined special cases.
- **Supported platforms split by data-feed provider**: CQG-routed accounts support NinjaTrader, Tradovate, and TradingView; Rithmic-routed accounts support MotiveWave, Quantower, Tradesea, Sierra Chart, Jigsaw, Bookmap, ATAS, R\|Trader Pro, and MultiCharts ("Lucid Trading Supported Platforms"). "All listed platforms work with all Lucid account types." This is the same CQG/Rithmic split already reflected in `live.md`'s different live-account session-close times (4:45 PM EST Tradovate vs. 4:15 PM EST Rithmic); this article is the first source in this tree naming the platforms behind each feed explicitly.
- **Payout delivery methods**: Plaid (U.S. traders, instant bank transfer), WorkMarket by ADP (U.S. and international, bank transfer or PayPal, "as early as the next business day"), and crypto (international, BTC/ETH/LTC/USDT/USDC) ("Payout Methods"). This article's own meta-description states payouts "averaging about 15 minutes once approved," which is faster than, but not necessarily in conflict with, the "within 2 business days" disbursement figure already confirmed in each plan's own Payouts section; read the former as Plaid's typical case and the latter as the stated outer bound across all methods, not as two sources disagreeing on the same number.
- **Card payment methods for account purchases**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club, and Maestro ("Accepted Payments Methods"). "Transactions are confirmed immediately upon approval."
- **Business-entity registration**: an account may be registered as personal or business at signup, but never converted between the two afterward, and a trader may hold only one Lucid profile ever, personal or business, not both ("Registering as a Business"). Business payouts require submitting incorporation, ownership, and 25%-plus-owner ID documents before either WorkMarket or Plaid payouts are enabled for that account.
- **Refunds: no refund once an account has been traded, at all, regardless of results.** "The Company maintains a strict no refund policy for accounts that have been traded, regardless of the number of trades placed, trading results, or any technical issues experienced by the Client. By initiating any trading activity, whether simulated or otherwise, the Client expressly waives any right to a refund." An untraded account may be refunded, but only "in its sole discretion," and "the determination of eligibility and approval of such refunds shall rest exclusively with the Company." Refund eligibility never applies to "account violations, disqualifications, or breaches of the Terms of Use." Per "Disclaimer of Liability, Refunds and Chargeback Policy" (main site, previously unread by this tree), which was not cited anywhere in this doc tree before this pass. Lucid "considers services rendered in full upon granting account access," regardless of whether the trader actually uses the account.
- **Chargebacks are a permanently-banning offense, not merely discouraged.** "Clients who initiate a fraudulent or improper chargeback shall be permanently banned from all current and future access to Lucid Trading's services and platforms." By purchasing, a trader "expressly agrees not to initiate a chargeback or payment dispute" for any properly-processed transaction; the Company additionally reserves the right to pursue recovery of the disputed funds plus attorneys' fees.
- **Isolated technical issues (data-feed errors, trade-copier malfunctions, third-party app errors, the trader's own connectivity problems, ordinary slippage) are never grounds for a refund, credit, or compensation**, and Lucid's total liability for any such issue is capped at "the greater of one hundred dollars ($100.00) or the amount paid by the Client to the Company in the immediately preceding month." Per the same "Disclaimer of Liability, Refunds and Chargeback Policy."
- **A real, unresolved tension on geographic eligibility, not silently reconciled.** The Terms of Use state Lucid's Website is provided "for use only by persons located in the United States" and that Lucid "make[s] no claims that the Website or any of its content is accessible or appropriate outside of the United States." This sits in real tension with the Restricted Countries rule above, whose ~80-country exclusion list implies by construction that most non-US countries are otherwise permitted. Both are genuinely Lucid's own current sources; per this repo's standing rule to surface conflicts rather than blend them, this is flagged, not resolved, here. Do not assume international traders are definitively eligible or definitively ineligible on the strength of either source alone.
- **Account eligibility exclusions**: a trader must be "18 years of age or older," and is ineligible to use Lucid's Website/Software/Services at all if they are "a convicted felon, have been disciplined by the National Futures Association, have been disciplined by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission, or have an outstanding balance with a trading firm." Per the Terms of Use.
- **Account/performance data cannot be transferred or combined between products or users**: "You may not transfer or combine your account's performance, service parameters, data, or any other information between products or with any other user." Per the Terms of Use.
- **Disputes may be routed to binding arbitration at Lucid's sole discretion**: "At Company's sole discretion, it may require you to submit any disputes arising from these Terms of Use or use of the Website... to final and binding arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the American Arbitration Association applying Delaware law." Per the Terms of Use.

### Profiles, business accounts, and account limits

- **One permanent profile per trader**: "A trader may only ever have one Lucid Trading profile, either personal or business. Profiles cannot be converted, deactivated, or replaced to create a new profile. Once a profile is created, it remains the trader's permanent profile with Lucid Trading."

- **Reserve accounts sit alongside the funded cap**: "Reserve Limit: You may keep 5 evaluation accounts in reserve while you have 5 funded accounts", and "You may trade evaluation accounts while they are in reserve."
- **A reset is a full restoration**: "A reset returns the account to its original starting conditions, including balance and risk parameters."
- **The restricted-country list names occupied regions separately**: Crimea, Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Sevastopol are listed alongside Ukraine rather than folded into it.
- **Correlated-asset groupings are by sector**: "Correlated assets go for groupings in equities, metals, energies, etc."

## Documentation Scope

- **LucidBlack** — explicitly labeled "(Legacy)" in Lucid's own help-center collection index. During a 2026-09-19 re-audit prompted by the user directly asking whether every sitemap article had genuinely been read, all 7 LucidBlack articles were independently re-fetched and read in full (not just the collection label) to check this exclusion against actual content, not assumed from a title. Legacy status held up: each article's own page metadata and breadcrumb name the parent collection "LucidBlack (Legacy)," and all 7 share a within-47-second edit timestamp consistent with an administrative reclassification — though the visible body prose itself reads in ordinary active-product style with no explicit deprecation language, so a reader seeing only the rendered page would not detect deprecated status from the prose alone. Still not documented in this tree, consistent with how this doc tree treats other firms' discontinued/legacy plans (e.g. Tradeify's Advanced Challenge).
- **LucidMaxx** — now documented in its own file, `lucidmaxx.md`, built during the same 2026-09-19 re-audit after all 4 of its articles were independently re-fetched and read in full. The invite-only framing in this file's earlier drafts was accurate but incomplete: it is invite-only for _initial_ entry only ("LucidMaxx status is earned, not requested," per the Overview article), after which it functions as an ordinary, repeatable, self-serve, dynamically-tiered-pricing evaluation cycle — which is why its own Eval Rules and Eval Pricing articles read as generally-documented content with no invite-only language of their own, a real but resolvable tension, not a contradiction.
- **The main-site Terms of Use's "Online Purchases; Subscriptions; Payment Details" section describes Lucid as providing "subscription based services to trading tools, programs, and educational resources," with subscriptions that "renew automatically unless cancelled before the renewal date."** This reads in tension with the Firm-Wide Rules' "all accounts are one-time purchases; no monthly subscription billing" statement, which is independently confirmed by "Simulated Account Fees" and the homepage's own FAQ. Not resolved here: the Terms of Use section may describe a genuinely different product line (educational resources, the Merch store, or similar) rather than evaluation/funded accounts, but no source read for this tree states that scoping explicitly. Do not assume evaluation or funded accounts are billed as an auto-renewing subscription on the strength of this section alone, and do not assume the "one-time purchase" rule is completely unqualified either.
- **"Legacy" live-transition structures for LucidPro, LucidFlex, and LucidDirect**: each plan has its own dedicated "Live (Legacy)" article, confirmed by the sitemap sweep below, describing a materially different, superseded live-transition structure (sixth-payout trigger instead of fifth, a per-tier "Max Moved Live" cap, and an Escrow-release mechanic) that applies only to accounts purchased or reset on or before 2/27/2026.
