## Overview

Lucid Trading documents four active plan families in this tree: **LucidPro**, **LucidFlex**, **LucidDirect**, and **LucidDaily**. LucidPro and LucidFlex both run a traditional Evaluation → Sim Funded progression with a purchasable Daily Loss Limit (DLL) toggle at checkout. LucidDirect skips evaluation entirely (instant funding), with a fixed DLL at $50K+ in its help-center articles (whether a DLL-OFF configuration exists is unresolved, see `direct.md`). LucidDaily has two independent toggles (DLL on/off, and an evaluation-stage-only drawdown-type choice between End-of-Day and Intraday) producing four purchasable configurations, documented together in one file rather than split, since the funded stage is identical (always Intraday) regardless of which evaluation drawdown type was chosen. All four converge on the same live-account stage, **LucidLive**, at the discretion of the Lucid risk team, except that LucidDaily's own Maximum Daily Profit trigger is a real, unresolved conflict between two of Lucid's own dedicated LucidDaily articles over whether that specific trigger is automatic or discretionary; see `daily.md`'s own Live Transition section.

A fifth product, **LucidBlack**, is explicitly labeled "(Legacy)" in Lucid's own help-center collection structure and is not documented here. Each LucidBlack article's breadcrumb and meta description also say "legacy" (re-fetched 2026-10-02). A sixth product, **LucidMaxx**, is a repeatable eval-to-live cycle reserved for traders who have already earned "LucidMaxx status" through "demonstrated performance" (the sources do not say which Lucid plan or plans that track record must come from); once earned, the sources describe a repeatable evaluation-to-live cycle with dynamically tiered pricing and daily, uncapped live payouts. Documented in its own file, **[LucidMaxx](lucidmaxx.md)**. See Documentation Scope.

## Plans

- **[LucidPro](pro.md)**: $25K/$50K/$100K/$150K, optional DLL toggle, funded-stage DLL replaced by a LucidScale DLL once the account closes above the Initial Trail Balance (its basis, "highest end-of-day account profit" vs. "60% of Peak EOD Balance," is a live source conflict, see `pro.md`), 40% payout-cycle consistency for accounts purchased or reset on or after 11/28/2025 at 3:00 PM EST (35% for earlier ones), no funded contract scaling plan stated, 90/10 split (100% on the first $10k of payouts for accounts purchased or reset before that cutoff; see `pro.md`).
- **[LucidFlex](flex.md)**: same four sizes, optional DLL toggle ($600/$1,200/$1,800/$2,700 On, from the pasted homepage config only; no dedicated help-center DLL article), no consistency once funded, no payout buffer, funded-stage contract scaling tied to simulated profit, capped at 5 lifetime payouts before live transition, 90/10 split.
- **[LucidDirect](direct.md)**: instant funding, no evaluation, fixed DLL at $50K+ only (none at $25K; whether a DLL-OFF configuration exists is unresolved), 100K/150K drawdown amounts genuinely differ from LucidPro/LucidFlex's own 100K/150K figures, 20% payout-cycle consistency, 90/10 split.
- **[LucidDaily](daily.md)**: four purchasable configurations (DLL × evaluation drawdown type), funded stage always Intraday regardless of configuration, no consistency once funded, hard-breach high-impact-USD-news restriction (unique to this plan), no per-request payout cap (instead a same-day "Maximum Daily Profit" ceiling that triggers a live-transition review; whether that transition is automatic or discretionary is an unresolved conflict between two of Lucid's own articles, see `daily.md`), 90/10 split.
- **[LucidMaxx](lucidmaxx.md)**: access gated on already-earned "LucidMaxx status," not purchasable directly. $25K/$50K/$100K/$150K, 40% eval consistency, 5-day minimum, dynamic 4-tier pricing keyed to a trader's own prior blown-live-account count, direct-to-live on passing (no Sim Funded stage), daily uncapped payouts, 90/10 split from the first payout, 2-week cooldown after a blown live account.

## Live Accounts

**[LucidLive](live.md)** is the shared live-account stage: every funded account across the four plans transitions into it at the discretion of the Lucid risk team (for LucidDaily's own Maximum Daily Profit trigger specifically, whether the transition is automatic or discretionary is an unresolved conflict between two of Lucid's own articles, see `daily.md`'s Live Transition section), and LucidMaxx moves directly from its evaluation into a live account. It is not purchased directly. "New Live Structure" states that all live accounts begin with a $0 starting balance, daily payouts, an End-of-Day drawdown and no daily loss limit; that the Max Loss Limit locks at $100 once live profit equals the account's Starting Live Drawdown (or when a live payout is requested first); and that "There are no consistency requirements for live accounts." A one-time Live Bonus is stated for a trader moved live the first time under the new program, with exclusions stated for LucidDaily-sourced accounts, for traders with LucidMaxx status, and for LucidDirect accounts purchased as of 1/18 at 3 PM EST; no article names LucidPro, LucidFlex or a post-2/27/26 LucidDirect account as eligible or excluded (see `live.md`). Live contract limits are two separately stated figures whose relation no article states: "New Live Structure" gives one flat figure per account size (2 minis/20 micros at $25,000 up to 10/100 at $150,000), and "New Live Scaling Plan" gives a ramp by live profit and exchange group (for the 50K CME column, 2/20 at $0 live profit up to 4/40 at $4,000+). The flat figure equals the ramp's CME top tier at every size and its CBOT/NYMEX top tiers below $150K, and exceeds COMEX's top tier at every size; those matches are observations about the numbers, not statements by either article (see `live.md`). LucidDaily's own transition into this program differs materially (a different entry trigger, a $15,000 flat cap on transferable sim profit, different transition timing, and no Live Bonus), documented in `daily.md`'s own Live Transition section rather than in `live.md`.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules stated by their own source as applying broadly across account types, not inferred by checking each plan's file individually.

- **Maximum 5 active funded accounts per household, shared across different types of funded accounts combined**: per "Maximum Number of Accounts" ("Different types of funded accounts combined may not exceed 5 accounts total"), which gives its own worked example naming two different plans explicitly: "If you have 3 LucidDirect accounts, you may only maintain 2 LucidPro funded accounts." The article does not enumerate which plan types count toward the 5, and `lucidmaxx.md` leaves whether LucidMaxx's own "Up to 5 simultaneous accounts" limit shares this pool unresolved. No plan-specific figure in this tree contradicts it. The same article also confirms up to 10 active evaluation accounts per household (shared with the funded-account count: "If you have 5 evaluation accounts, you may only hold 5 funded accounts"), and up to 5 active LucidLive accounts.
- **90% trader / 10% Lucid Trading profit split on every funded account, except LucidPro accounts purchased or reset before 11/28/2025 at 3:00 PM EST**: stated in each plan's own payout article ("All funded account payouts are split 90% to the trader and 10% to Lucid Trading" in LucidPro's and LucidDirect's; "All LucidFlex funded account payouts are split..." and "All LucidDaily funded account payouts are split..." in theirs), and for LucidMaxx in its Overview FAQ ("90/10. You keep 90% from the first payout."). The one stated exception is in "LucidPro Payouts": "Accounts purchased or reset before 11/28/2025 at 3:00 PM EST are still eligible for 100% on the first $10k" (see `pro.md`); no LucidFlex, LucidDirect or LucidDaily payout article read states a comparable cohort exception. For LucidLive the split is stated only for the Live Bonus ("The Live Bonus is subject to a 90/10 profit split"); `live.md` records the split on ordinary live profit as Not Confirmed.
- **30-calendar-day inactivity closure, evaluation and funded stages**: per "Inactivity Policy": "Accounts that have not been traded resulting in at least a net profit or loss of 1$ within 30 calendar days will be deemed abandoned and will be permanently deleted from our systems." Stated under a general "Active Status Policy" heading with no plan-type qualifier, and independently consistent with the confirmed 30-day figure in pro.md, flex.md, direct.md and daily.md; lucidmaxx.md marks its own Inactivity Rule Unconfirmed, so this is not corroborated there. Note this is separate from a breached (not merely inactive) account's own 30-day auto-deletion-if-not-reset window, described in the same article's "Breached Status Policy" section.
- **A breached account auto-deletes after 30 days unless reset**: "All breached accounts will be automatically deleted after 30 days if not reset. Traders can manually purchase a reset at any time before that 30-day period expires." Distinct from the 30-day inactivity closure above: this applies to an account that has already been breached, not merely idle. The sentence sits in a section whose first line is evaluation-scoped ("If an evaluation is breached (has hit Max Loss Limit), it is still eligible for a reset."), and the two sentences quoted here carry no evaluation qualifier and name no plan or stage. Whether they reach a breached Sim Funded or funded account of a given plan is Unconfirmed for that plan; see the Not Confirmed sections of `pro.md`, `flex.md`, `daily.md` and `direct.md`.

- **LucidPro Evaluation and LucidDirect accounts are one-time purchases with no subscription**: "LucidPro Evaluation and LucidDirect accounts are purchased by paying a one-time fee. There is no subscription and no automatic rebilling." ("Simulated Account Fees"). The LucidPro, LucidFlex and LucidDaily evaluation articles each list "One-time fee, no monthly rebilling" among their benefits; no article read states a billing model for LucidMaxx beyond its pricing article's meta description mentioning "the one-time fee."
- **No activation fee to upgrade a passed evaluation to a funded account**: "No activation fees to upgrade to funded account" in the LucidPro, LucidFlex and LucidDaily evaluation articles, and, for LucidPro specifically, "There is no activation fee to upgrade a LucidPro Evaluation to a LucidPro Funded." in "Simulated Account Fees."
- **Real-time objective updates on funded accounts, and upgrade to funded within 5-30 minutes**: the LucidPro, LucidFlex, LucidDirect and LucidDaily funded-account articles each state "Objectives on our dashboard update in real-time within 5-30 minutes of your last closed trade"; the LucidPro, LucidFlex and LucidDaily evaluation articles state "upgrade to funded account within 5-30 minutes of hitting profit target" (LucidDirect has no evaluation).
- **Drawdown-lock mechanism: each Sim Funded drawdown article states the Max Loss Limit locks once the account exceeds the tier's Initial Trail Balance, at the initial balance plus $100**: with dollar tables including the Locked MLL Balance, in "LucidPro Drawdown," "LucidFlex Drawdown," "LucidDirect Drawdown" and "LucidDaily Drawdown." The EOD system calculates the highest closing balance for LucidPro, LucidFlex and LucidDirect; LucidDaily's funded stage is always Intraday, and its article does not say how often the exceedance is checked. In every table the Initial Trail Balance equals the tier size + MLL Amount + $100, an observation about the tables, not a formula any article states. LucidLive's lock ($100, once live profit equals the Starting Live Drawdown, against a $0 starting balance) is stated separately by "New Live Structure."
- **Trading hours for LucidPro, LucidFlex and LucidDirect accounts: all positions must be closed by 4:45 PM EST Monday through Friday; trading resumes 6:00 PM EST Sunday through Thursday**: per "Allowed Trading Times," whose Sim section is headed "LucidPro, LucidFlex and LucidDirect Accounts" and which names no LucidDaily or LucidMaxx schedule. "Holding a position past this time does not result in a failed account"; open positions "will be automatically closed by Lucid Trading at 4:45 PM EST"; on market holidays with altered closing times positions "must be closed before the market closes." LucidLive accounts have their own times (4:45 PM EST on Tradovate Live accounts, 4:15 PM EST on Rithmic Live accounts) and "Swing trading is not allowed in the new LucidLive accounts."
- **Hedging live accounts is prohibited by both Lucid and the CME**, with violations resulting in a permanent ban ("Can I hedge my live accounts? Absolutely not.", the LucidLive FAQ in "New Live Structure" and "LucidDaily Live").
- **News trading is allowed on LucidPro, LucidFlex, and LucidDirect; not allowed on LucidDaily (hard breach)**, per "Other Trading Activities": "Allowed on Flex, Pro and Direct" and, as its own sub-bullet, "Traders may enter or exit positions around scheduled or unscheduled news events on these plans without it being a breach" vs. "Not allowed on Daily / Trading red folder news on the LucidDaily is a hard breach." The article states this at the plan level, not per-stage; see each plan's own file (Evaluation and Sim Funded tables) for how this resolves stage by stage.
- **Hedging across simulated accounts is prohibited in all forms**, per "Prohibited: Hedging": hedging across multiple accounts held by the same user, between different users' accounts, between different firms or funded platforms, and "any other form of hedging designed to bypass risk management rules." Going long and short the same contract is prohibited "in the same or separate accounts"; the one permitted same-contract case is a mini versus a micro of the same contract in the same account ("You can go long / short the same contract but one is micros and one is minis in the same account"), and it is prohibited across separate accounts. Going long one contract and short a different one is permitted within a single account but prohibited across separate accounts for correlated assets (example: "You cannot go long ES in one account and short NQ in a separate account"; "Correlated assets go for groupings in equities, metals, energies, etc."). First flag: "an email notice & the associated accounts will be reset to prior days balance." After repeated offenses "all involved accounts will be breached and you may be permanently restricted from using Lucid Trading services." (Distinct from the live-account hedging ban above, which states a permanent ban with no warning step.)
- **Microscalping is prohibited**, defined as capturing small price movements with outsized size in extremely short holding windows (a few seconds), aimed at exploiting simulated fill behavior rather than genuine market activity; distinct from genuine scalping, which is explicitly permitted. Flagged automatically if more than 50% of an account's profit comes from trades held 5 seconds or less ("Prohibited: Microscalping"). Enforcement: manual review, then, "If bad faith activity is confirmed, you may receive a written warning"; "If flagged behavior continues after a warning," all profits from microscalping activity are forfeited and the trader may be permanently restricted. Appeal available.
- **High-frequency trading is prohibited**, defined as submitting a high volume of trades within seconds or milliseconds via algorithmic strategies exploiting price inefficiencies at speed ("Prohibited: High Frequency Trading"). First offense: written warning. Repeated offenses: HFT profits removed, accounts closed, trader permanently restricted. Stated rationale: HFT "can result in hundreds of orders being placed within minutes, generating an unusually high load on platform infrastructure." Appeal available.
- **Scaling into trades (DCA), genuine scalping, automated strategies (bots/copiers), and "flipping" (fast in-and-out trades to satisfy minimum-day requirements) are all explicitly permitted**, per "Other Trading Activities." Martingaling is discouraged, not banned. Traders remain responsible for losses caused by their own automated-strategy errors.
- **Restricted countries**: traders who are citizens or residents of roughly 80 listed countries are ineligible for any Lucid Trading service ("Restricted Countries"). The list separately names Crimea, Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk, and Sevastopol alongside Ukraine itself, a redundancy present in Lucid's own list. See `SOURCES.md` for the fetch record rather than duplicating roughly 80 country names here.
- **General fair-use / anti-exploitation standard**, per "Trade with Integrity": Lucid prohibits "taking advantage of system errors or update delays," "exploiting discrepancies in simulated prices," and "tactics that do not reflect real-world trading conditions." Stated as a broad philosophy rather than a specific enforcement ladder with numeric thresholds.
- **Supported platforms split by data-feed provider**: "CQG supported platforms": NinjaTrader, Tradovate, TradingView; "Rithmic supported platforms": MotiveWave (Lucid Partner), Quantower (Lucid Partner), Tradesea, Sierra Chart, Jigsaw, Bookmap, ATAS, R\|Trader Pro, MultiCharts ("Lucid Trading Supported Platforms"). "All listed platforms work with all Lucid account types." (The same article's meta description says "All platforms work across LucidFlex and LucidPro accounts.") No article states whether this CQG/Rithmic split is the split behind `live.md`'s different Tradovate (4:45 PM EST) and Rithmic (4:15 PM EST) live close times.
- **Payout delivery methods**: Plaid (U.S. traders, instant bank transfer), WorkMarket by ADP (U.S. and international, bank transfer or PayPal, "as early as the next business day"), and crypto (international, BTC/ETH/LTC/USDT/USDC) ("Payout Methods"). This article's own meta-description states payouts "averaging about 15 minutes once approved," while each plan's own Payouts section states disbursement "within 2 business days"; the articles do not say how the two figures relate.
- **Card payment methods for account purchases**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club, and Maestro ("Accepted Payments Methods"). "Transactions are confirmed immediately upon approval."
- **Business-entity registration**: an account may be registered as personal or business at signup, but never converted between the two afterward, and a trader may hold only one Lucid profile ever, personal or business, not both ("Registering as a Business"). Business payouts require emailing proof of incorporation, proof of ownership and a government-issued photo ID for each owner of 25% or more for business verification; WorkMarket payouts additionally require KYC through WorkMarket Support directly, and Plaid payouts are enabled once Lucid approves the documents.
- **Refunds: the main site's own disclaimer states a strict no-refund policy for accounts that have been traded.** "The Company maintains a strict no refund policy for accounts that have been traded, regardless of the number of trades placed, trading results, or any technical issues experienced by the Client. By initiating any trading activity, whether simulated or otherwise, the Client expressly waives any right to a refund." An untraded account may be refunded, but only "in its sole discretion," and "the determination of eligibility and approval of such refunds shall rest exclusively with the Company." Refund eligibility never applies to "account violations, disqualifications, or breaches of the Terms of Use." Per "Disclaimer of Liability, Refunds and Chargeback Policy" (main site). Lucid "considers services rendered in full upon granting account access." **A real, unresolved conflict with two of Lucid's own live-transition articles, not silently reconciled here:** "New Live Structure" and "LucidDaily Live" both state that the evaluation cost of a funded account "not moved live because it has 0 payouts" will be refunded: conditionally, "as long as the account has equal to or greater than 50% of the total drawdown remaining," per "New Live Structure" (see `live.md`'s Eligibility and Transition section), and unconditionally per "LucidDaily Live" (see `daily.md`'s Live Transition section). No source says whether that 0-payout refund is an exception to the disclaimer's no-refund rule for traded accounts or covers only untraded ones. Do not assume the disclaimer overrides the live-transition articles' refund sentence, and do not assume that sentence makes a refund available outside that specific closure scenario.
- **Chargebacks are a permanently-banning offense, not merely discouraged.** "Clients who initiate a fraudulent or improper chargeback shall be permanently banned from all current and future access to Lucid Trading's services and platforms." By purchasing, a trader "expressly agrees not to initiate a chargeback or payment dispute" for any properly-processed transaction; the Company additionally reserves the right to pursue recovery of the disputed funds plus attorneys' fees.
- **Isolated technical issues (data-feed errors, trade-copier malfunctions, third-party app errors, the trader's own connectivity problems, ordinary slippage) are never grounds for a refund, credit, or compensation**, and Lucid's total liability for any such issue is capped at "the greater of one hundred dollars ($100.00) or the amount paid by the Client to the Company in the immediately preceding month." Per the same "Disclaimer of Liability, Refunds and Chargeback Policy."
- **A real, unresolved tension on geographic eligibility, not silently reconciled.** The Terms of Use state Lucid's Website is provided "for use only by persons located in the United States" and that Lucid "make[s] no claims that the Website or any of its content is accessible or appropriate outside of the United States." This sits in real tension with the Restricted Countries rule above, whose list of excluded countries reads as if countries outside the list are not excluded. Both are genuinely Lucid's own current sources; per this repo's standing rule to surface conflicts rather than blend them, this is flagged, not resolved, here. Do not assume international traders are definitively eligible or definitively ineligible on the strength of either source alone.
- **Account eligibility exclusions**: a trader must be "18 years of age or older," and is ineligible to use Lucid's Website/Software/Services at all if they are "a convicted felon, have been disciplined by the National Futures Association, have been disciplined by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission, or have an outstanding balance with a trading firm." Per the Terms of Use.
- **Account/performance data cannot be transferred or combined between products or users**: "You may not transfer or combine your account's performance, service parameters, data, or any other information between products or with any other user." Per the Terms of Use.
- **Disputes may be routed to binding arbitration at Lucid's sole discretion**: "At Company's sole discretion, it may require you to submit any disputes arising from these Terms of Use or use of the Website... to final and binding arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the American Arbitration Association applying Delaware law." Per the Terms of Use.

### Profiles, business accounts, and account limits

- **One permanent profile per trader**: "A trader may only ever have one Lucid Trading profile, either personal or business. Profiles cannot be converted, deactivated, or replaced to create a new profile. Once a profile is created, it remains the trader's permanent profile with Lucid Trading."
- **Business registration binds the payout route**: "If you register as a business, you must get paid out as a business", with verification documents submitted to compliance. Traders wanting WorkMarket payouts must contact WorkMarket support directly.
- **Reserve accounts sit alongside the funded cap**: "Reserve Limit: You may keep 5 evaluation accounts in reserve while you have 5 funded accounts", and "You may trade evaluation accounts while they are in reserve."
- **A reset is a full restoration**: "A reset returns the account to its original starting conditions, including balance and risk parameters."
- **The restricted-country list names occupied regions separately**: Crimea, Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Sevastopol are listed alongside Ukraine rather than folded into it.
- **Correlated-asset groupings are by sector**: "Correlated assets go for groupings in equities, metals, energies, etc."
