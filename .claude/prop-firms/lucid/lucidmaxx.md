## Overview

LucidMaxx is a repeatable eval-to-live program reserved for traders who have already earned "LucidMaxx status" through "demonstrated performance" (the source does not say which Lucid plan or plans that track record must be earned on): "This plan is not available to the general public. It can only be purchased by traders who have earned LucidMaxx status through demonstrated performance" ("LucidMaxx Overview"). Access is risk-team-initiated, not requested: "Our quantitative risk team uses defined performance criteria to identify traders who qualify. Once a trader meets the required benchmarks:", after which, as two separate list items in the source, "They will be notified directly via email" and "They will be granted access to purchase the LucidMaxx evaluation." The article closes the section with: "LucidMaxx status is earned, not requested."

Once a trader has earned that status, the sources describe a repeatable cycle: "LucidMaxx is designed as a repeatable cycle. If a trader earns profits above the live drawdown, there is no limit to how many times a trader may return to the LucidMaxx evaluation" ("LucidMaxx Cooldown"). The Eval Rules and Eval Pricing articles state no access gate of their own; the Overview article states it ("This plan is not available to the general public"; FAQ: "There is no public purchase option"). Do not read the absence of a gate statement in those two articles as evidence that LucidMaxx is purchasable without first earning status.

Once the evaluation is passed, the trader "transitions directly into a live account" ("LucidMaxx Eval Rules"); no Sim Funded stage is described in the cited articles.

### Pricing Mechanics

LucidMaxx's own eval/reset price is not fixed per size; the article ties it to a trader's live-account outcomes, and states that promotions and discounts do not apply:

- **Clearing live drawdown**: "Traders who successfully clear live drawdown: Keep their current tier eval pricing. Pricing does not increase, even if the account is later lost" ("LucidMaxx Eval Pricing"). That article does not define "clear live drawdown" (see Not Confirmed).
- **Blowing live accounts without clearing drawdown**: "Traders who repeatedly blow live accounts without clearing drawdown: Move into higher pricing tiers over time," by the Tier 1 to Tier 4 columns ("0–4 blown," "5–8 blown," "9–12 blown," "13+ blown") shown in the Evaluation table's One-Time Eval Fee row; "Pricing is determined by the number of live accounts blown without clearing drawdown."
- **No discounts, no promotions**: "No discounts are offered on LucidMaxx evaluations... The listed price is the final price paid... cannot be reduced through promotions" ("LucidMaxx Eval Pricing").

## How the Drawdown Works

"LucidMaxx Eval Rules" states "The live account drawdown is the same as the standard Lucid live structure," and no LucidMaxx article states a live drawdown mechanic or dollar figure of its own. Carried over from `live.md`'s Live Account table, not restated in any LucidMaxx article: an End-of-Day Max Loss Limit (MLL) that increases as the account balance grows "up to a defined point" (the live article does not state the level it starts at, see Not Confirmed), until live profit reaches the tier's Starting Live Drawdown ($1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500 for 25K/50K/100K/150K, equal to this file's Evaluation-table Drawdown Amount row), at which point the MLL locks at a flat $100 and stops trailing; requesting a live payout before that profit level also locks it ("New Live Structure"). The Eval Rules cross-reference is the only link between the two; see Not Confirmed.

### Worked Example

This example uses the literal-$0-based convention for the $50,000 tier's live account, carried over from `live.md` (no LucidMaxx article states a live starting balance, see Not Confirmed): live profit is tracked starting at a literal $0.

1. The LucidMaxx evaluation is passed, and the trader is moved directly into a live account starting at $0 live profit, with a $2,000 Starting Live Drawdown (the 50K tier's figure from `live.md`'s table, carried over via the "same as the standard Lucid live structure" statement, not stated in a LucidMaxx article).
2. The account's live profit reaches $1,000 (a new peak). The MLL has not locked yet, since profit has not yet reached the $2,000 Starting Live Drawdown.
3. The account's live profit reaches $2,000 exactly, the Starting Live Drawdown. Per `live.md`'s own Drawdown Lock row, the MLL locks at a flat $100 and stops trailing.
4. The account's live profit later grows to $5,000 (a new peak). The MLL does not move again: it stays fixed at $100.
5. Derived, not stated by any source: if the account's live profit ever fell to $100 or below, it would reach the locked MLL. This step applies the sim drawdown articles' rule ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached") to the live stage and reads the $100 lock as a live-profit level against the $0 starting balance; no live or LucidMaxx article states a live breach rule (see Not Confirmed).

## Live Transition

Passing the LucidMaxx evaluation moves the trader directly into a live account; there is no Sim Funded stage and no separate activation step described in the cited sources. See How the Drawdown Works and Payouts above for the live stage's own confirmed mechanics.

**Live Bonus:** "New Live Structure" (cited in `live.md`) states "The bonus is not eligible for traders with LucidMaxx status." This is stated in `live.md`'s source, not in LucidMaxx's own four articles, which never mention the Live Bonus at all.

**Max Active/Concurrent Accounts:** up to 5 simultaneous LucidMaxx accounts ("LucidMaxx Overview" FAQ). Not stated whether this pool is shared with the standard LucidLive 5-account household cap in `live.md`, or is its own separate allowance; see Not Confirmed.

**Max Contracts:** not independently stated for LucidMaxx in the cited sources. Do not assume `live.md`'s own Live Contract Scaling Plan table applies unchanged without a LucidMaxx-specific citation; see Not Confirmed.

### Cooldown and Re-Entry

LucidMaxx is designed as a long-term, repeatable cycle rather than a one-time program:

- **Clearing live drawdown:** a trader who earns profit above the live drawdown without breaching may return to the LucidMaxx evaluation an unlimited number of times ("LucidMaxx Cooldown").
- **Blowing a live account:** "There is a 2-week cooldown period after a trader blows their LucidMaxx live account." The cooldown begins only once every one of that trader's live accounts is closed ("If you have multiple live accounts, the cooldown will not start until all live accounts are closed"). A trader with active LucidMaxx evaluations "may continue trading and even move back to live"; the cooldown begins only once the last active live account is closed ("LucidMaxx Cooldown").
- **Reckless trading near the live Max Loss Limit** (the source's own example: full-porting positions near the limit) can escalate, at the discretion of the Lucid risk team, to "Extended cooldown periods" or "Permanent restriction from the program" ("LucidMaxx Cooldown"). No specific duration or dollar/percentage threshold is stated for what triggers this escalation beyond the qualitative "reckless"/"full porting" description; see Not Confirmed.

**Holding LucidMaxx alongside a Live account is allowed, buying more is not.** "You may trade and reset your existing LucidMaxx accounts alongside your Live account, however, you may not purchase new LucidMaxx accounts whilst you hold an active Live account."

Do not assume LucidPro's/LucidFlex's/LucidDirect's own Evaluation-stage figures for these rows carry over to LucidMaxx without a LucidMaxx-specific citation.
- **Minimum Balance at Start (Evaluation)** — not stated in any cited source for LucidMaxx. Do not assume it equals Starting Balance minus Drawdown Amount, or that it matches another Lucid plan's figure.
- **Live-stage starting balance and drawdown figures** — no LucidMaxx article states a live starting balance or a live drawdown dollar figure; "LucidMaxx Eval Rules" says only that "The live account drawdown is the same as the standard Lucid live structure," and "LucidMaxx Overview" lists "End-of-day drawdown" among its benefits without a stage. Neither `live.md`'s source ("New Live Structure") nor any LucidMaxx article states the level the live MLL starts at or a live breach rule (the sim drawdown articles state the breach rule for their own stages only), so Worked Example step 5 is a derived step. Do not assume the carry-over is stated for LucidMaxx, do not assume a starting MLL level or that the sim breach rule applies to live accounts, and do not assume the Evaluation stage uses End-of-Day drawdown.
- **Buffer Requirement (live stage)** — "LucidMaxx Overview" lists "No payout caps" and no minimum profit but states no buffer amount, and no other cited LucidMaxx article does. Do not assume the buffer of `live.md` or of another Lucid plan applies.
- **Minimum Payout Request (live stage)** — no cited LucidMaxx article states a minimum payout request amount, and its "There are no profit minimums once live" line does not say whether that covers the size of a request. - **Consistency on Payouts (live stage)** — the 40% figure confirmed above is stated only as an Evaluation-stage pass condition. No cited source states whether any consistency requirement applies once live. Do not assume either that it carries over or that it is waived; neither is directly stated.
- **Max Active/Concurrent Accounts, shared-pool question** — "LucidMaxx Overview" states a flat "Up to 5 simultaneous accounts" limit but never states whether this is the same 5-account household pool `live.md` describes for LucidPro/LucidFlex/LucidDirect/LucidDaily-sourced live accounts, or a separate LucidMaxx-only allowance. - **Max Contracts (live stage)** — LucidMaxx's own source confirms only that its live drawdown mechanic matches `live.md`'s; it says nothing about contract limits. Do not assume `live.md`'s own Live Contract Scaling Plan table applies to LucidMaxx accounts without an independent citation.
- **Maximum Total Payouts / Lifetime Cap (live stage)** — "LucidMaxx Overview" states "No payout caps" (a per-request cap) and "There are no profit minimums once live" (a minimum threshold), but neither statement addresses a lifetime or cumulative total. Do not assume either confirms the absence of a lifetime cap; the source is silent on it.
- **Definition of "clear live drawdown" (pricing tiers)** — "LucidMaxx Eval Pricing" keeps a trader's tier "if they successfully clear live drawdown" and counts "live accounts blown without clearing drawdown," but defines neither phrase; "LucidMaxx Cooldown" separately says "If a trader earns profits above the live drawdown."
