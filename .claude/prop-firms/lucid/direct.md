## Overview

LucidDirect is Lucid's instant-funded product: "a simulated straight-to-funded account which does not have an evaluation phase. When you purchase a LucidDirect account, you start building simulated capital to earn payouts immediately" ("LucidDirect Funded Account"). It is offered in four sizes ($25K/$50K/$100K/$150K). **Its Max Loss Limit amounts at the 100K and 150K tiers genuinely differ from LucidPro's and LucidFlex's own 100K/150K figures**: $3,500 and $5,000 respectively, versus $3,000 and $4,500 for the evaluation-based plans. Do not treat LucidPro/LucidFlex's 100K/150K drawdown figures as applying here; they are different numbers for a structurally different product, confirmed independently by this file's own dedicated source.

LucidDirect's own dedicated help-center articles (Funded Account, Daily Loss Limit, Drawdown, Payout Objectives, Consistency Percentage) describe only a fixed DLL figure at $50K/$100K/$150K with no "off" configuration, and no DLL at all at $25,000. The pasted homepage's `LucidPricingConfig`, added to this file's own sources 2026-09-23, complicates that picture: it gives LucidDirect the same ON/OFF `no-dll` addon object as LucidPro/LucidFlex/LucidDaily for the $50K/$100K/$150K tiers, mirroring the funded DLL figure down to "NONE" when Off, but with no price attached to either state, unlike the other three plans' own priced toggle. Whether a real, purchasable DLL-OFF LucidDirect account exists is therefore unresolved against the help-center articles, not settled either way; see Not Confirmed.

## How the Drawdown Works

"LucidDirect Drawdown" states an End-of-Day Drawdown: "At the end of each trading session, the system calculates the account's highest closing balance," "The MLL increases as your balance grows, up to a defined point," and once the account exceeds the Initial Trail Balance the MLL "locks and no longer moves," at the initial balance plus $100.

"If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached" ("LucidDirect Drawdown"); no recovery is stated for a breached funded LucidDirect account (see Not Confirmed, Reset Fee). A DLL hit is a soft breach: "you do not lose your account for hitting DLL as long as the Max Loss Limit (MLL) has not been reached" ("LucidDirect Daily Loss Limit"); the $25,000 tier lists no DLL. Once the account closes above its Initial Trail Balance, the Fixed DLL is replaced by a LucidScale DLL ("Highest end-of-day account profit × 60% = LucidScale DLL"), which "increases as your account grows" and "does not decrease, even if the account draws down" ("LucidDirect Daily Loss Limit"), so once it has risen it cannot shrink back down. "LucidDirect Funded Account" labels the same column "60% of Peak EOD Balance" (live source conflict, see Not Confirmed).

### Worked Example

This example uses the nominal tier-size convention of "LucidDirect Drawdown"'s own table for the $50,000 tier, tracked throughout. The funded Starting Balance is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed), so the $50,000 opening is that convention, not a stated Starting Balance.

1. The account opens at $50,000. The $2,000 Max Loss Limit is taken here to set an initial floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000 (derived: no article states the starting MLL level). The DLL starts fixed at $1,200.
2. The account closes at a new EOD high of $51,000 (still below the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance). The MLL is taken to trail up to $51,000 − $2,000 = $49,000 (derived: no article states a peak-minus-MLL-amount trailing formula, only that the MLL "increases as your balance grows, up to a defined point"). The DLL remains the fixed $1,200.
3. The account closes at a new EOD high of $52,200, above the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance. The MLL locks at $50,100 ("Locked MLL Balance" in "LucidDirect Drawdown"'s own table). The DLL switches to the LucidScale DLL, "Highest end-of-day account profit × 60%"; under this example's nominal convention the profit at a $52,200 close is $2,200 (derived), so the DLL would be $2,200 × 0.60 = $1,320 (derived by this file, not a quoted figure).
4. The account later closes at $56,000 (a new high), a derived profit of $6,000 under the same convention, so the LucidScale DLL would be $6,000 × 0.60 = $3,600 (derived). The MLL does not move again: it stays at $50,100 regardless of this new high.
5. If the account's balance ever fell to $50,100 or below, it would reach the (locked) MLL, which the drawdown article states breaches the account ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached"); what happens to a breached Sim Funded account afterward is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed).

Note that LucidDirect’s own article frames this against the profit goal, not against a buffer. The qualifying balance has to survive until the request is processed, not just until it is submitted.

## Live Transition

LucidDirect funded accounts transition into the shared **LucidLive** program at Lucid's discretion, documented in full in [`live.md`](live.md). Every LucidDirect funded account with at least one payout is moved to its own live account on transition, starting at $0 with an EOD drawdown, no Daily Loss Limit, and daily payout eligibility ("New Live Structure"). Live Bonus eligibility for LucidDirect is Unconfirmed: "LucidDirect Live (Legacy)," which opens with "This is for accounts purchased/reset on 2/27/26 and prior.", states "LucidDirect accounts purchased as of 1/18 at 3 PM EST are not eligible for Live Bonus, that feature is now exclusive to LucidBlack." Whether that exclusion reaches an account purchased or reset after 2/27/26 is not stated. See Not Confirmed.
