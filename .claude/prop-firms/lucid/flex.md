## Overview

LucidFlex is offered in four account sizes ($25K/$50K/$100K/$150K) with a single purchasable customization at checkout: Daily Loss Limit ON (lower evaluation price, DLL enforced on both stages) or OFF (higher price, no DLL at either stage): "LucidFlex Customization." The choice locks in for the life of the account: "Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidFlex account with your preferred options." **Correction, 2026-09-24:** an earlier draft of this file said no source ever states a dollar DLL amount for LucidFlex; that was true of the help center (no dedicated LucidFlex DLL article exists, confirmed by a full sitemap sweep, see Not Confirmed) but not of the pasted homepage `LucidPricingConfig`, already cited elsewhere in this file for the 50K eval/reset fee figures, which carries the identical per-tier `dll`/`dll_below_initial_trail` cells LucidPro's own config does: $600/$1,200/$1,800/$2,700 for 25K/50K/100K/150K. See the Evaluation and Sim Funded tables below and Not Confirmed for what the pasted config does and does not settle.

LucidFlex's defining feature relative to LucidPro is its funded-stage structure: no consistency rule at all once funded ("There is no Consistency Percentage on LucidFlex funded accounts", "LucidFlex Funded Account"), no payout buffer requirement, and a funded-stage contract scaling plan tied to simulated profit (LucidPro and LucidDirect both explicitly state they have no such scaling plan). Both evaluation and funded stages use an explicitly-confirmed End-of-Day drawdown.

## How the Drawdown Works

Both LucidFlex stages use an explicitly-confirmed End-of-Day Drawdown: the Max Loss Limit (MLL) trails the account's highest-ever closing balance, rising as the balance grows, and never moving down on its own. Once the account's EOD closing balance exceeds the tier's Initial Trail Balance (drawdown amount + $100 above starting balance), the MLL locks permanently at starting balance + $100 and stops trailing, directly confirmed by this plan's own dedicated drawdown article.

Separately, "Once you request a payout from LucidFlex, your MLL automatically adjusts to the Locked MLL Balance" ("LucidFlex Drawdown"), meaning a payout request itself can trigger the lock even before the account's own EOD balance has crossed the Initial Trail Balance, not only the ordinary balance-crossing trigger. This file does not have a worked scenario confirming exactly how these two trigger paths interact when the payout-triggered lock occurs at a _different_ balance than the trail would have implied; treated as a second, independent trigger path, not walked through numerically below.

Reaching the (trailing or locked) MLL is a breach. At the Evaluation stage it is reset-eligible: the firm-wide Inactivity Policy article, cited by this file, states "If an evaluation is breached (has hit Max Loss Limit), it is still eligible for a reset." At the Sim Funded stage no recovery path is confirmed for LucidFlex by any source read for this file. Both are distinct from a DLL hit, which only pauses trading until the next session (this file’s own cited "LucidFlex Customization" states it directly: "The DLL is a soft breach, so if you hit it you will simply be locked out until the next trading session.")

### Worked Example

This example uses the full nominal-balance convention for the $50,000 tier: the account's real balance, starting at a literal $50,000, tracked throughout.

1. The funded account opens at $50,000. The $2,000 Max Loss Limit sets an initial floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000.
2. The account closes at a new EOD high of $51,200 (a new peak, still below the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance). The MLL trails up to $51,200 − $2,000 = $49,200.
3. The account closes at a new EOD high of $52,100 exactly (the Initial Trail Balance). The MLL locks at $50,100 (starting balance + $100), per this plan's own directly-confirmed locked value, and stops trailing.
4. The account later closes at $54,000 (a new high). The MLL does not move again: it remains fixed at $50,100 regardless of any future high, since it already locked in step 3.
5. The account has 5 separate trading days each with at least $150 profit (the 50K tier's Minimum Daily Profit, see Payouts table) and positive net profit for the cycle. The trader requests a payout. Per "LucidFlex Drawdown," this request itself would also trigger the lock if it hadn't already happened. In this example it is already locked, so the payout request changes nothing about the MLL.
6. If the account's balance ever fell to $50,100 or below, it would breach the locked MLL and close.

**Payout requests cannot be undone, and can be self-sabotaged.** "Payout requests are final once submitted, they cannot be edited or canceled." Trading while one is pending is allowed but risky: "If you take a trade before your payout is processed that drops your balance below the required amount, your request may be denied." The qualifying balance has to survive until the request is processed, not just until it is submitted.

**Payout eligibility is conditional on rule compliance, not just numbers.** "You must not violate any Trading Rules, Terms & Conditions, or the LucidFlex Account Agreement."

**Circumventing platform position limits wipes the day's profit.** "Each platform/datafeed provider has guardrails in place to prevent/block this a much as possible, however users whom intentionally try to, or succeed in circumventing these limits may result in having profit wiped for the day." A separate consequence applies to repeated attempts to get around the contract-scaling limits themselves: "Our back-end systems track and adjust balances for this. If we see repeated behavior attempting to get around the limits, we may review your account" ("LucidFlex Contract Scaling Plan").

## Live Transition

LucidFlex funded accounts transition into the shared **LucidLive** program at Lucid's discretion, documented in full in [`live.md`](live.md). LucidFlex's own dedicated payout article independently confirms the "Traders may take up to 5 payouts from each LucidFlex account after which they will be moved live" structure referenced only generically in the shared live article. Every LucidFlex funded account with at least one payout is moved to its own live account on transition, starting at $0 with an EOD drawdown, no Daily Loss Limit, and daily payout eligibility. LucidFlex is eligible for the one-time Live Bonus described in `live.md`.

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Starting Balance (Evaluation and Sim Funded, all tiers)** — no source literally states a "Starting Balance" for LucidFlex at either stage. Evaluation figures are derived from each tier's own account-size label; the Sim Funded $0 figure is inferred by analogy to every other confirmed Lucid plan, not directly quoted for LucidFlex. Do not assume any source states these figures as literal dollar amounts rather than size-tier labels.
- **Daily Loss Limit dollar amounts, help-center silence vs. the pasted homepage config** — "LucidFlex Customization" and "LucidFlex Funded Account" both confirm a DLL toggle exists, but neither this file's Evaluation article, Funded article, nor Customization article states a dollar figure for any tier, and a full sitemap sweep of `support.lucidtrading.com` (see `SOURCES.md`) lists exactly 8 LucidFlex articles total, none of them a DLL article, unlike LucidPro, LucidDirect, and LucidDaily, which each have one. **Corrected 2026-09-24**: the pasted homepage `LucidPricingConfig` (already cited in this file for 50K eval/reset fee prices) does carry per-tier `dll`/`dll_below_initial_trail` cells for LucidFlex, identical to LucidPro's own figures ($600/$1,200/$1,800/$2,700), now transcribed into the Evaluation and Sim Funded tables above. What remains unconfirmed is narrower: what happens to the DLL once the funded-stage MLL locks past the Initial Trail Balance. LucidPro's own funded config (and LucidDirect's config) carries a `lucidscale_dll_above_initial_trail` field describing a 60%-of-peak-profit replacement formula; LucidFlex's funded config has no equivalent field, and no help-center article states what (if anything) replaces the fixed DLL for LucidFlex past that point. Do not assume LucidFlex's post-lock DLL behavior matches LucidPro's LucidScale formula by analogy; the pasted config gives LucidFlex no such field to confirm it.
- **Minimum Balance at Start (Evaluation)** — not stated in any source read for this file. Do not assume it equals the Starting Balance, and do not assume no such floor exists.
- **Minimum Trading Days (Evaluation)** — not stated as a formal rule. "Pass in two days" describes the fastest realistic pass time given the consistency-percentage math, not a stated minimum-days requirement. Do not conflate the two. Do not treat "pass in two days" as a stated minimum-trading-days rule.
- **One-Time Eval Fee and Reset Fee, 25K/100K/150K tiers** — the pasted `LucidPricingConfig` lists a one-time-fee and reset-fee WooCommerce product id for LucidFlex's 25K/100K/150K tiers too (its `front.one_time_fee.cells`/`front.reset_fee.cells` carry an entry per size, each resolving to its own list price via the config's `productPrices` map), so this is not a case of no source stating the figure at all; those figures simply have not been transcribed into this table, checked against the DLL-toggle add-on/promo, or modeled in the engine yet. The 50K figures ($146 off-DLL / $131 on-DLL eval, $105 off-DLL / $90 on-DLL reset) are confirmed via lucidtrading.com's own homepage pricing-card configuration script ("lucid-pricing-cards-js-extra", embedding `LucidPricingConfig`; dateModified 2026-09-21, user-pasted 2026-09-23), cross-checked against this repo's own engine (`LucidTrading.ts`, `FeeSchedule.ts`). Do not scale the 50K figures to the other tiers, and do not transcribe the config's other-tier figures without re-deriving each tier's own DLL-toggle adjustment first.
- **Drawdown lock vs. payout-request lock interaction** — "LucidFlex Drawdown" confirms both a balance-crossing trigger and a payout-request trigger for the same lock, but this file's sources do not describe a scenario where a payout is requested before the balance has crossed the Initial Trail Balance in enough detail to state exactly what locked value would result in that case. The Worked Example above does not attempt to walk through this specific ordering. Do not assume the two triggers are alternatives rather than both applying.
