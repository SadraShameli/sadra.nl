## Overview

LucidFlex is offered in four account sizes ($25K/$50K/$100K/$150K) with a single purchasable customization at checkout: Daily Loss Limit ON (lower evaluation price, DLL enforced on both stages) or OFF (higher price, no DLL at either stage): "LucidFlex Customization." The choice locks in for the life of the account: "Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidFlex account with your preferred options." No help-center article states a dollar DLL amount for LucidFlex: no dedicated LucidFlex DLL article exists (the sitemap lists 8 LucidFlex articles, none a DLL article). The DLL figures in the tables below come only from the pasted homepage `LucidPricingConfig`, whose per-tier `dll`/`dll_below_initial_trail` cells for LucidFlex read $600/$1,200/$1,800/$2,700 for 25K/50K/100K/150K; see Not Confirmed for what that config leaves unstated.

LucidFlex's defining feature relative to LucidPro is its funded-stage structure: no consistency rule at all once funded ("There is no Consistency Percentage on LucidFlex funded accounts", "LucidFlex Funded Account"), no payout buffer requirement, and a funded-stage contract scaling plan tied to simulated profit (LucidPro and LucidDirect both explicitly state they have no such scaling plan). The drawdown articles state an End-of-Day drawdown for both the evaluation and funded stages.

## How the Drawdown Works

Both LucidFlex stages use an End-of-Day Drawdown ("LucidFlex Drawdown"): "At the end of each trading session, the system calculates the account's highest closing balance," and "The MLL increases as your balance grows, up to a defined point." Once the account exceeds the tier's Initial Trail Balance, the MLL locks and "no longer moves" at the initial balance plus $100 ("Locked MLL Balance" in the article's table). Each Initial Trail Balance in that table equals the tier size + MLL Amount + $100, an observation about the table, not a formula the article states.

Separately, "Once you request a payout from LucidFlex, your MLL automatically adjusts to the Locked MLL Balance" ("LucidFlex Drawdown"), meaning a payout request itself can trigger the lock even before the account's own EOD balance has crossed the Initial Trail Balance, not only the ordinary balance-crossing trigger. This file does not have a worked scenario confirming exactly how these two trigger paths interact when the payout-triggered lock occurs at a _different_ balance than the trail would have implied; treated as a second, independent trigger path, not walked through numerically below.

Reaching the (trailing or locked) MLL is a breach. At the Evaluation stage it is reset-eligible: the firm-wide Inactivity Policy article, cited by this file, states "If an evaluation is breached (has hit Max Loss Limit), it is still eligible for a reset." At the Sim Funded stage whether a breach can be reset is Unconfirmed for LucidFlex: the same "Inactivity Policy" states, with no evaluation qualifier and naming no plan or stage, "All breached accounts will be automatically deleted after 30 days if not reset. Traders can manually purchase a reset at any time before that 30-day period expires." (see Not Confirmed). Both are distinct from a DLL hit, which only pauses trading until the next session (this file’s own cited "LucidFlex Customization" states it directly: "The DLL is a soft breach, so if you hit it you will simply be locked out until the next trading session.")

### Worked Example

This example uses the nominal tier-size convention of "LucidFlex Drawdown"'s own table for the $50,000 tier, tracked throughout. The funded Starting Balance is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed), so the $50,000 opening is that convention, not a stated Starting Balance.

1. The funded account opens at $50,000. The $2,000 Max Loss Limit is taken here to set an initial floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000 (derived: no article states the starting MLL level).
2. The account closes at a new EOD high of $51,200 (a new peak, still below the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance). The MLL is taken to trail up to $51,200 − $2,000 = $49,200 (derived: no article states a peak-minus-MLL-amount trailing formula, only that the MLL "increases as your balance grows, up to a defined point").
3. The account closes at a new EOD high of $52,200, above the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance. The MLL locks at $50,100 ("Locked MLL Balance" in the article's table) and no longer moves.
4. The account later closes at $54,000 (a new high). The MLL does not move again: it remains fixed at $50,100 regardless of any future high, since it already locked in step 3.
5. The account has 5 separate trading days each with at least $150 profit (the 50K tier's Minimum Daily Profit, see Payouts table) and positive net profit for the cycle. The trader requests a payout. Per "LucidFlex Drawdown," this request itself would also trigger the lock if it hadn't already happened. In this example it is already locked, so the payout request changes nothing about the MLL.
6. If the account's balance ever fell to $50,100 or below, it would reach the (locked) MLL, which the drawdown article states breaches the account ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached"); what happens to a breached Sim Funded account afterward is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed).

Payouts are deducted within a few minutes of approval; funds are disbursed within 2 business days.

**Payout requests cannot be undone, and can be self-sabotaged.** "Payout requests are final once submitted, they cannot be edited or canceled." Trading while one is pending is allowed but risky: "If you take a trade before your payout is processed that drops your balance below the required amount, your request may be denied." The qualifying balance has to survive until the request is processed, not just until it is submitted.

**Payout eligibility is conditional on rule compliance, not just numbers.** "You must not violate any Trading Rules, Terms & Conditions, or the LucidFlex Account Agreement."

**Circumventing platform position limits may wipe the day's profit.** "Each platform/datafeed provider has guardrails in place to prevent/block this a much as possible, however users whom intentionally try to, or succeed in circumventing these limits may result in having profit wiped for the day" ("LucidFlex Scaling Plan"). The same article adds: "Our back-end systems track and adjust balances for this. If we see repeated behavior attempting to get around the limits, we may review your account."

## Live Transition

LucidFlex funded accounts transition into the shared **LucidLive** program at Lucid's discretion, documented in full in [`live.md`](live.md). LucidFlex's own dedicated payout article independently confirms the "Traders may take up to 5 payouts from each LucidFlex account after which they will be moved live" structure referenced only generically in the shared live article. Every LucidFlex funded account with at least one payout is moved to its own live account on transition, starting at $0 with an EOD drawdown, no Daily Loss Limit, and daily payout eligibility. The one-time Live Bonus in `live.md` is stated for "the first time a trader is moved live under the new program" and names no plan as eligible or excluded; no LucidFlex article excludes it, none names LucidFlex as eligible, and whether a LucidFlex account qualifies is Unconfirmed (see `live.md`'s Not Confirmed).

## Not Confirmed By This Source

- **Starting Balance (Evaluation and Sim Funded, all tiers)** — no cited LucidFlex article states a Starting Balance at either stage; the Evaluation Account table lists account sizes with no balance field, and no Lucid sim-funded article read for this tree states a funded starting balance for any plan (the only "$0 starting balance" statement in the help center is for LucidLive accounts, "New Live Structure"). Do not assume the account-size label is the starting balance, and do not assume a $0 funded start.
- **Daily Loss Limit dollar amounts, help-center silence vs. the pasted homepage config** — "LucidFlex Customization" and "LucidFlex Funded Account" confirm a DLL toggle exists, but neither those two articles nor "LucidFlex Evaluation Account" state a dollar figure for any tier, and the sitemap lists exactly 8 LucidFlex articles, none a DLL article, unlike LucidPro, LucidDirect and LucidDaily, which each have one. The pasted homepage `LucidPricingConfig` carries per-tier `dll`/`dll_below_initial_trail` cells for LucidFlex, identical to LucidPro's ($600/$1,200/$1,800/$2,700), which are the only source for the DLL dollar cells in the tables above. What the config leaves unstated is what happens to the DLL once the funded-stage MLL locks past the Initial Trail Balance: LucidPro's funded config (and LucidDirect's config) carries a `lucidscale_dll_above_initial_trail` field whose cells read "60% of Peak EOD Balance" (the "Balance" side of the profit-vs-balance conflict that `pro.md` and `direct.md` record as unresolved against the help-center DLL articles' "Highest end-of-day account profit × 60%", not a profit formula), LucidFlex's funded config has no equivalent field, and no help-center article states what, if anything, replaces the fixed DLL for LucidFlex past that point. Do not assume LucidFlex's DLL stays fixed after the lock, and do not assume it matches LucidPro's LucidScale formula by analogy.
- **Minimum Balance at Start (Evaluation)** — not stated in any source read for this file. Do not assume it equals the Starting Balance, and do not assume no such floor exists.
- **Minimum Trading Days (Evaluation)** — no cited article states a minimum-trading-days requirement for LucidFlex. "LucidFlex Evaluation Account Benefits" says "The LucidFlex evaluation 50% consistency has cushion built in, so you can pass in two days," and "LucidFlex Consistency Percentage" says the cushion exists "so that traders that are super eager to pass in two days can do so." - **One-Time Eval Fee and Reset Fee, all tiers** — the pasted `LucidPricingConfig` (dateModified 2026-09-21, user-pasted 2026-09-23; `lucidtrading.com` still answers HTTP 403) states for 50K a $136 eval list price, a $95 reset list price, a +$10 no-DLL add-on, a -$5 default-state discount, a `discountPct` of 30 and a global `couponCode` of "VAULT" (with `resetDiscountPct` 0), and states no checkout total. Do not assume any derived sum is the price shown at a real checkout, do not assume the `discountPct` or "VAULT" is or is not applied there, and do not assume the 50K components scale to the other tiers.
- **Reset or recovery of a breached Sim Funded account** — no LucidFlex article states what happens after a Sim Funded breach. The firm-wide "Inactivity Policy" ("Breached Status Policy" section) opens with an evaluation-scoped sentence ("If an evaluation is breached (has hit Max Loss Limit), it is still eligible for a reset.") and then states, with no evaluation qualifier and naming no plan or stage, "All breached accounts will be automatically deleted after 30 days if not reset." and "Traders can manually purchase a reset at any time before that 30-day period expires."
