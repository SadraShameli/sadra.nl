## Overview

LucidPro is offered in four account sizes ($25K/$50K/$100K/$150K), each with an optional purchasable Daily Loss Limit (DLL) toggle selected at checkout: "LucidPro Customization" states DLL On gives a "lower evaluation purchase price" and DLL Off a "higher evaluation purchase price" with no DLL in either phase. The pasted homepage `LucidPricingConfig` states the price components of that toggle: a $15/$20/$35/$45 (25K/50K/100K/150K) "no-DLL" add-on charged when Off, and a $5/$5/$10/$10 automatic discount kept when the toggle stays at its default state. The same config also states a `discountPct` of 30 on every size and a global `couponCode` of "VAULT". The config exposes that default as `onLabel: "ON"` on a toggle titled "Daily Loss Limit," not as a literal "DLL ON" string; this file uses "DLL ON"/"DLL OFF" as its own shorthand. The config states no checkout total, so the Evaluation table shows no eval or reset price (see Not Confirmed). For the $25,000 tier the Funded stage is an exception: two of Lucid's own articles disagree on whether a Fixed DLL exists there at all (see Not Confirmed). The Evaluation-stage 25K DLL is also a live source conflict: both dedicated LucidPro support articles give the $25,000 Evaluation tier no Fixed DLL ("None"), while the pasted homepage config gives LucidPro 25K an evaluation `dll` cell of $600 and the same priced ON/OFF "no-dll" toggle every other tier has ($15 add-on when Off); do not assume either source is the correct one without checking the live checkout. The toggle choice is locked for the life of the account: "Once selected, these settings cannot be changed for an active account. If you want a different configuration, you'll need to purchase a new LucidPro account with your preferred options" ("LucidPro Customization"). This file documents both configurations side by side wherever the toggle affects a row.

After passing evaluation, traders enter a Sim Funded stage. The Max Loss Limit (MLL) uses an End-of-Day Drawdown at both stages ("LucidPro evaluation and funded accounts use an End-of-Day Drawdown (EOD Drawdown) system to calculate the Max Loss Limit (MLL)," "LucidPro Drawdown") and locks once the account exceeds the size-specific "Initial Trail Balance"; the DLL article states the Fixed DLL is replaced by the "LucidScale DLL" once the account closes above that same Initial Trail Balance. "LucidPro Funded Account Benefits" lists "No scaling plan, access to max contract size immediately" and "No simulated payout caps"; the Payouts article separately states per-payout maximums, and no article says how the two payout statements relate (see Not Confirmed).

## How the Drawdown Works

LucidPro's Max Loss Limit (MLL) uses an End-of-Day Drawdown at both stages ("LucidPro Drawdown"): the system calculates the account's highest closing balance at the end of each session, and the MLL increases as the balance grows, up to a defined point; the article's "MLL Amount" column gives each tier's drawdown amount.

Once the account exceeds the Initial Trail Balance, the MLL locks and no longer moves ("LucidPro Drawdown"); each tier's Initial Trail Balance in that table equals the tier size + MLL Amount + $100, an observation about the table, not a formula any article states. "LucidPro Daily Loss Limit" states that once the account closes above the same Initial Trail Balance, the Fixed DLL is replaced by the LucidScale DLL, "Highest end-of-day account profit × 60% = LucidScale DLL," which "does not decrease, even if the account draws down." **Live source conflict, not resolved here:** "LucidPro Funded Account" labels the same scaling column "60% of Peak EOD Balance," not profit, and so does the pasted homepage config (its `lucidscale_dll_above_initial_trail` cells read "60% of Peak EOD Balance" for every tier). A balance includes the starting principal and a profit does not, so the two readings compute different dollar figures, and no article says which governs. The Worked Example uses the DLL article's profit-based formula and says so (see Not Confirmed).

If the account's balance ever reaches the (trailing or locked) MLL, the account is breached ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached," "LucidPro Drawdown"); whether a breached LucidPro Sim Funded account can be reset is Unconfirmed: the cited "Inactivity Policy" states, with no evaluation qualifier and naming no plan or stage, "All breached accounts will be automatically deleted after 30 days if not reset. Traders can manually purchase a reset at any time before that 30-day period expires." (see Not Confirmed). A DLL hit is different: it only pauses trading until the next session ("All DLLs at Lucid Trading are considered soft breaches, meaning you do not lose your account for hitting DLL as long as the Max Loss Limit (MLL) has not been reached.", "LucidPro Daily Loss Limit").

### Worked Example

This example uses the nominal tier-size convention of "LucidPro Drawdown"'s own table for the $50,000 tier, DLL-ON variant ($52,100 trigger, $50,100 locked value), tracked throughout; the account profit used in the LucidScale steps is the balance minus that $50,000 opening. The funded Starting Balance is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed), so the $50,000 opening is that convention, not a stated Starting Balance.

1. The funded account opens at $50,000. The $2,000 Max Loss Limit is taken here to set an initial floor of $50,000 − $2,000 = $48,000 (derived: no article states the starting MLL level). The Daily Loss Limit starts fixed at $1,200.
2. The account has a profitable stretch and closes at a new EOD high of $51,500 (a new peak, but still below the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance). The MLL is taken to trail up to $51,500 − $2,000 = $49,500 (derived: no article states a peak-minus-MLL-amount trailing formula, only that the MLL "increases as your balance grows, up to a defined point"). The DLL is still the fixed $1,200 (balance has not yet crossed $52,100).
3. The account closes at a new EOD high of $52,200, above the $52,100 Initial Trail Balance. The MLL locks at $50,100 ("Locked MLL Balance" in "LucidPro Drawdown"'s own table) and no longer moves. The account has closed above the Initial Trail Balance, so the DLL switches from its fixed $1,200 to the LucidScale DLL, "Highest end-of-day account profit × 60%." Under this example's nominal $50,000 convention the account's profit at a $52,200 close is $2,200 (a derived figure), so the DLL would be $2,200 × 0.60 = $1,320 (derived by this file, not a quoted example figure).
4. The account later closes at $54,000 (a new high), a derived account profit of $4,000 under the same convention, so the LucidScale DLL would be $4,000 × 0.60 = $2,400, the same arithmetic as the DLL article's own example (highest end-of-day profit of $4,000 gives $2,400). If the "Peak EOD Balance" labeling in "LucidPro Funded Account" were the basis instead, the figure would differ (see Not Confirmed). The MLL does not move again: it remains at the locked value of $50,100, since it already locked in step 3.
5. If the account's balance ever fell to $50,100 or below, it would reach the (locked) MLL, which the drawdown article states breaches the account ("If your account balance reached the MLL, your account will be breached"); what happens to a breached Sim Funded account afterward is Unconfirmed (see Not Confirmed).

Payouts are approved and deducted from the account balance within a few minutes; funds are disbursed to the trader's payment method within 2 business days ("LucidPro Payouts").

**Payout requests cannot be undone, and can be self-sabotaged.** "Payout requests are final once submitted, they cannot be edited or canceled." A trade taken while a request is pending can sink it: "Important: If you take a trade before your payout is processed that drops your balance into the buffer, your request may be denied." The qualifying balance has to survive until the request is processed, not just until it is submitted. Payout eligibility itself also requires rule compliance: "You must not violate any Trading Rules, Terms & Conditions, or the LucidPro Account Agreement."

## Live Transition

LucidPro funded accounts transition into the shared **LucidLive** program at Lucid's discretion, documented in full in [`live.md`](live.md). In brief: a trader enters the "live review pool" after their final ("Payout 5" in "New Live Structure"; LucidPro's own help-center payout articles state no payout count, and the pasted homepage config lists "Payouts to Live: 5" on LucidPro's funded face, see Not Confirmed) payout, after being paid a significant lifetime amount, for exceptional sim-funded performance, or if previously moved live before; actual transition timing and selection is always at the Lucid risk team's discretion, not automatic upon meeting any single threshold ("New Live Structure"). Every LucidPro funded account that has received at least one payout is moved to its own live account, starting at $0 with an EOD drawdown, no Daily Loss Limit, and daily payout eligibility. The one-time Live Bonus in `live.md` is stated for "the first time a trader is moved live under the new program" and names no plan as eligible or excluded; no LucidPro article excludes it, none names LucidPro as eligible, and whether a LucidPro account qualifies is Unconfirmed (see `live.md`'s Not Confirmed).

- **Sim Funded Consistency Rule as an ongoing trading-activity requirement, distinct from the payout-request consistency** — not stated anywhere as a separate concept. Do not assume one exists beyond the confirmed 40% payout-cycle rule.
- **Minimum Trading Days (Evaluation)** — "LucidPro Evaluation Account Benefits" lists "Pass the evaluation in one trading day" as a benefit; no cited article states a minimum-trading-days requirement for LucidPro. Do not assume the minimum is 1 day, or that none exists.
- **LucidScale DLL basis: "Highest end-of-day account profit" vs. "60% of Peak EOD Balance"** — "LucidPro Daily Loss Limit" states the formula "Highest end-of-day account profit × 60% = LucidScale DLL," with an example ("If your highest end-of-day profit is $4,000, then... $4,000 × 0.60 = $2,400").
