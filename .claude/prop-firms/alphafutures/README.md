## Overview

Alpha Futures documents four currently sold simulated-capital plans in this tree, all at the $50K size the calculator engine models: **Zero**, **Standard**, and **Advanced** (each a monthly-subscription Evaluation that, once passed, becomes a Qualified Account that does not carry an equivalent recurring fee of its own, per the Monthly Subscription article and the signed Terms and Conditions' clauses 7.4.1(e)/10.1.1/10.1.2; the product pages' own Qualified-stage pricing display still shows a recurring price, a flagged marketing-copy inconsistency rather than an unresolved tie, see Firm-Wide Rules) and **Direct** (a one-time-fee purchase that skips the Evaluation and opens as a Qualified Account on day one). Alpha Futures calls the sim-funded stage a "Qualified Account"; the plan files use the tree-wide `## Sim Funded` heading for it. Every plan uses the same End of Day trailing Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) that stops trailing at the account's starting balance, and every plan uses a 70/80/90% tiered performance-fee split by payout count at the Qualified stage, per the signed General Service Agreement's "Virtual Performance Fees" clause. The product page and help center state a flat 90%, and the signed Terms and Conditions' Schedule 2 also states a flat 90% for every plan's Qualified stage, independent of the help center; this tree resolves the conflict between the two signed documents (the General Service Agreement and the Terms and Conditions) in favor of the Agreement's tiered clause, which is what `AlphaFutures.ts` models for Zero, Standard and Advanced as of 2026-09-23. That is the engine's own modeling choice, flagged in its notes for the user to re-confirm, not a resolved sourcing-hierarchy call (see Documentation Scope).

Two products are named by Alpha Futures' own sources but are not documented here: **Premium**, discontinued on July 12, 2026 ("The Premium plan has been discontinued. All active Premium accounts are being refunded and closed automatically", Performance Fee Rules Explained (2026), inline update note), and the **Legacy Live** structure for "Legacy Live Traders before June 21" (2026), which `live.md` records only where its source labels a figure Legacy. See Documentation Scope.

## Plans

- **[Zero ($50K)](zero.md)**: $139/month Evaluation with a $3,000 target, $2,000 EOD trailing MLL, $1,000 Daily Loss Guard, no Evaluation consistency rule, 1-day minimum; Qualified stage adds a 40% consistency rule and a profit-tiered Scaling Plan; $200 minimum / $1,500 maximum per payout request; 70/80/90% tiered split by payout count (resolved 2026-09-22, engine matching as of 2026-09-23).
- **[Standard ($50K)](standard.md)**: $129/month Evaluation with a $3,000 target, $2,000 EOD trailing MLL, no Daily Loss Guard during the Evaluation, 50% Evaluation consistency rule, 2-day minimum; Qualified stage has a $1,000 Daily Loss Guard, 40% consistency rule and a Scaling Plan; $500 minimum / $3,000 maximum per request; 70/80/90% tiered split by payout count (resolved 2026-09-22, engine matching as of 2026-09-23).
- **[Advanced ($50K)](advanced.md)**: $209/month Evaluation with a $4,000 target, $1,750 EOD trailing MLL at Evaluation and $2,000 once Qualified (resolved 2026-09-22; `AlphaFutures.ts` models the Qualified-stage figure separately from the Evaluation as of 2026-09-23), no Daily Loss Guard at either stage, 40% Evaluation consistency rule, 3-day minimum; no consistency rule and no Scaling Plan once Qualified; $1,000 minimum / $15,000 maximum per request; 70/80/90% tiered split by payout count (resolved 2026-09-22, engine matching as of 2026-09-23); capped at 3 Qualified Accounts.
- **[Direct ($50K)](direct.md)**: $519 one-time fee, no Evaluation; opens as a Qualified Account with a $2,000 EOD trailing MLL, $1,000 Daily Loss Guard, 20% consistency rule and no Scaling Plan; payouts are gated by a Withdrawal Profit Target ($3,000 first, $2,000 thereafter) instead of winning days; $500 minimum / $2,000 maximum per request (one cited article states $1,500, see `direct.md`); 70/80/90% tiered split by payout count (resolved 2026-09-22).

## Live Accounts

- **[Alpha Futures LIVE Account](live.md)**: the single post-Qualified stage shared by every plan above. A trader is "called to live" at the firm's review (for example after "5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account", Path To Live Structure); all Qualified Accounts close and one Live account is issued per Qualified Account that has reached at least one payout, opening at a $0 balance with an EOD drawdown set by the size of the Qualified Account it came from ($2,000 for 50K). The trader then chooses the **Alpha Futures Live Program** (80% performance split, no salary) or the **Alpha Prime Program** (60% split plus a 12-month salary funded from 50% of the Qualified Account's sim profit, capped at $75,000). Daily, uncapped payouts; no consistency rule; no news-trading restrictions; a "cool down" period of up to 3 months after a Live account is lost. Its Drawdown Lock now resolved (locks at $0, the Live account's own starting balance, confirmed 2026-09-24); minimum payout, inactivity rule and lifetime cap remain Unconfirmed in that file. The 50K-eligible, 80%-split path is modeled in `AlphaFuturesLive.ts`.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules whose own source states a firm-wide scope, or that are confirmed in every one of the four plan files. Where `live.md` marks a rule's Live applicability Unconfirmed, that is stated on the bullet.

- **The Maximum Loss Limit is an End of Day trailing drawdown on every account, and it locks at the starting balance**: "The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is EOD (end of day) trailing on all of our accounts here at Alpha Futures. None of our accounts utilize intraday trailing drawdown you will see elsewhere in industry." and "Maximum Loss Limit stops trailing at the account starting balance on all of our accounts." It is calculated "from your account balance high at the end of each trading day" and never trails down. Breaching it at any moment, "floating equity or closed balance of account", liquidates the account; on an Evaluation the account can no longer become Qualified, on a Qualified Account it is closed ("Maximum Loss Limit (MLL)"). Confirmed in `zero.md`, `standard.md`, `advanced.md`, `direct.md` and `live.md` (Live's own lock trigger and locked value are Unconfirmed there).
- **Inactivity: at least one trade every 10 trading days**: "Trading platforms archive trading data after 10 inactive trading days. To keep your account active you must place a trade at least once every 10 trading days (does not matter size of trade)." ("Inactivity Rule", no plan qualifier). Confirmed in the four plan files; `live.md` marks its Live applicability Unconfirmed because the Live rules article describes discretionary Performance Team oversight instead.
- **Trading hours and products (Evaluation and Qualified stages)**: "Trading hours are 6PM EST to 5PM EST the next day in Futures", "all trades must be closed before 4:20PM EST every day. We will close the trades for you if you forget". Products are the CME Group exchanges (CME, CBOT, NYMEX, COMEX); MBT and MET crypto futures are tradeable but "treated as full mini contracts" ("What and When you can Trade"). `live.md` documents Live's explicit exemption: "Trades do not need to be closed by 4:20pm EST (like they do on sim accounts)."
- **No trading within 2% of a CME price limit, on every account**: "we do not allow market participation when a product is trading within 2% of a price limit" and "This prohibited conduct is enforced on ALL Alpha Futures Accounts, not just Evaluations." ("Price Limit Trading"). The same article states the limits themselves: "Equity Products ES, MES, NQ, MNQ, RTY, M2K, YM, and MYM overnight price limits are 7%."
- **Prohibited trading practices, Evaluation and Qualified stages**: "The use of AI, bots, and other automated trading mechanisms is strictly prohibited across all account types"; HFT-style systems "particularly those resulting in over 100 trades per day" are restricted; semi-automated signals are allowed only when the trader manually places and manages the trade; "Tick or Micro Scalping" (trades under 10 ticks and under 2 minutes as a clear pattern), order-book spamming, exploiting the absence of slippage, "Account Rolling"/"Account Stacking", "Reverse Trading" or hedging across accounts, group trading, and VPN use to hide an IP are all prohibited; "Profits generated from prohibited trading practices will be void" and passed Evaluations are reviewed before a Qualified Account is issued ("Prohibited Trading Practices", scoped by its own text to "Evaluation and Qualified stages"). The same article also prohibits gap and illiquidity exploitation ("Initiating trades to profit from isolated fills in gapped or illiquid markets is not allowed.") and price-level order stacking ("Simultaneously placing multiple limit orders at the same price to manipulate order fills is prohibited."), names all-or-nothing sizing as unacceptable ("We have Daily Loss Guards in place to keep traders from going on tilt, but it does not replace a stop loss."), and states the consequence for any of them: "Alpha Futures reserves the right to terminate agreements immediately in the event of any breach by the trader."
- **Copy trading**: permitted "as long as it is a single user executing the trades, and it is the single individual listed on the Alpha Futures Accounts"; "Automated, Group, and Reverse Trading are strictly prohibited." ("Copy Trading").
- **One username per individual, one individual per household**, plus a per-plan Qualified Account cap: Zero 5, Direct 5, Standard 5 ("any standard accounts prior to July 27th, 2026 are limited to 3"), Advanced 3, and "Any user that has an active Advanced Qualified account will be limited to 3." ("Maximum Allocation").
- **Evaluation billing is a monthly subscription that rebills until the trader passes or cancels; the Qualified stage does not carry an equivalent recurring fee**: the Monthly Subscription article states "Your Monthly Evaluation Fee will rebill monthly from the sign-up date until you pass. The Evaluation subscription automatically ends once you pass." and "Qualified Traders do not pay a monthly subscription." The signed Terms and Conditions corroborate this rather than contradict it: clause 7.4.1(e) scopes the recurring monthly fee specifically to the "Analyst Account" ("Analyst Accounts are offered on a subscription-basis and recurring monthly evaluation fees will be payable on an automatic basis each month until you either pass the analyst assessment phase and open a Qualified Analyst Account or you choose to cancel your subscription"), clause 10.1.1 repeats the same Analyst-Account-only scoping, and clause 10.1.2 ("Qualified Analyst Account") states only the eligibility condition for reaching that account, with no recurring-fee clause of its own (re-fetched live 2026-09-26, 16:39 UTC, HTTP 200, "Effective from July 27, 2026"). Against that, a live re-fetch of `/product/zero`, `/product/standard` and `/product/advanced` on 2026-09-26 at 16:02 UTC (re-confirmed 16:39 UTC) found every Qualified pricing card still showing a recurring $/month price ("50K Zero Qualified" / "$139/month" with "Same price, Qualified" / "Your monthly stays the same after you qualify [dash] no surprise price jumps."; "50K Standard Qualified" / "$129/month," the same sentence; "50K Advanced Qualified" / "$209/month"), and each product page's own FAQ states the counter-reading most directly: "when you pass, you move straight to your Qualified account at the same monthly price." By this tree's own sourcing hierarchy a signed document a trader accepts at signup outranks a marketing/product page, and here the signed Terms side with the dedicated help-center article, not with the product pages, so this is resolved in favor of no recurring Qualified-stage fee; the product pages' persistent Qualified-card pricing and FAQ line are treated as inaccurate or stale marketing copy on this specific point, not as a live, unresolved tie. See `SOURCES.md`'s conflict ledger and `zero.md`/`standard.md`/`advanced.md`'s own Not Confirmed sections for the residual caution note advising a trader to confirm with support before relying on this at signup. After a rebill a failed Evaluation is reset to the original starting balance; an in-progress Evaluation is offered a reset-or-continue choice 24 hours before the rebill ("Monthly Subscription"). This billing structure applies to Zero, Standard and Advanced; Direct is a one-time purchase (`direct.md`). Buying a way back in does not move the billing date: "If you do not want to wait for the rebill date to try again, you may purchase a Reset or another Evaluation, but this does not affect your current rebill date."
- **Resets**: an Evaluation Reset "allows you to return your trading account to the default settings. This includes starting Account Balance, Maximum Loss Limit, and Trading Days", may be bought at any point during the subscription, and does not move the rebill date. Qualified Account Resets exist only for Zero and Standard ("Qualified Resets are only available on Zero and Standard Accounts"), usable "2 times on a singular account, if the account has never reached payout request", within 7 days of a breach ("Reset"). Per-size prices are in each plan file.
- **KYC is required before any Qualified Account is issued or any performance fee is paid**: "Before we issue any Qualified Trader Account or process Performance Fees, we require you to verify your identity and pass our KYC screening." Government ID plus selfie via Veriff; "The use of VPS/VPN during the KYC process is not permitted"; review takes "up to 48 hours"; a trader who cannot pass KYC "will not be eligible for the Qualified Trader account and instead be issued a full refund for your passed or currently active Evaluation". The same article states which documents are refused ("Please note that we DO NOT accept paper forms of Identification or any type of permit"), that the account holder must complete KYC personally ("Any attempt to circumvent this requirement, or Third Party Involvement at all, will be considered a breach of our terms and conditions"), and that VPN/VPS use carries a stated penalty ("The usage of one of these devices can lead to account closure") ("Completing KYC").
- **Activating a Qualified Account**: complete KYC, sign the contract within 30 days of passing ("You have 30 days to sign this contract from passing your Evaluation, after that is expires"), and, for Advanced accounts purchased before July 8, 2026, pay the activation fee ("Activating your Qualified Account"). The two activation articles disagree on delivery time: "You will receive your Qualified Account in 24 hours or less after these steps are completed!" ("Activating your Qualified Account") versus "the Qualified Trader will receive access to their account in 1-2 business days" ("Activation Fee"); neither is resolved here. The Evaluation account itself does not stay open: "Your account will be closed after reaching your Profit Target" ("Activating your Qualified Account").
- **Payouts are paid in USD by ACH (US bank accounts), Wire Transfer, SWIFT, Wise or Rise**; Rise requires a signed agreement before the first payout ("Payout Methods"). Once approved, "an Alpha Futures payout is paid in five business days or less" ("Payout Update: 5 Business Days or Less", main-site post); the older "48 business hours or less" figure still on the How It Works page is treated as superseded in every plan file.
- **Restricted countries**: 61 countries are listed as restricted; "To be able to trade with us, you need to be either a resident or a citizen of a non-restricted country. Please note: even if only visiting or traveling through any of the countries below, trading is not permitted." ("Countries with Limitations"). The list includes, among others, Bulgaria, China, Croatia, Malaysia, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines, Romania, Russia, Slovenia, Turkey, Ukraine, Venezuela and Vietnam.
- **Third-party payments are refused**: "we do not accept payments made via a third-party card or account. All transactions related to assessment purchases and account trading must be conducted in the name of the cardholder", cross-checked against the KYC profile ("Third Party Payments").
- **Platforms**: AlphaTrader (in-house, web), TradingView (via Plus500), WealthCharts, Deepchart by Volumetrica (complimentary "OrderFlow" package) and Quantower (desktop, dxFeed data); "platform choices are not interchangeable after purchase" ("Trading Platforms"). Every platform requires signing a Non-Professional Agreement before real-time data is enabled (the four "Getting Started" articles and "How to trade with TradingView").
- **Crypto purchases**: Evaluation Accounts may be bought via CoinPayments in ETH, USDT (ERC-20 or TRC-20), USDC (ERC-20 or Solana) or SOL; "Automatic billing is not possible via crypto", so rebills and resets must be paid manually from the dashboard. Three crypto-specific risks are stated in the same article: the quoted payment window is short ("An address and amount will then be provided for the payment to be made within a one hour time frame"), the wrong chain loses the funds ("Sending on the wrong network may result in the funds being lost. Alpha has no way of retrieving these funds for the trader."), and underpayment means no account at all ("Sending less than the amount stated during the checkout will result in the trading account not being issued and an email will be received from CoinPayments within 48 hours outlining steps to claim these funds back.") ("Crypto Payments").
- **Cooling-off: a 14-day cancellation right that is waived on the first trade**: the Terms and Conditions state "if you want to exercise your 14-day cooling off rights (“Cooling-Off Rights”) you must notify Alpha’s Customer Service Team ... within 14 days from opening your Alpha Futures Account", and that "You consent to the waiving of your Cooling-Off Rights at the time that you access your Alpha Futures Account to place your first virtual trade." The separate main-site Return Policy page instead states flatly "All sales are final, no refunds will be issued," with no exception; the two pages are not reconciled by anything read this pass.
- **Non-disparagement, enforceable by termination**: the Terms and Conditions state the User "irrevocably agrees ... not to disparage or denigrate Alpha in any manner that might be harmful to Alpha’s reputation or business interests", and that "Alpha reserves the right to terminate a Users access to the Site and/or the Services in the event of a breach of this condition 26.1."
- **Qualified Accounts must maintain the same level of risk**: the Terms and Conditions state "a Qualified Analyst is required to maintain the same level of risk on a Qualified Analyst Account (i.e. if a Qualified Analyst had a $100k exposure, they will need to maintain a $100k exposure on their Qualified Analyst Account), a breach of the risk will result in instant termination of the Services and the closure of your Qualified Analyst Account". No help-center article read this pass states this rule, and no plan file documents it.
