## Overview

The LIVE stage is Alpha Futures' post-Qualified program: per Path To Live Structure, "the Traders called to LIVE are trading real live capital and markets" rather than the simulated funds used at the Evaluation and Qualified stages. That article names no plan, only "Qualified Account" (see Not Confirmed). This file documents only that post-Qualified LIVE stage. There is no separate Evaluation step within Live itself: entry into Live is a transition out of a Qualified Account (see below), so, unlike a normal Evaluation-to-Funded plan file, this file has no `## Evaluation` section.

A trader called to Live has all of their Qualified Accounts closed and receives one Live account per "eligible" Qualified Account (an eligible Qualified Account is defined as one that has reached at least one payout). From there the trader chooses between two paths with different profit splits and different additional benefits, described in the Payouts section below.

## How the Drawdown Works

Alpha Futures' Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing drawdown applied firm-wide: "The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is EOD (end of day) trailing on all of our accounts here at Alpha Futures. None of our accounts utilize intraday trailing drawdown you will see elsewhere in industry... Maximum Loss Limit stops trailing at the account starting balance on all of our accounts." (Maximum Loss Limit (MLL).) The article says "on all of our accounts"; Path To Live Structure lists "EOD Style Drawdown" for Live accounts, and Live Cool Down Period says a Live account can be lost by "hitting their MLL." Neither Live article states how the Live floor is calculated, so applying the firm-wide calculation to Live (the floor calculated from the account's EOD balance high, rising on a new EOD high, never trailing down on a losing day, and enforced against floating or closed balance: "If you break MLL at any point, floating equity or closed balance of account, your account will be liquidated.") rests on the article's "on all of our accounts" wording.

A Live account opens at a $0 starting balance (per Eligible Qualified Account size, see the table above) with its own EOD Drawdown amount, set by the size of the Qualified Account it converted from ($1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500 for 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K). Applying the general MLL formula stated for Alpha Futures accounts (floor = EOD balance high minus the drawdown amount, until the floor reaches the account's own starting balance, after which it stops trailing), a Live account's floor would open at $0 minus its drawdown amount and lock at $0 once EOD balance reaches the drawdown amount. That application of the firm-wide rule ("Maximum Loss Limit stops trailing at the account starting balance on all of our accounts," re-fetched live 2026-09-24, HTTP 200, unchanged) to Live is this file's own and is Unconfirmed (see the Drawdown Lock row above and Not Confirmed); the size table's column headers, "Contracts (before MLL reaches $0 balance)" and "Contracts (after MLL reaches $0 balance)", refer to the MLL reaching $0 balance but state no trigger. See the Worked Example below.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the Live account's literal balance starting at $0 (matching the $0 shown in the Live Account Start Balance column of the source's own size table for a 50K Qualified Account), and never switches to a nominal-balance convention.

1. A trader holds a 50K Qualified Account that has reached at least one payout ("Eligible Qualified Account"), and satisfies one of the call-to-live conditions, for example "5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account."
2. The trader is called to live: the Qualified Account closes, and the trader receives one Live account with a Live Account Start Balance of $0 and a Live Account EOD Drawdown of $2,000 (the 50K row of the size table above).
3. At account open, the EOD trailing MLL floor would be $0 (starting balance) minus $2,000 (drawdown amount), equal to −$2,000 (derived: this file's application of the firm-wide MLL rule to the two source-stated figures in step 2, Unconfirmed for Live, see Not Confirmed).
4. Maximum position size at open (before the MLL reaches $0 balance) is 2 mini / 20 micro contracts; this expands to 4 mini / 40 micro once the MLL reaches $0 balance (50K row of the size table above).
5. The trader chooses the Alpha Futures Live Program path rather than Alpha Prime: profit split is 80%, and withdrawals are daily and uncapped on gains above the $0 starting balance.
6. Under this file's own application of the firm-wide MLL rule to Live (Unconfirmed for Live, see Not Confirmed): when the account's EOD balance high reaches $2,000 (its $0 starting balance plus the $2,000 drawdown amount), the MLL stops trailing and locks at $0, the account's own starting balance ("Maximum Loss Limit stops trailing at the account starting balance on all of our accounts."). From that point the floor never moves again for the life of the account, and the contract limit expands from 2 mini / 20 micro to 4 mini / 40 micro (step 4 above).

## Live Transition

**Eligibility triggers to be called to Live (Path To Live Structure):** "A trader may be reviewed or called to live immediately if they have met one or more of the following conditions: 5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account; A significant amount of lifetime performance fees; Outstanding performance in a Qualified Account; Former member of the Generic Live Program or Alpha Prime Program." A separate, main-site FAQ (`alpha-futures.com/how-it-works`) states a different-looking trigger for reaching "Alpha Prime" specifically: "Once your simulated Qualified Account reaches +$40,000 in payable balance, or after 5 payout cycles, our team reviews your trading history for the opportunity to move to Alpha Prime." Whether "5 payout cycles" is the same fact as the help centre's "5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account," and whether the "$40,000 payable balance" figure is an additional, undocumented-in-the-help-centre trigger, is not resolved here. See Not Confirmed.

**No published automatic transfer threshold (checked 2026-09-26):** none of Path To Live Structure, LIVE Account Rules and Parameters, `how-it-works`, or the Terms and Conditions states a single-day or cumulative-dollar figure that automatically moves a trader to Live without a discretionary review; every condition quoted above is framed as something that makes a trader eligible to be "reviewed or called," not a number that triggers a transfer on its own. This was checked directly against those four pages as part of the 2026-09-26 re-verification pass (`firm-catalog-recheck.md`); it is a confirmed absence, not an unsearched gap.

When called to live, all of a trader's Qualified Accounts close, one Live account is issued per eligible Qualified Account, and the trader chooses between the Alpha Futures Live Program and the Alpha Prime Program (Overview and Live Account table above). The choice is not cost-free on either side. Taking the 80% Alpha Futures Live Program forfeits the Qualified-stage payout and the salary: "Traders that decide to go with the Alpha Futures Live Program will be sacrificing any Qualified Account sim profits, salary, as well as forgoing any of the Alpha Prime resources." Declining both programs ends the relationship entirely: "Traders that deny our live program(s) will sacrifice all relations with any branch of the Alpha Group." (Path To Live Structure)

**Losing a Live account and the "cool down" period** (Live "Cool Down" Period): "All live traders face a 'cool down' period if their live account is lost. Live Traders can lose their live account by hitting their MLL, or those in the Prime Program that are abusing the program to receive their salary rather than committing to their trading career will have it revoked. The baseline target for a live account is 3 months. The longer a trader is live, the shorter this cool down is. So, if a trader is only live for one day, they will have a full 3 month 'cool down'. If a trader is live for 2 months then loses their account, they would only have a 1 month cool down period." No formula is stated for durations other than these two worked anchor points (1 day live leads to a 3 month cool down; 2 months live leads to a 1 month cool down). See Not Confirmed.

**Returning to sim / next Live account:** "Live traders may return to sim trading after the cool down period is complete, but anything over $10,000 in payout requests will go towards funding their next live account(s)." (Live "Cool Down" Period.)
