## Overview

The LIVE stage is Alpha Futures' post-Qualified program: traders who have proven themselves on a Qualified Account (Zero, Standard, Advanced, or Direct) are transitioned onto real trading capital rather than the simulated funds used at the Evaluation and Qualified stages. This file documents only that post-Qualified LIVE stage. There is no separate Evaluation step within Live itself: entry into Live is a transition out of an already-passed, already-paying Qualified Account (see below), so, unlike a normal Evaluation-to-Funded plan file, this file has no `## Evaluation` section.

A trader called to Live has all of their Qualified Accounts closed and receives one Live account per "eligible" Qualified Account (an eligible Qualified Account is defined as one that has reached at least one payout). From there the trader chooses between two paths with different profit splits and different additional benefits, described in the Payouts section below.

## How the Drawdown Works

Alpha Futures' Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is an End-of-Day (EOD) trailing drawdown applied firm-wide: "The Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) is EOD (end of day) trailing on all of our accounts here at Alpha Futures. None of our accounts utilize intraday trailing drawdown you will see elsewhere in industry... Maximum Loss Limit stops trailing at the account starting balance on all of our accounts." (Maximum Loss Limit (MLL).) Path To Live Structure confirms Live accounts use this same EOD mechanic ("EOD Style Drawdown"), and Live Cool Down Period confirms a Live account can be lost by "hitting their MLL." Both are consistent with Live sharing the same MLL mechanic used at the Evaluation and Qualified stages: the floor is calculated from the account's EOD balance high, rises on a new EOD high, and never trails down on a losing day, but is enforced against floating or closed balance at any point intraday ("If you break MLL at any point, floating equity or closed balance of account, your account will be liquidated.").

A Live account opens at a $0 starting balance (per Eligible Qualified Account size, see the table above) with its own EOD Drawdown amount, set by the size of the Qualified Account it converted from ($1,000 / $2,000 / $3,000 / $4,500 for 25K / 50K / 100K / 150K). Applying the general MLL formula stated for Alpha Futures accounts (floor = EOD balance high minus the drawdown amount, until the floor reaches the account's own starting balance, after which it stops trailing), a Live account's floor would open at $0 minus its drawdown amount. No source read for this file, however, states the literal Live-specific dollar figure at which this floor stops trailing and locks, or gives a worked example computing it for a Live account. This is not assumed here as fact; see Not Confirmed.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the Live account's literal balance starting at $0 (matching the $0 shown in the Live Account Start Balance column of the source's own size table for a 50K Qualified Account), and never switches to a nominal-balance convention.

1. A trader holds a 50K Qualified Account (Zero, Standard, or Advanced) that has reached at least one payout ("Eligible Qualified Account"), and satisfies one of the call-to-live conditions, for example "5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account."
2. The trader is called to live: the Qualified Account closes, and the trader receives one Live account with a Live Account Start Balance of $0 and a Live Account EOD Drawdown of $2,000 (the 50K row of the size table above).
3. At account open, the EOD trailing MLL floor is $0 (starting balance) minus $2,000 (drawdown amount), equal to −$2,000. This re-derives cleanly from the two source-confirmed figures in step 2.
4. Maximum position size at open (before the MLL reaches $0 balance) is 2 mini / 20 micro contracts; this expands to 4 mini / 40 micro once the MLL reaches $0 balance (50K row of the size table above).
5. The trader chooses the Alpha Futures Live Program path rather than Alpha Prime: profit split is 80%, and withdrawals are daily and uncapped on gains above the $0 starting balance.
6. No source read for this file states the literal dollar figure at which this account's MLL stops trailing and locks (that is, a Live-specific trigger and locked-floor value). This example stops here rather than computing one. See Not Confirmed.

## Live Transition

**Eligibility triggers to be called to Live (Path To Live Structure):** "A trader may be reviewed or called to live immediately if they have met one or more of the following conditions: 5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account; A significant amount of lifetime performance fees; Outstanding performance in a Qualified Account; Former member of the Generic Live Program or Alpha Prime Program." A separate, main-site FAQ (`alpha-futures.com/how-it-works`) states a different-looking trigger for reaching "Alpha Prime" specifically: "Once your simulated Qualified Account reaches +$40,000 in payable balance, or after 5 payout cycles, our team reviews your trading history for the opportunity to move to Alpha Prime." Whether "5 payout cycles" is the same fact as the help centre's "5 performance fees on a single Qualified Account," and whether the "$40,000 payable balance" figure is an additional, undocumented-in-the-help-centre trigger, is not resolved here. See Not Confirmed.

When called to live, all of a trader's Qualified Accounts close, one Live account is issued per eligible Qualified Account, and the trader chooses between the Alpha Futures Live Program and the Alpha Prime Program (Overview and Live Account table above). The choice is not cost-free on either side. Taking the 80% Alpha Futures Live Program forfeits the Qualified-stage payout and the salary: "Traders that decide to go with the Alpha Futures Live Program will be sacrificing any Qualified Account sim profits, salary, as well as forgoing any of the Alpha Prime resources." Declining both programs ends the relationship entirely: "Traders that deny our live program(s) will sacrifice all relations with any branch of the Alpha Group." (Path To Live Structure)

**Losing a Live account and the "cool down" period** (Live "Cool Down" Period): "All live traders face a 'cool down' period if their live account is lost. Live Traders can lose their live account by hitting their MLL, or those in the Prime Program that are abusing the program to receive their salary rather than committing to their trading career will have it revoked. The baseline target for a live account is 3 months. The longer a trader is live, the shorter this cool down is. So, if a trader is only live for one day, they will have a full 3 month 'cool down'. If a trader is live for 2 months then loses their account, they would only have a 1 month cool down period." No formula is stated for durations other than these two worked anchor points (1 day live leads to a 3 month cool down; 2 months live leads to a 1 month cool down). See Not Confirmed.

**Returning to sim / next Live account:** "Live traders may return to sim trading after the cool down period is complete, but anything over $10,000 in payout requests will go towards funding their next live account(s)." (Live "Cool Down" Period.)
