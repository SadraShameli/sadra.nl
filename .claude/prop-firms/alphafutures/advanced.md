## Overview

Advanced is one of Alpha Futures' Evaluation-to-Qualified products, sold as a monthly subscription rather than a one-time fee. To pass the Advanced Evaluation a trader must reach and maintain the profit target ("the trader must reach (+ maintain) the profit target", Advanced Account Overview) while staying inside the Maximum Loss Limit and satisfying a 40% consistency rule; the Consistency Rule article states this is checked so that "profits from one single trading day cannot be larger than 40% of your net profits made." This file documents the $50K size, the size modeled in the simulator engine (`src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/alphafutures/AlphaFutures.ts`); Advanced is also offered at other sizes not covered here (see Not Confirmed).

Relative to Alpha Futures' other plans, the Advanced Account Overview article lists Advanced's own stated benefits: the industry's largest per-request withdrawal cap ($15,000), no Daily Loss Guard on either stage, no Consistency Rule once Qualified, no Scaling Plan (full contract limit available immediately), and, per its own marketing, a flat 90% profit split "from the start, no tiered system" (the signed General Service Agreement instead ties this to a tiered virtual-payout schedule; see the Profit Split row in the Sim Funded table below). Advanced also no longer carries an activation fee on newer accounts (see the note under the Sim Funded table).

## How the Drawdown Works

Alpha Futures calls its trailing drawdown the Maximum Loss Limit (MLL), and it is the same end-of-day mechanic on both the Evaluation and Qualified stages: "The Maximum Loss Limit is calculated from your account balance high at the end of each trading day, not intraday equity high like many others out there." The floor trails up on a new EOD balance high and does not move down on a losing day. Once the floor's trailed value reaches the account's own starting balance, it stops trailing there permanently for the rest of the account's life: "Maximum Loss Limit stops trailing at the account starting balance on all of our accounts."

Breaching the MLL, on either a floating (open) or closed basis, liquidates the account immediately: "If you break MLL at any point, floating equity or closed balance of account, your account will be liquidated. This means any attempted orders after the breach will not execute." On an Evaluation Account this ends that attempt at Qualification (a Reset or the next monthly rebill starts a fresh attempt); on a Qualified Account it closes the account outright: "Hitting MLL on an Alpha Futures Qualified Account means the account has been breached and will be closed." Per the Reset article, Qualified-stage resets are not offered for Advanced at all ("Qualified Resets are only available on Zero and Standard Accounts"), consistent with the product page's 50K Advanced Qualified row, which lists a Resets field of None.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the account's full nominal balance throughout (e.g., a $50,000 account showing $4,000 profit reads as a $54,000 balance), not a $0-based profit figure, and never switches to a $0-based convention.

**Evaluation:**

1. Trader starts a 50K Advanced Evaluation ($209 for the first month), with a $4,000 profit target, a $1,750 Maximum Loss Limit, no Daily Loss Guard, and the 40% consistency rule.
2. The account opens at a $50,000 balance. The MLL floor starts at $50,000 minus $1,750, so $48,250.
3. The Consistency Rule article's own guidance for this exact size and target states the "Recommended Best/Largest Day" is "$1,600 or less" (40% of the $4,000 target), so the trader keeps every single day's profit at or under $1,600 while building toward the target.
4. Before the target is reached, the EOD account-balance high crosses $51,750 ($50,000 plus the $1,750 MLL): the floor, which had trailed up to $50,000, stops trailing and locks there for the rest of the Evaluation, per the firm-wide rule that the MLL "stops trailing at the account starting balance on all of our accounts."
5. The trader's EOD account balance reaches $54,000 ($50,000 starting balance plus the $4,000 profit target), satisfying both the profit target and, because no single day exceeded $1,600, the 40% consistency rule, across at least the 3 minimum trading days required for Advanced. The Evaluation is passed.

**Qualified:**

6. The trader activates the Advanced Qualified Account. This example assumes a fresh $50,000 starting balance for the Qualified stage (see Not Confirmed for whether the balance actually carries over from the Evaluation's $54,000 ending balance). At this stage the Maximum Loss Limit is $2,000, this file's currently-documented figure per the Terms and Conditions' Schedule 2 for "Advanced Qualified" (reopened as an unresolved conflict against a live product-page figure of $1,750 as of 2026-09-26; see the Sim Funded table's Drawdown Amount row and Not Confirmed), so the MLL starts trailing from a floor of $48,000 ($50,000 minus $2,000). There is still no Daily Loss Guard, and there is now no Consistency Rule at all.
7. If the EOD account-balance high reaches $52,000 ($50,000 plus the $2,000 MLL), the MLL stops trailing and locks permanently at $50,000, the account's own starting balance (see the Drawdown Lock row above for sourcing).
8. After 5 winning trading days of $200 or more profit each (not required to be consecutive), the trader may request a performance fee: minimum $1,000, up to 50% of account profit, capped at $15,000 per request, with no Consistency Rule to satisfy first. Because this would be the account's first virtual payout, the trader receives 70% of the requested amount under the signed General Service Agreement's tiered "Virtual Performance Fees" schedule (see the Profit Split row below).

From there, you can choose the Alpha Prime Program (60% performance split plus a 12 month monthly salary, weekly strategy calls, and access to the London trading floor) or the Generic Live Program (80% performance split, no salary, no Alpha Prime resources)."
