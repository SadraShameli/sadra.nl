## Overview

Apex Trader Funding documents three simulated-capital product lines in this tree plus one live stage. Every product runs the same two-stage shape: buy an **Evaluation**, pass it, pay a **PA Activation Fee** (EOD and Intraday: within 7 calendar days of passing; Legacy: no deadline stated, see `legacy.md`), and trade a **Performance Account (PA)**, Apex's own name for the simulated funded stage (the plan files use the tree-wide `## Sim Funded` heading for it). The three lines differ in how the drawdown threshold is measured: **EOD** recalculates it once per trading day at close, **Intraday** tracks the real-time peak balance including unrealized profit, and **Legacy** is the pre-March-2026 generation, which one Apex article calls retired for new purchase while the Legacy product page advertises it as Available Now (limited time); that conflict is unresolved (see `legacy.md`).

Apex also runs an invitation-only **Live Prop Trading Program**, a separate live-capital department with its own account structure, documented in `live.md`. It is not purchased and cannot be qualified for on demand: Apex selects traders at its discretion, closes their simulated accounts, and starts the trader with one $0-balance live account, with up to five live accounts possible (see `live.md`).

## Plans

- **[EOD Trailing Drawdown](eod.md)**: four sizes (25K/50K/100K/150K), one-time Evaluation purchase with 30 calendar days of access, no minimum trading days, an Evaluation consistency rule that is a live conflict (the articles say Not Applied, the catalog says 30), a fixed Evaluation Daily Loss Limit, and an end-of-day trailing threshold that on the PA stops trailing and locks at Starting Balance plus $100 (the dollar figure is confirmed for 50K only). The PA adds profit-tiered contract and Daily Loss Limit scaling, a 50% consistency rule, a Safety Net, and a six-payout lifetime cap at 100% split, per the dedicated EOD articles (the How It Works page conflicts on split, cadence, cap, daily drawdowns and contract scaling, see `eod.md`).
- **[Intraday Trailing Drawdown](intraday.md)**: the $50,000 size (the size the engine models), same two-stage shape, but the threshold follows the real-time peak balance including unrealized profit. No Evaluation Daily Loss Limit at all; the Evaluation-stage lock behaviour differs by trading platform. PA is profit-tiered with a 50% consistency rule, a $52,100 Safety Net, and a six-payout cap at 100% split, per the dedicated Intraday articles (the How It Works page conflicts on split, cadence and cap, see `intraday.md`).
- **[Legacy](legacy.md)**: the pre-March-1-2026 generation, "retired ... no longer available for purchase" per Legacy Products Overview, while the Legacy product page advertises it as "Available Now" (live conflict, see `legacy.md`). A recurring monthly Evaluation subscription (not a one-time fee), resettable Evaluations, a 7-trading-day minimum, a full-account trailing drawdown, a 30% PA consistency rule per Legacy's own articles (whether the firm-wide 50% article also binds a Legacy PA is a live conflict, see `legacy.md`), a 30% Negative P&L (MAE) rule, a one-direction rule, a 5:1 risk-reward rule, and a tiered 100%-then-90% profit split. Documented at the $50,000 size.
- Every plan file documents its own per-size figures where the source gives them; only the $50,000 tier is modeled by the engine.

## Live Accounts

- **[Apex Live Prop Trading Program](live.md)**: the firm's single live stage, reached by invitation only. On selection, all Evaluation Accounts close and all Performance Accounts are deactivated, with positive simulated balances tracked in a Bonus Vault. The live account starts at a $0 balance with a $3,000 end-of-day drawdown, scales through four profit-based levels, pays a flat 90% trader / 10% Apex split, and locks its drawdown at +$100 once the profit balance reaches +$3,100. A trader cannot hold Live and simulated accounts at the same time, and declining the invitation deactivates the Apex user account entirely. No source read names the EOD or Intraday PA as a feeder, states a threshold, or gives a timeline; each plan file records that as Unconfirmed. For Legacy, its Consistency Rules article states that Legacy PA trades are mirrored into a live account via API and that the trader is later contacted about a live account, without stating whether that is this program (see `legacy.md`).

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules whose own source states a firm-wide scope, or that are confirmed identically in every plan file documented above.

- **20 active Performance Accounts is the hard ceiling, counted per household, not per login**: "The total limit is 20 active PA accounts across all people in the same house, companies, and connections, whether the accounts are with Rithmic, Tradovate, or WealthCharts." Exceeding it makes a trader "ineligible for payout", may trigger "forfeiture of all funds" and "closure of all performance accounts", and can result in a "permanent ban from Apex Trader Funding" ("How Many Paid/Funded Accounts Am I Allowed to Have?"). Legacy, EOD and Intraday PAs all count toward the same 20. The same article confirms copy trading across a trader's own PAs is allowed under their personal and business names, but the hedging rule still applies: "Copy trading across PA accounts under your personal name and business name is allowed. However, the hedging rule still applies; all PAs must trade in the same direction and not hedge against correlated assets."
- **One Personal User Account and at most one Business User Account per person**: "Each person is allowed to create only ONE Personal User Account for personal use", a business entity may have its own account if "solely owned and controlled by the trader", and "Creating multiple personal or business User Accounts beyond the allowed limits is a violation of Apex Trader Funding's policy. Any additional accounts will be closed, and any profits or affiliate commissions from those accounts will be forfeited." A personal account cannot be converted to a business account after the fact ("Registering as a Person or Business"). **Opening any further account while an existing one carries special provisions is itself a separate, bannable circumvention attempt**: "You also may not create additional accounts in the household or associated parties if an existing user account has special provisions, such as an evaluation restriction or trader probation, as this would be viewed as an attempt to circumvent account provisions on an existing account" ("Prohibited Activities"). **Minimum age is 18**: "Traders need to be 18 years or older to sign up" ("Creating Your Account"). **A business account's ownership structure must stay above the 80% threshold on an ongoing basis, not just at signup**: "Any change in ownership that results in the majority shareholder holding less than 80% of the company will be considered a violation of this policy. If this occurs, the account will be deemed in breach of our terms and will be forfeited immediately without refund or recourse." **Non-profits are categorically ineligible**: "Apex accounts are available only to individual and for-profit business customers. Churches, charities, and other non-profit or tax-exempt organizations aren't eligible to open or maintain accounts." **A "professional" classification carries real added cost**: "Being classified as a 'professional' may result in additional exchange fees for you or your entity. For example, fees will be at least $115 per month for just CME professional data. Other additional exchange fees may apply."
- **All trades must be flat before 4:59 PM ET** (the Live program states a stricter 4:50 PM ET, see `live.md`), with earlier closing times for agricultural markets: "It is your responsibility to ensure that your account is flat before 4:59 PM ET. Apex will not take responsibility for trades that are left open throughout market close." ("Futures Trading Times"). All times firm-wide are Eastern ("What Time Zone Do You Operate In?").
- **50% consistency on payouts, stated for the EOD and Intraday PAs**: "no single trading day accounts for more than 50% of your total accumulated profit at the time of a payout request", measured "since your last approved payout, or since account inception if no payout has been made", resetting after each approved payout. Failing it does not breach the account: "The account remains active, and you may continue trading until the percentage falls below 50%." ("50% Consistency Requirement"). Legacy's own articles state 30% for its PA, and whether this 50% article also binds a Legacy PA is unresolved (see `legacy.md`).
- **Prohibited activities, stated without a stage or product qualifier** ("Prohibited Activities"): account sharing; paying with a card or bank account not in your own name; bad-faith chargebacks; false or misleading identity, residency or financial information; using "VPNs, proxy servers, cloud servers, anonymizing tools" to disguise identity or location or evade geographic restrictions; manipulating Apex systems, trade logs or communications; trading without a stop loss or risk management ("All trades must have either pending or mental stop losses"); high-risk strategies such as "a five-tick profit target with a 150-tick stop loss"; "Using the Trailing Threshold as a Stop Loss"; "Stockpiling Evaluation Accounts ... to cycle through and intentionally 'blow up' accounts"; and any strategy that "fails to demonstrate consistent growth and sustainability". The same article also states, separately from the manipulation clause above: **"No Hedging of Any Kind – Directional Trading only,"** with holding both long and short positions "on the same or correlated instrument... strictly prohibited"; **"No Automation or Algorithm Usage allowed"**; and that manipulation/exploitation of the simulated environment explicitly "includ[es] High Frequency Trading (HFT) or any other exploitative strategies" and "non-directional bracket trading, where orders are left open on both sides of the market, trying to catch a lucky windfall." The no-hedging and no-automation prohibitions are independently corroborated, stated as applying "on all account types" / to "All Apex accounts," by a separate article titled "Legacy Performance Account (PA) Compliance" (Legacy-titled; the quoted sentences carry no Legacy qualifier): violating the automation ban "will result in the immediate closure of your PA or Live account and the forfeiture of all funds and balances," and hedging/correlated-instrument trading (e.g. being "short NQ and Long ES") is barred across "the same account or in other accounts in any correlated markets," covering "indices, metals, grains, currencies, or any correlating instrument... micro, mini, etc."
- **Trade violations are tracked and shown to the trader**: the dashboard's "Trade Violations" view reports "Violation Pings" and "Violation Days" and "accounts for Scaling, Hedging, and MAE"; repeated pings within one Trade ID count as a single violation ("User Summary and Trade Violations").
- **Restricted countries, enforced on presence, not just residence**: a list of 87 countries cannot be serviced "WITH New or Additional ACCOUNTS". A trader originally from a restricted country may qualify if they hold "a valid government-issued ID from an approved (non-restricted) country" and their "primary residence, mailing address, and bank account" are all in a non-restricted country. While physically in a restricted country a trader may not purchase accounts, request payouts, or place trades. "The use or attempted use of any payment method issued from a restricted country is prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate account termination ... This applies to both successful and declined transactions" ("Restricted Countries"). Canadians are eligible.
- **Payout rails differ by country**: US-based traders are paid "via ACH direct deposit to your US-based bank account" and must supply an ACH (not wire) routing number and an SSN or TIN; traders outside the US are paid "through Plane, a secure international payment platform" after an emailed invitation to link a local bank account ("Payout Method: US-Based Users", "Payout Method: International Users"). **The bank account's country must match the trader's declared residency, in both directions**: "Your bank account must be located in the same country as your declared residency in Apex. For example, if you live in Spain, your bank account must also be based in Spain. Requests using a bank account from a different country than your country of residence will be rejected." The US-side article states the mirror-image rule: a US bank account requires a US-listed residency, and "if your residency is listed as outside the US, you will not be able to use a US-based bank account for payouts." **A personal account cannot pay out to a business bank account, or vice versa**: "A Personal User Account cannot be paid out to a Business Bank Account, and vice versa" ("Registering as a Person or Business"). **Typical processing timeline**: "Apex reviews your payout within 2 business days. If approved, funds are sent within 3-4 business days. Your bank or payment provider processes the deposit, usually within 3-7 business days. Most users receive payouts within 5-11 business days total" ("Payout Method Information").
- **US tax reporting**: "U.S. taxpayers who received PA payouts or referral fees from Apex during the prior tax year will receive a Form 1099-NEC", issued through TaxBandits, usually towards the end of January. Non-US members (W-8 status) get no 1099 and report under their own jurisdiction's law ("1099 Forms and Tax Information").
- **No refunds as the general rule, with two exceptions stated in other articles**: "We do not offer refunds, whether in whole or in part, for any purchases made through our website", explicitly including PA Activation Fees and data fees, and not for platform or data errors ("Refund Policy"). Exception 1, Live: when a trader is selected for the Live program, "Refunds will be issued only for Evaluation Accounts that are active at the time of selection" and blown Evaluation Accounts "are not eligible for a refund" ("Apex Live Prop Trading Program FAQ", see `live.md`). Exception 2, Legacy: "If you pass your Evaluation Account before 5 PM ET on the day before your rebilling date and fully activate your PA account within 48 hours, you are eligible for a refund of the evaluation renewal fee (NOT the original account fee)" (quoted in `legacy.md`, whose Sources list names the Legacy Refund Policies article; the Legacy article text was not re-read this pass). No source read states how the Refund Policy's wording relates to these two exceptions.
- **Coupons apply only at checkout and never retroactively**: "Once the payment is processed, coupon codes cannot be applied retroactively, and refunds or adjustments for missed discounts will not be provided." ("Coupon Codes").
- **Commissions are deducted automatically from the account balance on every platform**, at platform-specific per-side rates (for example NQ at $1.99 per side on Rithmic, $1.55 per side on Tradovate and WealthCharts), and NinjaTrader may not reflect them without extra configuration (the three "Commissions & Instruments" articles).
- **A payout request that falls below the required minimum balance after submission is denied automatically, not held**: "If your balance falls below the required minimum after submitting a payout request, the request will be denied automatically. There is no need to cancel or edit the request. You may continue trading until eligibility is met again" ("How to Request a Payout").
- **Unsupported payment methods are gift cards, American Express cards and crypto**: "Unsupported Payment Methods: Gift cards. American Express cards. Crypto. These forms of payment are not accepted." ("Payment Methods and Checkout Errors"). Distinct from the already-documented "card must be in your own name" prohibition above.
- **An inactivity-driven PA closure is permanent, with no reinstatement and forfeiture of accrued rewards**: "If a Performance Account is closed due to inactivity: Access to the account is permanently removed. Any accumulated rewards or payout eligibility are forfeited. Closed accounts cannot be reinstated" ("Inactivity Policy on Performance Accounts (PA)"), which scopes this rule to "all Intraday and EOD PAs, as well as promotional Legacy accounts purchased after March 1st, 2026,"; the pre-March-1 Legacy PA inactivity policy is a separate article (see `legacy.md`).
- **Accounts cannot be converted between trading platforms**: "Converting between Rithmic, Tradovate, and WealthCharts is not possible due to differences in account setups, data routing, and fee structures. Each platform operates independently and requires its own dedicated account and subscription plan" ("Choosing the Right Platform"), corroborated identically by "the account setups for Rithmic, Tradovate, and WealthCharts are different, and each platform has its own fees. If you wish to switch platforms, you must sign up for a new plan specific to that platform, as accounts cannot be converted between them" ("Legacy Evaluation Rules") and, for WealthCharts specifically, by "WealthCharts evaluations that have been passed can only be converted into WealthCharts Performance Accounts (PA). If you wish to have a Tradovate or Rithmic PA, you would need to purchase and successfully pass a corresponding evaluation for that specific platform." A trader who wants to switch platforms must buy a new Evaluation on the correct platform; there are no refunds for selecting the wrong one.
- **Holiday and CME half-day sessions merge into the next full trading day for day-counting purposes, not just for Daily Loss Limit resets**: "For most market holidays and CME half-day schedules, trading activity during the holiday or half-day session will be combined with the next full trading day and counted as a single trading day. Trades will be assigned to the next full trading day for trading-day calculations and payout eligibility purposes" ("Holiday Trading"). This affects how a trader should count qualifying days toward any plan's minimum-trading-day or payout-eligibility-day requirements around a holiday.

## Documentation Scope

- **The Apex help center is a WordPress site, not an Intercom or Zendesk help center.** Its articles display no "last updated" date on the rendered page, so most ledger rows in `SOURCES.md` still record "no article-stated update date." Seven pages re-pasted on 2026-09-23 (six articles and the homepage), however, turned out to carry a `dateModified` field in their embedded schema.org JSON-LD (not rendered on the page itself); see `SOURCES.md`'s 2026-09-23 update section for the dates. The homepage's field dates the page, not the `window.productPickerConfig` catalog embedded in it, which states no date. The remaining cited articles have not been re-checked for the same field. There is a second host, `support.apextraderfunding.com` (Zendesk), which returned HTTP 403 to every direct fetch; its own "How to Contact" article states the Zendesk helpdesk was discontinued. Whether help-center articles still live there, and whether any of them contradict the current site, is **unresolved**: that host has not been read in this pass.
- **Current EOD/Intraday prices come from the homepage product catalog, not from the help center.** No help-center article states an Evaluation purchase price or a PA Activation Fee amount for the current EOD/Intraday products. Those Evaluation, 5-Pack, No Activation Fee and PA-activation prices are sourced from `window.productPickerConfig` on the homepage, the same JSON the site itself renders its Products selector from, and are labelled catalog-sourced in `eod.md` and `intraday.md`. Other dollar figures come from their own sources: Legacy's $167 to $697 monthly prices and its $125 PA-fee tooltip from the Legacy product page (the catalog holds zero Legacy records), Legacy's $140 PA fee and its reset and billing mechanics from help-center articles, and the Live program's $199 Second Chance price and approximately $140 per month data fee from the Live FAQ. Four catalog fields conflict with the help center: a `reset_fee` field (65 in the 2026-09-23 pasted snapshot, which states no date for the catalog data, previously 80 in the 2026-09-19 dump) where the articles say there are no resets; a `consistency_rule_percentage: 30` on Evaluation products where the articles say consistency is Not Applied during the Evaluation; a flat `maxpayout` per size where the articles give a per-payout-number ladder; and `renewal_cycle: 1m` alongside its own `is_subscription: False` where the articles describe a one-time 30-day purchase. The funded DP (`optimize dp`) now enforces the PA Level Daily Loss Limit on the same session-open basis as the simulator for the EOD PA; the Intraday PA is not funded-DP eligible. The engine changes made since 2026-09-19 (PA Level tiers from the prior session close, the $590/$90 EOD fees, Apex Live Levels 1-3 and the $500 live minimum) are reflected in the plan files.
