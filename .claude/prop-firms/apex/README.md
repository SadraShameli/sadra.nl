## Overview

Apex Trader Funding documents three simulated-capital product lines in this tree plus one live stage. Every product runs the same two-stage shape: buy an **Evaluation**, pass it, pay a **PA Activation Fee** within a deadline, and trade a **Performance Account (PA)**, Apex's own name for the simulated funded stage (the plan files use the tree-wide `## Sim Funded` heading for it). The three lines differ in how the drawdown threshold is measured: **EOD** recalculates it once per trading day at close, **Intraday** tracks the real-time peak balance including unrealized profit, and **Legacy** is the pre-March-2026 generation, retired for new purchase but currently re-offered as a limited-time promotion.

Apex also runs an invitation-only **Live Prop Trading Program**, a separate live-capital department with its own account structure, documented in `live.md`. It is not purchased and cannot be qualified for on demand: Apex selects traders at its discretion, closes their simulated accounts, and issues a single $0-balance live account.

## Plans

- **[EOD Trailing Drawdown](eod.md)**: four sizes (25K/50K/100K/150K), one-time Evaluation purchase with 30 calendar days of access, no minimum trading days, no Evaluation consistency rule, a fixed Evaluation Daily Loss Limit, and an end-of-day trailing threshold that locks at Starting Balance plus $100. The PA adds profit-tiered contract and Daily Loss Limit scaling, a 50% consistency rule, a Safety Net, and a six-payout lifetime cap at 100% split.
- **[Intraday Trailing Drawdown](intraday.md)**: the $50,000 size (the size the engine models), same two-stage shape, but the threshold follows the real-time peak balance including unrealized profit. No Evaluation Daily Loss Limit at all; the Evaluation-stage lock behaviour differs by trading platform. PA is profit-tiered with a 50% consistency rule, a $52,100 Safety Net, and a six-payout cap at 100% split.
- **[Legacy](legacy.md)**: the pre-March-1-2026 generation, "retired ... no longer available for purchase" as a product line but currently re-sold as a limited-time promotion. A recurring monthly Evaluation subscription (not a one-time fee), resettable Evaluations, a 7-trading-day minimum, a full-account trailing drawdown, a 30% PA consistency rule, a 30% Negative P&L (MAE) rule, a one-direction rule, a 5:1 risk-reward rule, and a tiered 100%-then-90% profit split. Documented at the $50,000 size.
- Every plan file documents its own per-size figures where the source gives them; only the $50,000 tier is modeled by the engine.

## Live Accounts

- **[Apex Live Prop Trading Program](live.md)**: the firm's single live stage, reached by invitation only. On selection, all Evaluation Accounts close and all Performance Accounts are deactivated, with positive simulated balances tracked in a Bonus Vault. The live account starts at a $0 balance with a $3,000 end-of-day drawdown, scales through four profit-based levels, pays a flat 90% trader / 10% Apex split, and locks its drawdown at +$100 once the profit balance reaches +$3,100. A trader cannot hold Live and simulated accounts at the same time, and declining the invitation deactivates the Apex user account entirely. No source names any specific plan as a feeder, states a threshold, or gives a timeline; each plan file records that as Unconfirmed.

## Firm-Wide Rules

Only rules whose own source states a firm-wide scope, or that are confirmed identically in every plan file documented above.

- **20 active Performance Accounts is the hard ceiling, counted per household, not per login**: "The total limit is 20 active PA accounts across all people in the same house, companies, and connections, whether the accounts are with Rithmic, Tradovate, or WealthCharts." Exceeding it makes a trader "ineligible for payout", may trigger "forfeiture of all funds" and "closure of all performance accounts", and can result in a "permanent ban from Apex Trader Funding" ("How Many Paid/Funded Accounts Am I Allowed to Have?"). Legacy, EOD and Intraday PAs all count toward the same 20. The same article confirms copy trading across a trader's own PAs is allowed under their personal and business names, but the hedging rule still applies: "Copy trading across PA accounts under your personal name and business name is allowed. However, the hedging rule still applies; all PAs must trade in the same direction and not hedge against correlated assets."
- **One Personal User Account and at most one Business User Account per person**: "Each person is allowed to create only ONE Personal User Account for personal use", a business entity may have its own account if "solely owned and controlled by the trader", and "Creating multiple personal or business User Accounts beyond the allowed limits is a violation of Apex Trader Funding's policy. Any additional accounts will be closed, and any profits or affiliate commissions from those accounts will be forfeited." A personal account cannot be converted to a business account after the fact ("Registering as a Person or Business"). **Opening any further account while an existing one carries special provisions is itself a separate, bannable circumvention attempt**: "You also may not create additional accounts in the household or associated parties if an existing user account has special provisions, such as an evaluation restriction or trader probation, as this would be viewed as an attempt to circumvent account provisions on an existing account" ("Prohibited Activities"). **Minimum age is 18**: "Traders need to be 18 years or older to sign up" ("Creating Your Account"). **A business account's ownership structure must stay above the 80% threshold on an ongoing basis, not just at signup**: "Any change in ownership that results in the majority shareholder holding less than 80% of the company will be considered a violation of this policy. If this occurs, the account will be deemed in breach of our terms and will be forfeited immediately without refund or recourse." **Non-profits are categorically ineligible**: "Apex accounts are available only to individual and for-profit business customers. Churches, charities, and other non-profit or tax-exempt organizations aren't eligible to open or maintain accounts." **A "professional" classification carries real added cost**: "Being classified as a 'professional' may result in additional exchange fees for you or your entity. For example, fees will be at least $115 per month for just CME professional data. Other additional exchange fees may apply."
- **All trades must be flat before 4:59 PM ET**, with earlier closing times for agricultural markets: "It is your responsibility to ensure that your account is flat before 4:59 PM ET. Apex will not take responsibility for trades that are left open throughout market close." ("Futures Trading Times"). All times firm-wide are Eastern ("What Time Zone Do You Operate In?").
- **50% consistency on payouts, on every current product's PA**: "no single trading day accounts for more than 50% of your total accumulated profit at the time of a payout request", measured "since your last approved payout, or since account inception if no payout has been made", resetting after each approved payout. Failing it does not breach the account: "The account remains active, and you may continue trading until the percentage falls below 50%." ("50% Consistency Requirement"). Legacy's PA uses 30% instead; see `legacy.md`.
- **Prohibited activities, stated without a stage or product qualifier** ("Prohibited Activities"): account sharing; paying with a card or bank account not in your own name; bad-faith chargebacks; false or misleading identity, residency or financial information; using "VPNs, proxy servers, cloud servers, anonymizing tools" to disguise identity or location or evade geographic restrictions; manipulating Apex systems, trade logs or communications; trading without a stop loss or risk management ("All trades must have either pending or mental stop losses"); high-risk strategies such as "a five-tick profit target with a 150-tick stop loss"; "Using the Trailing Threshold as a Stop Loss"; "Stockpiling Evaluation Accounts ... to cycle through and intentionally 'blow up' accounts"; and any strategy that "fails to demonstrate consistent growth and sustainability". The same article also states, separately from the manipulation clause above: **"No Hedging of Any Kind – Directional Trading only,"** with holding both long and short positions "on the same or correlated instrument... strictly prohibited"; **"No Automation or Algorithm Usage allowed"**; and that manipulation/exploitation of the simulated environment explicitly "includ[es] High Frequency Trading (HFT) or any other exploitative strategies" and "non-directional bracket trading, where orders are left open on both sides of the market, trying to catch a lucky windfall." The no-hedging and no-automation prohibitions are independently corroborated, stated as applying "on all account types" / to "All Apex accounts," by a separate PA Compliance article: violating the automation ban "will result in the immediate closure of your PA or Live account and the forfeiture of all funds and balances," and hedging/correlated-instrument trading (e.g. being "short NQ and Long ES") is barred across "the same account or in other accounts in any correlated markets," covering "indices, metals, grains, currencies, or any correlating instrument... micro, mini, etc."
- **Trade violations are tracked and shown to the trader**: the dashboard's "Trade Violations" view reports "Violation Pings" and "Violation Days" and "accounts for Scaling, Hedging, and MAE"; repeated pings within one Trade ID count as a single violation ("User Summary and Trade Violations").
- **A confirmed PA trading-rule violation suspends payouts**: "The payout request feature on your account will be temporarily disabled for 8 trading days from the date of the violation", after which eligibility returns "only if no additional rule violations occur and you complete at least 5 qualifying trading days with a minimum profit of $150 per day". Additionally "All profits generated on the trading day in which the violation occurred will be removed", and payout eligibility stays limited to post-violation profits "until you successfully complete your next approved payout request at the maximum payout amount for your account size" ("New Feature: Early Account Update Notifications"). The plan files flag that this article's own example maximums match the catalog's flat `maxpayout` field rather than their per-payout ladders. **Repeated violations, including across multiple accounts, escalate to formal probation**: "multiple instances of rule-related activity, including across multiple accounts, may result in the account being placed on probation. Probation is intended to reinforce disciplined risk management and requires strict adherence to all trading rules for a defined period before normal account status is restored." A Legacy-scoped article corroborates the same mechanic with a further consequence: "If the account does not meet the requirements during the probation period, further action may be taken, including denial of payouts, profit removal, and/or account closure" ("Legacy PA Payout Parameters").
- **Restricted countries, enforced on presence, not just residence**: a list of 87 countries cannot be serviced "WITH New or Additional ACCOUNTS". A trader originally from a restricted country may qualify if they hold "a valid government-issued ID from an approved (non-restricted) country" and their "primary residence, mailing address, and bank account" are all in a non-restricted country. While physically in a restricted country a trader may not purchase accounts, request payouts, or place trades. "The use or attempted use of any payment method issued from a restricted country is prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate account termination ... This applies to both successful and declined transactions" ("Restricted Countries"). Canadians are eligible.
- **Payout rails differ by country**: US-based traders are paid "via ACH direct deposit to your US-based bank account" and must supply an ACH (not wire) routing number and an SSN or TIN; traders outside the US are paid "through Plane, a secure international payment platform" after an emailed invitation to link a local bank account ("Payout Method: US-Based Users", "Payout Method: International Users"). **The bank account's country must match the trader's declared residency, in both directions**: "Your bank account must be located in the same country as your declared residency in Apex. For example, if you live in Spain, your bank account must also be based in Spain. Requests using a bank account from a different country than your country of residence will be rejected." The US-side article states the mirror-image rule: a US bank account requires a US-listed residency, and "if your residency is listed as outside the US, you will not be able to use a US-based bank account for payouts." **A personal account cannot pay out to a business bank account, or vice versa**: "A Personal User Account cannot be paid out to a Business Bank Account, and vice versa" ("Registering as a Person or Business"). **Typical processing timeline**: "Apex reviews your payout within 2 business days. If approved, funds are sent within 3-4 business days. Your bank or payment provider processes the deposit, usually within 3-7 business days. Most users receive payouts within 5-11 business days total" ("Payout Method Information").
- **US tax reporting**: "U.S. taxpayers who received PA payouts or referral fees from Apex during the prior tax year will receive a Form 1099-NEC", issued through TaxBandits, usually towards the end of January. Non-US members (W-8 status) get no 1099 and report under their own jurisdiction's law ("1099 Forms and Tax Information").
- **No refunds**: "We do not offer refunds, whether in whole or in part, for any purchases made through our website", explicitly including PA Activation Fees and data fees, and not for platform or data errors ("Refund Policy").
- **Coupons apply only at checkout and never retroactively**: "Once the payment is processed, coupon codes cannot be applied retroactively, and refunds or adjustments for missed discounts will not be provided." A discounted PA Activation Fee does not travel with an Evaluation purchased during a promotion ("Coupon Codes").
- **Commissions are deducted automatically from the account balance on every platform**, at platform-specific per-side rates (for example NQ at $1.99 per side on Rithmic, $1.55 per side on Tradovate and WealthCharts), and NinjaTrader may not reflect them without extra configuration (the three "Commissions & Instruments" articles).
- **A payout request that falls below the required minimum balance after submission is denied automatically, not held**: "If your balance falls below the required minimum after submitting a payout request, the request will be denied automatically. There is no need to cancel or edit the request. You may continue trading until eligibility is met again" ("How to Request a Payout").
- **Accepted payment methods are limited to Visa, Mastercard, and Discover credit or debit cards**: "Unsupported Payment Methods: Gift cards. American Express cards. Crypto. These forms of payment are not accepted." ("Payment Methods and Checkout Errors"). Distinct from the already-documented "card must be in your own name" prohibition above.
- **An inactivity-driven PA closure is permanent, with no reinstatement and forfeiture of accrued rewards**: "If a Performance Account is closed due to inactivity: Access to the account is permanently removed. Any accumulated rewards or payout eligibility are forfeited. Closed accounts cannot be reinstated" ("Inactivity Policy on Performance Accounts (PA)"), which scopes this rule to "all Intraday and EOD PAs, as well as promotional Legacy accounts purchased after March 1st, 2026," meaning every PA type documented in this tree.
- **Accounts cannot be converted between trading platforms**: "Converting between Rithmic, Tradovate, and WealthCharts is not possible due to differences in account setups, data routing, and fee structures. Each platform operates independently and requires its own dedicated account and subscription plan" ("Choosing the Right Platform"), corroborated identically by "the account setups for Rithmic, Tradovate, and WealthCharts are different, and each platform has its own fees. If you wish to switch platforms, you must sign up for a new plan specific to that platform, as accounts cannot be converted between them" ("Legacy Evaluation Rules") and, for WealthCharts specifically, by "WealthCharts evaluations that have been passed can only be converted into WealthCharts Performance Accounts (PA). If you wish to have a Tradovate or Rithmic PA, you would need to purchase and successfully pass a corresponding evaluation for that specific platform." A trader who wants to switch platforms must buy a new Evaluation on the correct platform; there are no refunds for selecting the wrong one.
- **Holiday and CME half-day sessions merge into the next full trading day for day-counting purposes, not just for Daily Loss Limit resets**: "For most market holidays and CME half-day schedules, trading activity during the holiday or half-day session will be combined with the next full trading day and counted as a single trading day. Trades will be assigned to the next full trading day for trading-day calculations and payout eligibility purposes" ("Holiday Trading"). This affects how a trader should count qualifying days toward any plan's minimum-trading-day or payout-eligibility-day requirements around a holiday.

## Documentation Scope

- **The Apex help center is a WordPress site, not an Intercom or Zendesk help center.** Its articles carry no machine-readable "last updated" date, so every ledger row in `SOURCES.md` records "no article-stated update date" rather than a date. There is a second, older host at `support.apextraderfunding.com` (Zendesk) which returned HTTP 403 to every direct fetch; its own "How to Contact" article states the Zendesk helpdesk was discontinued. Whether help-center articles still live there, and whether any of them contradict the current site, is **unresolved**: that host has not been read in this pass.
- **Prices come from the homepage product catalog, not from the help center.** No help-center article states an Evaluation purchase price or a PA Activation Fee amount for the current EOD/Intraday products. Every dollar price in this tree is sourced from `window.productPickerConfig` on the homepage, the same JSON the site itself renders its Products selector from, and is labelled catalog-sourced in the plan files. Four catalog fields contradict the help center (a `reset_fee: 80` where the articles say there are no resets, a `consistency_rule_percentage: 30` on Evaluation products where the articles say consistency is Not Applied during the Evaluation, a flat `maxpayout` per size where the articles give a per-payout-number ladder, and `renewal_cycle: 1m` alongside its own `is_subscription: False` where the articles describe a one-time 30-day purchase). Each conflict is quoted with both sources in the plan file that carries it; none was silently resolved.
- **The `apextraderfunding.com/how-it-works/` marketing page contradicts the dedicated articles** on the profit split ("Receive 100% of the first $25,000 per Account and 90% Beyond That"), payout cadence ("Two Payouts per Month") and lifetime cap ("No Total Cap on Maximum Payouts"). None of its figures is used in any table. The same fetch also contains unrelated generic SaaS-template filler, which lowers confidence that its copy is current or product-specific. Flagged in `eod.md` and `intraday.md`.
- **Legacy is documented because it is currently being sold again.** "Legacy Products Overview" states the line was retired for new purchase as of March 1, 2026, but the live Legacy products page offers it "Available Now – Limited Time" at $167/$197/$297/$497/$597/$697 per month for 25K/50K/100K/150K/250K/300K. `legacy.md` documents the $50K tier under the promotional terms and flags the inactivity-rule split between pre- and post-March-1-2026 purchases.
- **Account sizes other than the ones each file documents**: EOD covers all four sizes in its tables; Intraday and Legacy document $50,000 in full and list the other sizes' sourced figures in Not Confirmed for context. Only $50,000 is modeled by the engine.
- **Help-center articles read and excluded on content** (70 of the 129 captured pages are not cited by any plan file). By group: 33 platform connection, setup, tutorial and troubleshooting guides (Rithmic, Tradovate, WealthCharts, NinjaTrader, BookMap, ATAS, EdgePro X, Finamark, Jigsaw, MotiveWave, Quantower, Sierra Chart, Volfix, DeepCharts, plus the four general troubleshooting articles); 6 billing-mechanics articles (invoices, checkout errors, payment methods, Legacy card updates and manual renewal); 8 account-admin articles (dashboard tour, 2FA, creating an account, knowledge-base guidance, how to contact support, whitelisting emails, status updates, past webinars); 4 hardware and testimonial articles; and the Affiliate Program, Code of Conduct, and "Choosing the Right Platform" articles.
