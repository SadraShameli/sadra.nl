## Overview

The End of Day (EOD) Evaluation is a 30-calendar-day assessment, offered in four account sizes, that measures profitability under a drawdown threshold recalculated once per day at market close and then enforced in real time during the next session. There is no minimum number of trading days and no consistency or scaling requirement during the Evaluation itself; a trader may pass as soon as the profit target is reached. Passing awards a 7 calendar day window to activate an EOD Performance Account (PA) of the same size, a Simulated Funded account that adds tier-based position-size and Daily Loss Limit scaling, a 50% consistency requirement on payouts, an inactivity rule, and a drawdown lock mechanic once the threshold has trailed far enough. Apex offers this account on three trading platforms, Rithmic, Tradovate, and WealthCharts, and its own help center states that the Evaluation-stage drawdown lock behaves differently depending on platform: see How the Drawdown Works below for the two distinct mechanics rather than a single collapsed rule. Approved PA payouts are paid at a 100% split, capped at six payouts per account. Evaluation list pricing, the PA activation fee, and 5-Pack bundle pricing are not stated in any help-center article read for this file; they are documented below from the homepage's own product-picker catalog JSON, the data source the site itself uses to render checkout prices, with several catalog fields that conflict with help-center statements flagged rather than silently merged, see Not Confirmed.

**Engine fee fields, now confirmed via the catalog.** An earlier draft of this file flagged the simulator engine's 50K EOD `activation: 139` and `evalCost: 550` fields as an engine/doc mismatch, since no help-center article read for this file stated either dollar figure. The homepage product-picker catalog, now read for this file, independently confirms both figures exactly for the 50K standard-path product (`price: 550`, `pa_features.price: 139`). This is a confirmed match, not a mismatch, and does not extend to the 25K/100K/150K catalog figures below, which the engine does not model at all (it models only the 50K size for this firm).

**PA activation window (PA Activation Process & Deadline Explained):** "You have 7 calendar days from the moment the Evaluation is marked as Passed to complete the PA Activation Fee. The 7-day period begins on the date the account is officially marked as Passed after market close, not when the profit target is reached during the trading session." and "If the activation fee is not completed within 7 calendar days of the Evaluation being marked as Passed, the activation link expires permanently. The opportunity cannot be reinstated. You must pass a new Evaluation to qualify again."

**Market data can be lost between passing and PA activation.** If the just-passed Evaluation was the trader's only active Evaluation account, the passed account is immediately locked to prevent further trading, and the trader "will no longer have market data until you activate your new Performance Account or purchase another Evaluation." A trader relying on a single Evaluation account should activate promptly or keep a second Evaluation active to avoid a market-data gap during the 7-day activation window.

**Depth of Market (DOM) data fees are billed and expire differently by platform.** On Rithmic, purchased DOM "will expire on the last day of the month and will not auto-renew. You must manually purchase it again if needed, regardless of the purchase date." On Tradovate and WealthCharts, additional data feeds instead auto-renew and must be cancelled directly through that platform "if you no longer wish to be billed." Level 1 (L1) data is included in the account fee at no extra charge on all platforms, per "Evaluation Plan Fees and Access Explained."

## How the Drawdown Works

The EOD Threshold is recalculated once per trading day at 4:59:59 PM ET, based on the account's closing balance. It always trails the highest achieved EOD balance and never moves downward on a losing day. Each account begins with the threshold set at Starting Balance minus the size's Max Drawdown. Although it is only recalculated at end of day, it is enforced in real time during the next session: if the account balance touches or falls below the threshold at any point, all open positions are automatically liquidated, and the Evaluation is failed or the PA is permanently closed. The Daily Loss Limit (DLL) is a separate mechanic that pauses trading for the rest of the day if breached but never fails the account; it resets each trading day at 6:00 PM ET, except that a holiday or CME half-day session does not itself create a new DLL reset point, so the DLL carries over unreset until the following full trading day.

**Evaluation Drawdown Lock, platform-dependent:**

- Rithmic and WealthCharts: "EOD Threshold stops trailing and becomes fixed when it reaches an amount equal to the Target Profit balance," i.e. Starting Balance + Profit Target. For 50K, the source's own worked figure is a Threshold Stop Level of "$53,000" (quoted here as printed in the source, which itself renders it "$53.000" with a period), triggered "when the Highest EOD Balance closes above $55,000 ($53,000 + $2,000)." The same formula applied to the other sizes gives: 25K locked $26,500 (trigger above $27,500), 100K locked $106,000 (trigger above $109,000), 150K locked $159,000 (trigger above $163,000). Only the 50K figures are an explicit worked example in the source; the others are this file's own arithmetic from the stated formula, see Not Confirmed.
- Tradovate: "EOD Drawdown trails indefinitely with the peak EOD account balance." There is no lock at all on Tradovate Evaluations; the threshold continues rising with every new EOD high for the life of the account.

**PA Drawdown Lock:** the source's "When EOD Drawdown Stops Trailing" section, under its own "Performance Accounts" subheading, states no platform split, so this lock (Starting Balance + $100, triggered at Starting Balance + Max Drawdown + $100) is presented as applying the same way on all three platforms; see the Sim Funded table above for the per-size figures.

**PA Daily Loss Limit / Max Contracts tiers (Scaling Levels), confirmed for all four sizes:**

- $25K PA: Level 1 profit $0-$999, 1 contract, $500 DLL. Level 2 profit $1,000-$1,999, 2 contracts, $500 DLL. Level 3 profit $2,000+, 2 contracts, $1,250 DLL (max tier).
- $50K PA: Level 1 profit $0-$1,499, 2 contracts, $1,000 DLL. Level 2 profit $1,500-$2,999, 3 contracts, $1,000 DLL. Level 3 profit $3,000-$5,999, 4 contracts, $2,000 DLL. Level 4 profit $6,000+, 4 contracts, $3,000 DLL (max tier). "Scaling Levels (PA) Explained" prints this same Level 4 breakpoint as "$5,999 & Up" rather than "$6,000 & Up," a one-dollar wording inconsistency between two corroborating firm-wide articles, not adopted, see Not Confirmed; the $6,000 figure used here also matches the simulator engine's own `minProfit: 6000` breakpoint.
- $100K PA: Level 1 profit $0-$1,999, 3 contracts, $1,750 DLL. Level 2 profit $2,000-$2,999, 4 contracts, $1,750 DLL. Level 3 profit $3,000-$4,999, 5 contracts, $1,750 DLL. Level 4 profit $5,000-$9,999, 6 contracts, $2,500 DLL. Level 5 profit $10,000+, 6 contracts, $3,500 DLL (max tier).
- $150K PA: Level 1 profit $0-$1,999, 4 contracts, $2,500 DLL. Level 2 profit $2,000-$2,999, 5 contracts, $2,500 DLL. Level 3 profit $3,000-$4,999, 7 contracts, $2,500 DLL. Level 4 profit $5,000-$9,999, 10 contracts, $3,000 DLL. Level 5 profit $10,000+, 10 contracts, $4,000 DLL (max tier).

Tier Levels are set once per trading day before the session begins, based on the prior session's closing balance, and never change mid-session. A balance drop below Level 1's own threshold keeps the account at Level 1; limits do not fall further.

**Payout-request suspension for rule violations (New Feature: Early Account Update Notifications):** distinct from the standard Payouts table below, a confirmed PA Trading Rule violation triggers an account update notification and a payout request suspension of "8 trading days from the date of the violation." Afterward, payout eligibility is restored "only if no additional rule violations occur and you complete at least 5 qualifying trading days with a minimum profit of $150 per day," a flat $150/day figure, not the size-scaled Min Daily Profit used for ordinary payout eligibility (25K $100 / 50K $250 / 100K $300 / 150K $350). The same article states two further consequences: "Profit Removal: All profits generated on the trading day in which the violation occurred will be removed in accordance with Apex risk-management policy." and "Payout Eligibility Limitation: Following a violation, payout eligibility will be limited to profits earned after the violation date. This limitation remains in place until you successfully complete your next approved payout request at the maximum payout amount for your account size (for example, $2,000 for a $50K account or $2,500 for a $100K account). Partial payout requests will not satisfy this requirement." Those two example figures match the catalog's flat `maxpayout` field (50K $2,000, 100K $2,500), not the per-payout-number ladder in the Payouts table; see the catalog `maxpayout` bullet in Not Confirmed. None of this is modeled in the simulator engine.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the account's full nominal balance throughout (a $50,000 account showing $2,000 profit reads as a $52,000 balance), for the 50K size, and never switches to a $0-based convention. It uses the Rithmic/WealthCharts evaluation-lock behavior in the Evaluation phase, since that is the only variant with an explicit worked source example; under Tradovate the Evaluation threshold would keep trailing indefinitely instead of locking at Step 5 below.

**Evaluation, quoting the source's own worked progression through Day 4:**

1. Starting Balance $50,000, Max Drawdown $2,000. Initial EOD Threshold: $50,000 minus $2,000 equals $48,000.
2. Day 1 closing balance $50,800, a new EOD high. New EOD Threshold: $50,800 minus $2,000 equals $48,800.
3. Day 2 closing balance $51,600, another new high. New EOD Threshold: $51,600 minus $2,000 equals $49,600.
4. Day 3 closing balance $50,900, a losing day (below the $51,600 high). The threshold does not move down; it stays at $49,600.
5. Day 4 closing balance $52,000, a new high. New EOD Threshold: $52,000 minus $2,000 equals $50,000.
6. Day 5 (not in the source example, computed under the same confirmed rules): closing balance rises to $53,000, exactly the $3,000 profit target for a 50K account. This is still below the $55,000 Rithmic/WealthCharts lock trigger, so the threshold has not yet locked; it simply trails to $53,000 minus $2,000 equals $51,000. The Evaluation is passed at this point because the profit target is met, regardless of whether the lock has triggered.

**PA, sources read for this file do not state whether the PA's opening balance carries forward the Evaluation's ending balance ($53,000 above) or resets to the nominal Starting Balance. This example assumes a fresh $50,000 reset, see Not Confirmed:**

7. Trader activates the 50K EOD PA at a fresh $50,000 balance, $2,000 Max Drawdown (threshold again at $48,000), Level 1 tier ($1,000 DLL, 2 contracts). If the standard-path Evaluation was purchased ($550 per the catalog), a $139 PA Activation Fee is paid here; on the "No Activation Fee" path ($1,190 upfront), this step costs $0.
8. PA Day 1 closing balance $52,100, a new EOD high and exactly the lock trigger (Starting Balance + Max Drawdown + $100 equals $52,100). The threshold stops trailing and locks permanently at $50,100 (Starting Balance + $100). The account also crosses into Level 2 territory at $2,100 profit; Level 2 spans $1,500-$2,999 profit, so DLL becomes $1,000 and max contracts becomes 3 for the next session.
9. Trader trades 5 separate qualifying days (each $250 or more net profit, the 50K Min Daily Profit) and satisfies the 50% consistency rule, reaching a balance of $52,600, the Minimum Balance to Request a payout for 50K.
10. Trader requests the first payout of $1,500 (Payout #1 on the 50K ladder). New balance after the payout is $52,600 minus $1,500 equals $51,100, which is $1,000 above the locked $50,100 floor.

## Live Transition

None of this plan's own sources describes a transition out of the EOD Performance Account; profits are distributed through the payout mechanism above (100% split, up to weekly, capped at 6 payouts). Apex does run a separate, invitation-only live stage, the Apex Live Prop Trading Program, documented in the shared [live.md](live.md): per its FAQ, "Apex may move a trader into live trading at any time based on trading style, P&L, account count, speed of simulated profit accumulation, and trader history with Apex", "Evaluation Accounts and Performance Accounts are closed or deactivated when the trader is selected for live", and "The live account is not based on separate $25K, $50K, $100K, or similar simulated account sizes." No source names the EOD PA specifically as a feeder into that program, and there is no trader-triggerable qualification path; see Not Confirmed.
