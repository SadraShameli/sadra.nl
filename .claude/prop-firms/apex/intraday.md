## Overview

The Intraday Trailing Drawdown path takes a trader from an Intraday Evaluation account, through a one-time PA activation step, into an Intraday Performance Account (PA), Apex's own term for its funded trading stage. Apex confirms the PA is a Simulated Funded account, not a live brokerage account (see Live Transition). Unlike Apex's End-of-Day (EOD) Drawdown product, the Intraday Trailing Drawdown threshold is enforced in real time throughout the session (including unrealized profit and loss) rather than only recalculating at end of day, and it stops trailing once specific profit milestones are reached. Those trigger and locked-floor figures differ between the Evaluation and PA stages, and during the Evaluation stage only, they also differ by trading platform (Rithmic, Tradovate, WealthCharts): see How the Drawdown Works. This file documents the $50,000 size, the only size currently modeled in this repository's simulator engine (`src/lib/prop-calculator/firms/apex/ApexTraderFunding.ts`); sourced figures for the other sizes Apex offers (25K, 100K, 150K) are listed in Not Confirmed for context only. Apex's discontinued Legacy Evaluation/Performance Account product line (retired March 1, 2026, per Legacy Products Overview) is a separate product with its own rules and is out of scope for this file; none of its figures are used here.

## How the Drawdown Works

The Intraday Trailing Threshold follows the account's highest-ever balance (Peak Balance), including unrealized gains, and only ever moves up, never down. It is enforced in real time: if net balance touches or falls below the threshold at any moment during the session, all open positions are liquidated automatically and the account is failed (Evaluation) or closed (PA) immediately. This is a distinct mechanic from a Daily Loss Limit (DLL), which is fixed for the session, does not trail, and only pauses trading for the rest of the day without failing the account.

**Peak Balance is calculated from the next available Bid/Ask, not the last traded price.** In a low-liquidity market (most commonly a non-front-month contract), the Bid/Ask spread can widen sharply, so "your account can be liquidated instantly, even if the market price looks normal." Trading a low-liquidity or non-front-month contract carries real, elevated liquidation risk beyond what the visible market price would suggest.

**A Hedging & Correlated Instruments Rule violation closes the PA immediately.** "Violation of the Hedging & Correlated Instruments Rule results in immediate Performance Account closure," per the Intraday PA article. No hedging-specific figures or definitions are given in this plan's own sources beyond this consequence statement; see legacy.md for the fuller Hedging Rule mechanic documented there for the Legacy product line, which should not be assumed to carry over verbatim.

The point at which the threshold stops trailing and locks at a fixed floor differs by stage, and, during the Evaluation stage, by trading platform:

- **Evaluation, Rithmic and WealthCharts:** the threshold stops trailing once it reaches the Profit Target Balance (Starting Balance plus the profit target, $53,000 for this $50K plan). This occurs once the highest balance reaches Profit Target Balance plus Max Drawdown ($55,000 for this plan). From that point the threshold stays fixed at $53,000.
- **Evaluation, Tradovate:** the threshold never locks. It "trails indefinitely with the peak account balance" for the full life of the Evaluation.
- **Performance Account (all platforms):** the threshold stops trailing once it reaches Starting Balance + $100 ($50,100 for this $50K plan), triggered once the highest balance reaches Starting Balance + Max Drawdown + $100 ($52,100 for this plan). The source states this PA-stage rule without a platform qualifier.

### Worked Example

This example tracks the account's full nominal balance throughout (a $50,000 account showing $900 profit reads as a $50,900 balance), not a $0-based profit figure, and never switches to a $0-based convention.

**Evaluation (Rithmic or WealthCharts):**

1. Trader purchases a $50K Intraday Evaluation. The account opens at a $50,000 balance, a $3,000 profit target, no Daily Loss Limit, and a $2,000 Intraday Trailing Drawdown. Initial threshold: $50,000 - $2,000 = $48,000.
2. Unrealized profit raises the balance intraday to $50,900, a new Peak Balance. The threshold adjusts immediately (no closing trade required) to $50,900 - $2,000 = $48,900.
3. The balance later declines to $50,200. The threshold does not move down; it remains $48,900.
4. The trade closes at $50,300. Peak Balance remains $50,900 and the threshold remains $48,900.
5. On a later session the account reaches $53,000 (Starting Balance $50,000 plus the $3,000 profit target). The profit target is met and no rule has been breached, so the Evaluation passes immediately (no minimum trading-day count applies). Because $53,000 is below the $55,000 balance that would trigger this platform's drawdown-lock, the lock is never reached in this example.

**Performance Account (PA):** sources read for this file do not state whether the PA's opening balance carries forward the Evaluation's ending balance ($53,000 in this example) or resets to the nominal $50,000 starting balance; this example assumes a fresh $50,000 reset (see Not Confirmed), matching the source's own PA worked example, which also opens its narrative at a $50,000 starting balance.

6. Trader completes the 7-calendar-day activation window and the PA opens at a fresh $50,000 balance, a $2,000 Intraday Trailing Drawdown (floor again at $48,000), and a Level 1 Daily Loss Limit of $1,000 with a 2-contract max.
7. Trading continues and the highest balance (realized or unrealized) reaches $52,100 (Starting Balance + Max Drawdown + $100). This is the exact PA drawdown-lock trigger: the threshold stops trailing and locks permanently at $50,100.
8. The account accumulates 5 qualifying trading days, each with at least $200 net profit (the 50K PA's minimum daily profit), and the balance reaches $52,600, the Minimum Balance to Request a payout for this size. Per the source, this figure already reflects the starting balance, the $52,100 Safety Net, and the $500 minimum payout amount.
9. Trader requests the minimum payout of $500. New balance: $52,600 - $500 = $52,100, exactly at the $52,100 Safety Net floor that must be maintained for the lifetime of the PA.

## Live Transition

Apex confirms the Performance Account itself is a Simulated Funded account, not a live account ("No, the Performance Account is a Simulated Funded (Sim Funded) account."). None of this plan's own sources describes a transition from the Intraday PA into a live-capital account. Apex does run a separate, invitation-only live stage, the Apex Live Prop Trading Program, documented in the shared [live.md](live.md): per its FAQ, "Apex may move a trader into live trading at any time based on trading style, P&L, account count, speed of simulated profit accumulation, and trader history with Apex", "Evaluation Accounts and Performance Accounts are closed or deactivated when the trader is selected for live", and "The live account is not based on separate $25K, $50K, $100K, or similar simulated account sizes." No source names the Intraday PA specifically as a feeder into that program, and one cited source uses the phrase "50K Live Intraday Trailing Evaluation"; see Not Confirmed for both.

The stage transition documented by these sources is Evaluation to PA, not PA to Live: once the Evaluation is marked Passed (reviewed at market close, 4:59:59 PM ET, and marked Passed after 6 PM ET), the trader has exactly 7 calendar days to complete a one-time PA Activation Fee from the dashboard; if the fee is not completed within that window, the activation opportunity expires permanently and a new Evaluation must be passed to try again. No help-center article states the activation fee's dollar amount; the homepage product catalog's `pa_features.price` field lists $59 for the 50K Intraday PA, uniform across Rithmic, Tradovate, and Wealthcharts, matching this repo's engine `activation: 59` (catalog-sourced only, see Not Confirmed). A no-activation-fee Evaluation variant, confirmed to exist for the 50K Intraday product, sets this same fee to $0 instead.

**Market data can be lost between passing and PA activation.** If the just-passed Evaluation was the trader's only active Evaluation account, the passed account is immediately locked to prevent further trading, and the trader "will no longer have market data until you activate your new Performance Account or purchase another Evaluation." A trader relying on a single Evaluation account should activate promptly or keep a second Evaluation active to avoid a market-data gap during the 7-day activation window.

**Depth of Market (DOM) data fees are billed and expire differently by platform.** On Rithmic, purchased DOM "will expire on the last day of the month and will not auto-renew. You must manually purchase it again if needed, regardless of the purchase date." On Tradovate and WealthCharts, additional data feeds instead auto-renew and must be cancelled directly through that platform "if you no longer wish to be billed."
