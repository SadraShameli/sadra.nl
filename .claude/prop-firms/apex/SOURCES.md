# Apex Trader Funding — Source Ledger

One row per primary-source document used anywhere in this firm's tree. Update this file every time a source is added, re-fetched, or re-verified; this is what makes a future re-verification pass possible without re-deriving which document said what.

**How these were captured.** `apextraderfunding.com` returns HTTP 403 to `curl` and to any non-browser fetch, and this repo does not use the Wayback Machine. The user ran a fetch script in their own logged-in browser on **2026-09-19** against the live site's `sitemap_index.xml`, producing two dumps: the full help center (129 pages, every URL the sitemap listed under `/help-center/`) and six site pages including the homepage. The help center is a WordPress site whose article pages carry **no machine-readable or displayed "last updated" date**, so every row below reads "not stated" for that column rather than guessing one.

Prices are not in the help center at all. Every dollar price in this tree comes from `window.productPickerConfig`, the JSON blob embedded in the homepage HTML that the site itself renders its Products selector from (90 product records; extracted to `homepage-product-picker-catalog.txt`). Plan files label those figures catalog-sourced, never help-center-sourced.

## Completeness verification (sitemap-sourced, not just what was searched)

- The live `sitemap_index.xml` was fetched in the user's browser on 2026-09-19. **All 129 help-center pages it listed were captured and are on disk**; 127 distinct URLs plus the two site pages captured alongside them.
- **66 are cited** by a plan file or by `README.md`'s Firm-Wide Rules. **63 are not cited.** None was skipped on its title: every one of them was scanned for rule-shaped content (dollar figures, percentages, and the words prohibit, violation, breach, consistency, payout, drawdown, rule, policy, fee), and the ones that scored were read in full. That scan is what surfaced the restricted-countries policy, the one-account rule, trade-violation tracking, the two payout-rail articles, 1099 reporting, and the three per-platform commission schedules, all of which are now carried in `README.md`'s Firm-Wide Rules. The rest carry no rule a trader could breach or be paid under.
