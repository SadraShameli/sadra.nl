# Apex Trader Funding: Source Ledger

One row per primary-source document used anywhere in this firm's tree. Update this file every time a source is added, re-fetched, or re-verified; this is what makes a future re-verification pass possible without re-deriving which document said what.

**How these were captured.** `apextraderfunding.com` returns HTTP 403 to `curl` and to any non-browser fetch, and this repo does not use the Wayback Machine. The user ran a fetch script in their own logged-in browser on **2026-09-19** against the live site's `sitemap_index.xml`, producing two dumps of the help center and of site pages including the homepage. The capture record is internally inconsistent about the counts (129 help-center pages plus six site pages in one place, "127 distinct URLs plus the two site pages" in another); see Completeness verification below, which does not reconcile them. The help center is a WordPress site whose article pages carry **no machine-readable or displayed "last updated" date**, so every row below reads "not stated" for that column rather than guessing one.

Current EOD/Intraday Evaluation, 5-Pack, No Activation Fee and PA-activation prices are not in the help center. Those prices come from `window.productPickerConfig`, the JSON blob embedded in the homepage HTML that the site itself renders its Products selector from (90 product records; extracted to `homepage-product-picker-catalog.txt`), and `eod.md` and `intraday.md` label them catalog-sourced, never help-center-sourced. Other dollar figures in the tree come from their own sources: Legacy's monthly prices and the $125 PA-fee tooltip from the Legacy product page (the catalog holds zero Legacy records), Legacy's $140 PA fee and its reset and billing mechanics from help-center articles, and the Live program's $199 Second Chance price and approximately $140 per month data fee from the Live FAQ.

## Completeness verification (sitemap-sourced, not just what was searched)

- The live `sitemap_index.xml` was fetched in the user's browser on 2026-09-19, and the capture was recorded then as 129 pages ("127 distinct URLs plus the two site pages captured alongside them"). **This ledger does not reconcile with that record**: it names 126 help-center URLs (69 in the cited table, 57 in the excluded list below) and 3 site rows. The sitemap now answers HTTP 403, so the gap cannot be re-diffed, and which captured pages the ledger omits is unresolved.
- **69 help-center pages are cited** by a plan file or by `README.md`; **57 are read and excluded on content**. None was skipped on its title: every one of them was scanned for rule-shaped content (dollar figures, percentages, and the words prohibit, violation, breach, consistency, payout, drawdown, rule, policy, fee), and the ones that scored were read in full. That scan is what surfaced the restricted-countries policy, the one-account rule, trade-violation tracking, the two payout-rail articles, 1099 reporting, the accepted-payment-methods article, and the three per-platform commission schedules, all of which are now cited from `README.md`'s Firm-Wide Rules and listed in the table above. The rest carry no rule a trader could breach or be paid under.
